TMS Hotel Da Nang Beach

4-Star Beachfront Hotel · My Khe Beach

“The only hotel on My Khe that doesn't charge extra for espresso.”

8.1/ 10
Editorial Score
Rooms 7.6 Location 8.9 Facilities 7.8 Food+Drink 8.3 Service 8.2 Value 8.9
Quick Verdict

Book it if you want direct My Khe beach access without paying resort rates. TMS is a 155-room mid-range hotel that gets four specific things right: a rotating breakfast buffet with genuinely good variety, free espresso brought to the table (rare enough on this strip to be worth mentioning), a rooftop infinity pool with a real sunset view over My Khe, and staff who fix problems fast when something goes wrong. No kids' club, no extensive spa, no multiple restaurant formats, TMS doesn't have resort infrastructure and doesn't pretend to. What it has is a good beach, a good breakfast, and room rates that leave budget for the seafood grills two minutes down the sand. If those four things are enough for your trip, TMS is hard to beat at its price point.

Our Take

TMS is one of the only hotels on My Khe Beach that doesn't charge extra for espresso at breakfast. Americano, latte, cappuccino, included, and a server brings it to the table rather than pointing you at a machine in the corner. Every other hotel in this price bracket either charges separately or calls a self-serve machine complimentary. It's a small thing, but it's a specific thing, and it tells you something about how this hotel thinks about its guests. The breakfast buffet itself rotates daily, hot dishes change, local specialties vary, you're not eating the same spread on day four as day one. Multiple guests call it the best breakfast they've had on My Khe Beach at any price point.

The infinity pool on the roof is the main attraction beyond breakfast, a good view, atmospheric in the evening, photographically pleasing. On busy days it gets crowded, it's not a large pool, and the hotel has 155 rooms. The rooftop bar runs alongside it with cocktails and snacks, and the view at sunset over My Khe Beach is one of those things that makes a mid-range hotel feel like it punches above its rate. One honest complaint worth flagging: road noise from below is audible from the rooftop bar on the first night for some upper-floor guests. It settles after the first evening, or you stop noticing it.

The Rooms

The Rooms

Rooms run 30-38 square metres, not large, but clean and maintained consistently. Upper-floor ocean-view rooms deliver views that are genuinely worth the slight premium over the price of a standard category. One guest arrived to find a refrigerator that wasn't working; it was replaced within five minutes, and staff responsiveness is the consistent positive across maintenance reviews. One guest did report finding what they described as a bloodstain on a wall, which is obviously unacceptable and worth flagging on arrival if you see anything similar, hotels at this scale have variation, but the response when things are flagged appears reliable.

Which room would we choose? An upper-floor ocean-view room. The premium over a standard room is modest and the view over My Khe is the main reason to pick this hotel over a similarly priced option a block inland.

The Reason to Book It

The Rooftop Pool

The infinity pool on the roof is the single best reason to pick TMS over a similarly priced hotel set back from the beach. Good view, genuinely atmospheric in the evening, the kind of photo that makes a mid-range hotel look like it costs more than it does.

It gets crowded on busy days, this is not a large pool and the hotel runs 155 rooms, so go early or late if you want space to actually swim rather than just perch at the edge. The rooftop bar alongside it runs cocktails and snacks into the evening.

Pool + Beach

The Beach

Direct My Khe Beach access is the hotel's strongest asset for its price point, no road crossing, no shuttle, no secondary pool that substitutes for sand. Sun lounger and umbrella service sits directly in front of the property. The My Khe seafood restaurant strip runs within 2-5 minutes in either direction, which means most evenings you're eating fresh grilled fish and drinking Saigon beer at a fraction of what an on-site restaurant would cost. Over a week-long stay, the math works out clearly in your favor.

Food + Breakfast

Food + Drink

The rotating breakfast buffet and the included espresso are the two things worth building your morning around here, hot dishes and local specialties change daily so a longer stay doesn't mean repeat meals. For dinner, skip the idea of eating on-site altogether and walk the two minutes to the seafood strip instead.

Our recommendation: eat breakfast at the hotel every morning, it's genuinely one of the better spreads on this stretch of beach, then walk to the grills each evening. That combination is where TMS earns its value.

Who It's For

Families, Couples & Who It Suits

Budget-to-midscale travellers who want to be on the beach without paying resort hotel rates are the clear fit here. Couples looking for a straightforward beach stay, small groups of friends, and families who don't need a kids' club or elaborate facilities all do well at TMS. It won't compete with the Hyatt Regency or Sheraton Grand on room quality or facilities depth, and that isn't the point. The point is clean, well-located beachfront accommodation with good service and walking distance to the My Khe restaurant strip.

The Tradeoff

This is a mid-range hotel, not a resort, and the rooms show it: 30-38 square metres, functional rather than spacious. The rooftop pool is genuinely popular and genuinely small, on busy days you're competing for loungers rather than swimming laps. Road noise from below carries up to the rooftop bar and some upper floors on the first night, most guests report it settling in or becoming background noise by night two. There's no kids' club, no extensive spa, and no multiple restaurant formats, if resort infrastructure matters to your trip, look elsewhere. One guest's account of a bloodstain found on a wall is worth flagging too, it appears to be an isolated maintenance lapse rather than a pattern, but it's the kind of thing worth checking your room for on arrival regardless of where you stay.

Location

Location

Direct My Khe Beach frontage, one of Da Nang's best urban beach stretches, with the seafood restaurant strip 2-5 minutes' walk in either direction. The airport is 8km away, about 12 minutes by taxi, and Dragon Bridge is a similar 12-minute Grab ride. What this means in practice: you can land, be on the sand within 20 minutes, and never need a car again except for a Hoi An day trip. This is a beach-first location, not a sightseeing base, everything within walking distance is beach, food, and the pool. If you want to explore the city centre or Han River regularly, budget taxi time and cost into the trip.

Would We Stay Here

YES

Yes, for the value. The free espresso, the rotating breakfast, the rooftop view, the direct beach access, TMS over-delivers on the things it actually does well. Pay the rate, eat at the hotel every morning, then walk to the seafood grills every evening, and you come out significantly ahead of what a five-star alternative would cost. For that kind of trip, TMS is the most honest value calculation on My Khe Beach.
