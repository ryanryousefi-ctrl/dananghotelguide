Editor's Pick

Where I'd Stay

If you put a gun to my head and asked for one hotel at the beach where almost everyone will be happy, this is the answer. It has the best pool complex on My An Beach, a genuine beach, and rates that are consistently 20-30% below the InterContinental for a broadly comparable experience at the luxury end.

My An Beach — $160-$360 / night

Sheraton Grand Danang Resort

5-star · Beachfront · 15 min from airport

The water complex here is the main reason to book. Six interconnected pools, a lazy river, waterslides, and a proper beach club, all directly on My An Beach. Families use every square metre of it. The rooms are large and well-lit, the breakfast is extensive, and the staff are consistently good in a way that's hard to fake at scale. The beach here is quieter and wider than My Khe, but you'll need a taxi for restaurants and nightlife. That's the honest trade-off: better resort infrastructure, less city convenience. For anyone whose holiday is beach-first, it's the right call.

Who should book luxury

Spending $250 or more a night in Da Nang is justified in three situations: you're on a honeymoon or anniversary where the resort itself is part of the experience, you're staying four or more nights and plan to use the pool complex daily, or you have hotel points that make the rate irrelevant. At that level the InterContinental is in a category of its own — the peninsula location, the multiple infinity pools, and the level of service genuinely have no equivalent in the city. The Hyatt and Sheraton are compelling for points users and for families who need room to spread out. Fusion Maia is the right call if you want all-inclusive spa treatments baked into the rate. For everyone else, the jump from mid-range to luxury costs more than the experience difference justifies. Book the Furama and spend the savings on a day trip to Hoi An.

Who should book mid-range

The $75–$160 bracket is where Da Nang delivers its best value. You get a pool, direct beach access, and usually breakfast included — all on My Khe, one of the longest stretches of accessible urban beach in Southeast Asia. The trade-off versus luxury is room size and service ratio, not location or facilities. If you're spending most of your days on the beach or out exploring the city, you won't miss the extra square footage. Couples on a first visit, solo travellers, and groups splitting costs all do well here. The ceiling of this bracket — Furama at $120 — is genuinely a five-star experience at a four-star price, and it's where most visitors should stop before jumping into the luxury tier.

Who should book boutique or villas

Boutique and villa stays make sense when privacy is the point. The Marriott Non Nuoc Villas give you a standalone structure with your own plunge pool — something the tower hotels on My Khe can't replicate regardless of room category. It suits couples who want to feel like they're at a private retreat rather than a hotel, and families who need space without the chaos of a large resort lobby. The Memory Hotel works differently: it's a long-stay apartment-style property better suited to digital nomads or anyone in Da Nang for work rather than a beach holiday. One thing both have in common — they reward guests who stay put. If your itinerary involves full days at the pool, these deliver. If you're using the hotel as a base for day trips, you'll pay for space you won't use.

Who should book Han River

Han River hotels exist for a specific kind of trip: you're in Da Nang for a short stay, you want Dragon Bridge on the doorstep, and the beach is a Grab ride rather than the main event. The Novotel Han River is the practical choice — consistent, central, Accor points eligible, and 15 minutes from My Khe. The Brilliant is a local institution worth knowing for its rooftop bar views over the river. Neither property is where you want to be for a beach holiday. But if you're transiting between Hoi An and Hue, attending a conference, or doing a 2-night layover before a flight, the Han River area saves you the Grab fare every morning and puts the Han River night market and the city restaurant strip within walking distance. Rates run 30–40% cheaper than comparable beachfront options, which over a short trip adds up.

A Note on Location

The area you choose matters more than the hotel brand. Da Nang's beach zones run north to south: My Khe is the main tourist strip, My An is the large resort corridor, Non Nuoc is the quietest and southernmost. Son Tra Peninsula is something else entirely.

  • My Khe Beach — most walkable, best restaurant access, An Thuong nightlife within reach
  • My An Beach — wider, quieter beach, dominated by large 5-star resorts, taxi-dependent for evenings
  • Non Nuoc Beach — Da Nang's quietest beach, near Marble Mountains, Hoi An day trips 30 min
  • Son Tra Peninsula — protected forest reserve, one hotel (InterContinental), complete seclusion
  • Han River / Hai Chau — city centre, Dragon Bridge, no beach, best for city-only trips

Common Questions

Da Nang Hotels FAQ

Is it better to stay by the beach or the river in Da Nang?

For first-time visitors, the beach is the right call, specifically the My Khe strip. You are 2 minutes from the water, surrounded by Da Nang's best restaurants, and never need a taxi for an evening out. The riverfront (Han River / Hai Chau) suits business travellers and people who want easy access to Dragon Bridge, Han Market, and Hoi An day trips without paying beach-zone premiums. If you can only choose once: beach. The one exception is a business-focused trip of 2-3 nights where city access matters more than sand.

How much do hotels cost in Da Nang in 2026?

Da Nang has the widest price spread of any comparable beach destination in Southeast Asia. Expect to pay $18-35/night for a decent budget guesthouse, $55-110 for a solid 3-4 star property with a pool, $120-200 for quality beachfront with proper resort facilities, and $200-600+ for the true luxury tier (InterContinental, Hyatt Regency, TIA Wellness). Prices drop 25-40% outside peak season. High season runs November through February (cool, dry) and June through August (hot, sunny). The shoulder months of March-May are excellent: fewer crowds, lower prices, still good weather. See our Da Nang travel cost guide for a full breakdown.

When is the best time to visit Da Nang?

March through August is peak beach season: low humidity, sustained sunshine, calm South China Sea, water temperatures around 28°C. The shoulder months of February and September offer excellent conditions with noticeably fewer crowds. November through January is the northeast monsoon: prices are lowest but rain is frequent and ocean swimming is rough. Avoid October if possible, it is statistically the wettest month (average 400mm rainfall). For a detailed month-by-month guide, see our best time to visit Da Nang guide.

Are Da Nang hotels safe?

Da Nang is one of Vietnam's safest cities and consistently ranks among Southeast Asia's safest destinations for solo travellers, including women travelling alone. Petty crime exists as in any tourist area but is genuinely rare by regional standards. Every reputable hotel on this list uses 24-hour security, in-room safes, and key-card access. The beach areas are well-lit and patrolled at night. As of 2026, Da Nang has had no significant security incidents affecting tourism.

Where do Korean tourists stay in Da Nang?

Korean visitors, the largest foreign tourist group in Da Nang, concentrate heavily in the My Khe Beach zone, particularly the northern stretch near An Thuong village. Properties like Wyndham Soleil, TMS Hotel Da Nang Beach, and A La Carte attract significant Korean clientele. The An Thuong neighbourhood has evolved into a Korean-friendly dining and cafe corridor, with Korean-language signage visible in many restaurant windows. Non Nuoc Beach (near the Marble Mountains) is the second preference for Korean group tours.

How far are Da Nang hotels from the airport?

Da Nang International Airport (DAD) is one of the most conveniently positioned airports in Southeast Asia. My Khe Beach hotels: 10-12 minutes. My An Beach hotels: 15-18 minutes. Hai Chau city centre: 15-20 minutes. Non Nuoc Beach: 20-25 minutes. Son Tra Peninsula: 20-30 minutes. Grab taxi is reliable and inexpensive: expect to pay $4-8 for most hotel zones.
