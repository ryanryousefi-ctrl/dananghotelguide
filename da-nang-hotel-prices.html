Best for: luxury, resort trips

Vietnamese summer holidays and international demand hit at the same time, so July–August is when this tier is most expensive. Aim for March–May or October–November (weather permitting) if you want the best rates.

Common Questions

What is the cheapest month to book a hotel in Da Nang?
October and November, no contest. 5-star resorts often run 35–45% below July peak rates. The catch is that these are also Da Nang's rainy and typhoon months, so you're trading price for a weather gamble. If you want the savings without rolling those dice, January (right after New Year) or March–April get you most of the discount with dry skies.
How much does a 5-star hotel in Da Nang cost per night?
In shoulder season (March–May), plan on $190–320/night for the established 5-star resorts along Non Nuoc, places like the Hyatt Regency, Marriott, or Sheraton Grand. Peak season (July–August) pushes those same hotels to $260–420. The InterContinental on Son Tra sits apart from this pattern and typically runs $300–500+ year-round because of where it's positioned.
Is Da Nang cheaper than Bali or Phuket for hotels?
Yes, and not by a little. Comparable 4- and 5-star properties run consistently 30–50% cheaper here. A beachfront 4-star in Da Nang goes for $90–130/night in shoulder season, while the equivalent room in Seminyak or Patong runs $120–200. The gap widens further once you get into the luxury tier.
Does Da Nang have a shoulder season?
It does. March–May is the main one: dry weather, prices below summer levels, and noticeably fewer crowds. September works as a secondary shoulder too, prices keep falling as the weather starts to turn, though typhoon risk is beginning by then. Either window beats paying July–August peak rates.
Are Da Nang hotel prices cheaper on weekdays?
A little, yes, though don't expect a dramatic swing. It shows up most at 3- and 4-star city hotels, where Monday–Thursday can run 10–15% cheaper than weekends. 5-star resorts barely move on this, mostly because resort guests tend to book multiple nights rather than a single weekend.
