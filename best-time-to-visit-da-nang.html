Best Time to Visit Da Nang

When to come for good weather, solid beach days, an easy family trip, or just to dodge the rain.

Local editor - Based in Da Nang since 2022

Editor's Pick - Best All-Round Window

March-April

This is the easiest call for most travelers. It's not as hot as peak summer (temperatures hold around 27-30C) and the rain risk stays low. The beach genuinely delivers: calm seas, clean sand, comfortable for hours at a time. Sightseeing works better too. Son Tra, the Marble Mountains, and the Hoi An day trip are all more pleasant when you're not melting.

Crowds build through April but don't hit the summer peak. Hotel rates are healthy but not inflated. If your dates are flexible, this is the window to aim for.

Cooler temperatures (27-30C)
Low rain risk
Beach: very good
Sightseeing: excellent
Hoi An day trip: excellent
Pre-peak crowds
Da Nang Signature Pick - Peak Family Summer

July-August

Kids are out of school and families travel. The beach is fully switched on: busy, buzzing, water warm enough to swim in all day. Da Nang's resorts and beachfront hotels are at their liveliest. This is when the city actually feels like a summer vacation destination, because that's exactly what it is.

The Fireworks Festival typically runs through June and into July, and the beachfront at night is as energetic as Da Nang gets. If you want a full-on beach holiday with atmosphere attached, this is the window that delivers it.

The Tradeoff Hotter (34-38C), more humid, busier, and often pricier. Outdoor sightseeing needs to be scheduled early morning or late afternoon. Midday is pool time.

Cooler and Quiet

Post-Tet calm settles in. Around 22-24C, comfortable for sightseeing but cool for the beach, and seas can run rough. Hotel rates are lower. A good month for Hoi An and exploring the city.

Warming Up

Weather starts improving, around 24-26C. Tet (late Jan/early Feb) brings closures and domestic travel crowds, so check the dates. Once Tet passes, it's quieter and pleasant.

Beach: Fair
Crowds: Tet spike
March

Editor's Pick Begins

The sweet spot kicks in. 27-29C, low rain risk, the beach in good shape, seas calming down. Comfortable for sightseeing all day, crowds still moderate. The easiest month to visit, full stop.

Rain: Low
Beach: Very Good
Crowds: Moderate

Peak of the Easy Season

29-31C, still manageable, with low rain and an excellent beach. Reunification Day (April 30-May 1) brings a domestic crowd surge, so book early if your dates overlap.

Rain: Low

Getting Hot

31-33C, still fairly dry. The beach is excellent, but midday heat starts to matter. This is the start of hot season, so plan your schedule around it. Good value before peak summer pricing kicks in.

Fireworks + Early Summer

32-35C. The Da Nang International Fireworks Festival typically runs through June. It's hot, but the beach is active and rates are often still reasonable before the summer crush. Good energy around the city.

Signature Pick - Peak Summer

34-36C. School holidays are in full swing, and yes, the beaches are packed, but that's the whole point. Resorts run at capacity. Plan sightseeing for early morning, save midday for the pool. Evenings are excellent.

Hot, Busy, Full Summer

34-36C, the hottest and busiest month of the year. School holidays peak, and resort pools and the beach become the center of daily life. Great for families, but book hotels and activities well ahead.

Temp: 34-36C
Rain: Low-Moderate
Beach: Peak Season
Crowds: Peak
September

Transitional - Watch the Forecast

29-32C, with rainfall picking up. Still manageable, but the weather turns variable. National Day (September 2) brings a crowd bump. Keep an indoor backup plan since rain can derail outdoor days.

Temp: 29-32C
Rain: Moderate
Beach: Fair

October

Rainy Season - High Risk

25-29C. Da Nang's wettest month, with heavy rain, possible typhoons, and flooding in low-lying areas. The beach is a no-go. A lot of visitors underestimate just how much it rains. Travel insurance and flexible plans aren't optional here.

Temp: 25-29C
Rain: Very High
Beach: Poor
Crowds: Low
November

Still Wet - Easing Late Month

23-26C, the second wettest month, with typhoon risk still around. Late November usually improves. Hoi An floods periodically in this window, so check conditions before heading to the Ancient Town.

Temp: 23-26C
Rain: Very High
Beach: Poor
Crowds: Low
December

Recovering - Good for Sightseeing

21-24C, with rain mostly finished. Pleasant for sightseeing and Hoi An trips, but too cool and choppy for beach swimming. Hotel rates run lower than peak season, aside from a brief bump over Christmas and New Year.

Temp: 21-24C
Rain: Low
Beach: Fair-Poor

Rainy Season

What Rainy Season Actually Means

Da Nang's rainfall is unusually concentrated. October and November receive roughly 600-800mm combined - more than many cities get in a full year. This is not occasional showers. It can be sustained heavy rain for days at a stretch, with flooding in lower areas of the city and Hoi An.

Typhoons are possible (not common) during this window. When they hit, outdoor activities are completely off. Beach is closed. Transport to Hoi An and Ba Na Hills may be affected. If you visit October-November, build in flexibility and have indoor options ready. Travel insurance is not optional.

September is transitional and more manageable. December usually clears quickly. If October-November are your only option, go - just plan around it rather than against it.

Common Questions

When is the best time to visit Da Nang?
March and April are the easiest all-round recommendation: warm (27-30C), low rain risk, good beach, comfortable for sightseeing. July and August are peak family summer - hot and busy but excellent beach conditions and full vacation atmosphere. October and November have the highest rainfall and typhoon risk.
Does Da Nang have a rainy season?
Yes. October and November are the wettest months, receiving 600-800mm combined - heavy, sustained rain with possible typhoons. Hoi An can also flood during this period. September is transitional. December usually recovers quickly and can be quite pleasant.
Is Da Nang good in December?
Yes for sightseeing, less so for beach swimming. Rain is mostly over by December. Temperatures are 21-24C - comfortable for exploring but cool for beach. Seas are rough. Good for Hoi An and city exploration. Lower hotel rates than peak season outside of Christmas-New Year.
When is Da Nang most crowded?
June to August is peak domestic and international tourism. Vietnamese public holidays - Reunification Day (April 30-May 1), National Day (September 2), and Tet (late January/February) - also bring significant crowd surges. Book accommodation well in advance for any of these periods.
