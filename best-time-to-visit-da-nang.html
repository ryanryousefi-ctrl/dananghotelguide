When to Go — Da Nang — 2026

Best Time to Visit Da Nang

February to May is the easiest weather window. Here's what each season actually delivers.

Updated 2026 · Local editor

When to Come — From Someone Who Lives Here

The rule of thumb is simple: the earlier in the year, the better. January through May is your window. The weather is dry, the skies are clear, the beach actually looks like the photos you saved on Instagram. March and April are the sweet spot — warm without being punishing, and the crowds haven't fully arrived yet.

After May, things change. June through August brings the kind of heat that rewrites your daily schedule whether you planned for it or not. Then comes the rain. September through November in Da Nang is the kind of wet you've seen in movies — think the Vietnam scenes in Forrest Gump. That rain is real, it is relentless, and nobody wants to deal with typhoon warnings on a week off.

I've been here through all of it. The difference between visiting in March and visiting in October is not just inconvenient — it can completely change your impression of the city. Da Nang in good weather is one of the best places in Southeast Asia. Da Nang in a typhoon is a very different experience.

If you can only travel during the rainy months, go anyway — just plan around it. But if you have flexibility, spend the extra on flights during the dry season. It is worth every dollar.

February – May

Best Overall

Dry, warm, and manageable. The beach is at its best — calm seas, good visibility, clean sand. Temperatures rise from ~26°C in February to ~31°C in May. Crowds build through April but haven't hit summer peak.

June – August

Hot Peak Season

Vietnam's domestic holiday peak. The beach is busy — loud, jet-ski-filled by 10am. Very hot (34–38°C with humidity). Early morning beach visits still work well. Best deals on Ba Na Hills tickets book out; accommodation prices peak.

Temp32–38°C RainLow–Moderate BeachGood (mornings only) CrowdsPeak Best forFamilies, budget flexible visitors
September – November

Wet Season — Avoid Peak Rain

Da Nang's rainy season concentrates heavily in October and November. Typhoons are possible — not common but real. Flooding occurs in low-lying areas. September is transitional. December recovers quickly. October and November are the most risky months for outdoor plans.

Temp24–30°C RainVery High (Oct–Nov) BeachPoor — rough seas CrowdsLow (except Sept holidays) Best forBudget travellers with flexibility
December – January

Cool & Quiet

The rain is mostly gone by December. Temperatures drop to 20–24°C, which is pleasant for sightseeing but cool for beach swimming. Seas are rough. Good for Hoi An visits and city exploration. Tet (late January/early February) brings closures and crowds — check dates for your year.

Common Questions

When is the best time to visit Da Nang?
February to May is the best overall window. March and April hit the sweet spot: dry, warm (26–30°C), beach at its best, and crowds haven't peaked. June to August is good for beach weather but very hot and busy. October and November have the highest rain risk.
Does Da Nang have a rainy season?
Yes. October and November are the wettest months, with the highest typhoon risk. Da Nang's rainfall is heavily concentrated in this window — unusually so compared to the rest of Vietnam's coast. September is transitional; December usually recovers.
Is Da Nang good in December?
Yes for sightseeing, less so for beach swimming. The rain is mostly over by December. Temperatures are pleasant at 20–24°C, but seas are rough and cool. Good for Hoi An visits and city exploration. Cheaper hotel rates than peak season.
When is Da Nang most crowded?
June to August is peak domestic and international tourism. Vietnamese public holidays — Reunification Day (April 30–May 1), National Day (September 2), and Tet (late January/February) — bring significant crowd surges. Book accommodation well in advance for these periods.
