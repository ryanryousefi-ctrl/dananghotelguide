Meliá Da Nang Beach Review

RY
Ryan Yousefi
Da Nang Hotel Guide · Last updated March 2026 · ★★★★ · From $100/night
Mid-RangeMy KheInfinity PoolCouples

The Melia is Da Nang's most underrated Non Nuoc resort — consistently good value in a cluster where the Hyatt and Marriott absorb most of the attention and the Melia quietly delivers comparable quality for less.

The Melia Da Nang Beach sits in the southern Non Nuoc cluster at a price point that typically runs 15–20% below the Hyatt and Marriott for comparable room categories. That gap is hard to justify when you look at what the Melia actually delivers: a long beachfront pool, direct Non Nuoc access, well-maintained rooms, and a breakfast operation that gets consistently strong reviews.

The brand is Spanish — Meliá Hotels International — which is less immediately recognisable to most international travellers than Hyatt or Marriott. That's probably part of why rates are lower. The operations are to the same standard. The beach position is indistinguishable from the Hyatt's.

I'd recommend the Melia to anyone who asks me which Non Nuoc resort offers the best value right now. The answer has been the same for three years.

Location & Getting There

19 Truong Sa Street, Ngu Hanh Son District — Non Nuoc Beach, 13km from Da Nang city centre. Same resort strip as the Hyatt Regency and Marriott Resort. Airport is 20–22 minutes. Hoi An is 30–35 minutes. Marble Mountains are 12 minutes by Grab.

The Non Nuoc trade-off applies here as with all properties on this strip: excellent beach quality, but city dining requires Grab trips that compound over a week-long stay. An Thuong restaurant district is 18–20 minutes north. The beach and on-property facilities are strong enough that most guests stop counting trips by day three.

Beach quality on Non Nuoc at this position is consistently good — wide, clean, calmer water than My Khe, with the Marble Mountains visible from the shoreline. For beach-first trips, this is one of the better positions in Da Nang.

The Rooms

Rooms run 48–60sqm in standard categories — generous dimensions by any four/five-star standard. The design is contemporary with warm tones and Vietnamese decorative touches that don't feel laboured. Sea-view rooms from mid-floors upward have proper ocean views. Balconies are usably sized.

Beds use Meliá's signature sleep concept — good quality, reliably comfortable. Bathrooms are above average: rain showers standard, tubs in premium categories. AC is powerful and quiet. Blackout curtains work. The room maintenance standard is consistent throughout a stay, not just on arrival day.

The Meliá loyalty programme (MeliáRewards) is less widely used than Bonvoy or Hilton Honors, which means elite status upgrade probability here is higher than at the Marriott or Sheraton where Platinum members are common. If you have Meliá Gold or Platinum status, the room upgrade likelihood is meaningful. Rates from $100–150/night in shoulder season.

Pool, Beach & Facilities

The pool complex is a long beachfront pool positioned to face the ocean — genuinely functional as a swimming pool while also having the aesthetic appeal of the horizon view. Pool management is good: lounger availability is maintained into late morning in peak season, which is a discipline many Non Nuoc resorts fail at.

Non Nuoc Beach in front of the Melia is the same quality as the rest of the southern Non Nuoc strip — wide, clean, properly attended. Beach service with sunbeds and umbrellas runs efficiently. Lifeguards are present during beach hours.

Spa treatments are a highlight — therapist quality and treatment depth comparable to Furama's spa operation, which is the benchmark for this price bracket in Da Nang. Pre-booking strongly recommended in peak season as the spa reaches capacity. Kids' club is present and supervised.

Food & Drink

Alma Restaurant handles breakfast and all-day dining. Breakfast is the strongest meal — a wide Vietnamese and international spread with made-to-order sections. The pho station is proper and the Vietnamese breakfast selection is better than most Non Nuoc resort offerings. Take a slow morning: the breakfast room overlooks the garden and pool.

La Playa beach bar is the afternoon and evening social point — cocktails, beach views, light food service that extends into dinner-adjacent territory on busy nights. The happy hour pricing is reasonable by resort standards.

For serious dinner: An Thuong and the city are a Grab trip away. Two or three evenings leaving the resort for Nen, Madame Lan's, or the Bach Dang Street seafood area is the right mix for a week-long stay. The Melia's in-house dining covers the other nights.

Same Non Nuoc beach, comparable quality, lower rate than the Hyatt or Marriott — the Melia is the answer to the question 'where's the best value on this strip?'

The Good Stuff

  • Consistently 15–20% cheaper than Hyatt and Marriott for comparable Non Nuoc beach positioning
  • Breakfast quality among the best on the Non Nuoc strip
  • Higher upgrade probability for MeliáRewards elites than at Bonvoy-saturated competitors
  • Beachfront pool designed for actual swimming, not just aesthetics
  • Spa therapist quality consistently above the Non Nuoc average

Watch Out For

  • Meliá brand less recognised internationally than Marriott/Hyatt — which is why it's cheaper
  • No private pool villa category comparable to Marriott's beach villas
  • City dining still requires Grab — same Non Nuoc constraint as all southern resorts
  • Kids' club less developed than Camp Hyatt

Who Should Stay Here

Couples, honeymooners, and adults who want a genuine luxury beach resort with strong spa facilities at the most competitive price among the Non Nuoc five-stars. The wrong choice for families with young children.
