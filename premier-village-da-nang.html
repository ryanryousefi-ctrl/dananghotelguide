Premier Village Danang Resort

“A villa host on WhatsApp who knows your name before your taxi reaches the gate.”

Our Take

Premier Village does something unusual: months before your stay, a dedicated villa host creates a WhatsApp group and starts responding to questions.

By the time the taxi pulls through the gates, the staff waiting at the entrance already know your name, how many are traveling, and what you asked for, without reading it off a clipboard.

The position on Son Tra Peninsula is hard to describe without it sounding oversold.

The resort occupies the southern tip, so the sea is in almost every direction: My Khe Beach stretching south on one side, the quieter waters of the bay on the other, the Son Tra forest rising behind.

At dusk, the colors in the water change faster than expected.

The combination of the peninsula position, the private pool with sea views, waking up to the sun already rising over the water, and a team that knew who you were before you showed up, that's not something you can replicate by booking a nicer room at a regular hotel.

This is the kind of stay you build the trip around, specifically for the beachfront villas rather than the hillside category.

The Rooms

The Villas

All 111 villas have private infinity pools. Two-bedroom hillside villas run around 200 square metres, three-bedroom beachfront options push to 600. The beachfront villas are the point of the place for families, multiple bedrooms, a fully equipped kitchen and dining area, outdoor living space, and a pool that faces the water. Design is contemporary tropical: high ceilings, local timber, indoor-outdoor flow that actually works rather than gesturing toward it.

The buggies run on-demand 24 hours, and the operator gives an accurate arrival estimate, a small thing that matters when you're managing small children between buildings. Which room would we choose? A three-bedroom beachfront villa if traveling as a family, worth the jump from hillside for the kitchen and the near-instant beach access.

The Reason to Book It

Three-Sided Ocean Views + Private Pools

Every villa comes with its own private pool, and the Son Tra Peninsula position gives three-sided ocean views no other Da Nang resort can offer. Kids can swim in the villa pool while adults sit with drinks and watch the horizon, and what stays in your mind isn't the design language so much as waking up, pulling back the curtains, and finding the sun already rising over the sea.

The buggies running 24 hours on-demand with accurate ETAs make the layout work in practice, some older reviews flagged wait times, but recent stays consistently praise the service as reliable now.

Pool + Beach

Pool + Beach

The private beach in front of the resort is clean and quiet, with full sun lounger and beach service, and the water here is generally calmer than the open ocean sections further south. The buggies run down to the sand without having to ask twice, and if you book a beachfront villa, you're close enough to walk in under two minutes. Between the villa pool and the beach, there's rarely a reason to want for more water time during a stay.

Food + Breakfast

Breakfast at Lemongrass

The breakfast spread at Lemongrass restaurant is the kind that makes you slow down. There's a pho counter with broth and toppings you assemble yourself, a salad bar that has no business being as good as it is before 9am, fresh fruit, pastries, and hot dishes that rotate. Staff take tea and coffee orders at the table and bring them personally, they don't point you at a machine, and by the second morning they've usually remembered what you had.

Dining is limited to on-property options otherwise, so plan accordingly. If you want to eat street food every night, budget for taxis both ways, most guests staying three or four nights don't find that a real problem given how much there is to do without leaving.

Who It's For

Families, Couples & Who It Suits

This is arguably the best family resort option in the city: children swim safely in their own villa pool, the beach is directly accessible, and the layout gives families genuine privacy that a standard hotel room can't match. Couples do just as well in the smaller hillside villas, where the same private pool and dual-ocean setting apply without the extra bedrooms. It suits travelers who will spend most of their time on the beach and around the villa rather than chasing an active resort social scene, the gap in dining depth and activities compared to the Hyatt Regency or InterContinental is largely irrelevant if that's your trip style.

What We Like

What We Like

Not a shared feature, beachfront villas in particular are among the best private-pool product in Vietnam.

Build-your-own broth and toppings, plus tableside coffee service that remembers your order by day two.

The Tradeoff

You're 22 minutes from Dragon Bridge and 40 minutes from Hoi An, not prohibitive, but not walkable-to-anything either. The resort functions as a destination in itself, and most guests staying three or four nights don't find that a problem, but if you want street food every night, budget for taxis both ways. All-villa format means no entry-level room option, so rates reflect that from the start. Dining is limited to on-property, three restaurants isn't a deep scene. Peak season, December through February, books out fast, confirm well in advance for beachfront villas specifically.

Location

Location

On the tip of Son Tra Peninsula, directly facing My Khe Beach, about 10km from the city centre and 13km from the airport. What this means in practice: you're in resort mode, not city-base mode. Dragon Bridge is 22 minutes by taxi and Hoi An is about 40 minutes, both doable as day trips but not casual pop-ins. The upside is the position itself, sea on nearly every side, which no city-centre or My Khe-strip hotel can replicate. The My Khe restaurant strip is a five-minute walk if you want fresh seafood grills without a full taxi trip into town.

Common Questions

Premier Village Danang Resort: FAQ

Is Premier Village Danang Resort worth staying at in Da Nang?

Yes, for families and groups wanting private villa accommodation. Premier Village is one of the most impressive family-focused villa resorts in Da Nang, combining beachfront access with genuinely private living spaces.

How much does Premier Village Danang Resort cost per night?

Villa rates start from around $300/night in low season. Peak season villas run $500–$900+ per night.

Where is Premier Village Danang Resort located in Da Nang?

On the tip of Son Tra Peninsula, directly facing My Khe Beach. About 10 km from the city centre and 13 km from Da Nang Airport.
