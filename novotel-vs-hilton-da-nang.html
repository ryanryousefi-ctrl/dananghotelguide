Hotel Overview

Novotel Danang Premier Han River

  • ★★★★★ Upscale city hotel
  • 323 rooms, one of Da Nang's tallest hotels
  • Han River, Tran Phu Street
  • Rooftop pool + sky bar on 36F
  • Accor ALL loyalty programme
  • From $80/night

Hilton Da Nang

  • ★★★★★ Full-service city hotel
  • 258 rooms
  • Han River, Bach Dang Street
  • Rooftop infinity pool with river views
  • Hilton Honors loyalty programme
  • From $90/night

Both hotels are strong picks for the Da Nang riverfront. The Novotel is taller, sitting above the Han River with high-floor rooms offering unobstructed views, and slightly more affordable.

The Hilton is on Bach Dang Street's prime Dragon Bridge strip and has the better pool setup. Both give you easy access to Da Nang's best restaurants, shopping, and the fireworks viewpoint near Dragon Bridge.

The Hilton is positioned slightly closer to Dragon Bridge, relevant if you're visiting for the Da Nang International Fireworks Festival (DIFF). Both hotels are excellent bases for exploring the city without a car, with the night market, Han Market, and riverside promenade all walkable.

Both hotels offer comparable amenities. The Novotel's Level 36 Sky Bar is one of Da Nang's most popular rooftop spots, a genuine asset for a pre-dinner drink with river views.

The Hilton's rooftop infinity pool is considered the more photogenic of the two. Neither has beach access directly (both are city hotels), so plan your beach transport in advance.

Who Each Hotel Is Best For

Choose Novotel Danang Premier if…

  • You're an Accor ALL member
  • High-floor river panorama views are a priority
  • Budget is slightly tighter, Novotel often runs cheaper
  • The Level 36 Sky Bar appeals
  • Visiting for business with an executive floor
  • You prefer Accor's reward ecosystem

Choose Hilton Da Nang if…

  • You're a Hilton Honors member (upgrades, points)
  • The rooftop infinity pool is a priority
  • Dragon Bridge fireworks viewing matters, closer position
  • You prefer Hilton's room quality and consistency
  • DIFF fireworks festival stay, prime riverside location
  • Travelling with a partner, pool atmosphere is better

Final Verdict

For loyalty members, the decision is straightforward: Accor ALL goes Novotel, Hilton Honors goes Hilton. Both programmes offer meaningful upgrades at these properties.

For everyone else: if you want the best views from your room, book a high-floor river-facing room at the Novotel, its height advantage is real. If the pool is your priority, the Hilton's rooftop infinity setup is slightly better for the Instagram shot and for leisure swimming.

For the Da Nang International Fireworks Festival, the Hilton's Bach Dang position edges ahead, you're a five-minute walk from the prime viewing spot near Dragon Bridge.

Common Questions

Novotel vs Hilton Da Nang: FAQ

Is Novotel or Hilton better in Da Nang?+

Both are strong Han River hotels. Novotel Premier has height on its side, high-floor rooms have impressive river panoramas and Level 36 is one of Da Nang's best sky bars.

Hilton has the better rooftop pool and is marginally closer to Dragon Bridge for fireworks viewing. The right choice depends on your loyalty programme and what you value most.

Which is best for Dragon Bridge fireworks viewing?+

Hilton Da Nang has the edge, it's on Bach Dang Street, a five-minute walk from the best Dragon Bridge viewing areas. Both hotels work for the DIFF fireworks festival, but the Hilton is slightly more convenient.
