Is Hoi An
Cheaper?

Hotels, food, transport, and daily costs compared honestly. The answer depends on how you travel, where you eat, and where you stay.

Home › Hoi An › Is Hoi An Cheaper Than Da Nang?

The short answer

Hoi An is slightly cheaper at the budget end and slightly more expensive at the mid-range. If you eat street food and stay in guesthouses, both cities cost about the same. If you're sitting in Old Town tourist restaurants three times a day, Hoi An will cost noticeably more than Da Nang. Transport inside Hoi An is more expensive once you're relying on bicycle hire and tuk-tuks instead of Grab.

The Hoi An trap

Many people spend more in Hoi An than expected because tourist restaurants are inescapable inside the Ancient Town. Walk 10 minutes to residential streets and prices drop back to Da Nang levels. The same logic applies to the My Khe beach strip in Da Nang — the premium is location, not the city itself.

The real comparison

"Budget travellers often find Hoi An cheaper simply because there's less to do. No clubs, no late-night bar tabs, no impulse taxis across a big city."

Bottom line

Which city wins on cost?

Neither city wins cleanly. It comes down to how you travel.

Hoi An is cheaper if you...

  • Stay in An Bang or residential areas, not Old Town
  • Eat at local spots away from the tourist zone
  • Hire a bicycle and stick to the Old Town area
  • Skip nightlife, which barely exists in Hoi An
  • Would otherwise take day trips from Da Nang (each trip costs 400,000–600,000 VND round trip)

Da Nang is cheaper if you...

  • Eat out frequently at sit-down restaurants
  • Move around a lot by Grab or taxi
  • Need supermarket access for groceries
  • Want more accommodation choice at lower prices
  • Stay a week or more where daily transport costs compound

The combined-trip calculation

If you're planning to see both cities and deciding where to base yourself, factor in transfer costs. Da Nang to Hoi An by Grab costs 200,000–300,000 VND each way. Four day trips across a week adds up to 1,600,000–2,400,000 VND in transfers alone — sometimes more than just staying in Hoi An would have cost.

Common questions

Cost comparison FAQ

Is Hoi An cheaper than Da Nang?
It depends on the category. Street food is similar in both cities. Hoi An's Old Town tourist restaurants are noticeably more expensive than comparable Da Nang spots. Transport inside Hoi An costs more once you're relying on tuk-tuks and bicycle hire rather than Grab. Mid-range hotels are similar; budget guesthouses are sometimes cheaper in Hoi An outside the Old Town. Da Nang wins on transport and daily living costs; Hoi An's slower pace means people often just spend less overall.
How much does a budget day in Hoi An cost?
A genuine budget day, hostel dorm or cheap guesthouse, street food meals, bicycle hire, free Old Town wandering, runs $20–30 USD per person. Add a cooking class or boat trip and you're at $50–60. Sitting in Old Town tourist restaurants for every meal pushes a day to $60–80 easily.
Are restaurants more expensive in Hoi An?
Tourist-facing restaurants in the Ancient Town are noticeably more expensive than similar spots in Da Nang. Local com tam and bun joints around residential Hoi An are comparable in price, but you walk further from the tourist zone to find them. Da Nang's My Khe beach strip has its own expensive tourist pricing, so location within each city matters as much as the city itself.
Is Hoi An or Da Nang better value for a week-long stay?
For a week, Da Nang often works out better value — more accommodation choice, cheaper transport, easier supermarket access, and less tourist-price inflation on daily necessities. But Hoi An's slower pace means many people spend less just because there's less to spend money on. If you'd be taking day trips from Da Nang to Hoi An, staying in Hoi An can actually be cheaper overall when you factor in transfer costs each way.
Can I do both cities on a budget trip?
Yes. The most common approach is 2–3 nights Da Nang and 3–4 nights Hoi An, or vice versa. Getting between them costs 200,000–300,000 VND by Grab. Many budget travellers stay in Hoi An's An Bang area (slightly cheaper accommodation, beach access) and day-trip to Da Nang rather than the reverse.
