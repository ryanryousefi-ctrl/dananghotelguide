Hoi An vs Da Nang

Is Hoi An Cheaper Than Da Nang?

Hotels, food, coffee and transport, compared honestly rather than with a made-up daily budget number.

Updated August 2026

Quick Answer

Hoi An is slightly cheaper at the budget end and slightly more expensive at the mid-range. If you eat street food and stay in guesthouses, both cities cost about the same. If you're sitting in Old Town tourist restaurants three times a day, Hoi An will cost noticeably more than Da Nang. Transport inside Hoi An is pricier too, once you're relying on bicycle hire and tuk-tuks instead of Grab.

Worth knowing

The Hoi An Trap

Many people spend more in Hoi An than expected because tourist restaurants are inescapable inside the Ancient Town. Walk 10 minutes to residential streets and prices drop back to Da Nang levels.

The same logic applies to the My Khe beach strip in Da Nang, the premium is location, not the city itself.

The tradeoff

Which City Wins on Cost?

Hoi An is cheaper if you...
  • Stay in An Bang or residential streets
  • Eat local, not tourist restaurants
  • Hire a bicycle instead of taxis
  • Skip nightlife entirely
  • Would otherwise day-trip from Da Nang (400,000-600,000 VND round trip each time)
Da Nang is cheaper if you...
  • Eat out frequently
  • Move around by Grab or taxi
  • Need supermarket access
  • Want more accommodation choice
  • Stay a week or more, where daily transport costs compound
The Combined-Trip Calculation

If you're seeing both cities and deciding where to base yourself, factor in transfer costs. Da Nang to Hoi An by Grab runs 200,000-300,000 VND each way. Four day trips across a week adds up to 1,600,000-2,400,000 VND in transfers alone, sometimes more than just staying in Hoi An the whole time would have cost.

Our Verdict

Hoi An wins on boutique-hotel value. Da Nang wins on sheer choice.
Neither city is dramatically cheaper once you account for what each dollar actually buys.
