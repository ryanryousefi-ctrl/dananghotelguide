Quick Picks

The best budget hotels in Da Nang aren't always the cheapest ones. Location, walkability, and whether you'll actually enjoy the area matter more than saving $8–12 a night.

For most first-time visitors, a mid-tier budget hotel near My Khe Beach or the Han River at $35–55/night is a smarter booking than an ultra-cheap option that forces constant Grab rides.

Cheap vs Good Value in Da Nang
The Cheapest Room Is Not Always the Smartest Booking

A hotel can be cheap but still a bad deal. If it's 3km from the beach with no food options nearby, you're spending $5–8/day on Grab rides that eliminate whatever you saved on the room.

If it's in a construction corridor, you're waking up at 7am to drilling through the wall. If the reviews are 18 months old, the quality you're paying for may not exist anymore.

Da Nang has genuine budget value, but you have to look past the price column to find it.

Local Perspective
Where I'd Stay on a Budget

The $40–70 range delivers genuinely good hotels in Da Nang. A La Carte Beach Hotel sits on My Khe with a rooftop pool — it competes with 4-star hotels at a fraction of what you'd pay in Phuket.

Below $40/night, quality drops noticeably. The photos often don't match reality.

If you're on a tight budget, I'd rather spend $50 somewhere with clean rooms and an honest location than $25 at a place 3km from the beach requiring a Grab every time.

The best budget strategy in Da Nang is to pick a hotel on or very near the My Khe strip. You'll spend less on transport and eat better, because the street food around An Thuong is cheaper than anything hotel-adjacent at most non-central locations.

Worth Paying Extra For?

Worth It Walkable beach location. The premium for a hotel actually on or within 200m of My Khe Beach is $10–20/night. For a beach holiday, that's worth every dollar, you'll use the access every day.
Worth It Breakfast included. A hotel at $42 with breakfast often beats a $34 hotel without when you factor in the $5–8 cost of buying breakfast nearby. Ask before you assume it's included.
Worth It Newer building. Hotels built or renovated in the last 5 years in Da Nang tend to have significantly better soundproofing, WiFi infrastructure, AC efficiency, and bathrooms. The $10–15/night premium over older comparable properties is usually worth it.
Sometimes Worth It Rooftop pool. A hotel with a rooftop pool costs $10–20 more than one without. In Da Nang's heat, the pool gets used. If you're staying 4+ nights, it's worth it. For a 2-night transit stay, probably not.

Where Budget Travelers Go Wrong

⚠
Common Budget Hotel Mistakes

Booking only by lowest price. A $19 hotel that's 4km from the beach and restaurants isn't cheap, it's expensive once you add Grab rides. Ignoring location on the map. "Near the beach" can mean anything from 50m to 2km in hotel marketing.

Open Google Maps and check before you book. Choosing a hotel without nearby food. Da Nang has excellent local restaurants but they're not evenly distributed, check that there are options walking distance from your hotel.

Not reading recent reviews. Da Nang's hotel quality changes quickly, a hotel with great reviews from 18 months ago may have declined. Filter for reviews from the last 6 months.

Expecting luxury service at ultra-budget prices. A $20/night hotel in Da Nang is a $20/night hotel, clean bed, basic breakfast if you're lucky, limited English. Set expectations accordingly.

Booking during rainy season without adjusting expectations. October–November monsoon rains change how much you'll use the beach and outdoor spaces. A hotel with good indoor common areas matters more then.

The Honest Verdict

For most budget travelers visiting Da Nang for the first time, the right call is a well-reviewed hotel near My Khe Beach or An Thượng at $35–55/night. That tier gets you beach access, a walkable neighborhood, breakfast, decent WiFi, and a hotel that won't frustrate you.

It's not the cheapest option in the city, but it's the one most likely to result in a good trip.

If you're traveling solo on a tight budget or just need a transit stay, the $20–30 city-centre options (A25, Nhat Linh, Dai A) are perfectly functional. Don't expect the beach vacation experience, just clean and reliable.

Avoid the temptation to book the absolute cheapest option without checking the map. In Da Nang, convenience has a real monetary value: a hotel $15/night cheaper but 3km further from the action costs you $5–8/day in Grab rides and wasted time.

The math rarely works out.

Frequently Asked Questions

Budget Hotels in Da Nang FAQs

What is the best budget hotel area in Da Nang?
My Khe Beach is the best all-round area for budget travelers, beach access, walkable restaurants and cafes, and a wide hotel range from $20 guesthouses to $60 mid-range. An Thượng (just inland from My Khe) is the second-best option for café culture and walkability. The Han River area works well for city-focused travelers who don't prioritize beach access.
Can you get a good hotel in Da Nang under $50?
Yes, $35–50 is Da Nang's sweet spot for budget travel. At that price you can find clean, modern hotels with breakfast, decent WiFi, and good locations near My Khe Beach or the Han River. Under $25, the options are more basic, fine for short stays or solo travelers, but not the polished beach hotel experience most tourists expect.
Is it better to stay near My Khe Beach or Han River on a budget?
My Khe Beach for most travelers. You get beach access, more walkable restaurant and café options, and better hotel variety at budget prices. The Han River area suits travelers who prioritize city exploration and Dragon Bridge over beach time, it's closer to the riverfront bars and restaurants, but 15–20 min from the beach.
Are cheap hotels in Da Nang safe?
Yes, Da Nang is one of the safest cities in Vietnam. The main risks at very cheap hotels are quality issues (dated rooms, thin walls, poor WiFi) rather than personal safety. Read recent reviews on Booking.com or Google before committing to anything under $20/night.
What should budget travelers avoid when booking hotels in Da Nang?
Booking by price alone without checking location. A hotel 3–4km from the beach feels fine on a map but costs $5–8/day in Grab rides that eliminate any savings. Also: hotels with reviews more than 18 months old, hotels on active construction corridors (check Street View), and serviced apartments marketed as hotels (hotel prices, apartment-level service).
Is breakfast included at budget hotels in Da Nang?
Often yes at $35–60 hotels, sometimes yes at $25–35 hotels, rarely at under $25. A hotel at $38/night with breakfast included often beats a $30/night hotel without it when you factor in the $4–6 cost of buying breakfast nearby. Always confirm before booking.
Is Non Nuoc a good budget area?
Not ideal for most budget travelers. Non Nuoc is 20–25 minutes south of Da Nang and primarily has large resort hotels. Sandy Beach Non Nuoc Resort offers decent value for a semi-resort experience, but the isolation means you'll spend more on transport and food. For budget travelers wanting beach access, My Khe gives much better overall value.
How far in advance should I book a cheap hotel in Da Nang?
For June–August (peak season), book 3–4 weeks ahead. For shoulder months (March–May, September–November), 1–2 weeks out is usually fine. During the monsoon (October–November), last-minute bookings get the best prices as hotels discount to fill rooms. December and January are busier than expected around Vietnamese holidays, book early.
