Cheap Hotels in Da Nang: Where to Stay for Less in 2026

Da Nang has hundreds of inexpensive places to sleep, but when you're paying this little, location can matter more than the room. Use the map below to find a cheap hotel near the part of the city you actually plan to explore.

Location First

Where you sleep changes the trip

Cheap-hotel shoppers often care less about where they sleep than what the room costs, and that's perfectly reasonable in Da Nang, where inexpensive hotels can cost less than a meal in many Western cities. But the location still changes the trip. A cheap room near My Khe lets you walk to the beach, while a cheap room downtown puts the Han River, restaurants and city sights closer. If your hotel is mainly somewhere to shower, sleep and leave your luggage, use the map before worrying about anything else.

Common Questions

Cheap Hotels in Da Nang FAQ

Where are the cheapest hotels in Da Nang?

Spread across the whole city, not concentrated in one area. You'll find inexpensive options near My Khe Beach, in the An Thuong grid, around the city center in Hai Chau, along the Han River, and in the quieter Da Nang Bay area near Thanh Khe. Use the map above to see exactly where each one sits.

Is it better to stay near the beach or downtown on a cheap budget?

Depends what you're doing each day. A cheap room near My Khe means you can walk to the sand. A cheap room downtown puts Dragon Bridge, the Han River and city restaurants closer, but you'll take a Grab to the beach. Neither is objectively better, it's about which part of the city you'll actually spend time in.

Are cheap hotels in Da Nang safe?

Yes. Da Nang is one of the safer cities in Vietnam, and that doesn't change much at the budget end. The main trade-offs with very cheap hotels are usually about room quality, dated furniture, thin walls, basic wifi, not personal safety.

Can I find cheap hotels near Hoi An too?

Yes. A handful of inexpensive hotels and hostels sit around Hoi An, An Bang Beach and Cua Dai, listed separately near the bottom of this page since it's a different part of the trip than central Da Nang.

Do cheap hotels in Da Nang include breakfast?

It varies by property and isn't something we list here since it changes often. Check the specific hotel's current amenities on the booking page before you reserve.
