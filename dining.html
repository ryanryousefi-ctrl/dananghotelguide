One Michelin Star — Da Nang's only

La Maison 1888

Da Nang's only Michelin Star restaurant sits in a restored colonial villa at the InterContinental Sun Peninsula Resort, set into a forested hillside above a private bay. French fine dining with Vietnamese ingredients, executed with precision. The setting is among the most dramatic in Southeast Asia.

Reserve the table by the window. Book dinner weeks ahead in peak season. The resort runs a complimentary city transfer when you call ahead.

Son Tra Peninsula · ₫₫₫₫ · Tasting menus from approx. $120

Michelin Green Star

Nén Danang

Vietnam's first Michelin Green Star restaurant

Chef-owner Summer Le built Nén's seasonal tasting menu on a single principle: 99% local sourcing. She works directly with fishing families and small farms across central Vietnam. The kitchen's output is contemporary Vietnamese cooking that actually tastes of where it comes from. The Green Star recognizes commitment to sustainable gastronomy — distinct from a traditional Michelin Star.

Ngũ Hành Sơn · 16 My Da Tay 2 Street · ₫₫₫ · Book 48 hours ahead on weekends

About the 2026 Michelin Guide in Da Nang

What Michelin actually means here

Michelin has covered Vietnam since 2023. Da Nang's 2026 selection spans 47 establishments: one Michelin Star (La Maison 1888), one Green Star (Nén Danang), 23 Bib Gourmand listings, and 23 Michelin Selected. Nén holds both a Green Star and appears in the Selected category, which is standard Michelin practice.

The Bib Gourmand category is particularly useful for visitors — it identifies restaurants offering excellent food at reasonable prices, generally under $20 per person. Most of Da Nang's Bib Gourmand entries are under $5.

What's new in 2026

Three restaurants joined the Bib Gourmand list in 2026: Bà Vui (chicken noodle soup and sticky rice, Hải Châu), Bánh Bèo – Bánh Đập (Central Vietnamese rice cakes, Hải Châu), and Lê Gia Khang (bún bò Huế xí quách, Ngũ Hành Sơn). One restaurant entered the Selected category: Dì Hoa (rice paper snacks, Hải Châu).

Bib Gourmand: best value picks

For most visitors, the Bib Gourmand list is where the real value is. The recognition is specifically for restaurants that offer outstanding food at moderate prices. Almost all of Da Nang's Bib Gourmand restaurants serve breakfast and lunch-focused Vietnamese street food under $5 per person. View all Bib Gourmand restaurants →

Da Nang's food geography

The best cheap eats cluster in Hải Châu district, particularly along Le Hong Phong Street (three Michelin-recognized noodle shops in one block). The beachfront strip along Vo Nguyen Giap has the best seafood options. Ngũ Hành Sơn has the highest concentration of Bib Gourmand restaurants south of the Han River. Fine dining concentrates at resort properties on the Son Tra Peninsula. Neighbourhood guide →

What to order in Da Nang

The city's own dishes: mì Quảng (turmeric noodles with pork, shrimp, peanuts, fried rice crackers), bún chả cá (fish cake vermicelli), and bánh tráng thịt heo (grilled pork with rice paper). All three appear on the Michelin list. Bún bò Huế — Hue-style spicy beef noodle soup — is technically a Hue dish but Da Nang does an excellent version. Seafood is cheapest and freshest at the local market-style restaurants rather than the hotel dining rooms.

Green Star vs. Michelin Star

The Michelin Green Star is a distinct recognition for sustainability in gastronomy — awarded for a restaurant's sourcing practices, waste reduction, and environmental commitment, not for the food's technical excellence alone. Nén Danang received Vietnam's first Green Star in 2024 and retains it in 2026. The Green Star does not replace a traditional star; Nén also appears in the Michelin Selected category.
