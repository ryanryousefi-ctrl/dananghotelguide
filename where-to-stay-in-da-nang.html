Da Nang Area Guide · 2026

Where to Stay in Da Nang

My Khe, An Thuong, the Han River, Son Tra or Non Nuoc? Pick the right part of Da Nang first. The hotel comes second.

Updated August 2026 · Written by a local · Five areas, honest trade-offs

Editor's Pick — Best Area for Most First-Time Visitors

My Khe Beach

The safest answer when you don't know Da Nang yet

If this is your first trip and you're not sure where to stay, don't overthink it. My Khe is the right call. You get a long, clean beach on your doorstep, hotels at every price point from $40 to $250, walkable access to the An Thuong restaurant and bar strip, and Grab availability that makes everywhere else in Da Nang feel 10 minutes away. The airport is 10 minutes by taxi. The Dragon Bridge is 20. Son Tra Peninsula starts at the northern end of the same beach. Almost everything worth doing in Da Nang is reachable from here without it being a production.

The tradeoff: this is the tourist-heavy side of Da Nang. Hotel towers, tour groups, beach bars. If you want a more genuinely Vietnamese city experience rather than a resort strip, the Han River is the better answer. But for a first trip — or any trip where you want beach, convenience and good food nearby — My Khe is hard to beat.

Beach on the doorstep An Thuong walkable All price points 10 min to airport Easy Grab everywhere

Tradeoff: hotel-heavy and tourist-oriented. Han River suits those wanting a more local city feel.

Da Nang Signature Pick — Best Area for Walkability

An Thuong

Where we'd stay if being able to walk everywhere matters

An Thuong isn't a separate city — it sits just inland from My Khe and bleeds into the My An neighborhood, part of the same beachside stretch of Da Nang. What makes it different is the street grid. It's compact, shaded and full of things: Vietnamese coffee shops, bakeries, noodle spots, international restaurants, bars and a handful of good boutique hotels within a few blocks of each other.

The beach is a five-minute walk. Nightlife is outside the door. If you hate needing Grab every time you want coffee or dinner, this is the right pocket. It skews more international and busier than it once did, but it remains the most genuinely walkable part of Da Nang and our personal preference for longer stays.

5 min walk to beach Best restaurants nearby Bars & cafes walkable Digital nomad-friendly Compact streets

Tradeoff: getting busier and more tourist-oriented than it used to be.

Best for First-Time Visitors

My Khe Beach

The easiest all-around base in Da Nang

Da Nang's main beach district runs for several kilometers of wide, accessible sand. The hotel strip on the landward side goes from budget guesthouses to beachfront towers. An Thuong's restaurant and bar cluster is at the northern end. Grab covers anything the legs won't. The airport is 10 minutes away, the Dragon Bridge 20.

Best for Walkability

An Thuong / My An

Da Nang's most walkable and liveable pocket

An Thuong is part of the broader My Khe beachside area, not a separate city. What sets it apart is density of good things within a small area: Vietnamese and international restaurants, specialty coffee, cocktail bars, bakeries and the beach within walking distance of each other. It's compact enough that a week here rarely requires Grab for anything except airport runs.

ExcellentWalkable

Tradeoff: Increasingly international and tourist-heavy. Busier than it was five years ago.

If you hate needing transportation every time you want coffee, dinner or a drink — stay here.

Best for City Life

Han River / Hai Chau

Da Nang's downtown and river district

Bach Dang waterfront, Dragon Bridge, Han Market, local pho shops open at 5am — the Han River side of Da Nang is the city that exists independent of the tourist beach strip. The rooftop views across the river at night are legitimately spectacular. The beach is a 20-minute Grab away, not a 5-minute walk.

Tradeoff: You don't have My Khe outside the door. The beach requires transportation every time.

Choose the river if experiencing the city matters more to you than waking up beside the beach.

Best for Resorts & Families

Non Nuoc

When the resort itself is part of the vacation

Non Nuoc sits south of My Khe, past the Marble Mountains on the way toward Hoi An. The beach here is quieter and the hotels are bigger — full resort complexes with large pools, multiple restaurants and beachfront grounds. The Sheraton Grand, Hyatt Regency, Furama and Marriott are all here. You don't need to leave the hotel to have a good day. That's the point.

Tradeoff: Not walkable for Da Nang's restaurant and nightlife scene. You need a car or Grab to explore the city.

Choose Non Nuoc when the resort itself is the vacation — pools, beach and space over urban exploration.

Best for Scenery & Seclusion

Son Tra Peninsula

Extraordinary when you pick the right part of it

Son Tra varies enormously depending on where exactly you stay. The southern and urban fringe near My Khe is a completely different proposition from staying deep on the peninsula — jungle, monkeys, coastline views and a real sense of remove from Da Nang's city life. The InterContinental Sun Peninsula is in this second category: extraordinary setting, genuinely isolated, and one of the finest resort experiences in Vietnam.

If you're looking at properties near the Lady Buddha or further north on the peninsula, check how far they are from restaurants. Some Son Tra addresses are walking distance from My Khe. Others require a dedicated Grab for every meal.

Tradeoff: Depending on location, very removed from restaurants and nightlife. Verify distances before booking.

For an extraordinary resort trip Son Tra is unbeatable. For a first visit centered on exploring Da Nang, choose My Khe instead.

Specific Hotels, By Area

Once you've picked an area above, here's where to actually book.

My Khe Beach
CHICLAND Hotel
CHICLAND is the boutique statement on My Khe: a vertical garden climbs the tower's facade, and the rooftop infinity pool looks straight down the beach. It's a smaller property than the resort brands nearby, which means fewer facilities but a more design-led feel in the public spaces. Access to the sand is direct, no road crossing required. Rooms lean compact rather than spacious, in keeping with its boutique positioning.
Best for: Design-focused couples

M Hotel Da Nang
M Hotel sits squarely in the mid-range My Khe bracket: a straightforward tower property with a pool, easy beach access, and none of the surprises that can come with cheaper independents. It won't compete with the big beachfront resorts on facilities, but the location and consistency make it a safe default. Good fit if you want a reliable base without paying resort prices.
Best for: Couples and solo travelers

Grandvrio Ocean Resort
Grandvrio is Japanese-managed, and it shows in the level of operational polish: a tighter, more consistent standard of service than most independent My Khe properties manage. It's beachfront, with the pool and public areas oriented toward the water. The trade-off for that consistency is a slightly more formal, less lively atmosphere than some of the beach's younger boutique competitors.
Best for: Those who value service quality

An Thuong / My An
Saha Casa Boutique Hotel
Saha Casa is a small, contemporary-design property sitting inside the An Thuong grid, close enough to walk to the restaurant and bar cluster without needing a Grab. The pool is compact, in keeping with a boutique footprint, but the location does most of the work here. Suits travelers who want to be based in the neighborhood itself rather than on the beach strip proper.
Best for: Couples, longer stays

Hadana Boutique Hotel
Hadana is one of the better value plays in An Thuong: a compact boutique property with a pool, priced below what the location and design level would suggest. It won't have the scale or facilities of a resort, but for a couple who mainly wants a comfortable base to walk out from, it punches above its price point. Book direct comparisons against Saha Casa and Sofiana before deciding, since all three sit in a similar bracket.

Sofiana My Khe Hotel & Spa
Sofiana adds a rooftop pool and an on-site spa to the An Thuong boutique bracket, at a price that undercuts what those two features usually cost elsewhere in the neighborhood. It sits close enough to both the beach and the restaurant strip that either is an easy walk. The styling reads more upscale than the rate, which is the main reason it gets recommended here.
Best for: Couples on a budget

Han River / Hai Chau
Caro Hotel Da Nang
Caro is the most considered hotel on the Bach Dang waterfront, with a rooftop pool that looks directly across the river at the Dragon Bridge. Design and finish are noticeably more current than most of the Han River competition, which skews older and more generic. It's a city hotel first, not a beach one, so factor in the 15 to 20 minute Grab to My Khe if you want sand time too.
Best for: Couples, design-focused travelers
See Caro Hotel Da Nang Prices →
Wink Hotel Danang Riverside
Wink brings a smart, modern design sensibility to the Han River strip at a rate well below Caro or the international chains nearby. River-view rooms are worth the small upcharge, and the rooftop bar is genuinely one of the better ones downtown, not just a hotel amenity. It's the best value option on this stretch of the river if design and location matter more to you than resort-scale facilities.
Best for: Solo travelers, city-focused visitors

Courtyard by Marriott Da Nang
Courtyard by Marriott is the dependable, full-service option on the river: predictable Marriott standards, Bonvoy points, and the kind of consistency business travelers and families both value over boutique character. It sits close to the Han River bridges and downtown, which makes it a practical base for anyone whose trip is centered on the city rather than the beach. Not a design destination, but it does what it promises.
Best for: Business stays, reliability

Non Nuoc
Danang Marriott Resort & Spa
The Marriott sits on Non Nuoc Beach with over 200 rooms, arranged as low-rise villa buildings through tropical gardens rather than a single tower, which keeps it from feeling like it's processing guests at that scale. Garden-view rooms are a genuine surprise: quieter, more private, and better value than the beach villas most people book by default. It delivers a full five-star resort experience, pools, multiple restaurants, direct beach access, without the intimidating size some competitors carry.
Best for: Families, resort stays
See Danang Marriott Resort & Spa Prices →
Furama Resort Da Nang
Furama was one of Da Nang's first international resorts, open since 1997, and the three decades since have given the grounds time few newer properties can match: the palm garden between the pools and the beach is genuine tropical density, not recent landscaping. That heritage also means operationally it has had a long time to get the details right. It's the property people describe as "old school" and mean as a compliment, consistently full for a reason.
Best for: Families, classic resort stays
See Furama Resort Da Nang Prices →
Sandy Beach Non Nuoc Resort
Sandy Beach is the more affordable route into Non Nuoc's resort format: beachfront, a pool, and the same quieter stretch of coastline as the Marriott and Furama, at a lower rate than either big-brand neighbor. The trade-off is fewer dining outlets and a less polished facilities list. Reasonable pick if the Non Nuoc setting matters more to you than the specific brand attached to it.
Best for: Value-conscious resort seekers
See Sandy Beach Non Nuoc Resort Prices →
Son Tra Peninsula
InterContinental Da Nang Sun Peninsula
The InterContinental is Bill Bensley's clifftop resort deep on Son Tra, and it earns the reputation: a funicular descends through 200 metres of jungle to a private bay that no one outside the hotel can access. Rates start around $350 a night, reflecting a category above Da Nang's other resorts rather than competing with them directly. It's genuinely isolated, jungle hillside, private coves, extraordinary architecture, which is exactly what makes it one of the finest resort experiences in Vietnam and exactly why it's the wrong pick for anyone wanting easy access to the city.
Best for: Honeymoon, luxury splurge, seclusion
See InterContinental Da Nang Sun Peninsula Prices →
Premier Village Da Nang Resort
Premier Village is Da Nang's private-villa resort: every room category is a two-storey villa with its own pool, no standard rooms and no shared hotel corridors anywhere on the property. It sits on the rocky headland between My Khe and Non Nuoc, giving it sea views uninterrupted by neighboring resort towers, a position unique to this stretch of Son Tra. Because every unit has a private pool, it works unusually well for families who want space without needing to negotiate a shared pool deck with other guests.
Best for: Families, villa stays

Beach, City or Resort?

Three ways to do Da Nang. Pick the one that matches your trip.

Beach Neighborhood

My Khe / An Thuong

My Khe · An Thuong · My An

Beach outside, restaurants and bars walkable, Grab handles everything else. The right choice when you want an active, convenient base. Best for: first trips, couples, food-focused travelers, anyone wanting flexibility.

City

Han River / Hai Chau

Han River · Bach Dang · Hai Chau

Dragon Bridge, markets, local restaurants and the city's downtown. Best for travelers who care more about experiencing Vietnam than having a beach 100 meters away. Best for: city exploration, business, shorter stays.

Resort

Non Nuoc / Son Tra

Non Nuoc · Son Tra Peninsula

Large pools, beachfront grounds, space and quiet. The hotel does more of the work. Best for: families who want a complete resort experience, couples wanting privacy, anyone happy to base themselves at the property.

For most first trips: Beach neighborhood. For a short urban break: Han River. For a family vacation or relaxing escape: Resort.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where should first-time visitors stay in Da Nang?
My Khe Beach. You get beach access, walkable access to An Thuong's restaurants and bars, hotels at every price point, and short Grab rides to everything else in Da Nang. If you're still unsure after reading this guide — book My Khe.
What is the most walkable area in Da Nang?
An Thuong. You can reach coffee, dinner, bars and the beach on foot without opening Grab. It's the most genuinely liveable pocket in Da Nang and the area we'd choose for a longer stay.
Is it better to stay near My Khe or the Han River?
My Khe for beach access and convenience. Han River for a more urban Vietnamese city experience — Dragon Bridge, markets, local restaurants and Bach Dang waterfront. Most visitors, especially first-timers, prefer My Khe.
Is Non Nuoc worth staying in?
Yes, if you want a full resort experience: large pools, beachfront, space and quiet. The Sheraton Grand, Hyatt Regency, Marriott and Furama are all in Non Nuoc. It's not the right base if you want to explore Da Nang's restaurants and nightlife without a car.
Is Da Nang or Hoi An better to stay in?
Stay in Da Nang and day-trip to Hoi An. Da Nang has better hotels at every price point, an international airport, a real beach and lower prices. The drive is 30–40 minutes. Hoi An is worth a day or two but gets expensive and crowded as an overnight base.
What area of Da Nang is best for digital nomads?
An Thuong. There are enough good cafes with wifi and power to sustain a proper working day, plus the restaurants and beach are walkable. My Khe works too, especially if you're near An Thuong, but the neighborhood itself has the strongest nomad infrastructure in Da Nang.
