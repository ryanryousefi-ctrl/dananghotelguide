Best Riverfront Hotels in Da Nang

The river puts you where Da Nang actually happens after dark. Walk Bach Dang, watch Dragon Bridge, eat downtown and Grab to My Khe when you want the beach.

Updated August 2026 · Independently selected · We went. We picked.

Editor's Top Pick — Best Boutique Han River Stay

Caro Hotel Da Nang

Bach Dang · Boutique Hotel · Han River

The most considered hotel on the riverfront. Caro sits directly on Bach Dang with river-view rooms, a rooftop pool overlooking the Han, and an interior design sensibility that makes every other property on this strip look like a Marriott points redemption. Dragon Bridge is visible from the upper floors. The restaurant is genuinely good enough to eat at twice. For travelers who want to be inside the city's best evening scene without surrendering to a generic business hotel tower, this is the call.

Da Nang Signature Pick — Best Modern Riverside Stay

Wink Hotel Danang Riverside

Han River · Modern Hotel · Rooftop Bar

Feels nothing like a business hotel. River views, social spaces, sharp design.

Wink stripped out the traditional hotel playbook and replaced it with something younger: compact but well-designed rooms, a rooftop bar with Han River views, communal spaces that people actually use, and pricing that's honest about what it is. The service is casual rather than stiff, which suits the crowd. Dragon Bridge is close enough to walk to on show nights. For solo travelers, couples, and digital nomads, the value-to-experience ratio here beats the full-service towers on the same stretch.

River or Beach?

Han River

  • Restaurants and dining within walking distance
  • Dragon Bridge fire show on Fri & Sat nights
  • Nightlife, bars, and the city's social scene
  • Markets, coffee shops, city exploration
  • Better hotel value per dollar
  • Short trips where every hour counts

My Khe Beach

  • Actual beach and ocean swimming
  • Sunrise on the water
  • Full resort facilities and pools
  • Ocean views from your room
  • Families with young children
  • Longer vacations built around relaxation

Our Take

First trip and the beach matters? Stay at My Khe. Food, nightlife, and exploring the city matter more? Stay on the Han River.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best Han River hotels in Da Nang?
Caro Hotel Da Nang is the best boutique riverfront stay, with direct Bach Dang access and genuine design character. Wink Hotel Danang Riverside is the best modern option with rooftop river views. For a full-service hotel, the Novotel Danang Premier Han River is the most reliable large property on the waterfront.
Is it better to stay on the Han River or at My Khe Beach?
For a first trip where the beach is the priority, stay at My Khe. If restaurants, Dragon Bridge, nightlife and city exploration matter more, stay on the Han River. The river puts you within walking distance of the best dining in Da Nang and the Dragon Bridge fire show on Friday and Saturday nights. Grab from the river to My Khe takes 15-20 minutes and costs around 50,000-80,000 VND.
Can you see Dragon Bridge from Han River hotels?
Yes. Several Han River hotels have direct or near-direct views of Dragon Bridge, especially those on Bach Dang. Caro Hotel, Wink Hotel, the Novotel, and the Grand Mercure all have rooms or rooftop spaces with Dragon Bridge views. The Grand Mercure is the closest of any hotel on this list to the bridge itself.
How far is the Han River from My Khe Beach?
Han River hotels are approximately 15-20 minutes from My Khe Beach by Grab, costing around 50,000-80,000 VND each way. You can also reach the beach by motorbike or bicycle in under 20 minutes. Both sides of the city are easily accessible from either base.
Are there luxury hotels on the Han River in Da Nang?
The Han River is more of a business and boutique hotel zone than a luxury resort zone. True resort luxury is at the InterContinental on Son Tra Peninsula or the Sheraton Grand on My An Beach. The best upper-end options on the river are Caro Hotel (boutique luxury), Novotel Premier (full-service), and Grand Mercure (Accor upscale).
