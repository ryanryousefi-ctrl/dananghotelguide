TIA Wellness vs Naman Retreat

Da Nang's two best wellness-tier resorts. TIA sells guaranteed adults-only quiet, Naman sells villa design and a spa people book flights specifically to use.

Hotel A
TIA Wellness Resort
VS
Hotel B
Naman Retreat
Editor's Pick

Naman Retreat

Why It Wins

Naman Spa is a genuine destination, not a hotel amenity, and the villa design is the most architecturally striking property in this tier. TIA is the better pick only if guaranteed adults-only quiet matters more to you than spa quality and design, in which case book TIA instead.

The Property

TIA Wellness
Naman Retreat

TIA is a small, understated, roughly 50-room property on My Khe Beach, about 12 minutes from the city. Naman Retreat is 72 villas on Non Nuoc Beach, more architecturally striking, about 20 minutes from the city but closer to Hoi An.

Naman Retreat — for design

The Rooms

TIA Wellness
Naman Retreat

TIA is room-based, no villas, roughly 50 keys at an intimate scale. Naman mixes villas, some with private pools, with high-rise rooms in a separate building, so the room type you book changes the experience more than at TIA.

Naman Retreat — for villa living

Final Verdict

Which one should you actually book?

If guaranteed adults-only quiet is the whole point of your trip, book TIA. The strict 18+ policy, lower price, and easier access to city dining make it one of a kind in Da Nang and nothing else here replicates that certainty.

For everyone else, Naman Retreat is the stronger hotel. Naman Spa is one of the best hotel spas in Vietnam, the villas are more distinctive than TIA's rooms, and Hay Hay is a better restaurant than anything on-site at TIA. It also sits closer to Hoi An for day trips.

Our Pick: Naman Retreat, unless guaranteed adults-only quiet is your top priority

Common Questions

TIA vs Naman: FAQ

Is TIA Wellness Resort adults-only?+

Yes. TIA Wellness Resort is strictly adults-only - guests under 18 are not permitted.

This is a core part of the resort's wellness concept and is enforced consistently. It's one of the only adults-only resorts in Da Nang, which makes it a genuine option for couples wanting guaranteed quiet.

Which has a better spa - TIA or Naman Retreat?+

Naman Spa at Naman Retreat is widely considered one of the best hotel spas in Vietnam - large, beautifully designed, and with a strong treatment menu. TIA's spa is wellness-focused and good, but Naman Spa is the standout if spa quality is your priority.

Note that Naman Spa treatments are not included in room rates.

Which resort is closer to Hoi An - TIA or Naman?+

Naman Retreat is closer to Hoi An at approximately 30 minutes by car. TIA Wellness Resort is on My Khe Beach, which is about 45-50 minutes from Hoi An.

If day trips to Hoi An are on your itinerary, Naman's position is a genuine advantage.
