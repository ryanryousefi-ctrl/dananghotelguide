TIA Wellness Resort vs Naman Retreat

Da Nang's two best wellness resorts compared honestly. Adults-only intimacy at TIA versus the villa luxury and world-class spa at Naman. Same premium tier, very different stays.

Hotel Overview

TIA Wellness Resort

  • Adults-only (18+ strictly enforced)
  • ~50 rooms, intimate scale
  • My Khe Beach, northern Da Nang strip
  • $180-380/night
  • Wellness-focused cuisine and programming
  • Smaller, quieter, genuinely curated

Naman Retreat

  • 72 villas, adult atmosphere (families allowed)
  • Non Nuoc Beach, south Da Nang
  • $200-450/night
  • Naman Spa - exceptional, world-class
  • Hay Hay restaurant, outstanding Vietnamese
  • More design-forward, architecturally striking

These are the two properties most often mentioned when people ask about wellness-focused hotels in central Vietnam. They're genuinely different in concept. TIA is built around the idea that a wellness resort should have no children, no noise, and food that supports health goals. Naman is built around exceptional design, villa living, and a spa that could compete with anything in Southeast Asia.

Both deliver on their promises. The question is which promise matters more to you.

Location is actually a meaningful differentiator here. TIA's My Khe position gives you easier access to Da Nang's restaurant scene - the An Thuong area with its concentration of good Vietnamese and international restaurants is a short Grab ride away. Naman's Non Nuoc position is more isolated but considerably closer to Hoi An, which matters if you're planning day trips.

Spa & Wellness

TIA Wellness Approach

  • Integrated wellness programming
  • Healthy cuisine designed around nutrition
  • Yoga and fitness classes included
  • No children means genuine quiet
  • Spa treatments available on-site
  • Wellness-focused from arrival to departure

Naman Spa

  • One of the best hotel spas in Vietnam
  • Large, architecturally beautiful facility
  • Extensive treatment menu
  • Private treatment rooms, quality therapists
  • Not included in room rate - paid separately
  • Destination spa quality, not just hotel amenity

This is where the two resorts genuinely diverge. TIA wraps wellness into everything - the food, the programming, the absence of children - but the spa itself is not remarkable by regional standards. Naman Spa is the standout: it's a facility that people travel specifically to use, not just a hotel amenity. If your trip is primarily about a spa experience, Naman is the clearer choice. If you want a holistic wellness environment with guaranteed adult peace, TIA delivers something Naman can't replicate.

Note that Naman Spa is not included in your room rate - budget separately for treatments. TIA's wellness programming is more integrated into the room price.

Final Verdict

Choose TIA Wellness Resort if you want a guaranteed adults-only environment, lower entry price, and a resort that takes wellness seriously as a concept rather than an amenity. The smaller scale, the healthy cuisine approach, and the strict no-under-18s policy make it one of a kind in Da Nang. You won't find the same certainty of quiet anywhere else on My Khe.

Choose Naman Retreat if you want premium villa living, Naman Spa (genuinely world-class), the Hay Hay dining experience, and a more architecturally impressive property. Families are technically allowed at Naman, but the atmosphere trends adult and the villa setup naturally creates separation. Being 30 minutes from Hoi An is also a practical advantage.

Couples who prioritise spa quality: Naman. Couples who prioritise guaranteed quiet and lower price: TIA. It's a genuine split, which is unusual for a comparison this close in tier.

Common Questions

TIA vs Naman: FAQ

Is TIA Wellness Resort adults-only?+

Yes. TIA Wellness Resort is strictly adults-only - guests under 18 are not permitted. This is a core part of the resort's wellness concept and is enforced consistently. It's one of the only adults-only resorts in Da Nang, which makes it a genuine option for couples wanting guaranteed quiet.

Which has a better spa - TIA or Naman Retreat?+

Naman Spa at Naman Retreat is widely considered one of the best hotel spas in Vietnam - large, beautifully designed, and with a strong treatment menu. TIA's spa is wellness-focused and good, but Naman Spa is the standout if spa quality is your priority. Note that Naman Spa treatments are not included in room rates.

Which resort is closer to Hoi An - TIA or Naman?+

Naman Retreat is closer to Hoi An at approximately 30 minutes by car. TIA Wellness Resort is on My Khe Beach, which is about 45-50 minutes from Hoi An. If day trips to Hoi An are on your itinerary, Naman's position is a genuine advantage.
