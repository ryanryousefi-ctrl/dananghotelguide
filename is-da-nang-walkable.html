Yes in the right neighborhoods. For longer trips, Grab makes the city easy.

Editor's Pick - Most Walkable Base

Stay in An Thuong / My Khe

If you want a walkable daily routine, An Thuong and My Khe is the base. The beach is at the end of the street. Cafes, restaurants, bars, convenience stores, massage shops and breakfast spots are all within five to ten minutes on foot. You can genuinely spend most of a day without needing transport at all.

Then use Grab to reach the Han River, Son Tra, Marble Mountains, the airport, or anywhere else in the city. The combination of a walkable home base plus cheap on-demand Grab is the most practical way to move around Da Nang.

Da Nang Signature Tip

Walk Local. Grab Across Town.

Da Nang is a spread-out city. My Khe to the Han River is about 3km. My Khe to Marble Mountains is 8km. Son Tra Peninsula is a winding drive up a hill. None of these are practical walks, especially in summer when midday temperatures hit 34-38C.

The practical rule is simple: walk everything within your neighborhood, and use Grab for anything that crosses a district or takes you to a main attraction. A Grab from My Khe to the Han River costs around 40-60K VND and takes 10 minutes. There is no reason to force long walks in heat when Grab is that cheap.

Da Nang is walkable where you live your day - not necessarily where you sightsee.

By Neighborhood

Where Da Nang Is Actually Walkable

An Thuong / My Khe
Excellent
The most walkable area in Da Nang for daily life. Beach, cafes, restaurants, bars, convenience stores, spas and nightlife all within easy walking distance. This is where to base yourself if walkability matters to you.
Han River / Hai Chau
Good
The Han River promenade is one of Da Nang's best walking strips - flat, wide, riverfront cafes and views of the bridges. The surrounding Hai Chau district has walkable streets with local food and city life. Less concentrated than An Thuong.
My Khe Beach Promenade
Very Good
The beachfront walk along Vo Nguyen Giap is flat and pleasant. Seafood restaurants, beach access points and breakfast spots line the road. Best on foot in the morning before traffic builds and the heat sets in.
Son Tra Peninsula
Mixed
Walkable in pockets near the base, but attractions are spread across a hilly peninsula. The drive up to the Lady Buddha and the lookout points is better by Grab or scooter. Not a walking destination in the usual sense.
Non Nuoc / Marble Mountains
Limited
Resort-oriented area south of My Khe. Distances between properties and the main road are longer. The Marble Mountains site itself is compact and walkable once you are there, but getting between attractions in this area requires Grab.
Airport Area
Not Useful for Walking
The airport sits between the city and the beach. There is no walking-oriented neighborhood here. Grab from the airport to My Khe takes 10-15 minutes and costs around 80-120K VND - use it every time.

Common Questions

Can I Walk There?

Can I walk from An Thuong to the beach?
Yes
My Khe Beach is 2-5 minutes on foot from most An Thuong streets. This is the whole point of basing yourself here.
Can I walk along the Han River?
Yes
The Han River promenade on both banks is flat, wide and pleasant. Best in the morning or evening. Dragon Bridge is at one end.
Can I walk from My Khe to Dragon Bridge?
Possible, not recommended
About 3km each way. Manageable in cool weather, unpleasant in summer midday. Grab takes 10 minutes and costs 40-60K VND.
Can I walk from My Khe to Marble Mountains?
No
8km along a busy highway. Take Grab - 15-20 minutes, around 60-90K VND.
Can I walk from My Khe to the airport?
No
About 4km through busy roads with no useful walking infrastructure. Grab takes 10-15 minutes, costs 80-120K VND.
Can I walk to Hoi An from Da Nang?
No
Hoi An is 30km south. Grab a private car - 35-40 minutes, around 200-300K VND. A very easy trip.
Can I walk along My Khe beach itself?
Yes
My Khe is a long, flat beach. Walking along the sand for 30-60 minutes is one of the better morning activities in Da Nang.
Can I walk to Son Tra and the Lady Buddha?
No
The Lady Buddha sits high on the peninsula. The road up is winding and long. Take Grab or a scooter - about 15-20 minutes from My Khe.
Weather

Heat Changes Everything

In March-May, mornings and evenings are comfortable for extended walks. The beach at 7am is one of the genuinely pleasant experiences in Da Nang - cool, quiet, low traffic. Late afternoon from around 4:30pm onwards is also comfortable most of the year.

June-September Midday

Temperatures reach 34-38C with high humidity. Even moderate walks of 20-30 minutes become genuinely uncomfortable. Plan outdoor walking for before 10am or after 4:30pm. Midday is pool, cafe, or air-conditioned time.

Rain can arrive fast in the afternoon. A 10-minute Grab is always more practical than getting caught in a downpour halfway to your destination.

Safety

Crossing Streets in Da Nang

The practical approach: Use pedestrian crossings and traffic lights where they exist. On busier roads without lights, wait for a natural gap, make eye contact with approaching drivers, and move at a steady predictable pace - do not dart or stop suddenly mid-crossing. Motorbikes will flow around you if you move consistently. Watch for vehicles turning left across pedestrian paths at junctions. The Han River promenade and An Thuong streets are low-traffic and straightforward. Major arterials like Vo Nguyen Giap (beach road) require more attention. Never cross while distracted by your phone.
Evening

Walking at Night

An Thuong and My Khe are active and well-lit at night. Restaurants, bars and cafes are open until late, and the streets have foot traffic. The Han River promenade is pleasant in the evening - the Dragon Bridge area is busy on Saturday and Sunday nights for the fire show.

Quieter side streets away from the main strips are darker and have less foot traffic after 10pm. This is not a safety issue in most cases, but take the usual common-sense precautions - keep your phone out of sight, stick to lit routes, and take Grab home if you are returning late from a distance.

Grab works well at night. No surge pricing equivalent to other markets. Reliable pickup within a few minutes in the main areas.

Common Questions

Is Da Nang a walkable city?
Da Nang is walkable within the right neighborhoods, not across the whole city. An Thuong and My Khe are excellent on foot - beach, cafes, restaurants and bars all reachable without transport. Between neighborhoods, Grab is fast, cheap and removes the need to walk long distances in heat or rain.
Can I walk from My Khe Beach to Dragon Bridge?
Technically yes - about 3km. In the morning or evening it is manageable for fit walkers. In summer midday heat it is not recommended. Grab takes about 10 minutes and costs 40-60K VND. Most visitors find Grab the better option.
Can I walk from My Khe to Marble Mountains?
No. Marble Mountains is about 8km south of My Khe Beach along a busy coastal highway. Take Grab - 15-20 minutes, around 60-90K VND.
Is the Han River promenade walkable?
Yes. The Han River promenade is one of Da Nang's best walking areas - flat, wide, lined with cafes and riverside scenery. Both banks have walkable paths. Best in the morning or evening. The Dragon Bridge is at one end. The fire show runs Saturday and Sunday at 9pm.
