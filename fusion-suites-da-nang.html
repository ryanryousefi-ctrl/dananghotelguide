Fusion Suites Da Nang

“Daily included spa treatments, not a promotion.”

Aparthotel · Vo Nguyen Giap, Son Tra

Our Take

Fusion Suites runs a model that sounds too good to be actually true: the listings advertise daily foot reflexology as part of the stay, built into the way the place is sold rather than treated as a promotional add-on.

The foot reflexology included in the stay is one of the better foot reflexology sessions available in Da Nang, professionally executed, in a spa that stays calm even when it's busy.

The fact that it comes with your room and not as a line item on checkout changes how you relate to the spa entirely.

You go because it's already paid for. Then you go again.

The property is a high-rise aparthotel on Vo Nguyen Giap Street, across the street from the beach, with suite-style rooms rather than standard hotel rooms.

The smallest units are apartment-style studios with a kitchen wall and big windows.

The beds have four-inch pillow tops and proper duvets, and one guest described them as the most comfortable beds found in Vietnam.

That's a bold claim, but nothing about the room contradicts it.

The Rooms

The Rooms

Large, light, open, with a kitchen layout and full-wall windows giving two sides of light in the studios and more in the one- and two-bedroom options. The ocean-view suites on the upper floors are the pick of the portfolio, worth paying the difference for. All categories share the same quality of bed and a generous amount of space that most hotels at this price point in Da Nang simply don't offer.

Which room would we choose? An ocean-view suite on a higher floor. The view justifies the upgrade and the extra distance from any street noise at ground level is a bonus.

The Reason to Book It

Included Daily Spa

Foot reflexology is included every day of your stay, not a one-time welcome perk, and it's the reason to pick this hotel over an equivalently priced standard room somewhere else. The math is simple: if you'd pay for even one spa treatment per stay anyway, the rate here becomes competitive with or better than hotels that charge separately for everything. Spa slots do fill up, so book each morning rather than assuming a walk-in slot will be free later in the day.

Pool + Beach

Pool + Beach

Here's the thing to know before you arrive: the hotel is across the street from the beach rather than on the sand, so getting to the water means crossing a road. The outdoor pool is on site, and the guest-reported downside is traffic and construction noise in the area, which can carry to the loungers. For most guests this is a minor inconvenience rather than a dealbreaker. Once you cross over, the beach itself is sand and waves with local cafes along it.

Food + Breakfast

Food + Drink

The Fusion breakfast concept is that it can be served anywhere, any time, brought to your room, the pool, or the beach. In practice this holds up and is one of the more memorable hotel details in Da Nang. One guest called the variety absolutely incredible, unable to think of a cuisine that isn't catered for, though some guests have noted inconsistency between buffet and a la carte formats day to day. Book the flexibility deliberately: have breakfast delivered to the beach at least once, it's the version of this hotel worth experiencing.

Who It's For

Families, Couples & Who It Suits

This is built for couples and wellness-inclined travelers who'll actually use a daily spa treatment and want a boutique-scale stay near the beach over a big resort. It works less well for families needing a kids' club or a dramatic pool complex, the scale here is smaller and more personal than the major resort hotels further down the coast. If included daily spa access and generous suite space matter more to you than pool drama, this beats most alternatives at the same price point.

What We Like

What We Like

Included spa

Daily foot reflexology listed as part of the stay, not a promotional extra. One of the better reflexology sessions available in the city, professionally executed in a calm space.

Suite-style rooms

Suite-style rooms with kitchen walls and big windows giving genuinely generous light and space.

The beds

Four-inch pillow tops and proper duvets. Consistently described by guests as the most comfortable beds found in Vietnam.

The staff

The most consistently praised element of any hotel on My Khe, described repeatedly by guests as exceptional and next level.

The Tradeoff

The hotel is across the road from the beach, requiring a street crossing, and this is the one location flaw worth knowing about. Listings also warn of noise from nearby construction, so light sleepers should ask for a higher floor away from street-facing windows. Breakfast format varies day to day between buffet and a la carte, which can be a minor inconsistency if you're expecting the same spread each morning. None of these are dealbreakers, but they're worth knowing going in rather than discovering on arrival.

Location

Location

On Vo Nguyen Giap Street in Son Tra, across the street from the beach and roughly 7 km (about 15 minutes) from the airport. This is a city-accessible hotel, not a remote resort, and that's a feature rather than a limitation. Restaurants and cafes are nearby, so evenings out are easy. Marble Mountains and Hoi An are both manageable day trips by taxi.

Airport: ~15 minRestaurants: nearby
Would We Stay Here

YES

Fusion Suites is the hotel to recommend to people who want a stay that actually over-delivers relative to the nightly rate. The combination of the suite-style rooms, the included reflexology, the flexible breakfast, and a genuinely exceptional front-line team makes it one of the best values in Da Nang's mid-range bracket. The road noise and the street crossing to the beach are real. They're also minor compared to what comes back in return.
