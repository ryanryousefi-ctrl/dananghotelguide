Fusion Suites Da Nang

“Daily included spa treatments, not a promotion.”

Boutique All-Suite Hotel · My Khe Beach

Our Take

Fusion Suites runs a model that sounds too good to be actually true: every room comes with daily spa treatment credits and breakfast included, always, not as a promotional add-on but as the permanent structure of the place.

The 45-minute foot reflexology included in the rate is one of the better foot reflexology sessions available in Da Nang, professionally executed, in a spa that stays calm even when it's busy.

The fact that it comes with your room and not as a line item on checkout changes how you relate to the spa entirely.

You go because it's already paid for. Then you go again.

The property sits on My Khe Beach with 123 suites across a tower, no standard rooms at all.

The smallest unit is an apartment-style studio at around 45 square metres with a kitchen wall and full windows on two sides.

The beds have four-inch pillow tops and proper duvets, and one guest described them as the most comfortable beds found in Vietnam.

That's a bold claim, but nothing about the room contradicts it.

The Rooms

The Rooms

Large, light, open, with a kitchen layout and full-wall windows giving two sides of light in the studios and more in the one- and two-bedroom options. The ocean-view suites on the upper floors are the pick of the portfolio, worth paying the difference for. Penthouse suites with private terraces sit at the top of the range. All categories share the same quality of bed and a generous amount of space that most hotels at this price point in Da Nang simply don't offer.

Which room would we choose? An ocean-view suite on a higher floor. The view justifies the upgrade and the extra distance from any street noise at ground level is a bonus.

The Reason to Book It

Included Daily Spa

Foot reflexology is included every day of your stay, not a one-time welcome perk, and it's the reason to pick this hotel over an equivalently priced standard room somewhere else. The math is simple: if you'd pay for even one spa treatment per stay anyway, the rate here becomes competitive with or better than hotels that charge separately for everything. Spa slots do fill up, so book each morning rather than assuming a walk-in slot will be free later in the day.

Pool + Beach

Pool + Beach

Here's the thing to know before you arrive: the pool is across the street from the main hotel building. The pool itself is long and attractive with beach club access and good sea views, but there's traffic noise from the adjacent main road audible from the loungers, and crossing the street gets mildly annoying by the fifth or sixth time in a day. For most guests this is a minor inconvenience rather than a dealbreaker. The beach is directly in front of the pool building, sand, waves, full beach service, and that part works without complication.

Food + Breakfast

Food + Drink

The Fusion breakfast concept is that it can be served anywhere, any time, brought to your room, the pool, or the beach. In practice this holds up and is one of the more memorable hotel details in Da Nang. One guest called the variety absolutely incredible, unable to think of a cuisine that isn't catered for, though some guests have noted inconsistency between buffet and a la carte formats day to day. Book the flexibility deliberately: have breakfast delivered to the beach at least once, it's the version of this hotel worth experiencing.

Who It's For

Families, Couples & Who It Suits

This is built for couples and wellness-inclined travelers who'll actually use a daily spa treatment and want a boutique beachfront stay over a big resort. It works less well for families needing a kids' club or a dramatic pool complex, the scale here is smaller and more personal than the major resort hotels further down the coast. If included daily spa access and generous suite space matter more to you than pool drama, this beats most alternatives at the same price point.

What We Like

What We Like

Included spa

Daily foot reflexology built into every rate, not a promotional extra. One of the better reflexology sessions available in the city, professionally executed in a calm space.

All-suite rooms

Every room is a suite, minimum 45sqm, with kitchen walls and full-wall windows giving genuinely generous light and space.

The beds

Four-inch pillow tops and proper duvets. Consistently described by guests as the most comfortable beds found in Vietnam.

The staff

The most consistently praised element of any hotel on My Khe, described repeatedly by guests as exceptional and next level.

The Tradeoff

The pool is across the main road from the hotel, requiring a street crossing, and this is the one design flaw that comes up in nearly every review. Road noise is real and audible from the pool area and some lower-floor rooms, so light sleepers should ask for a higher floor away from street-facing windows. Breakfast format varies day to day between buffet and a la carte, which can be a minor inconsistency if you're expecting the same spread each morning. None of these are dealbreakers, but they're worth knowing going in rather than discovering on arrival.

Location

Location

My Khe Beach, 12 minutes from the airport and 12 minutes by Grab to Dragon Bridge. This is a city-accessible hotel, not a remote resort, and that's a feature rather than a limitation. The My Khe restaurant strip is a five-minute walk, so evenings out don't require a taxi. Marble Mountains is about 20 minutes south by taxi, and Hoi An is a 30-minute drive for a day trip.

Airport: 12 minRestaurants: 5 min walk
Would We Stay Here

YES

Fusion Suites is the hotel to recommend to people who want a stay that actually over-delivers relative to the nightly rate. The combination of the all-suite format, the included spa, the flexible breakfast, and a genuinely exceptional front-line team makes it one of the best values in Da Nang's mid-range bracket. The road noise and the pool location across the street are real. They're also minor compared to what comes back in return.
