Sheraton Grand Danang Resort

“Vietnam's longest infinity pool and a restaurant strip you can actually walk to.”

Our Take

The Sheraton Grand Danang opened in 2018, which means everything is still fresh, the rooms feel maintained rather than managed, the pool infrastructure is solid, the kids' club is properly resourced.

On a long stretch of My Khe Beach that mixes hotels of every era and quality level, the Sheraton sits in a reliable tier: 785 rooms, Marriott Bonvoy integration, a beach that's directly in front rather than across a road.

The 250-metre infinity pool is the longest in Vietnam, with a water slide feeding straight into a shallow kids' area so toddlers and confident swimmers both get their own space.

Room maintenance is the detail guests most consistently praise: nothing broken, nothing missing, everything functioning as it should.

Over a week's stay, that matters more than dramatic design touches.

The local seafood restaurants on the My Khe strip are a 5-10 minute walk, which matters more than it sounds, being able to eat well outside the hotel without a taxi is a genuine quality-of-life factor over a longer stay.

The Sheraton's strength is operational consistency and family infrastructure.

Its limitation is that it doesn't have a single dining experience that makes it unmissable on its own terms.

Sometimes that's exactly what you want.

The Rooms

The Rooms

Entry-level Superior rooms run around 45 square metres with balconies and ocean-facing windows. White tones, rattan accents, a contemporary resort aesthetic, nothing wildly distinctive, but well-maintained and consistent. Bathrooms in the higher categories have bathtubs, and family suites with separate sleeping areas are available for larger groups.

Which room would we choose? An ocean-view room on an upper floor. It's the most popular category for a reason, and it earns the small upgrade cost if you're planning to spend evenings on the balcony.

The Reason to Book It

Vietnam's Longest Infinity Pool

At 250 metres, this is the longest infinity pool in the country, running directly adjacent to the beach with a water slide that feeds into a dedicated shallow section for younger kids. The kids' club splits into three separate age-based rooms with real programming, not a single playroom with a TV running in the corner, which is the detail that separates a genuine family resort from one that just says it is on the website.

Sun lounger areas are managed carefully enough that you can usually find a spot without a towel-reservation standoff at 7am. An indoor pool handles wet-season and early-morning swimming without the wind.

Pool + Beach

Pool + Beach

The beach is direct, wide, clean, with full umbrella and towel service, one of the better stretches on the My Khe strip rather than a token patch of sand behind a pool deck. On Vietnamese public holidays the pool area gets genuinely busy, if you're traveling around Tet or National Day, plan around it or get to a lounger early. The children's pool section sits at one end of the main lagoon, close enough to supervise from a normal-depth chair without hovering.

Food + Breakfast

Food + Drink

Market 39 runs a breakfast buffet that's one of the more substantial on My Khe, Vietnamese stations alongside international options, fresh fruit, pastry, eggs cooked to order. It consistently earns praise in guest reviews. Dinner at Market 39 is competent but not a reason to stay in, and that's fine here specifically because you don't have to.

Walk the 5-10 minutes to the restaurant strip for dinner instead, most guests eat out at least two or three nights a week, and the beachside bar plus the Chill Skybar cover cocktails and lighter food through the rest of the day.

Who It's For

Families, Couples & Who It Suits

With children, this is an easy yes, specifically for the kids' club, the lagoon pool, and direct beach access. For a couple without children, weigh it against Melia or Naman Retreat depending on budget and how much the pool design matters to you, the Sheraton's family infrastructure is its clear differentiator, and a couple traveling without kids won't get the same value from three age-segmented kids' rooms. Marriott Bonvoy members should factor in Elite status: breakfast and room upgrades are available, subject to availability, and the programme runs properly here rather than existing on paper only.

What We Like

What We Like

Room maintenance

Consistently praised by guests. Nothing broken, nothing missing, everything functioning as it should, which matters more than flash over a week's stay.

The kids' club

Three separate age-based rooms with real daily programming, not one general playroom with a television running in the background.

The 250m infinity pool

The longest in Vietnam, running beach-adjacent, with a slide feeding directly into a dedicated shallow kids' area.

Restaurant strip walkability

Five to ten minutes on foot to real local seafood restaurants, no taxi required, which keeps a week's stay from feeling contained.

The Tradeoff

On-site dinner is competent but not a dining destination, Market 39 handles the job without ever becoming a reason to stay in. The pool area gets busy on Vietnamese public holidays and during peak season, and even Sheraton's own management has acknowledged room for improvement on pool and grounds upkeep and pool-area music volume in guest responses. It's a 15-minute taxi to the city centre, mild isolation compared to Son Tra or Non Nuoc resorts, but real if you were picturing a city-adjacent base. Rates also run higher than non-branded beachfront alternatives of similar quality, you're paying partly for the Marriott name and loyalty programme.

Location

Location

My Khe Beach, at the quieter central section of the strip, which avoids both the isolation of the southern resorts like Hyatt Regency and the noise of the northern tourist cluster. The city centre is 15 minutes north by taxi, 7km to Dragon Bridge and 9km to the airport. What this means in practice: you get a genuine beach resort feel without the full remoteness of Son Tra or Non Nuoc, and the restaurant strip 5-10 minutes on foot keeps the trip from feeling contained to hotel grounds. The Marble Mountains are 15 minutes south, Hoi An is about 35 minutes by car.

Common Questions

Sheraton Grand Danang Resort: FAQ

Is Sheraton Grand Danang Resort worth staying at in Da Nang?

Yes, particularly for families and groups. The Sheraton Grand delivers a polished resort package on My Khe Beach with excellent children's facilities and Marriott Bonvoy benefits.

How much does Sheraton Grand Danang Resort cost per night?

Rates start around $160/night in low season, rising to $280–$400/night in peak season.

Where is Sheraton Grand Danang Resort located in Da Nang?

On My Khe Beach in Da Nang's coastal strip, 7 km from Dragon Bridge and 9 km from Da Nang Airport.
