About This Directory

This directory is maintained independently by Da Nang Hotel Guide. The starting inventory is our master hotel database, built from properties we track in the city, and every entry is checked for current operating status before it lands here. Hotels that have closed, rebranded or turned out to sit outside Da Nang city limits are removed rather than left to rot in the list.

Official website links are verified individually, and where we cannot confirm a working official site we leave the field blank rather than guess at a URL. Editor's Scores appear only where Da Nang Hotel Guide has actually stayed in or inspected the property. Everything else shows NR, and NR is not a criticism, it just means we have not rated it yet.

Typical nightly costs are approximate reference bands, not quotes. Rates in Da Nang swing hard between low season and the summer peak, so use Check Prices for real numbers on your dates. Hotel locations are mapped from verified coordinates only, which is why the map holds fewer pins than the table holds rows. How we score hotels.
