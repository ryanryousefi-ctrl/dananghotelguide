Son Tra Peninsula · Ultra-luxury

InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort

Private bay · from ~$350/night

Built into the Son Tra hillside above a private sheltered bay. No neighbouring properties, no road noise, no vendors on the beach. Bill Bensley's architecture terraces down the cliff via funicular. The water below is genuinely clean. Frequently rated among Asia's best resorts, and the price, while high for Da Nang, is a fraction of what comparable experiences cost in the Maldives or Thailand. Worth the money if a self-contained resort experience is the point. If you are planning to leave daily, you are paying for something you won't use.

Worth Knowing

What Luxury Costs in Da Nang

Entry-level five-star on My Khe Beach starts around $140-160 in shoulder season (February-March, October-November). Peak season (June-August, December-January) typically adds 30-50%. The InterContinental ranges from roughly $350 in shoulder to $700+ for peak villas. All of this is considerably less than comparable resorts in Bali, Thailand, or the Maldives — Da Nang remains one of Asia's best-value luxury markets.

Common Questions

Luxury Hotels FAQ

What is the best luxury hotel in Da Nang?

The InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort on Son Tra Peninsula is in a category of its own: private bay, no neighbours, genuinely one of the best resorts in Asia. For luxury on My Khe Beach, the Sheraton Grand and Hyatt Regency are the most consistent five-star picks. For wellness-focused luxury, TIA Wellness Resort on Non Nuoc is the standout.

How much do luxury hotels in Da Nang cost per night?

Entry-level five-star resorts start around $140-160/night in shoulder season. Hyatt Regency and Sheraton Grand run $160-250/night. The InterContinental on Son Tra ranges from ~$350 to $800+ depending on room type and season. Villa categories at Non Nuoc resorts start around $200-300/night. All significantly less than comparable resorts elsewhere in Asia.

Which luxury hotel in Da Nang is best for families?

Hyatt Regency Danang Resort & Spa is the strongest family-luxury resort: four pools, a dedicated kids club, and gentle water on the southern beach. Premier Village works well for families who want private villa space, as every unit has its own pool. The Sheraton Grand also has strong family facilities without climbing to the very top of the price range.

Is luxury accommodation in Da Nang good value compared to other Asian destinations?

Yes, significantly. A comparable resort in Bali or the Maldives would cost 2-3x what you pay here. The InterContinental, frequently rated among Asia's best resorts, prices at a fraction of what equivalent properties charge elsewhere. The trade-off is that Da Nang's city lifestyle scene — fine dining, nightlife, cultural depth — is still developing compared to Bangkok or Singapore.
