Luxury Hotels Da Nang · 2026

Best Luxury Resorts & Hotels in Da Nang

First, decide whether you want to disappear into a resort or still feel connected to the city. That one decision shapes everything else on this list.

Updated August 2026 · Independently selected · We went. We picked.

Editor's Top Pick — Best Luxury Hotel in Da Nang

InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort

Son Tra Peninsula · Ultra-Luxury Resort

InterContinental feels like a trip inside the trip. The peninsula setting is the reason to book it - a clifftop resort above its own private bay with architecture that uses the jungle and the South China Sea as the design brief. The restaurants include HARZEN, one of Vietnam's most dramatic dining experiences. Service is serious. The beach is private. Most guests don't leave the property for two or three days and don't feel the need to. The tradeoff is real: you are 20 minutes from My Khe, 30 minutes from An Thuong, and won't be walking out for a beer at whatever bar caught your eye on Google Maps.

Da Nang Signature Pick — Best Classic Luxury Resort

Sheraton Grand Danang Resort

My An Beach · Full-Service Resort · IHG

The most complete resort experience on the My An stretch. Beach, pools, space and service all delivered at scale.

Sheraton Grand is what most people mean when they say they want a luxury beach resort in Da Nang. Long pool flanking the beachfront, direct My An sand access, multiple food and drink options, a strong spa, and the operational reliability of a major international brand running at full capacity. Families, couples, and groups all fit here without friction. The resort is large enough that it never feels crowded. Tradeoff: it is not a walking-to-An-Thuong-every-night type of hotel. You're in resort mode here.

Which Luxury Area Should You Choose?

Son Tra Peninsula

Best for privacy, dramatic scenery and true destination luxury. One hotel - the InterContinental - and one decision: you're going to disappear. Not for first-timers who want beach and city in one trip.

My Khe Beach

Best for high-end hotels with beach access while staying connected to the city. Furama, Pullman, M Hotel, SALA and voco all give you the beach without the resort isolation. An Thuong is a short Grab or a walk.

My An Beach

Best for larger beachfront resorts in a quieter environment. Sheraton Grand, TIA and Premier Village all sit here. More removed than My Khe, but not as far as Non Nuoc. The resort-vacation sweet spot for most travelers.

Non Nuoc Beach

Best for maximum space, villa options, families and quiet. Hyatt Regency and the Marriott are both here. The furthest from the city, the largest resort grounds, the most removed from nightlife and restaurants.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best luxury hotel in Da Nang?
InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort is the best luxury hotel in Da Nang by most measures - architecture, setting, restaurants, privacy and service. It sits on Son Tra Peninsula above its own private bay. The tradeoff is isolation. For a classic full-resort experience with better city access, the Sheraton Grand Danang Resort is the strongest alternative.
Which Da Nang luxury hotel is best for families?
Hyatt Regency Danang Resort and Spa is the most family-focused luxury hotel in Da Nang - large resort grounds, multiple pools, a dedicated kids club, and a Non Nuoc beach that works for children. Sheraton Grand is also strong for families. TIA Wellness Resort is adults-only and unsuitable for families with young children.
What is the best Da Nang luxury hotel for couples?
InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort is the top choice for a romantic trip - the isolation and the setting make it feel like a destination in itself. Premier Village Da Nang Resort suits couples who want villa privacy with a My An beachfront address. TIA Wellness Resort is the call for couples focused on spa and wellness.
Are there luxury hotels near An Thuong in Da Nang?
Most luxury hotels in Da Nang are resort-focused and not designed around An Thuong restaurant access. The closest high-end options to the city are voco Ma Belle Danang and SALA Danang Beach on My Khe, both within short Grab distance of An Thuong. Most beach resorts at My An and Non Nuoc require a 15-20 minute Grab to reach the restaurant scene.
What is the difference between My Khe, My An and Non Nuoc beach areas?
My Khe is the central beach with the best city access and a mix of luxury and mid-range hotels. My An is the established resort zone, 5-10 minutes further south, with larger resort complexes and quieter surroundings. Non Nuoc is the furthest south, with the largest resort grounds, the most villa options, and the best pool and spa infrastructure - but the least city access.
