About Vietnam Time

Da Nang runs on Indochina Time (ICT) — UTC+7 — year-round. Vietnam has not observed Daylight Saving Time since 1975, which makes it one of the simpler time zones to calculate: your offset to Da Nang only ever changes when your clocks move, not theirs.

ICT is shared across Vietnam, Thailand, Laos, and Cambodia — so if you know Bangkok time, you know Da Nang time. It sits one hour behind China Standard Time (CST) and two hours behind Japan Standard Time (JST).

Quick mental math: From London (GMT), Da Nang is +7 in winter and +6 in summer. From New York (EST), Da Nang is +12 in winter and +11 in summer. From Sydney (AEST), Da Nang is −3 in winter, −4 in summer.

Need to know

Vietnam Time Zone Facts

🕐
ICT — Indochina Time

Vietnam uses Indochina Time (ICT), which is UTC+7. It covers all of Vietnam, Thailand, Laos, and Cambodia.

🔄
No Daylight Saving

Vietnam has never observed Daylight Saving Time. The clocks never change — Da Nang is always UTC+7, making it one of the most predictable time zones for travellers.

🌏
Same Zone as Bangkok

Da Nang shares its exact time zone with Bangkok, Ho Chi Minh City, Phnom Penh, and Jakarta. If it's noon in Bangkok, it's noon in Da Nang.

✈️
International Arrivals

Most flights to Da Nang (DAD) land in the morning or afternoon local time. See the flight arrival guide below to plan your first-day schedule.

📅
Date Line Caution

Travellers flying from the US West Coast cross the International Date Line and gain a full calendar day. Book your Da Nang hotel for the day after you depart Los Angeles.

📞
Calling Da Nang Hotels

Da Nang's international dialling code is +84 236. Most hotels answer calls from 7 AM to 10 PM ICT (UTC+7). Reception at large resorts is available 24 hours.

Understanding Da Nang Time for Travellers

Vietnam Standard Time — or Indochina Time (ICT) — is one of the most stable time zones in Asia. Unlike much of Europe, North America, and Australia, Vietnam has no seasonal clock changes. The country settled permanently on UTC+7 after reunification in 1975 and has not adjusted since.

This matters for travellers in two ways: it simplifies scheduling, and it means the jet lag calculation from Western countries is consistent year-round. Travellers arriving from the UK, for example, always face a 7-hour forward shift in winter — not a variable 6 or 7 hours depending on when they travel.

Jet Lag Tips for Da Nang

Da Nang's time zone means most Western visitors are flying eastward (against their body clock). The common advice:

  • Adjust meal times to Vietnam hours on your first day, even if you're not hungry.
  • Get morning sunlight on My Khe Beach — it's the fastest way to reset your circadian rhythm.
  • Avoid napping after 3 PM local time on arrival day.
  • Book a hotel with blackout curtains — most Da Nang beach-strip hotels have them.

Most visitors from East Asia and Australia report minimal jet lag in Da Nang — the 1–3 hour difference rarely disrupts sleep significantly. Travellers from North America and Europe typically need 2–4 days to fully adjust.

Da Nang vs Ho Chi Minh City Time

There is no time difference between Da Nang and Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon). All of Vietnam runs on a single time zone — ICT, UTC+7. Whether you're in Hanoi, Da Nang, or HCMC, your clocks read the same. This simplifies domestic travel scheduling considerably compared to large countries like Australia, the US, or China where domestic time zones vary.

What Is Da Nang's International Dialling Code?

To call a Da Nang number from abroad: dial +84 236 followed by the 7-digit local number. The +84 is Vietnam's country code; 236 is Da Nang's area code. Most hotel reservation lines also accept WhatsApp or email for international guests, which avoids international call charges.
