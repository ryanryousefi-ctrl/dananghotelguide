Melia Vinpearl Da Nang Review

RY
Ryan Yousefi
Da Nang Hotel Guide · Last updated March 2026 · ★★★★★ · From $140/night
Most Complete City HotelHan RiverVingroupFull Facilities

The Melia Vinpearl is the Non Nuoc resort for people who want a large-scale Vietnamese brand property with full resort facilities — not the answer for everyone, but a serious option at its price point.

Vinpearl is Vietnam's largest domestic hotel chain, owned by Vingroup. The Melia Vinpearl Da Nang is a branded partnership — Meliá operating standards applied to a Vinpearl-built resort. What that means practically: large scale, full facilities, Vietnamese ownership with international management.

The property is genuinely large — multiple buildings, extensive grounds, more rooms than most Non Nuoc competitors. Scale creates both advantages (comprehensive facilities, multiple pool options, range of dining) and disadvantages (the impersonal quality that large resort operations can develop if the management isn't attentive).

The case for Melia Vinpearl is straightforward: a full-facility Non Nuoc resort at rates that undercut the Hyatt and Marriott, with the benefit of direct beach access, multiple pools, and a dining setup that handles everything on-site.

Location & Getting There

Non Nuoc Beach, Ngu Hanh Son District — same resort strip as the Hyatt Regency and Da Nang Marriott. 13–14km from the city centre. Airport is 20–22 minutes. Hoi An is 30–35 minutes south.

The Non Nuoc beach position is the property's primary asset — the same wide, calm, well-maintained beach that gives the whole resort cluster its appeal. At this southern point the Marble Mountains are visible on clear days about 3km north, which is one of the better inland views from any Da Nang resort.

City access follows the same Non Nuoc pattern: 80,000–120,000 VND each way by Grab, adding up over a long stay. Most guests commit to the resort after day two and use the on-site facilities. For a large property with this much to offer, that's the right choice.

The Rooms

Room categories span standard rooms through suites to villa-format accommodation. The large property scale means more room variety than boutique competitors. Standard rooms run 50–55sqm and are well-maintained, if less design-forward than some competitors. Suite categories offer sea views and proper balcony space.

The Melia management standard is applied consistently — beds and linens are to Meliá's brand quality, AC and blackout systems work reliably, bathrooms meet the international standard. The room design is competent rather than distinctive, which is an appropriate description for a large-scale resort that serves a broad market.

Rates from $140–200/night in shoulder season for standard categories. MeliáRewards points apply. The rate positions this property below the Marriott Resort but above the Melia Da Nang Beach — in the middle of the Non Nuoc price stack.

Pool, Beach & Facilities

Multiple pools across the extensive grounds — the large resort scale means pool capacity that smaller competitors can't match. Main outdoor pool faces the beach. Additional pools serve different property zones. Pool management and lounger availability vary by area, with the primary beachfront pool receiving the most attentive service.

Non Nuoc Beach access is direct. Beach service is standard for the strip — sunbeds, umbrellas, attendants. The beach in front of the property is maintained and lifeguarded in season.

Kids' facilities are comprehensive — a dedicated kids' pool, supervised activities, and space designed for family use. For families who need full resort infrastructure, the Melia Vinpearl's scale means the facilities exist and are generally available without the capacity constraints of smaller properties.

Spa and fitness centre are full-scale operations appropriate to the property size. Treatment menu is extensive. Gym is properly equipped.

Food & Drink

Multiple dining outlets matching the resort scale — main restaurant for breakfast and all-day dining, beach bar, pool bar, at least one specialty restaurant. Breakfast is a substantial buffet spread with Vietnamese and international options. The Vietnamese morning food station is well-stocked — proper pho and bun bo Hue, not hotel approximations.

The scale of the property means food and beverage operations can feel less intimate than at boutique competitors. Service at larger resort restaurants is harder to make feel personal, and the Melia Vinpearl follows that pattern. The food is good; the experience is efficient rather than warm.

Beach bar and pool bar cover daytime needs without the need to leave the property. For evening dining with more character, An Thuong is a Grab trip north.

The Melia Vinpearl is the Non Nuoc full-resort-facilities choice for travellers who want Vietnamese brand ownership with international management standards.

The Good Stuff

  • Full resort scale — multiple pools, comprehensive kids' facilities, extensive spa
  • Non Nuoc Beach position comparable to Hyatt and Marriott at lower rates
  • MeliáRewards loyalty benefits with better upgrade probability than at Bonvoy-heavy competitors
  • Room variety accommodates families and groups better than boutique alternatives

Watch Out For

  • Large scale can feel impersonal compared to smaller Non Nuoc boutique properties
  • Design less distinctive than newer or boutique competitors
  • Same Non Nuoc city-distance trade-off as all properties on this strip
  • No private pool villa category comparable to Marriott's beach villas

Who Should Stay Here

Families with children who want Non Nuoc Beach access with waterpark infrastructure at the most competitive rates among the Non Nuoc resorts. Not for adult couples seeking the quiet Melia Beach Resort atmosphere.
