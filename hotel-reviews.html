Common Questions

Which hotel in Da Nang is the best?
The InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort — a funicular through jungle, four infinity pools above a private bay, a MICHELIN-starred restaurant. If the price is outside your range, the Hyatt Regency Danang is the most reliably excellent luxury resort on the beach for families. At the mid-range, the A La Carte Da Nang Beach makes a strong case at a fraction of the price.
What is the average hotel price in Da Nang?
Budget guesthouses start around $20–40/night. The My Khe Beach mid-range tier — clean rooms, pool, beachfront — runs $55–120. Beachfront resorts with full facilities sit at $130–220. The luxury tier starts around $250 and goes past $600 for the best villas. Rates shift 30–40% between peak summer and November–January. See the full hotel price guide for month-by-month breakdowns.
Do Da Nang hotels include breakfast?
Depends on the property and rate type. The buffets at the Hyatt Regency, Sheraton Grand, and Hilton are substantial. Fusion Suites includes all-day breakfast as standard in every rate, which is unusual. At budget hotels, breakfast typically isn't included, but a banh mi from the cart outside costs $1 and is usually better anyway.
Is it safe to book Da Nang hotels online?
Yes. Each rate shows its own cancellation conditions, so check them before you book. All hotels on this page link directly to their Trip.com listings so you can check live pricing against your dates.
