Common Questions

Da Nang Hotel Reviews: FAQ

Which hotel in Da Nang is the best? +

The InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort — a funicular through jungle, four infinity pools above a private bay, a MICHELIN-starred restaurant. If the price is outside your range, the Hyatt Regency Danang is the most reliably excellent luxury resort on the beach for families. At the mid-range, the A La Carte Da Nang Beach — that rooftop infinity pool at sunset makes an argument that's hard to counter at $75 a night.

What is the average hotel price in Da Nang? +

Budget guesthouses start around $20–40/night. The My Khe Beach mid-range tier — clean rooms, pool, beachfront — runs $55–120. Beachfront resorts with full facilities (Pullman, Hilton, Furama) sit at $130–220. The luxury tier starts around $250 and goes past $600 for the InterContinental's best villas. Rates shift 30–40% between peak summer and November–January. See the full hotel price guide for month-by-month breakdowns.

Do Da Nang hotels include breakfast? +

Depends on the property and rate type. The buffets at the Hyatt Regency, Sheraton Grand, and Hilton are substantial — the Hyatt's pho and banh mi stations justify the add-on cost. Fusion Suites includes all-day breakfast as standard in every rate, which is unusual and changes the value calculation. At budget hotels, breakfast typically isn't included, but a banh mi from the cart outside costs $1 and is usually better than what a budget hotel kitchen produces anyway.

Is it safe to book Da Nang hotels online? +

Yes, without reservation. Booking.com has the widest inventory and the cancellation policies are displayed accurately — most properties offer free cancellation on standard rates. All hotels on this page link directly to their Booking.com listings so you can check live pricing against your dates. For luxury resorts — InterContinental, Naman, TIA — it's worth calling the hotel directly after finding your rate online; they'll sometimes match the price and add an upgrade or early check-in.

Ready to book your Da Nang stay?

Da Nang's rates shift more between seasons than most cities I've tracked — November and January can be 30–40% cheaper than July for the same room. Check live availability against your actual dates. Free cancellation on most properties.
