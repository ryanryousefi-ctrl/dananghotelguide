Common Questions

Da Nang Hotel Reviews: FAQ

Which hotel in Da Nang is the best? +

The InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort consistently ranks as Da Nang's best hotel. It occupies a private clifftop on Son Tra Peninsula with jungle, sea views, a private bay, and a MICHELIN Guide restaurant. For a more accessible beach resort at significantly lower rates, the Hyatt Regency Danang and Sheraton Grand Danang are the top picks on the main Non Nuoc/My An beach strip.

What is the average hotel price in Da Nang? +

Da Nang hotels span a wide range: budget guesthouses from $20-40/night, solid 3-4 star options at $60-120, beachfront resorts from $130-220, and luxury tier from $250-600+. The city is among the best-value resort destinations in Southeast Asia at the $150-250 midpoint. See our full hotel price guide for month-by-month breakdowns.

Do Da Nang hotels include breakfast? +

Most 4-5 star hotels include or offer breakfast at extra charge. It varies by property and booking type - check when booking. Fusion Suites Da Nang includes all-day breakfast in every rate. Most local guesthouses don't include it but local breakfast options are plentiful and cheap.

Is it safe to book hotels in Da Nang online? +

Yes. Booking.com, Agoda, and direct hotel websites are all reliable booking platforms for Da Nang. Most properties offer free cancellation on advance bookings. All hotels on this site have been independently reviewed and linked directly to their Booking.com listings.
