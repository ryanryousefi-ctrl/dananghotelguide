I've spent years living in Da Nang, and I've walked the lobbies, tested the pools, eaten the breakfasts, and watched the sunrises at every property on this page. These reviews aren't assembled from press releases. They're built from direct experience, cross-referenced against what hundreds of guests repeatedly notice — the things that actually determine whether you leave happy or wish you'd stayed somewhere else.

The range here is deliberate. At the top, you have the InterContinental Sun Peninsula — a resort that genuinely stops people in their tracks the first time they see it. At the other end, the Brilliant Hotel delivers Dragon Bridge views from your window at a price that's hard to argue with. In between, the beach strip along Pham Van Dong — Four Points Sheraton, TMS Hotel, A La Carte — is where most first-timers land, and usually for good reason.

Luxury Resort

InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort

I wasn't ready for the first glimpse of this place. You descend into Son Tra Peninsula through jungle, and then suddenly there are four infinity pools cascading down a cliff above a completely private bay. Bill Bensley designed every detail — the lantern-lit pathways, the open-air cable car to the beach, the hand-painted tiles — and it shows in a way that most luxury hotels only claim. The MICHELIN-listed La Maison 1888 is worth booking a table at even if you're not staying here. Guests consistently say they never wanted to leave, and I completely understand why.

5-Star Resort

Hyatt Regency Danang Resort and Spa

What surprised me most about the Hyatt Regency was how much space there is. Five pools spread across the beachfront, a lazy river, and 200 metres of uncrowded Non Nuoc sand — even at full occupancy it doesn't feel packed. The rooms are well-sized, the breakfast spread is genuinely impressive (I kept going back for the banh mi station), and the service hits a consistency that makes it the most reliably good luxury resort in Da Nang for families. The marble mountains are a short drive. The beach never needs one.

5-Star Resort

Sheraton Grand Danang Resort

I'll admit I expected a competent Sheraton and found something better. The beachfront here is genuinely long — you can walk it in the morning with almost no one around — and the pool complex is more dramatic than it looks in photos, with a slide that kids will ride fifty times and a separate quiet pool that parents will appreciate. The Kids Club runs proper activities, not just babysitting, and the breakfast included most mornings is one of the strongest in the category. Bonvoy members get outsized value at this price point versus comparable properties in the region.

5-Star Resort

Da Nang Marriott Resort & Spa

The pool villa experience here is the real draw. You open your villa door, step onto your private terrace, and your personal plunge pool is right there with a view of the garden or the sea depending on which category you book. I found myself spending more time in that villa than I planned to. The spa is one of the largest in Da Nang and the therapists are consistently praised. It's a quieter resort than the Hyatt — more couples and honeymooners, fewer families — and the Non Nuoc beach in front of it is genuinely beautiful in the early morning.

5-Star Villas

Premier Village Danang Resort

The concept here is simple and it works: every single accommodation is a villa with its own private pool. Walk out of your front door and the South China Sea is on one side, the East Sea on the other — the tip of Son Tra is a narrow strip of land, and Premier Village sits right on the point of it. Families who stay here tend to book larger villas and barely leave the property for the first two days. Kids swim between ocean views, parents drink wine on the terrace. The on-site restaurants are solid enough that you don't feel trapped.

Beachfront

Hilton Da Nang

The rooftop infinity pool at the Hilton is the one that gets photographed most, and you'll see why the first time you walk up there — it sits above My Khe Beach with the ocean stretching out in front and the city behind you. The rooms are large by Da Nang standards, the beds get consistent praise, and the location is the best combination of beach access and city convenience on the strip. I was walking on the sand within two minutes of leaving the lobby. The seafood restaurants on Nguyen Van Thoai are a five-minute walk and worth the trip every evening.

5-Star Beach

Pullman Danang Beach Resort

The Pullman has a design quality that separates it from the mid-range crowd on My Khe — the lobby feels like somewhere you'd actually want to sit and have a coffee, and the rooms have a warmth that Accor's premium tier has gotten better at recently. What I kept noticing was the pool-to-beach transition: the main pool runs along the back garden and a short path takes you straight onto the sand. The breakfast is buffet-style but well-stocked, and the cocktail bar at sunset sees a genuinely nice crowd. A solid step up from the Four Points without the full resort price.

Melia Danang Beach Resort

Melia gets the atmosphere right in a way that most resorts don't even attempt. It's not the right choice if you're travelling with children. But couples who want a resort that feels cool without trying too hard consistently rate it among the best on this stretch of Non Nuoc.

5-Star Beach

Radisson Blu Resort Danang

I watched a family check in at the Radisson Blu and their kids immediately ran towards the water park before their parents had finished signing paperwork. Boutique Retreat

Naman Retreat Da Nang

Walking into Naman Retreat feels genuinely different from every other resort in Da Nang. The bamboo architecture by Vo Trong Nghia creates a dappled, jungle-filtered light that I didn't stop noticing even after two days — pathways shaded by soaring bamboo columns, pools that feel like they're suspended in a forest, the sound of wind through the structure at night. The villa pool rooms are the ones to book: private gardens, plunge pools, and a level of quiet that's unusual this close to My Khe. Guests who know architecture and design consistently rate this as their favourite stay in Vietnam.

Wellness Resort

TIA Wellness Resort Da Nang

TIA is built around a premise that most wellness hotels say and few actually deliver: the spa isn't optional, it's the point. Every rate includes daily spa treatments, and you'll find yourself genuinely looking forward to them by day two. The pool villas are calm, the beach access is direct, and the kitchen produces food that's healthy without being joyless. Yoga happens every morning with the sea breeze coming through the studio. I noticed guests slowing down noticeably by their second day, talking less, moving more slowly — that's the sign this kind of place is working.

All-Suite

Fusion Suites Da Nang Beach

The thing that converts people on Fusion Suites is realising the spa is already paid for. Every stay includes two spa treatments per day, all-day breakfast, and afternoon tea — which sounds like marketing until you actually experience it and realise you've essentially been handed a full spa resort at what looks like a mid-range price. The suites are all-suite by design, so the smallest room has a proper living area and a kitchen. The beach access is there, the location on My Khe is solid, and the staff consistently surprise guests with small, unprompted gestures. Repeat bookers are unusually common here.

Melia Vinpearl Da Nang

The Melia Vinpearl makes the most sense if you're planning a week or longer in Da Nang and want the city at your feet. Vincom Plaza is literally attached to the building — cinema, supermarket, restaurants, and a Starbucks are accessible without going outside. The Han River is right there, and the Dragon Bridge is a short walk. Rooms are spacious and the views from upper floors are legitimately good. It doesn't have the beach access of the strip hotels, but if you want to actually explore the city rather than lie by the pool, this is the most convenient base in Da Nang.

Japanese Resort

Mikazuki Japanese Resorts & Spa Da Nang

There is genuinely nowhere else like Mikazuki in Da Nang, and that's the whole point. The onsen complex is real — natural hot spring mineral water, multiple indoor and outdoor baths, traditional Japanese bathing etiquette — and the contrast of sitting in a rotenburo with views of the South China Sea is something I kept finding myself thinking about afterwards. The water park adds entertainment value that makes it particularly good for Japanese families or anyone wanting a culturally distinct stay. The food leans Japanese throughout, and the staff handle the unusual combination of resort and onsen with practiced ease.

City Hotel

Novotel Danang Premier Han River

Waking up with Dragon Bridge visible from the window — especially on Friday or Saturday when it breathes fire — is the reason people choose the Novotel over beach hotels. The location on the Han River puts you minutes from the best restaurants and the Han Market, and the airport is so close that arrivals feel almost frictionless. Sky36 is on the 36th floor of this building, so even if you're not staying in a river-view room, the rooftop bar is always there for sundowners. The rooms are Novotel-standard but well-maintained, and the breakfast gets consistently strong reviews for variety.

Best Mid-Range Hotels in Da Nang

This is the tier where Da Nang's real value story lives. A direct beachfront room on My Khe, a rooftop pool, a gym, and a breakfast buffet — for $65-120 a night. I've watched travellers arrive expecting something serviceable and leave genuinely surprised by how much they got. These hotels may not have the architecture of the InterContinental, but they have the beach, the views, and the service consistency that makes a holiday feel like a holiday.

4-Star Beach

Four Points by Sheraton Danang

The Four Points does exactly what it says: Sheraton reliability, a direct path to My Khe Beach, and Bonvoy points that add up fast at this price. I kept noticing how competent the service was — nothing flashy, just consistently helpful. The rooms are modern and the beds are genuinely comfortable. The pool is smaller than the luxury resorts but well-maintained and rarely crowded. If you're a Bonvoy member who wants to earn points without paying Sheraton Grand rates, this is the obvious choice on the strip. The seafood restaurants nearby make the location feel even better.

Grand Mercure Da Nang

What the Grand Mercure offers that the beach hotels don't is perspective. The upper floor rooms have Han River views and Dragon Bridge in the frame, which is particularly good when the bridge lights up on weekend nights. The rooftop pool gets afternoon sun and the view from it — city on three sides, river below — genuinely makes you feel like you've figured something out that other travellers haven't. It's a short walk to the best pho in the city, the Con Market, and the Cham Museum. For anyone who wants to actually feel Da Nang rather than just lie on its beach, this is the right hotel.

Furama Resort Danang

Almost thirty years of operating has given Furama something money can't fast-track: mature tropical grounds. The gardens are dense and established in a way that makes the property feel genuinely lush rather than freshly landscaped. Muong Thanh Luxury Da Nang Hotel

Brilliant Hotel Da Nang

The Brilliant Hotel has been making the same case for years and it holds up: a river-facing room with Dragon Bridge in the window, walking distance to the Han Market and the city's best pho, and rates that feel almost too low for what you get. Why mid-range hotels are popular in Da Nang

Put it this way: a direct beachfront room with a pool and breakfast on My Khe Beach costs $65-120 a night. The equivalent in Phuket or Bali is $150-250. The Four Points Sheraton, TMS Hotel, and Muong Thanh Luxury are popular because they deliver real beach access and real comfort at a price point that lets you extend the trip. First-time visitors especially tend to book here, spend their mornings on the beach, and use their evenings to explore Dragon Bridge and the waterfront — which are both walkable.

Common Questions

Da Nang Hotel Reviews: FAQ

Which hotel in Da Nang is the best? +

Nothing in Da Nang rivals the InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort for sheer drama and quality — it sits on a private clifftop above Son Tra Peninsula's jungle with a private bay, four infinity pools, and a MICHELIN-listed restaurant. If that's outside your budget, the Hyatt Regency Danang is the most consistently praised luxury resort on the beach strip, and the Sheraton Grand Danang is the family benchmark. For mid-range, the A La Carte Da Nang Beach is hard to beat for what it delivers at the price.

What is the average hotel price in Da Nang? +

Budget guesthouses start at $20-40/night in the city centre. The My Khe Beach mid-range tier — clean rooms, pool, direct beach access — runs $55-120/night, which is where most first-time visitors land. Beachfront resorts with full facilities sit at $130-220/night, and the luxury tier starts around $250 and goes well past $600 for the InterContinental and Naman's best villas. Compared to equivalent destinations in Phuket, Bali, or Maldives, Da Nang's pricing is significantly more accessible across every tier. See our full hotel price guide for month-by-month breakdowns.

Do Da Nang hotels include breakfast? +

It depends on your rate and the property. Most 4-5 star hotels on My Khe offer breakfast-included rates alongside room-only options — the breakfast buffets at the Hyatt Regency, Sheraton Grand, and Hilton are all substantial and worth the add-on if it's available at a reasonable premium. Fusion Suites includes all-day breakfast as standard in every rate, which is part of why the value calculation there is so compelling. At guesthouses and budget hotels, breakfast is usually not included, but local Vietnamese breakfast — banh mi, pho, bun bo Hue — is everywhere and costs under $2.

Is it safe to book hotels in Da Nang online? +

Yes, completely. Booking.com and Agoda are the two platforms that work best for Da Nang — both have extensive inventory, accurate cancellation policies displayed clearly, and genuine guest reviews. All the hotels reviewed on this site link directly to their Booking.com pages so you can check live availability and read recent guest feedback before committing. Most properties in Da Nang offer free cancellation on advance bookings, which makes it low-risk to lock in a rate early. Direct hotel booking occasionally produces a marginally better rate or a room upgrade — worth checking for the luxury properties once you've done your research here.
