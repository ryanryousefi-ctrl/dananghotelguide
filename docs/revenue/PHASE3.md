- **Component library** `scripts/revenue3/components.js` + `dn-commerce.css`: A hero price-check, B hotel price card, C compare module/group, D editor's verdict, E booking band, F dates reminder, G return/bookmark, H seasonal advice. Every Trip.com anchor is built from `data/tripcom/registry.json` (VERIFIED only); the library creates no URLs.
- **Flagship `where-to-stay-in-da-nang.html`** (`apply-where-to-stay.js`): price-check hero under the page hero, six-area chooser (suits / advantage / drawback / area guide / hotels), six hotel pick cards, hotel buttons inside each area card, three compare pairs, seasonal advice, return module, final "Ready to choose?" section with disclosure. Existing content kept; URL unchanged. City search is never presented as area-filtered.
- **Homepage** (`apply-home.js`): price-comparison module + finder link after the intro, six editor's picks after neighbourhoods, a compare module after reviews. Slider, search form and Trip.com header button untouched.
- **Sitewide rollout** (`apply-rollout.js`, rules in `rules.json`): 107 existing pages by type (35 hotel lists, 10 area guides, 19 hotel comparisons, 20 reviews, 23 planning guides) plus the flagship and homepage = 109 pages with new commercial modules. Placement per type: lists = early price-check + dates reminder + compare + return; area guides = early module + return; comparisons = early side-by-side with both hotels' verified links; reviews = save/compare tools after the Phase 1 early module (no duplicate CTA); guides = one contextual module (seasonal guides also get H). 16 more pages got contextual text links (`apply-links.js`).
- **Hotel comparison tool** `compare-hotels.html` + `dn-compare.js`: 2 to 3 hotels from the 21 reviewed, side-by-side rows, deep links `?h=a,b`, verified Trip.com button per hotel, "Not verified" for unknown fields, no prices or scores.
- **Shortlist** `dn-shortlist.js`: Save on cards and finder results, localStorage only, copy-link, load into compare; no account, no network, no fake states. Finder results now have Save and Compare.
- **Sticky bar**: one script (`dn-sticky-cta.js`) extended to guides and lists with a top module; still phones only, still one bar.
- **Four new guides** from the facts dataset: `da-nang-hotels-at-a-glance`, `da-nang-hotels-without-a-car`, `how-to-check-da-nang-hotel-prices`, `da-nang-hotels-for-three-nights` (in the sitemap, linked from hubs).

## Verified hotel facts dataset
`data/hotels/facts.json` (built by `build-facts.js` from `data/hotels/research/*.json`, `editorial.js`, `tags.js`): 21 reviewed hotels with Trip.com registry key, review URL, sourced objective facts, editorial opinion in a separate block, and `display` text used by the tool and cards.
- **Method and honesty:** research was done 23 Nov 2026 from public listings (Trip.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, Booking.com, Agoda) plus Nominatim coordinates; most official hotel sites were unreachable (403/timeouts). Confidence is medium for most, low for Radisson, Naman, TIA, Azura, Fusion Suites. Low-confidence hotels show "Not verified" for everything except what our own review states.
- **Distances** are labelled by kind. Only straight-line distances (great-circle from OSM coordinates to OSM-geocoded airport, Dragon Bridge, Han Market, rounded to 0.5 km) are shown; walking and driving distances were not verifiable and are not shown as facts.
- **Pool wording is withheld** where listings contradict each other or our own review (Novotel, Meliá Vinpearl, Marriott, Muong Thanh, Brilliant, Hilton, Grand Mercure): the tool says "confirm on the hotel page".
- **Corrections made:** Hilton Da Nang review claimed "8 km, fastest airport transfer of any quality hotel" and "15 minutes to Han Market"; map coordinates put it about 4.5 km from the airport in a straight line and about 0.5 km from Han Market, so the claims were rewritten. Tet 2027 page said Tet is January 29 (it is February 6, 2027), carried invented price multipliers and scarcity claims, and showed a visible "[VERIFY]" marker; all rewritten. Visible `[VERIFY: ...]` markers were also removed from the Singapore, Australian and worst-time pages, replacing unverified flight/visa specifics with "check current terms".
- **Open owner questions:** the Brilliant, Novotel, Grand Mercure and Hilton reviews describe rooftop pools while listings mostly show indoor/outdoor pools; the reviews read as firsthand so they were not changed. Listings say Sheraton Grand and Marriott are on the Truong Sa coast, not My Khe, and our site labels differ. "Radisson Blu Da Nang": listings show Radisson Hotel Danang (170 Vo Nguyen Giap); we kept the review name. Azura, Fusion Suites and Silk Path still have no verified Trip.com deep link.

## Language policy
Approved: Check Current Prices, Check Prices for Your Dates, Compare Hotel Rates, Check Room Availability, Compare Da Nang Hotel Prices, Check This Hotel on Trip.com. Not used anywhere: lowest/cheapest/guaranteed, scarcity, "price drops", "book before prices increase", "exclusive", "best time to book is now". No live prices, no savings claims, no claim that Trip.com is cheaper.
