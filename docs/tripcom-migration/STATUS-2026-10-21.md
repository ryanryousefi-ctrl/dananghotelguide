## Findings that need your decision
1. **Logo (agreement 9.2):** use of Trip.com logos needs Trip.com's prior written consent. Buttons are therefore **text-only** ("... on Trip.com") until `data/tripcom/branding.json` records consent. Ask support (see `SUPPORT-REQUEST-BULK-LINKS.md`, question 7).
2. **Legal wording** on privacy, terms, about, editorial policy and why-trust-us was changed per `legal-and-disclosure-drafts.md`. Needs your approval (and ideally a legal read) before launch.
3. **Domain:** the generator returns `www.trip.com`. Whether a Vietnam-focused site must use `vn.trip.com` is unconfirmed: still open.
4. **Automation:** the Online Cooperation Agreement has no clause about scripts, automation or bulk use (searched for automation, robot, crawl, scrape, API, bulk, batch). It does say the registered email/account is for the owner's own use. The dashboard has no bulk or API tool. Links were generated one at a time in your own signed-in session at about 13 s per link. Trip.com support was not asked; confirm if you want it in writing.
5. **`trip_sub1`:** the generator lets you label links (documented in the user guide). We left it empty like your first two links. A label per placement would multiply the link count; a single label (for example per pilot) is possible if you want it.
6. **Dashboard test:** the dashboard says "Test affiliate material tracking: check if materials register clicks and bookings; you can test with friends on multiple devices." That is the only official test route. It conflicts with the agreement's ban on fictitious orders if it means making bookings, so only a single, honest click-through check would be reasonable. Your call; not done.
7. **Search Box:** exact code in `data/tripcom/search-box.json` (banner S20398814, Da Nang, English). It is saved in your account under Affiliate Tools > Search Box > My Website (delete there if unwanted). Loaded lazily; third-party cookies from Trip.com follow its policy: the privacy text now says so. Static/dynamic banners were not added (they would duplicate the booking buttons and the editorial design).
8. **Editorial:** 15 remaining mentions of Booking.com are legitimate references (Booking.com ratings/reviews as a source, the "different from Booking.com" comparison, Korean tips about its review filter). The Editorial commit `b4ab778` (Prince Hotel rename) is still only on `tripcom-migration`.
