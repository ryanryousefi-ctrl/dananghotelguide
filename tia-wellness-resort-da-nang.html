TIA Wellness Resort

5-Star Wellness Resort · My Khe Beach

“Check-in happens over welcome tea, not a front desk, and the rate covers everything after that.”

8.9/ 10
Editorial Score
Rooms 8.8 Location 8.9 Facilities 9.3 Food+Drink 8.1 Service 9.2 Value 8.4
Quick Verdict

Book it if you need an actual reset, not a beach holiday. TIA is Da Nang's only dedicated wellness resort, and the nightly rate covers accommodation, all meals, unlimited spa treatments, yoga, breathwork, tai chi and fitness consultations, everything, no reaching for your wallet after check-in. Only 58 rooms keeps it human-scaled, every villa comes with a private plunge pool, and the yoga classes are accessible enough to convert people who don't think yoga is for them. This only works if you stay four nights or more and book your spa slots on day one, good times fill fast. If you want a lively pool scene, a rooftop bar, or nightlife, this is the wrong hotel entirely.

Our Take

Check-in at TIA doesn't happen at a front desk. It happens at a table in the dining area, with welcome tea in front of you while someone walks you through the programme. One reviewer described the ritual as "a bit awkward," which is fair, but it's also unusual in a way that makes you realize you're not in a regular hotel anymore. A weight falls away. TIA operates on a model nothing else in Da Nang matches: the nightly rate covers accommodation, all meals, unlimited spa treatments, yoga classes, breathwork, tai chi, fitness consultations, everything. The property has just 58 rooms and villas set among lush vegetation on the beach.

Yoga turned out to be the real surprise. Classes here are structured to be genuinely accessible, not intimidating, and turn out to be among the most useful 60 minutes of any day, even for guests who aren't particularly flexible and don't have a spiritual relationship with the practice. When guests leave after four days, several report sleeping better for two weeks straight than they had for two months before arriving. That's the actual product being sold here, not a beach view.

The Rooms

The Villas and Rooms

All rooms are private villas with plunge pools. Each one has a garden patio with sofas and loungers, a large bathroom with a plunge bathtub and rain shower, and an outdoor shower set into the garden. The setting is lush enough that you feel contained, not enclosed, but genuinely removed from the city-beach noise that follows you into most My Khe hotels.

One honest note: in the rainy season, some villas can feel cold and damp inside. Guests who flagged this had a portable heater and dehumidifier delivered quickly. Know it's possible, know it gets addressed when raised. Which room would we choose? Any entry-level villa works, since every category includes the plunge pool and garden patio, the differences up the ladder are mostly about size rather than what's included.

The Reason to Book It

The Spa and the Programme

Here's where you need to pay attention: treatments are included, but booking slots fill up fast. One guest found that by the time they tried to schedule, they were left with 9am and 6pm slots that cut through the day in inconvenient ways. Book your treatments for the whole stay as soon as you arrive, front-load the schedule on day one. The treatments themselves are excellent, the spa team is professional and practiced, and the experience is genuinely restorative rather than superficially pampering. This, not a beach view, is what you're actually paying for.

Pool + Beach

Pool + Beach

This is not a beach resort in any conventional sense. There's no lively pool scene, no DJ sets on the sand, no rooftop bar where you drink cocktails at sunset with strangers. The beach is there and it's lovely, but TIA is built around programme and stillness rather than beach-resort energy. If that's what you're coming for, it delivers at a level that's genuinely hard to find anywhere in Vietnam at this price point. If you want a resort in the usual sense, look elsewhere.

Food + Breakfast

Food + Drink

The breakfast spread is specific and curated: congee, pho, banh mi, hot dishes, gluten-free pastries, organic eggs to order, fresh fruit, yogurt bowls, daily energy shots, juices, Vietnamese salads, mini banh mis. Book the wider spread, not the pho specifically, one reviewer noted "bland broth, overly soft noodles, tough beef," and that's an honest assessment. The fruit, the pastries, and the energy shots are genuinely good.

All meals are included, and dinner has enough rotating variety to sustain a week without feeling repetitive. Go in expecting curated wellness food rather than a destination pho bowl, and the meal plan delivers exactly what it promises.

Who It's For

Families, Couples & Who It Suits

This suits burned-out professionals and couples who want a genuine reset over a beach holiday, and it works especially well for anyone skeptical of wellness programming who's willing to actually try the yoga rather than skip it. It is not built for families with young kids or travelers chasing nightlife and a social pool scene. The My Khe location gives more city access than you'd get at a Non Nuoc or Son Tra property, Dragon Bridge is 12 minutes by Grab, Hoi An is 30 minutes by car, so the retreat doesn't require total isolation if you want the occasional excursion.

What We Like

What We Like

The all-inclusive model

Meals, unlimited spa treatments, and all classes are genuinely included in the rate, no add-on pricing hiding behind a low headline number.

Only 58 rooms

The resort scale stays human, not corporate, and it shows in how personal the check-in ritual and service feel.

Every villa's plunge pool

Private plunge pool and garden patio at every category, not reserved for the top-tier rooms.

Accessible yoga

Classes genuinely work for non-practitioners, not just the already-committed, and multiple guests report the same surprise.

The Tradeoff

Book spa slots immediately on arrival, good times fill fast, and if you wait you'll be left with inconvenient 9am and 6pm windows that break up the day. Villas can feel cold and damp in rainy season, though flagging it gets a heater and dehumidifier delivered quickly. The pho at breakfast is genuinely average, bland broth, overly soft noodles, tough beef, even though the rest of the food is considerably better. And this is not a conventional beach resort: no lively pool scene, no rooftop bar, no nightlife. It's designed for immersive stays of four nights or more, and a single night here won't deliver the value the concept is built around.

Location

Location

On My Khe Beach, which gives more city access than you'd have at a Non Nuoc or Son Tra property. Dragon Bridge is 12 minutes by Grab, Hoi An is 30 minutes by car, and the airport is about 7km away. What this means in practice: the retreat concept doesn't require total isolation. You can step out for a day trip without undoing the point of the stay, though most guests who commit to the programme find they don't want to leave the property much at all.

Would We Stay Here

YES - BUT ONLY WHEN NEEDED

Yes, but only when it's actually needed. TIA isn't a beach holiday, it's a reset. After four days here, guests report sleeping better for two weeks than they had for two months before arriving. That's the product. If you're burned out, overworked, or just want to hand a week over to something that will genuinely restore you, this is one of the most effective places in Vietnam to do it.
