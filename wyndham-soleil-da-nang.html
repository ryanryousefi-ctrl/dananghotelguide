Wyndham Soleil Danang

“Ocean views from upper floors, but check current reviews first.”

4-Star Beachfront Hotel · My Khe Beach

Our Take

Wyndham Soleil Danang opened recently and already has a notable cluster of maintenance complaints in its reviews, mold on bathroom shutters, dirty grouting, guests needing room changes on arrival.

These are serious concerns for a new building that should be clean without effort.

Advice: check current reviews before booking to see if this pattern persists, and if you arrive to find a room with mold, request a change immediately and ask for a written confirmation of the swap.

Staff have been described as excellent and responsive when issues are raised, the building quality is the concern, not the team.

With that said, the upper-floor views from the 34th floor are genuinely striking, the staff consistently earn the warmest descriptions in guest reviews, and the beach is a minute's walk.

There is a legitimate hotel here.

It just requires clear eyes about what might turn up.

The Rooms

The Rooms

The 34-floor tower gives good return on height. Two-bedroom family suites on the 34th floor have ocean views from every room and a balcony that looks out over My Khe Beach and the city skyline together, on clear mornings the sunrise from bed has been described by guests as one of the best things they experienced in Da Nang. The warning: partial sea view rooms at mid-height are genuinely partial, one guest described having to crane their neck off the balcony to see a sliver of ocean. Book high or book ocean-facing if views matter, and check the bathroom carefully on arrival given the recurring mold and grouting complaints in recent reviews.

Which room would we choose? A two-bedroom family suite on the 34th floor or as close to it as available, ocean-facing specifically. Anything billed as partial sea view at mid-height should be avoided unless the price gap is significant.

The Reason to Book It

High-Floor Ocean Views

Rooms on the 34th floor deliver some of the best ocean views on My Khe Beach, with sunrise views guests describe as unmissable. On a good day, in a well-maintained room, the Wyndham Soleil has views that more expensive neighbours can't replicate without more height. That's the entire case for booking here over a lower-rise alternative, but it only holds if the room is genuinely high and ocean-facing.

Pool + Beach

Pool + Beach

An outdoor pool with sea views, long and attractive per the more positive reviews, not very attractive per another, the pool appears more atmospheric on quieter days and less so during peak season crowding. The beach is a minute's walk, that part is straightforward and genuine, and it's the one element of this hotel with no asterisk attached to it.

Food + Breakfast

Food + Drink

Multiple guests describe a plentiful breakfast, generous and varied. At least one December 2025 review mentioned live insects on food during breakfast and staff not reacting, a serious hygiene complaint worth checking has been resolved before committing. If recent reviews show the issue addressed, the breakfast spread is otherwise a genuine strength of the stay.

Who It's For

Families, Couples & Who It Suits

This suits Wyndham Rewards members and travelers who will commit to booking a high, ocean-facing floor and who are comfortable checking recent reviews before arrival. Two-bedroom family suites on the top floors work well for families wanting real space and a view. It's a poor fit for anyone who wants a guaranteed, worry-free stay without doing homework first, or for light sleepers arriving on a weekend given the park noise across the street.

What We Like

What We Like

High-floor ocean views

Among the best on My Khe Beach from floor 30 and above, with sunrise views guests describe as unmissable.

Staff quality

The most consistently praised element of the whole stay, excellent and responsive when issues are raised.

The sunrise

Described by guests as one of the best things they experienced in Da Nang, visible from bed on clear mornings.

Beach proximity

A genuine minute's walk from the entrance, no exaggeration needed here.

The Tradeoff

Mold and maintenance issues, bathroom grout, ventilation fans, mold on shutters, have been reported often enough in this newly opened building to be a real pattern, not a one-off. Ask about current status before booking, and request a room change on arrival if anything looks off. Weekend karaoke from the park across the street is confirmed and unavoidable on lower floors, the hotel has acknowledged this and it isn't going away. Partial sea view rooms significantly underdeliver, and a breakfast hygiene complaint from December 2025 is worth checking has been resolved before you commit.

Location

Location

My Khe Beach, a minute's walk from the entrance, with the airport 8km and about 12 minutes away. What this means for the trip: the beach access here is as good as it gets in Da Nang, genuinely a minute's walk, not a marketing exaggeration. Dragon Bridge is about 10 minutes by taxi, and the restaurant strip is two minutes on foot.

Beach: 1 min walkAirport: 8 km
Would We Stay Here

POSSIBLY - WITH CONDITIONS

In a high-floor, ocean-facing room, after verifying current maintenance and hygiene reviews, possibly. The views are the real thing and the staff make it feel looked-after in ways the building itself hasn't fully earned yet. But this is only worth booking if recent reviews show the mold and breakfast issues have been addressed. When they're resolved, this becomes a straightforwardly good hotel. Until then, eyes open.
