Hilton Da Nang

“The fastest airport transfer of any quality hotel in the city.”

Our Take

The Hilton Da Nang sits on the Han River, not on the beach, and that distinction matters more than it sounds.

The river views at sunrise are good, genuinely good, with Dragon Bridge visible from the river-facing rooms and the Executive Lounge looking out over Da Nang Bay.

But the Han River itself runs brown during the day from red clay sediment in the water, and nobody warns you about this.

If you're picturing turquoise water from your room, you won't get it here.

What you do get is a well-positioned city hotel with airport access that rivals anything else in Da Nang, 8km, a 12-minute taxi, a banh mi station at breakfast worth going back to more than once, and a location 10 minutes from Dragon Bridge and 15 from Han Market by taxi.

This is not a beach resort. It's a city hotel with beach proximity, and it performs that role well.

The Rooms

The Rooms

Rooms are spacious and clean, with floor-to-ceiling windows that work in your favor on the upper floors, the river views actually earn the room rate at that height. Executive Lounge floors add complimentary breakfast plus evening cocktails, which changes the value calculation significantly for business travellers. Standard rooms on lower floors are fine if you're not spending much time in them, less impressive if you are. The Executive Lounge itself has views of the nearby beach and Da Nang Bay, and from that vantage point the city's geography makes more sense than it does from the river, on a clear day you can trace the coastline south toward the Marble Mountains.

Which room would we choose? An Executive Lounge floor room facing the bay rather than the river. The lounge access pays for itself on a multi-night stay, and the bay view avoids the brown-water disappointment entirely.

The Reason to Book It

Executive Lounge + Fast Transfers

The Executive Lounge and the city's fastest airport transfer make this the practical pick for travelers who value convenience over beach immersion. At 8km and a 12-minute taxi ride, no other quality hotel in Da Nang gets you from the runway to a shower faster, which matters more than it sounds on a red-eye arrival or an early departure. Hilton Honors members also get real value here, the points and status perks stretch further at this property than at some of the beach resorts further south.

Pool + Beach

Pool + Beach

The rooftop bar delivers a better view than expected, city skyline, the river, and in the right light, the coast beyond, and it's a reasonable spot for sunset cocktails if you want something other than a seafood grill on the beach strip. The pool adjacent to it is small relative to what resort properties offer, it does the job if you want a swim with a view rather than a proper lap. My Khe Beach itself is a short taxi away, not walkable, so this is not the hotel for guests who want to roll out of bed onto the sand.

Food + Breakfast

Food + Drink

The breakfast buffet covers Vietnamese pho, Chinese, Korean, and Western dishes, but the banh mi station is where most of the time goes, a make-your-own setup with proper ingredients rather than hotel approximations. The breakfast room is large and reasonably well-managed, though it gets crowded and noisy by 8:30am. Eat before 8 if you want the more relaxed version of the same spread.

Who It's For

Families, Couples & Who It Suits

This makes sense for a city-focused stay, Dragon Bridge at night, street food runs, museum visits, a day trip to Hoi An, and for business travellers who want a reliable brand with fast airport access and real Hilton Honors value. It does not make sense for anyone building a trip around beach time and resort living, for that, the properties on My Khe Beach proper or the Non Nuoc stretch further south deliver more directly. Families who want a pool complex and kids' club should also look elsewhere, this hotel does not attempt that.

What We Like

What We Like

The airport transfer

8km, a 12-minute taxi, the fastest of any quality Da Nang hotel. Genuinely useful on early or late flights.

The Executive Lounge

Complimentary breakfast and evening cocktails with views over Da Nang Bay, a real value-add for longer or business stays.

Bridge and river views

Dragon Bridge and the Han River visible from upper floors, best appreciated at sunrise before the water turns brown.

The banh mi station

A make-your-own setup with proper ingredients, the clear standout of the breakfast buffet.

The Tradeoff

This is not a beach hotel, whatever the branding around it might imply. The Hilton sits on the Han River, and My Khe Beach requires a short taxi ride, not a walk across the road. The river itself runs brown during the day from red clay sediment, so a river-view room bought for the water shouldn't be the centerpiece of the stay. The rooftop pool is small compared to beach resort competitors, better for a view than a swim, and the breakfast room gets crowded and loud after 8:30am. Know what this hotel actually is before booking a river-view room expecting resort scenery.

Location

Location

The Hilton sits on the Han River near the river mouth, not on My Khe Beach itself, 8km from the airport, a 10-minute taxi to Dragon Bridge, and 15 minutes to Han Market. What this means for the trip: you get some of the fastest access to the airport and the city's cultural core of any hotel in Da Nang, but beach time means a short taxi rather than a walk out the door. For a city-focused stay with beach visits as a side trip, that tradeoff works in this hotel's favor. For a beach-first trip, it doesn't.

Would We Stay Here

YES - FOR A CITY STAY

For the right trip, yes. For a city-focused stay, Dragon Bridge at night, street food runs, museum visits, a day trip to Hoi An, the Hilton makes sense. For beach time and resort living, the better choice is something on the sand. This hotel does what it is very well. The only requirement is knowing what it is before booking.
