A La Carte vs TMS Da Nang

A La Carte and TMS sit on the same stretch of My Khe Beach and compete for the same mid-luxury beach customer. One has a better pool. One has better value.

A La Carte for the pool deck and design. TMS for better rates at same quality.
Overview Location Rooms Amenities Best For Verdict

Hotel Overview

A La Carte Da Nang Beach

  • ★★★★★ Design-led beach hotel
  • 224 studio apartments
  • My Khe Beach, An Thuong area
  • Rooftop infinity pool + sky bar
  • All-studio format, separate living area
  • From ~$70/night

TMS Luxury Da Nang Beach Hotel

  • ★★★★★ Boutique beach hotel
  • 179 rooms
  • My Khe Beach, My Khe area
  • Rooftop pool, beachfront location
  • Vietnamese-owned, strong local service
  • From ~$60/night

A La Carte and TMS are the two most-booked properties in the My Khe boutique tier. They are not chain hotels, they are not cheap, and they are not trying to be. Both have rooftop pools and direct beach access, and both appeal to the same category of traveller: someone who wants a real beach hotel without the formality of a big resort.

A La Carte's all-studio format means every room has a separate living area with a kitchenette. This is a meaningful advantage for longer stays or anyone who wants space. The TMS is more conventional in room format but consistently runs slightly lower rates. Both are genuinely good hotels for My Khe. See the boutique hotels guide.

A La Carte's position near An Thuong is worth noting. Walking out of the hotel and being able to reach cafes, independent restaurants, and convenience stores on foot is a meaningful quality-of-life advantage over a more isolated resort location. TMS is a short Grab from all the same options.

For short stays where the room is just a place to sleep, TMS competes well at a lower rate.

A La Carte's rooftop Sky Bar is its best amenity. It's genuinely popular and one of the better sunset viewing spots on the beach strip. TMS's spa is better than A La Carte's. Both have beach service and comparable pool setups.

Who Each Hotel Is Best For

Book A La Carte if…

  • Stays of 4+ nights where space matters
  • Travellers who want to self-cater occasionally
  • Anyone who values the An Thuong cafe neighbourhood
  • Design-conscious travellers who find chain hotels sterile
  • Those who will use the rooftop bar regularly

Book TMS Hotel if…

  • Short stays of 1-3 nights
  • Budget-conscious guests in the mid-luxury tier
  • Guests who want beach proximity as the main priority
  • Those who prioritise spa over kitchen facilities
  • Travellers who found A La Carte fully booked

Final Verdict

A La Carte is the better hotel by design quality and the studio format alone justifies choosing it for longer stays. The rooftop situation is strong, the An Thuong proximity is useful, and the beach access is equivalent.

TMS is the right call if A La Carte is $20+ more expensive for your dates and the room size is not a priority. It is not a compromise hotel — it is a genuinely good My Khe property that simply costs less.

For anything over four nights: book A La Carte's studio. For a weekend stay focused on beach time: TMS makes perfect financial sense and the experience gap is minor.
