Why Stay on the Han River?

The Han River gives you Da Nang the city, not Da Nang the beach resort. Bach Dang waterfront is walkable, restaurants are close, Dragon Bridge is nearby, and the night market runs Thursday to Sunday a short walk away. Hotels here generally cost 20-40% less than comparable quality at the beach.

If you're here for business, a longer stay, or you want city life with the beach as a day trip - this is the smarter base.

The tradeoff: My Khe Beach requires a Grab - about 10-15 minutes and 40,000-60,000 VND each way. Not far, but not walking out your door onto the sand.

Stay Here If

You want city walkability, Dragon Bridge, river views, and a lower nightly rate than the beach.

Skip It If

You want to walk onto My Khe every morning without booking a Grab.

Staying Near Dragon Bridge

The evening riverfront atmosphere is one of Da Nang's genuine pleasures - locals, street food, riverside cafes, and the bridges lit up after dark. Walking Bach Dang at night is a different city than the beach strip.

The Dragon Bridge fire and water show runs on weekend evenings. Verify the current schedule directly with Da Nang authorities before planning around it - times have changed over the years. Best viewing is from the west bank of the river. Several Han River hotels have rooftop or high-floor views of the show.

Common Questions

Are Han River hotels cheaper than beach hotels?
Generally yes - 20-40% cheaper at the same quality level. Brilliant and Muong Thanh Luxury offer river addresses at mid-range prices. The tradeoff is My Khe Beach requires a 10-15 minute Grab ride.
Can you see Dragon Bridge from Han River hotels?
Some can, some can't depending on room orientation. CARO, Novotel, and Brilliant are all close enough to walk to the bridge. For guaranteed views from your room, confirm the view direction when booking - ask for a river-facing room.
How far are Han River hotels from My Khe Beach?
About 3-4km - a 10-15 minute Grab costing 40,000-60,000 VND each way. Easy as a day trip or beach evening, but not walkable.
Is the Han River area safe at night?
Yes. Bach Dang is one of Da Nang's most pleasant and safe evening strips. Well-lit, consistent foot traffic, families and couples out late. Da Nang overall has a low crime rate.
Which Han River hotel has the best rooftop?
Novotel's Sky36 is the most established - proper bar, river views, Dragon Bridge sightlines. CARO's rooftop pool and bar is newer and more design-forward. Both are genuinely good.
