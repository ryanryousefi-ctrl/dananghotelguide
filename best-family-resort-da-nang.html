Non Nuoc Beach Safety for Families

Important: Non Nuoc Beach has strong currents and rough conditions from October through March. During those months the red flag goes up often, and when it's up, swimming is prohibited, not just discouraged.

May through September is when the water is actually safe for kids. Check the flag system every time before letting children in, even if the water looks calm. Calm-looking and safe aren't the same thing at this beach.

Most of Da Nang's best family resorts sit on Non Nuoc Beach, south of the city. Lifeguards are posted during the safe swimming season, and the resorts mark out swimming zones with flags.

Outside that window, plan on the resort pool doing the work instead of the ocean.

My Khe Beach, where Pullman and a few others sit, follows the same seasonal pattern. If you're visiting October through March, treat the pool as your swimming option at any Da Nang beach hotel, not the sea.

01
Hyatt Regency Da Nang Resort & Spa
Non Nuoc Beach · Camp Hyatt · 6 pools + 120m lazy river
$165-380/night

This is the top family pick in Da Nang, and it isn't a close call. Camp Hyatt is a real programme with age-specific activities and trained staff running it, not a playroom you drop the kids in and hope for the best.

Six pools, including a 120-metre lazy river, gives kids more to do in the water than anywhere else in the city. The resort is big enough that families have breathing room without feeling like they're navigating a maze, and the beach is right there.

World of Hyatt members get real loyalty value here. The one catch: Non Nuoc puts you about 20 minutes from the city centre, so if you're planning to get out and explore Da Nang proper, budget for taxis.

02
Sheraton Grand Da Nang Resort
Non Nuoc Beach · Largest waterpark in Da Nang · 785 rooms
$110-280/night

Sheraton Grand has the largest water complex of any hotel in Da Nang: multiple slides, dedicated kids' pools, a separate adult pool, and an aquatic area that's genuinely impressive in person. At 785 rooms, it's a big property, but it absorbs that volume without turning into a scrum at the pool.

It's noticeably cheaper than Hyatt Regency for beach access and water facilities that are in the same league, which makes this the better value pick. The kids club is looser than Camp Hyatt's structured programme, but for most families the waterpark more than makes up for it.

It's also a shorter drive if you're planning a Hoi An day trip.

03
Mikazuki Japanese Resorts & Spa Da Nang
Japanese water park concept · Popular with East Asian families · Multiple pools
$80-180/night

Mikazuki is a Japanese-branded resort built around a distinctive water park: multiple pools, an indoor heated pool, slides, and an onsen-influenced spa area for the adults who need a break. It's a magnet for Japanese, Korean, and Chinese family groups.

It's cheaper than Hyatt or Sheraton, and if your family is going to spend most of the trip in the water anyway, the aquatic entertainment holds up fine against the pricier options. The food leans Japanese, which some families love and others find limiting if picky eaters are involved.

If the concept appeals to you, this is the best value waterpark stay on the list.

04
Furama Resort Danang
Coral Kids Club · Mature gardens · My Khe Beach · Good value luxury
$120-250/night

Furama is Da Nang's original five-star, and the tropical gardens have had decades to mature into something newer resorts just can't replicate: real shade, real space, a sense of place instead of a fresh coat of paint. The Coral Kids Club does the job even if it's not as structured as Camp Hyatt.

The lagoon pool photographs well and swims even better. My Khe Beach also puts you closer to the city's restaurant scene than the Non Nuoc properties.

If you want character and garden space with solid beach access, and Hyatt's rates feel like a stretch, Furama at $120-250 is the sensible middle ground. La Maison 1888 is there for the parents once the kids are asleep.

05
Premier Village Da Nang Resort
All-villa concept · Private pools per unit · Older kids love it
$250-500/night

Premier Village is an all-villa resort, meaning every unit comes with its own private pool. For families with older kids (10 and up) who want pool time without an audience, this is about as good as it gets in Da Nang.

The villa layout buys genuine privacy: no shared pool logistics, no staking out sun loungers at dawn. That privacy is exactly what you're paying for, and it's the most expensive option on this list.

Families with toddlers who need constant supervision may find a private pool is more work than relaxation, not less. But for families with older kids who want space of their own, Premier Village is hard to beat.

Family Booking Tips

School holiday windows (June-July and December-January) push prices up 30-50% across every family resort in the city. If you're locked into peak June-July dates, book 3-4 months out. Rooms fill and rates climb as availability drops, not the other way around.

May and September-October are the sweet spot: good swimming conditions on Non Nuoc and My Khe, meaningfully lower rates, and facilities that aren't packed. October carries some rain risk, but it's often the best value stretch of the year if you can live with a shower or two.

Common Questions

Family Resorts Da Nang: FAQ

Is Non Nuoc Beach safe for kids?+

Yes, during May-September, when seas are calmer and lifeguards are on duty. October through March brings stronger currents and rougher conditions, and the red flag goes up often enough that swimming gets prohibited outright.

Check the flag system before letting kids into the water no matter what month it is.

What is the best family resort in Da Nang for toddlers?+

Hyatt Regency and Sheraton Grand handle toddlers best. Hyatt Regency has Camp Hyatt's age-specific programming, shallow pool areas, and a resort layout that's easy to keep an eye on.

Sheraton Grand has the bigger water complex with dedicated children's pools. Both sit on Non Nuoc Beach and are set up well for families with young kids.

Which Da Nang hotels have waterparks?+

Sheraton Grand Da Nang has the largest waterpark of any hotel in the city, with multiple slides and pools. Mikazuki Japanese Resorts runs a Japanese-themed water complex with slides and an indoor heated pool.

Hyatt Regency isn't a waterpark exactly, but its 120m lazy river and 6 pools compete just fine. Most of the waterpark-style hotels cluster on Non Nuoc Beach.
