Non Nuoc Beach Safety for Families

Important: Non Nuoc Beach has strong currents and rough conditions October through March. During these months the red flag system is frequently active and swimming is prohibited.

The safest months for families with children swimming at Non Nuoc are May through September. Always check the flag system - red flag means no swimming, regardless of how calm the water looks.

Most of Da Nang's best family resorts are on Non Nuoc Beach, south of the city. This beach has lifeguards posted during the safe swimming season (May-September) and the resorts have designated swimming areas with flag systems.

Outside this window, families should rely on resort pools rather than ocean swimming.

My Khe Beach (where Pullman and others are located) follows a similar seasonal pattern for swimming conditions. For families travelling October-March, resort pools are the practical alternative to ocean swimming at any Da Nang beach hotel.

01
Hyatt Regency Da Nang Resort & Spa
Non Nuoc Beach · Camp Hyatt · 6 pools + 120m lazy river
$165-380/night

The top family pick in Da Nang, and it's not particularly close. Camp Hyatt is a structured programme with age-specific activities (not just a room with toys).

Six pools including a 120-metre lazy river gives children more to do in the water than any other Da Nang hotel. The resort is large enough that families have space without feeling crowded, and Non Nuoc Beach is direct-access.

World of Hyatt members get meaningful loyalty benefits. The only caveat is the Non Nuoc location puts you 20 minutes from the city - if you're planning to explore Da Nang, factor in transport costs.

02
Sheraton Grand Da Nang Resort
Non Nuoc Beach · Largest waterpark in Da Nang · 785 rooms
$110-280/night

Sheraton Grand has the largest water complex of any hotel in Da Nang - multiple slides, children's pools, adult pool, and a sprawling aquatic area that is genuinely impressive. The resort is vast (785 rooms) which means it handles volume well without feeling chaotic.

The price point is notably lower than Hyatt Regency for broadly similar beach access and waterpark scale, making this the better value family pick. The kids club is not as structured as Camp Hyatt but the waterpark more than compensates for most families.

Also closer to Hoi An day trips.

03
Mikazuki Japanese Resorts & Spa Da Nang
Japanese water park concept · Popular with East Asian families · Multiple pools
$80-180/night

Mikazuki is a Japanese-branded resort with a distinctive water park concept - multiple pools, indoor heated pool, slides, and a Japanese onsen-influenced spa area for adults. It's extremely popular with Japanese, Korean, and Chinese families.

The price point is lower than Hyatt or Sheraton, and for families who will spend most of their time in the water, this delivers comparable aquatic entertainment. The food quality trends toward Japanese preferences which some families appreciate and others find limiting.

If the Japanese concept appeals and you want the best value waterpark experience, Mikazuki delivers.

04
Furama Resort Danang
Coral Kids Club · Mature gardens · My Khe Beach · Good value luxury
$120-250/night

Furama is Da Nang's original five-star and the mature tropical gardens are genuinely good for families - space, shade, and atmosphere that newer resorts lack. The Coral Kids Club is adequate if not as structured as Camp Hyatt.

The lagoon pool is large and photogenic. My Khe Beach access means you're closer to city dining and restaurants.

For families who want character and garden space alongside good beach access, and who find Hyatt's higher pricing a stretch, Furama at $120-250 is a sensible choice. La Maison 1888 dining is available for parents once kids are settled.

05
Premier Village Da Nang Resort
All-villa concept · Private pools per unit · Older kids love it
$250-500/night

Premier Village is an all-villa resort where each villa has its own private pool. For families with older children (10+) who want private pool time and space from other guests, this is an exceptional experience.

The villa layout means family groups have genuine privacy - no shared pool logistics, no fighting for sun loungers. The price reflects the concept and it's the most expensive option on this list.

Families with toddlers or young children who need continuous supervision might find the private pool concept creates more work than relaxation. For older-kids families who want premium space: Premier Village is hard to beat.

Family Booking Tips

School holiday periods (June-July and December-January) see 30-50% price spikes across all family resorts in Da Nang. For peak June-July dates, book 3-4 months ahead - rooms fill quickly and rates increase as availability drops.

The shoulder seasons (May and September-October) offer a useful balance: good swimming conditions on Non Nuoc and My Khe, lower rates than peak, and less crowded facilities. October brings some rain risk but is often excellent value.

Common Questions

Family Resorts Da Nang: FAQ

Is Non Nuoc Beach safe for kids?+

Non Nuoc Beach is safe for kids during May-September when seas are calmer and lifeguards are active. October through March sees stronger currents and rough conditions - the red flag system is frequently active and swimming is often prohibited.

Always check the flag system before letting children enter the water, regardless of month.

What is the best family resort in Da Nang for toddlers?+

Hyatt Regency and Sheraton Grand are best for toddlers. Hyatt Regency has Camp Hyatt with age-specific programming, shallow pool areas, and a contained resort environment.

Sheraton Grand has the largest water complex including dedicated children's pools. Both are on Non Nuoc Beach and handle families with young children well.

Which Da Nang hotels have waterparks?+

Sheraton Grand Da Nang has the largest waterpark of any Da Nang hotel, with multiple slides and pools. Mikazuki Japanese Resorts has a Japanese-themed water complex with slides and an indoor heated pool.

Hyatt Regency has a 120m lazy river and 6 pools (not a waterpark per se, but excellent water facilities). Most waterpark hotels are on Non Nuoc Beach.
