TMS Hotel Da Nang Beach vs Sala Danang

Two popular mid-market My Khe Beach hotels compared. TMS brings volume and value; Sala brings personality and a quieter experience, the right choice depends on what kind of beach stay you're after.

✎ Written by Ryan Yousefi · 📅 Last updated: May 2026 · ⏰ 7-min read
TMS Hotel Da Nang Beach
Sala Danang Beach Hotel
Overview Location Rooms Amenities Best For Verdict

Hotel Overview

TMS Hotel Da Nang Beach

  • ★★★★ Superior beachfront hotel
  • Large property with pool and beach access
  • My Khe Beach, central strip
  • Consistently high review scores
  • Good family and group setup
  • From $65/night

Sala Danang Beach Hotel

  • ★★★★ Boutique-style beachfront hotel
  • Smaller, more intimate property
  • My Khe Beach, central-north strip
  • Design-forward interiors
  • Good for couples and independent travellers
  • From $60/night

Both hotels sit in the popular mid-market tier on My Khe Beach, not full five-star resorts, but well-positioned hotels with direct beach access at genuinely competitive rates. TMS is the more mainstream pick with a strong track record for consistency.

Sala has more personality and suits travellers who find larger hotels generic.

Both hotels are on My Khe, very close to each other in location terms. The key advantage of the My Khe strip is proximity to An Thuong's restaurants and cafes (~5 min Grab) and relatively easy access to Dragon Bridge and the city centre.

Both hotels work equally well as a base for day trips to Hoi An (45–50 min) or Marble Mountain (25 min).

Rooms & Style

TMS Hotel, Rooms

  • Standard rooms comfortable for the price point
  • Ocean-view rooms on upper floors recommended
  • Clean, functional design, nothing surprising
  • Good interconnecting family room options
  • Balconies on many room types
  • Reliable consistency across stays

Sala Danang, Rooms

  • Rooms with distinctive Vietnamese-influenced design
  • Ocean-facing rooms notably good value
  • More stylish interiors than typical 4-star
  • Better suited to couples and solo travellers
  • Fewer rooms, quieter, less congested
  • Check specific room type at booking

TMS edges ahead on family suitability, larger pool, more room type variety, and a setup better suited to groups. Sala's strength is atmosphere, it's a more pleasant environment for couples or solo travellers who find high-volume hotels impersonal.

Both are independent hotels with no loyalty programme.

Who Each Hotel Is Best For

Choose TMS Hotel if…

  • You want a proven, consistent beachfront hotel
  • Travelling as a family or group
  • Pool access is important during your stay
  • You value reliability over personality
  • Budget is tighter, TMS often great value
  • You want a central My Khe position

Choose Sala Danang Beach if…

  • Travelling as a couple
  • Design and atmosphere matter to you
  • You prefer a smaller, quieter property
  • Generic large-hotel feel is not appealing
  • You want Vietnamese-influenced interior design
  • Direct beach access in a calmer setting

Final Verdict

TMS Hotel is the safe, reliable default, consistently rated, well-positioned, and good value. For families or groups who want a pool, beach access, and predictable quality, TMS is hard to beat at this price point on My Khe.

Sala Danang earns its following for a different reason: it's a more considered hotel with personality, and if you're after a quieter beachfront base with better-than-average design, the slight premium over a generic 4-star is worth it.

If neither fully satisfies, consider upgrading to Furama Resort for a genuine five-star at a modest premium, or see our full beach hotels guide for more options across the My Khe and Non Nuoc strips.
