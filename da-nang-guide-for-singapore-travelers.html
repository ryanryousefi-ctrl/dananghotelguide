Da Nang for Singapore Travellers

1 hour 45 minutes by direct flight. Five-star beach resort for less than a Sentosa hotel weekend. Long-weekend friendly. This is why Da Nang keeps appearing on every Singapore shortlist.

Da Nang is one of the easiest international getaways from Singapore. Direct flights on Scoot, VietJet, Singapore Airlines, and Vietnam Airlines take under two hours. A decent five-star resort costs SGD $150–250/night. A bowl of pho costs SGD $2. Singaporeans already make up one of the highest-converting visitor segments on this site, and most arrive having already decided — they just want hotel and timing confirmation. Here's both.

Flights from Singapore to Da Nang

Da Nang International Airport (DAD) has multiple direct daily services from Singapore Changi (SIN):

  • Scoot: Budget carrier, multiple weekly departures. Best for flexible dates and advance booking. [VERIFY: 2026 frequency]
  • VietJet Air: Low-cost, direct. [VERIFY: 2026 schedule]
  • Singapore Airlines / Vietnam Airlines: Full-service options, typically more expensive but better for miles/points and rebooking flexibility.
  • Flight time: Approximately 1 hour 45 minutes direct.

Return fares from Singapore in 2026 run roughly SGD $120–350 per person depending on airline, booking lead time, and travel dates. Scoot advance purchases on non-holiday weeks regularly come in under SGD $180 return. Public holiday windows (Chinese New Year, Deepavali, Hari Raya) spike 40–60%, so either book 10–12 weeks out or travel the week after.

Da Nang airport is 10 minutes from the beach and 15 minutes from the city centre by Grab. It is significantly less chaotic than Hanoi Noi Bai or Ho Chi Minh Tan Son Nhat. Immigration and baggage usually clear in under 30 minutes.

Long-Weekend Itineraries from Singapore

Da Nang works exceptionally well for Singapore's public holiday structure. With a Friday departure and Monday return, you get three full days:

3-Night Beach Itinerary

  • Day 1 (arrive Friday evening): Transfer to hotel on My Khe Beach. Dinner at An Thuong strip (5 min Grab from most beach hotels).
  • Day 2 (Saturday): Full beach day. Morning swim, resort pool, afternoon sunbed. Evening: rooftop bar at A La Carte or Novotel Premier Level 36 for sunset.
  • Day 3 (Sunday): Day trip to Hoi An Ancient Town (25 min south, SGD $15 by Grab). Afternoon back, massage at hotel spa. Dinner in resort.
  • Day 4 (depart Monday): Morning beach or pool. Airport transfer for afternoon/evening flight back to SG.

3-Night Culture + Beach Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrive, check in city-centre hotel (Novotel Premier Han River or equivalent). Riverside dinner, Dragon Bridge at night (fireworks Saturday/Sunday 9pm).
  • Day 2: Marble Mountains morning (20 min south), My Khe Beach afternoon. An Thuong dinner.
  • Day 3: Full Hoi An day trip — Ancient Town, tailors, Thu Bon River, evening market.
  • Day 4: Depart.

Hotel Prices in SGD

Approximate 2026 hotel pricing in Singapore dollars. These are rack rates — Booking.com often shows better with advance booking:

  • Mid-range beach hotels (A La Carte, TMS): SGD $90–130/night
  • Four-star beach (Four Points, Holiday Inn): SGD $100–160/night
  • Five-star resort (Hyatt Regency, Sheraton Grand): SGD $150–250/night
  • Top luxury (InterContinental Sun Peninsula): SGD $280–420/night

For context: a Sentosa resort of comparable standard to Da Nang's Hyatt or Sheraton runs SGD $400–700/night. The flight to Da Nang and three nights at a five-star resort often costs less than two nights in Singapore at the same tier.

When to Go

Da Nang's good beach season runs March to August. Singapore public holidays that align well:

Singapore Holiday Da Nang Conditions Notes
Chinese New Year (Jan/Feb) Cool, some rain, rough seas Not ideal for beach. Hoi An is better this window.
Good Friday / Easter (April) Dry season, excellent beach, 28–30°C ✓ One of the best windows of the year
Labour Day, Vesak Day (May) Peak beach season, 30°C, low winds ✓ Excellent. Shoulder rates still in effect early May.
National Day (Aug) Hot, 32–34°C, late-dry season ✓ Good beach, book ahead
Deepavali / Hari Raya (Oct/Nov) Wet season transitioning, rougher seas △ Rain likely, not beach-optimal
