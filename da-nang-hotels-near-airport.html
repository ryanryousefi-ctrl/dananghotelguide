Da Nang Hotels Near the Airport 2026

The hotels closest to Da Nang International Airport, and an honest read on when that proximity is worth choosing over a beach location.

By Ryan Yousefi · Updated May 2026

The Honest Answer Airport Transport Closest Hotels FAQ

Is Airport Proximity Important in Da Nang?

For most stays, no, and it's worth being upfront about that. Da Nang International Airport sits about 3km from the city centre, making it one of the most centrally positioned airports anywhere in Vietnam or Southeast Asia.

The runway runs parallel to the Han River, essentially inside the city grid.

What that means in practice: a taxi or Grab from the airport to any hotel on My Khe Beach takes 10-15 minutes. To city centre hotels on the Han River, it's 8-10 minutes.

Even the furthest hotels on the northern My Khe strip stay under 15 minutes at normal traffic times. There's no equivalent here to landing at a sprawling hub and facing an hour-long crawl into town.

Early departure note: A city centre or My Khe beach hotel still lets you leave at 5:30am and reach the airport in under 15 minutes, even for a 6am check-in. You don't need to book a "near airport" hotel just to make an early flight.

The entire My Khe strip is effectively airport-adjacent by Vietnam standards, so an early wake-up call doesn't force a compromise on where you sleep the night before.

So where does proximity actually earn its keep? Two cases: a one-night layover where you land late and fly out again within 24 hours, and a genuinely brutal departure time where shaving even 5-10 minutes off the ride matters more than where you spend the evening.

Outside those two situations, pick your hotel on beach versus city preference, price, and facilities. Airport-adjacent hotels are rarely great leisure locations, since they're built around convenience rather than views, and the location was chosen for road access, not for what's out the window.

Don't downgrade the rest of your stay to save a few minutes on a ride that's already short everywhere in Da Nang.

Getting from Da Nang Airport to Your Hotel

Grab (recommended): Download the app before you arrive. Grab runs fixed-price rides from Da Nang Airport, so there's no negotiating and no surprise fare.

Typical fares: city centre or Han River hotels $4-6, My Khe Beach hotels $3-6, the further My Khe strip $5-8. Metered, no haggling, and reliable enough that it's the default choice for almost every arrival.

Official taxis: Mai Linh and Vinasun are the reputable Da Nang taxi companies, with pricing comparable to Grab.

Skip the unlicensed drivers who approach you in arrivals. They're the one real hazard at an otherwise easy airport.

Hotel transfers: Most mid-to-upper tier hotels can arrange airport transfers for $15-25 each way. It's convenient but costs several times what Grab charges for the same ride.

Worth paying for if you're landing late with heavy luggage and want someone holding a sign with your name on it, less worth it for a short daytime hop where Grab does the same job for less.

Grab tip: Book your ride from inside the terminal, before you head out to the taxi stand area. Airport WiFi is enough to get the app open and a driver assigned.

The Grab pickup zone at Da Nang Airport is clearly marked near the departures level, so it's a short walk either way.

Common Questions

Da Nang Airport Hotels: FAQ

How far is Da Nang Airport from My Khe Beach?+

Da Nang Airport to My Khe Beach runs about 10-15 minutes by taxi or Grab, depending on traffic and which stretch of beach you're headed to. The northern My Khe strip (Pullman, A La Carte area) is closer to 10-12 minutes.

Grab fares typically run $3-6, so there's no real cost penalty for choosing the beach over an airport-adjacent hotel.

Is there a free airport shuttle from Da Nang hotels?+

Most Da Nang hotels don't offer free airport shuttles. Some five-star properties run paid transfers at $15-25.

For most travelers, Grab (Vietnam's equivalent of Uber) is the more efficient option: fixed-price rides from the airport to any hotel in 10-20 minutes for $3-8. See our transport guide for full details.

Which hotel is closest to Da Nang Airport?+

City centre hotels on the Han River sit closest to Da Nang Airport, around 8-10 minutes out. Novotel Da Nang Premier Han River and Grand Mercure Da Nang are among the closest major hotels.

But since even the furthest city or beach hotels are only 15-20 minutes away, airport proximity rarely settles anything on its own in Da Nang. Choose based on beach versus city preference, and save the closest-hotel question for layovers and early departures.
