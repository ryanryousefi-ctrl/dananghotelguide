Hotels Near Da Nang Airport 2026

The honest answer: Da Nang Airport is so central that every city and beach hotel is "near" it. Here's what the distances actually mean and which hotels are genuinely closest.

✎ Written by Ryan Yousefi · 📅 Last updated: May 2026 · ⏰ 5-min read
The Honest Answer Airport Transport Closest Hotels FAQ

Is Airport Proximity Important in Da Nang?

No - and this is worth saying clearly. Da Nang International Airport is approximately 3km from the city centre, making it one of the most centrally positioned airports in Vietnam (and indeed Southeast Asia).

The runway runs parallel to the Han River, essentially inside the city.

What this means practically: a taxi or Grab from the airport to any hotel on My Khe Beach takes 10-15 minutes. To city centre hotels on the Han River: 8-10 minutes.

Even the furthest hotels on the northern My Khe strip are under 15 minutes at normal traffic times.

Early departure note: Any city centre or My Khe beach hotel allows you to leave at 5:30am and reach the airport in under 15 minutes - even for 6am departure check-in times. You do not need to book a "near airport" hotel for early flights.

The entire My Khe strip is effectively airport-adjacent by Vietnam standards.

The practical advice: choose your hotel based on beach versus city preference, price, and facilities. Don't compromise on location or quality just to be slightly closer to an airport that is already less than 15 minutes from virtually everywhere you'd want to stay.

Getting from Da Nang Airport to Your Hotel

Grab (recommended): Download the Grab app before arrival. Grab operates fixed-price rides from Da Nang Airport.

Typical fares: city centre / Han River hotels $4-6, My Khe Beach hotels $3-6, further My Khe strip $5-8. No haggling, metered, safe.

Official taxis: Mai Linh and Vinasun are the reputable Da Nang taxi companies. Comparable pricing to Grab.

Avoid unlicensed drivers who approach you in arrivals.

Hotel transfers: Most mid-to-upper tier hotels can arrange airport transfers at $15-25 each way. Convenient but significantly more expensive than Grab.

Worth it if you're arriving late with heavy luggage and want a guaranteed meet-and-greet.

Grab tip: Book your Grab from inside the terminal before exiting to the taxi stand area. You can connect to airport WiFi to do this.

The pickup zone for Grab in Da Nang Airport is clearly marked near departures level.

"Hotels near Da Nang airport are genuinely useful for short layovers or very early/late connections. The airport is only 5km from the My Khe hotel strip — so 'near the airport' and 'near the beach' often mean the same area."

Common Questions

Da Nang Airport Hotels: FAQ

How far is Da Nang Airport from My Khe Beach?+

Da Nang Airport to My Khe Beach is approximately 10-15 minutes by taxi or Grab, depending on traffic and which section of the beach you're heading to. The northern My Khe strip (Pullman, A La Carte area) is about 10-12 minutes.

The fare by Grab is typically $3-6.

Is there a free airport shuttle from Da Nang hotels?+

Most Da Nang hotels do not offer free airport shuttles. Some five-star properties offer paid airport transfer services at $15-25.

The most efficient option for most travellers is the Grab app (Vietnam's equivalent of Uber), which gives fixed-price rides from the airport to any hotel in 10-20 minutes for $3-8. See our transport guide for full details.

Which hotel is closest to Da Nang Airport?+

City centre hotels on the Han River are closest to Da Nang Airport - approximately 8-10 minutes. Novotel Da Nang Premier Han River and Grand Mercure Da Nang are among the closest major hotels.

However, given that even the furthest city/beach hotels are only 15-20 minutes away, airport proximity is rarely a meaningful deciding factor in Da Nang. Choose based on beach vs city preference instead.
