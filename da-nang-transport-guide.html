Editor's Pick

Grab

City-wide · Ride-hail · All distances

Grab is the default answer for most visitors. Open the app, see the fare, get in. You're usually across town for under $2 USD. No negotiating, no language barrier, no wondering if you're being overcharged — the price is locked before you confirm.

Available city-wide with 2–5 minute wait times. GrabCar for luggage or AC comfort; GrabBike for short hops. Works for My Khe, An Thuợng, Han River, airport, Marble Mountains, and Hoi An. Download the app before you land.

Upfront pricing No negotiating 2–5 min wait GrabCar & GrabBike City rides: 40,000–80,000 VND
Download Grab before you land. Airport Wi-Fi is reliable, but having the app ready saves you from anyone quoting fixed prices at arrivals.
Editor's Pick
Da Nang Signature Pick

Scooter Rental

Daily rental · 100,000–200,000 VND/day

The most Da Nang way to get around — if you're comfortable in the traffic.

A scooter gives you total freedom. Son Tra Peninsula at dawn, a hidden cafe off a back road, My Khe at any hour, the coastal road south past Marble Mountains. No waiting for Grab, no surge pricing, easy parking almost everywhere. Rental shops near My Khe charge 100,000–200,000 VND per day.

Da Nang is more manageable than Hanoi or HCMC. But traffic is still fast, lane discipline is loose, and an accident without the right license means insurance won't pay. If you've ridden in Southeast Asia before, this is the move. If you haven't, use Grab — it's cheap enough that a scooter rarely saves meaningful money on a short trip.

Total freedom Son Tra & coastal roads Easy parking 100,000–200,000 VND/day
THE TRADEOFF: Vietnamese traffic is faster and less predictable than most Western cities. An accident without a valid motorcycle license voids most travel insurance. Ride only if you're genuinely experienced.

Vietnam's dominant ride-hail. City-wide coverage, 2–5 minute wait. GrabCar for comfort and luggage; GrabBike for short hops. No language required — destination typed in app.

Taxi

Vinasun and Mai Linh are the reliable metered companies. Always insist on the meter. Grab is usually cheaper and easier, but taxis remain an option if the app isn't working.

Flag-fall ~15,000 VND, then ~12,000–15,000 VND/km

Private Car with Driver

Book through your hotel for full-day trips to Hoi An, Hai Van Pass, Ba Na Hills, or a combined loop. One driver, one price agreed upfront — no waiting for Grab between stops.

Hoi An full day: ~1,200,000–1,800,000 VND · Ba Na Hills: ~1,000,000–1,500,000 VND
Private VanGroups

Private Van

For groups of 4–8. Fixed price agreed upfront, driver waits. Airport runs, Hoi An day trips, and multi-stop itineraries all work well with a van. Book through hotel or local operators.

Airport to city: ~300,000–500,000 VND · Hoi An day: ~1,500,000–2,000,000 VND
Ride-hail MotoShort Hops

GrabBike

Motorbike version of Grab — faster through traffic, cheaper for short distances. Good for solo riders on quick city hops. Helmet provided. Not recommended with luggage or on long routes.

Short city hops: 20,000–45,000 VND
AirportUse Grab

Airport Transfer

Da Nang airport is 3–5km from city center, 5–8km from My Khe. Open Grab on arrival — it works in the terminal. Faster and cheaper than pre-booked hotel transfers for most hotels.

To My Khe: 60,000–120,000 VND · 10–20 min
Hotel airport transfers average 200,000–400,000 VND — rarely worth it.
On FootCompact Zones

Walking

An Thuợng and the My Khe beachfront are walkable. The Han River promenade is a good evening walk. Between major zones — beach to city, city to Son Tra — distances require a ride.

Free · Best before 10am and after 5pm (heat)
BicycleLimited Use

Bicycle

Some hotels lend or rent bicycles. Good for the beachfront strip and flat An Thuợng area at low-traffic hours. Not practical for cross-city or day trips. Avoid peak traffic.

Hotel rental: free–50,000 VND/day
TrainHue Only

Train

Da Nang station connects north to Hue (2.5–3 hrs, from ~100,000 VND) and south to Saigon. For Hoi An: trains don't stop there — use Grab. Scenic Hai Van Pass crossing on the Da Nang–Hue route.

Da Nang–Hue: from ~100,000 VND · Book at dsvn.vn

Before you rent

Scooter rental — good to know

1
License: An International Driving Permit endorsed for motorcycles is technically required. Enforcement is inconsistent, but an accident without it voids most travel insurance.
2
Photograph the bike: Before riding away, document every existing scratch and dent with photos or video. Show the rental shop. Prevents disputes on return.
3
Helmet: Mandatory by law and included with rental. Wear it. The fines are real, and it matters.
4
Passport deposit: Some shops ask for your passport as a deposit. Many travellers prefer to leave a copy and pay a small cash deposit instead. Both are common practice.
!
Traffic comfort: Da Nang traffic is faster and less predictable than most Western cities. Roundabouts and lane merging require active awareness. If you've ridden in Vietnam or SE Asia before, you'll be fine. If not, use Grab.
!
Rain: The wet season (Sept–Nov) brings heavy rain. Wet roads plus unfamiliar traffic is high-risk. Check the forecast before an all-day scooter trip.

Grab tips

Getting the most out of Grab

Confirm the license plate in the app before getting in. Plates match 99% of the time, but worth a glance.
Type your destination in the app rather than telling the driver — eliminates any language barrier entirely.
At the airport, follow signs to the Grab pickup zone — it's separate from the taxi queue and well-marked.
Screenshot your hotel's address in Vietnamese before leaving if your connection is unreliable.
If a fare seems unusually high (surge), cancel and retry in 5 minutes. Prices normalise quickly after peak moments.
GrabBike arrives faster than GrabCar late at night when car supply drops.

Common Questions

What is the best way to get around Da Nang?
Grab for virtually everything. Reliable, cheap, upfront pricing, no language needed. Most city rides cost 40,000–80,000 VND. Download the app before you land.
How much does a Grab cost in Da Nang?
Short city rides: 30,000–60,000 VND. My Khe to city center: 50,000–80,000 VND. Airport to My Khe: 60,000–120,000 VND. Da Nang to Hoi An: 200,000–280,000 VND. Prices vary with demand and time of day.
Should I rent a scooter in Da Nang?
Only if you're already comfortable riding in Vietnamese traffic. Rentals cost 100,000–200,000 VND per day. An accident without a valid motorcycle license voids most travel insurance. If uncertain, use Grab — it's cheap enough that a scooter rarely saves meaningful money on a short trip.
How far is Da Nang airport from the city?
3–5km from city center, 5–8km from My Khe Beach hotels. Grab takes 10–20 minutes and costs 60,000–120,000 VND. One of the most conveniently located airports in Vietnam.
