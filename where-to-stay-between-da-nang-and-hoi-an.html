Where to Stay Between Da Nang and Hoi An

Several beach resorts sit south of Da Nang on the way to Hoi An, and a few more on My Khe. Who they suit, and the drive times we recorded.

The Short Answer

If you want a resort stay with Hoi An as a day trip, base yourself on the coast south of Da Nang: Non Nuoc and Bac My An Beach. In our reviews Hoi An is about 25 to 30 minutes by car from Marriott Resort, Naman Retreat, Furama Resort and TIA Wellness Resort, which is easier than from most city-centre hotels.

The trade-off is the city. These resorts are not walking distance from street food or the Han River, so evenings in Da Nang mean a taxi or Grab.

Rule of thumb: More resort days and one or two Hoi An trips: south of the city. More city evenings: stay in the centre or on My Khe.

Beach Resorts, Nearest to Hoi An First

Drive times are as recorded in each review and vary with traffic. Rates and availability change daily, so check your dates on Trip.com.

01
Da Nang Marriott Resort & Spa
Non Nuoc Beach · Private pool villas
Hoi An: ~25 min

On Non Nuoc Beach, about 13 km south of the city centre and 15 km from the airport. The Marble Mountains are five minutes by taxi and Hoi An about 25 minutes by car (listings put the distance at roughly 15 km), so a day trip is not a full-day commitment.

Every villa has a private pool. It is a resort-mode stay: street food and Han River evenings are a taxi each way.

Naman Retreat Da Nang
Non Nuoc Beach · Long infinity pool
Hoi An: ~25 min

About 13 km and a 25-minute taxi from the city centre, with Hoi An roughly 25 minutes south (listings give about 11.5 km). Our review says it is built around settling into the property rather than wandering into town.

Our reviewer stayed in a high-rise room and notes the value depends entirely on room type, so choose your room carefully.

03
Furama Resort Danang
Bac My An Beach · Established gardens
Hoi An: ~30 min

About 8 km south of the city centre and 10 km from the airport, with Dragon Bridge around 15 minutes away and Hoi An roughly 30 minutes south. A shuttle to Hoi An is available for day trips.

It is a settled, unhurried resort rather than an isolated one, and the local restaurant strip is walkable.

04
TIA Wellness Resort
My Khe Beach · Wellness retreat, 58 rooms
Hoi An: ~30 min

On My Khe Beach, so it gives more city access than a Non Nuoc property: Dragon Bridge is 12 minutes by Grab, Hoi An 30 minutes by car (listings give about 19 km), and the airport about 7 km away.

It is an all-inclusive wellness resort with only 58 rooms, so it suits a retreat rather than a sightseeing base.

Sheraton Grand Danang Resort
My Khe Beach · Families and Bonvoy
Hoi An: ~35 min

At the quieter central section of My Khe Beach: the city centre is 15 minutes north by taxi, Dragon Bridge 7 km away and the airport 9 km. The Marble Mountains are 15 minutes south and Hoi An about 35 minutes by car.

It balances a full beach resort with reasonable city access.

Son Tra Peninsula · Villa stays
Hoi An: ~40 min

On the tip of Son Tra Peninsula, facing My Khe Beach, about 10 km from the city centre. Dragon Bridge is 22 minutes by taxi and Hoi An about 40 minutes: doable as day trips but not casual pop-ins.

How to Choose

For the shortest drive to Hoi An, look at Marriott Resort and Naman Retreat. For a quieter resort with the city still reachable, Furama or TIA Wellness. For a beach resort with the best city access of the group, Sheraton Grand. For private villas and a stay-put trip, Premier Village.

Not sure Hoi An is the right base at all? Read Da Nang or Hoi An: where to stay, or see our Hoi An hotel guide if you would rather sleep in the old town.

Common Questions

Da Nang Airport Hotels: FAQ

How far is Da Nang Airport from My Khe Beach?+

Da Nang Airport to My Khe Beach runs about 10-15 minutes by taxi or Grab, depending on traffic and which stretch of beach you're headed to. The northern My Khe strip (Pullman, A La Carte area) is closer to 10-12 minutes.

Grab fares typically run $3-6, so there's no real cost penalty for choosing the beach over an airport-adjacent hotel.

Is there a free airport shuttle from Da Nang hotels?+

Most Da Nang hotels don't offer free airport shuttles. Some five-star properties run paid transfers at $15-25.

For most travelers, Grab (Vietnam's equivalent of Uber) is the more efficient option: fixed-price rides from the airport to any hotel in 10-20 minutes for $3-8. See our transport guide for full details.

Which hotel is closest to Da Nang Airport?+

City centre hotels on the Han River sit closest to Da Nang Airport, around 8-10 minutes out. Novotel Da Nang Premier Han River and Grand Mercure Da Nang are among the closest major hotels.

But since even the furthest city or beach hotels are only 15-20 minutes away, airport proximity rarely settles anything on its own in Da Nang. Choose based on beach versus city preference, and save the closest-hotel question for layovers and early departures.
