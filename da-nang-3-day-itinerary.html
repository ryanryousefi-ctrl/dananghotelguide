Da Nang Itinerary

Da Nang in 3 Days

The highlights, without turning your vacation into a race.

Updated August 2026 · By Ryan Yousefi

Is this enough time?

Yes, but choices matter. Three days covers My Khe beach, Marble Mountains, Son Tra and the Lady Buddha comfortably. The one real decision is Day 3: Hoi An or Ba Na Hills, you cannot do both well in one day. Pick one, save the other for next time.

Editor's Pick

Hoi An

Day 3 · Half Day Minimum

Even with only three days, don't skip it. Hoi An is a genuine place with real history, a preserved trading port that Ba Na Hills, fundamentally a theme park, cannot match. 45 minutes by Grab, worth prioritising over Ba Na Hills on a first visit.

45 min by Grab Ancient Town Best before 9am

Where to Stay

Stay here and make transportation easy. On the beach, restaurants and cafes immediately around you, and every Grab on this itinerary starts short. The Non Nuoc resort zone works if the property itself is the priority, but factor in longer rides to everything else.

Day 1
Arrive, Beach, Son Tra
Easy Beach Arrival
Morning
Check in near My Khe. Walk to the beach, wide, clean, uncrowded before 10am. Swim if you want. Breakfast at a cafe on or near An Thuong street.
Lunch
An Thuong food street. Banh mi, fresh seafood, Vietnamese rice dishes, all solid, English menus. $3-6 per person.
Afternoon
Grab to Son Tra Peninsula ($4-5). Drive up to the Lady Buddha statue at Linh Ung Pagoda. The view covers the whole city, coastline, and Hai Van Pass. 45-60 minutes on site, then back via the coastal road.
Evening
Walk Bach Dang riverside along the Han River, street food stalls, lit-up bridges. Saturday or Sunday: be near Dragon Bridge at 9pm for the fire and water show. Worth doing once.
Why Today Works

Don't schedule anything ambitious on arrival day. This gets you oriented, on the beach, and into one genuine Da Nang moment (Son Tra's view) without fighting jet lag or a packed schedule.

💡
Local Tip

If you arrive late or the afternoon runs short, skip Son Tra and move it to Day 2 morning instead. It's a flexible slot, not a fixed appointment.

Grab
Eat This

An Thuong street for banh mi and fresh seafood, $3-6 per person.

Day 2
Marble Mountains + Beach
Moderate Culture Beach Early Start
8:30 AM
Grab to Marble Mountains ($3-4 from central My Khe). Arrive early, before the tour groups. Climb Thuy Son, the main peak, caves, pagodas, a viewing platform over Non Nuoc beach. 1.5-2 hours.
10:30 AM
Walk or Grab (5 minutes) to Non Nuoc beach. The stretch near the Hyatt/Sheraton area is clean and accessible. Swim and relax into the afternoon. Beach chairs available from vendors.
1:00 PM
Lunch at a Non Nuoc beachside restaurant. Fresh seafood here is good value, grilled fish, clams, prawns.
Afternoon
Back to your hotel, freshen up. Optional second beach session at My Khe if you want more.
Evening
Con Market or Han Market. Han Market (indoor) has the largest selection of Vietnamese goods and local snacks. Con Market is bigger and more local. Both within Grab distance of My Khe ($3-4). Dinner near your hotel.
Why Today Works

Start Marble Mountains early. The difference between 8 a.m. and noon is enormous, the light through the caves is genuinely spectacular in the morning, and by 10:30am the tour buses change the whole atmosphere. Beach and market afterward keep the day relaxed.

💡
Local Tip

Entry is around 40,000 VND (~$1.60) plus a small elevator fee if you want to skip the stairs. Go before 9am, not after.

Day 3

The Big Decision: Hoi An or Ba Na Hills

Both require a full day. You cannot squeeze both in. Here's the honest breakdown.

Option A · Recommended
Hoi An Day Trip
Big Day Culture Day Trip
7:30 AM
Grab departs for Hoi An. 45-50 minutes, roughly $13-15. Worth booking a return driver for the day for flexibility.
8:30 AM
Arrive before the main crowds. Walk the old town lanes, the Japanese Covered Bridge, the merchant houses. Ancient town entry ticket covers most sites.
12:00 PM
Lunch: cao lau, white rose dumplings, Hoi An chicken rice. All local specialties worth trying here specifically.
Afternoon
Optional: An Bang Beach (15 min by motorbike taxi or Grab) for a swim. Or keep exploring, visit a tailor if you want custom items (minimum 24-hour turnaround, not useful on a day trip unless you're returning).
5:00 PM
Stay for the lantern evening if timing allows, or head back before traffic. Return Grab around $13-15.
Why Today Works

Hoi An delivers a more memorable experience than Ba Na Hills for most first-time visitors. The ancient town is genuinely well-preserved and the early morning atmosphere is excellent. If you have three days and haven't been to Hoi An, this is the priority.

💡
Local Tip

The Hoi An morning is the best part of the day trip. Ancient Town before 9am, before the crowds, in the early light, that's when it looks like the postcards. Afternoon is good too but different.

Option B
Ba Na Hills Full Day
Big Day Theme Park Day Trip
7:00 AM
Grab departs. Ba Na Hills is about 25km west, 30-40 minutes. $12-15 each way, or book a return driver for the day.
8:00 AM
Gates open. Buy tickets online in advance to skip the gate queue, around $35-40 per person. The cable car ride up is genuinely impressive, one of the world's longest.
9AM-3PM
French Village, the Golden Bridge (do it first thing), Fantasy Park indoor entertainment. Gets very crowded after 10am.
3:30 PM
Cable car down. Grab or driver back to Da Nang. Evening at leisure.
Why Today Works

Ba Na Hills is impressive and the Golden Bridge is genuinely photogenic. It's also unambiguously a theme park experience, you'll be surrounded by large tour groups for most of the day. Manage expectations accordingly.

🗣️
Ryan's Take

I've been to both more times than I can count. My honest recommendation for a first-time visitor: Hoi An. It's a genuinely different experience from Da Nang, a preserved trading port with a distinct character Ba Na Hills can't match. Ba Na Hills is impressive engineering and the Golden Bridge is visually striking, but it's fundamentally a theme park. Hoi An is a real place. If you've already done Hoi An, or you're travelling with people more interested in views and cable cars than old town lanes, Ba Na Hills is worth the full day, just book tickets online in advance and arrive early.

Keep it fun, not a checklist

Do This, Skip This

Do This on 3 Days
  • My Khe beach, morning or evening
  • Son Tra Peninsula and the Lady Buddha
  • Marble Mountains before 9am
  • Dragon Bridge fire show, Saturday or Sunday, 9pm
  • Hoi An, even as a half-day
Skip on a 3-Day Trip
  • The museum circuit, none are essential this trip
  • Walking across Dragon Bridge in daylight, hot and unremarkable, see the fire show instead
  • Organised Han River sunset cruises, $15-30 for a view you get free from Bach Dang riverside
  • Combining Ba Na Hills, Marble Mountains, and Hoi An in one trip, pick priorities instead
  • Hue, needs its own day and doesn't fit in three
⚠️
Worth Knowing

The Da Nang International Fireworks Festival runs selected weekends from late April through June. During competition nights, roads around the Han River are blocked and city-wide prices increase. Check dates before booking, it won't ruin a trip but it changes logistics.

Getting around

Grab Handles Everything

Download it before you land, buy a Viettel SIM at the airport. Those two things remove 80% of the friction from this itinerary. The price shown is fixed, no negotiation. For Ba Na Hills or Hoi An, consider a private driver for the day (roughly $40-60) instead of individual bookings.

Common questions

3-Day Itinerary FAQ

Is 3 days enough in Da Nang?

Yes. Three days covers My Khe beach, Son Tra Peninsula and the Lady Buddha, Marble Mountains, and either Hoi An or Ba Na Hills. You won't feel like you missed the essentials if you use the time well. Add a 4th or 5th day for both, or for Hue.

Should I do Ba Na Hills or Hoi An on a 3-day trip?

Hoi An, for most first-time visitors. It's a genuine place with real history, not a theme park. Ba Na Hills is impressive engineering and the Golden Bridge is worth seeing, but if you've already done Hoi An or care more about cable cars and views, Ba Na Hills is worth the day instead.

Do I need a scooter for 3 days in Da Nang?

No. Grab is cheap enough, roughly $2-15 per trip depending on distance, that there's little reason to deal with scooter rental, traffic, and parking on a short trip.

Where should I stay for 3 days in Da Nang?

My Khe beach, specifically the An Thuong area. You're on the beach within walking distance, restaurants and cafes are immediately around you, and Grab distances to every sight on this itinerary are short.

What should I skip with only 3 days in Da Nang?

Skip the museum circuit, none are essential on a short trip. Skip walking across Dragon Bridge during the day, see the fire show at 9pm on Saturday or Sunday instead. Skip organised sunset cruises, the Bach Dang riverside gives you the same view for free.
