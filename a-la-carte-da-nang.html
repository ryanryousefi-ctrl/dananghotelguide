A La Carte Da Nang Beach Hotel Review

Last reviewed: March 2026

From $75/night ★ 8.5 Booking.com Reviewed 2026

Overview

A La Carte Da Nang Beach Hotel is a locally designed boutique property that has built a strong reputation for its spectacular rooftop infinity pool — one of the most visually striking hotel features on the entire Da Nang coast. The pool appears seemingly suspended above My Khe Beach, with the South China Sea extending to the horizon. It consistently appears in social media coverage and travel photography of Da Nang.

Beyond the pool, the hotel delivers a genuine boutique experience: 248 rooms across a 22-floor tower, modern Vietnamese-inspired design, breakfast included in most rates, and a service culture that punches above the four-star norm. Its direct My Khe Beach access and central location make it a strong all-rounder for the price.

Location

The My Khe Beach location is central and walkable to the local restaurant strip. Dragon Bridge is 4 km, the airport 6 km. Its northern My Khe position — between the Hilton Da Nang and the main boutique strip — gives it convenient access to both the upscale hotel zone and the local dining street.

  • Airport: 6 km / 10 min taxi
  • Dragon Bridge: 10 min taxi
  • Local restaurants: 5 min walk
  • Hoi An: 30 min by car
  • My Khe Beach: direct access

Rooms and Design

248 rooms with a contemporary Vietnamese design language — warm tones, local artwork, and floor-to-ceiling windows. Most rooms have a balcony. Ocean-facing rooms on upper floors are the most popular category. The design is considerably more characterful than the international chain hotels on the same stretch of beach.

Pools, Beach, and Amenities

The rooftop infinity pool is the signature feature and genuinely delivers on its reputation. Located on an upper floor, it appears to merge with My Khe Beach below. A second pool is available on a lower floor. Direct beach access is well-managed with sunlounger and umbrella service. A spa and fitness centre complete the amenities.

Dining Options

Breakfast is included in most rates — a wide Vietnamese and international spread. The rooftop restaurant and bar adjacent to the pool serve all-day dining and cocktails. A beachside bar operates on the sand. The balance of on-site and local dining options is one of the better setups on My Khe.

Typical Price Range

  • Low season (May–August): $75–$110/night (incl. breakfast)
  • Shoulder season: $105–$165/night
  • Peak season (December–February): $140–$230/night

Who This Hotel Is Best For

Couples, design-conscious travellers, and social media-savvy guests who want the rooftop pool experience at a competitive rate. Also strong for travellers who want boutique character over chain anonymity, and anyone who wants to be close to both My Khe Beach and the local restaurant strip.

Pros
  • Rooftop infinity pool is genuinely spectacular — one of Da Nang's best
  • Boutique design with real character vs chain hotels
  • Breakfast included in most rates
  • Direct My Khe Beach access
  • Close to local restaurants and cafes
Cons
  • Rooftop pool crowded in peak season
  • 248 rooms — larger than most boutique definitions
  • Rooms on smaller side in entry categories
  • Pool bar service can be slow during busy periods

Final Verdict

A La Carte Da Nang Beach Hotel delivers one of the most memorable individual features of any hotel on My Khe — the rooftop infinity pool is a genuine standout. Combined with direct beach access, included breakfast, and boutique design at accessible rates, it makes for an excellent mid-market beach hotel. The pool photos alone will make your Da Nang trip memorable.

Is A La Carte Da Nang Beach Hotel Worth It?

A La Carte Da Nang Beach is one of the My Khe strip's most recognisable independently branded hotels — the rooftop infinity pool is one of the most-photographed hotel amenities in Da Nang and has driven the property's social media profile significantly above what its size and category would normally warrant. For the Instagram era, it punches well above its weight. The rooms are contemporary and comfortable, the beach access is direct, and the location is central on My Khe. Where it falls slightly short is in service consistency and food quality compared to international branded competitors at the same price. For travellers who want a stylish, photogenic beachfront stay on a mid-range budget, it is absolutely worth it.

Nearby Attractions & Getting Around

One of Da Nang's advantages as a destination is how compact it is — most major attractions are within 20–30 minutes of any hotel in the city. From A La Carte Da Nang Beach Hotel, key landmarks and beaches are easy to reach by taxi or GrabCar, and both services are reliable, metered, and inexpensive by international standards.

  • My Khe Beach — On-site. Direct beachfront — one of the most central My Khe hotel positions.
  • Dragon Bridge — 10 min taxi. Da Nang's iconic bridge — easy taxi from the hotel.
  • Han River — 12 min taxi. City-centre riverside dining and the Love Bridge.
  • My Khe Restaurant Strip — 2 min walk. Seafood restaurants and beach bars directly adjacent.
  • Da Nang Airport — 8 km / 12 min taxi. One of the quickest airport connections on My Khe.
  • Marble Mountains — 20 min taxi south. Half-day cultural trip to the five limestone formations.
