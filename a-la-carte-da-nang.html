Hotel Review, 2026

A La Carte Da Nang Beach Hotel

“The rooftop pool that seems to spill straight into the South China Sea.”

Boutique Beachfront Hotel · My Khe Beach

Our Take

Book it for the rooftop pool. That's not a knock, it's the reason this hotel outperforms its price bracket. The infinity pool is the most photographed hotel feature on My Khe Beach, and unlike a lot of hotel photography, this one holds up in person.

From the upper floors of the 22-floor tower, the water appears to run straight into the South China Sea. That illusion is real from the right angle, not a partial effect that falls apart once you're actually in the water. We've swum in infinity pools that oversell the view. This one doesn't need to.

At 248 rooms, it's large for something marketed as boutique, but small enough to avoid the anonymous scale of the international chains further down the strip. Vietnamese-inspired design, warm tones, local artwork: it has more personality than its neighbors, even if the room count undercuts the "boutique" label a little.

Breakfast is included in most rates and the beach sits directly out front.

The catch: the pool gets crowded once the hotel fills, and 248 rooms means real competition for loungers in December through February. If you want it quiet, with the beach stretched below and the city still asleep, be up before 7:30am. By 9 it's full.

The Rooms

The Rooms

Contemporary Vietnamese design runs through the rooms: warm tones, local artwork, floor-to-ceiling windows, balconies on most categories. Ocean-facing rooms on the upper floors are the obvious pick. Entry-category rooms run small, and the view is doing a lot of work to compensate for the square footage, but the design still reads as more individual than the Pullman or Four Points a short walk away.

Which room would we choose? Upper floor, ocean-facing, no question. The small footprint is most noticeable on the entry-level rooms, and the whole appeal of staying here is the view, so it's worth the modest premium to actually get it.

The Reason to Book It

The Rooftop Pool

The infinity pool sits above the beach and is the single most photographed spot on this stretch of My Khe. It's the reason people pick this hotel over the larger resorts nearby, and it earns that reputation: from the right angle, the water genuinely appears to merge with the ocean beyond it.

Bar service slows down during busy periods, which matters more up here than at a ground-level beach bar, since you're a captive audience with nowhere else to order from. Don't expect it to yourself once the hotel fills in peak season. This is the property's headline feature, and everyone staying here knows it.

Pool + Beach

Pool + Beach

The beach directly below is well managed: sun lounger and umbrella service, easy access from the hotel, a beachside bar on the sand. The seafood restaurant strip on the beach road is five minutes in either direction. Between the rooftop and the sand, you get two genuinely different ways to spend a beach day here. Up top for the view and the photos, down on the beach for the more traditional version.

Food + Breakfast

Food + Drink

Breakfast included in most rates is a genuine plus: a wide Vietnamese and international spread that covers the basics properly. The rooftop restaurant runs all-day dining and cocktails next to the pool, though it gets busy along with everything else up there at peak times.

Our advice: eat breakfast at the hotel, then let the restaurants on the beach road handle dinner. They do it well, and there's no reason to stay on-site every night if you'd rather be outside in the evening air.

Who It's For

Families, Couples & Who It Suits

This suits couples, first-time Da Nang visitors, and anyone who wants a stylish, photogenic beachfront stay without paying five-star prices for it. The rooms are contemporary and comfortable, beach access is direct, and the location sits central on My Khe. Where it falls short is service consistency and food quality against the international branded competitors at a similar price point, so if flawless service is the priority, this isn't that hotel. If a genuinely good pool and a design that photographs well matter more, it punches above its weight.

What We Like

What We Like

The rooftop pool

Infinity-edge, appears to merge with the sea, and it's the single best reason to book this hotel over its neighbors.

The rate

Boutique character and a headline pool for well under what the five-star tier a few blocks away charges.

The Vietnamese design

Warm tones and local artwork give it real identity next to the chain hotels on the same strip.

The location

Central My Khe, 10 minutes to the airport, direct beach access, restaurant strip fully walkable.

The Tradeoff

At 248 rooms, this isn't the intimate boutique experience the branding implies, and the elevators feel undersized for it. Expect waits at peak checkout and check-in times. The rooftop pool and bar get genuinely crowded, not just busy, once the hotel fills, and that's the defining flaw of an otherwise excellent facility. Entry-level rooms are small, so book an upper-floor ocean-facing room for the version of this hotel that's actually worth the money. Service and food quality trail the international branded competitors nearby at a similar price point. This is a design-and-view hotel first, a service hotel second.

Location

Location

Central My Khe Beach position, direct beach access, restaurant strip walkable in either direction, airport about 10 minutes away and Dragon Bridge a similar 10-minute Grab ride. This is one of the more central spots on the whole My Khe strip: close enough to the city for easy exploration, close enough to the beach for an actual beach holiday. In practice, that means you're not choosing between city convenience and beach access. You get a workable version of both without a long taxi ride either direction.

Would We Stay Here

YES

Yes. The pool is the reason most people book, but the hotel holds up beyond it. The design is thoughtful, beach access is direct, breakfast is solid, and the rates make this one of the more honest value propositions on My Khe Beach. Book an upper-floor ocean-facing room, set an early alarm for the pool, and don't overthink it.
