Best Hotels for Tet 2027 in Da Nang

Tet is the biggest travel event of the Vietnamese year. Hotels sell out months in advance. Here's where to stay and when to book.

By Ryan Yousefi · Updated 2026

Tet Nguyen Dan 2027 (the Vietnamese Lunar New Year) falls on Friday, January 29, 2027. Da Nang is a top domestic travel destination for Tet and hotels fill up fast, especially anything near the beach or with fireworks views. If you are travelling to Da Nang for Tet 2027, book now.

Tet 2027 Key Dates

  • New Year's Eve: January 28, 2027 (Giao Thua, midnight fireworks)
  • Tet Day 1: January 29, 2027
  • Main holiday period: January 29 – February 4, 2027
  • National holiday (public offices closed): Approximately 5–7 days around the 29th [VERIFY: 2027 official holiday schedule]

What to Expect in Da Nang at Tet

A lot of Vietnamese businesses close for at least three days of Tet, sometimes longer. This includes most local restaurants, street food stalls, markets, and shops. The tourist-facing businesses (hotels, tour operators, most restaurants on An Thuong) stay open, but the city itself is quieter and some of the street-level character of Da Nang disappears temporarily.

What doesn't close: hotels, resort restaurants, beach, Hoi An Ancient Town (major tourist attraction, stays operational), the Dragon Bridge (fireworks on Tet Eve are significant), and the Hyatt, Sheraton, Pullman. The full-service resorts operate normal service throughout.

The upside: the beach is much less crowded in January weather-wise (cool, some cloud, 22–25°C, not swimming weather for most), and the fireworks on Tet Eve are significant. Dragon Bridge and the waterfront area are worth being near for the midnight display.

Hotels That Work for Tet 2027

For Tet, proximity to Dragon Bridge fireworks and having a full-service resort (F&B, spa, entertainment on-site) matters more than beach access, because the weather is not beach-optimal and restaurants outside the resort may be closed.

★ Best for Fireworks: Novotel Premier Han River

Level 36 Sky Bar has some of the best Dragon Bridge views in the city. Walking distance to the waterfront. Full-service hotel, restaurant and bar open through Tet. Book the river-facing rooms on a high floor.

Book Early: How Much Prices Rise

Tet pricing in Da Nang follows a steep curve:

  • 6+ months out: Normal rack rates, good availability
  • 3–4 months out: 30–50% above normal, selection narrows
  • 6–8 weeks out: 100–200% above normal, availability very limited at five-star properties
  • 2 weeks out: Whatever is left costs two to three times the normal rate

For Tet 2027 (January 29), the booking window opens now. The Hyatt, Sheraton, InterContinental, and Pullman will have limited availability by October 2026 at any reasonable rate. Choose a rate with free cancellation on Trip.com where one is offered, so you can rebook if plans change. Locking in early and cancelling is better than being left with no options.

Da Nang or Hoi An for Tet?

Both are worth considering. Hoi An is more atmospheric at Tet: the Ancient Town does lantern releases on the river, the streets are decorated, and it is designed as a heritage experience even when businesses are partly closed. If the cultural angle of Tet matters to you, Hoi An (25 min from Da Nang) is the better base.

Da Nang is better if you want a full-service beach resort with everything on-site, or if the Dragon Bridge fireworks on Tet Eve is the priority. Most international travellers choose Da Nang for the resort infrastructure and do a day trip to Hoi An for the atmosphere.
