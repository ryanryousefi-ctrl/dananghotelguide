Hotel Comparison

Premier Village vs Sheraton Grand Da Nang

Two resorts on the same stretch of My Khe Beach. One sells private-pool villas, the other sells family infrastructure at half the price.

Hotel A
Premier Village Da Nang
VS
Hotel B
Sheraton Grand Da Nang
Editor's Pick

Premier Village Da Nang Resort

Why It Wins

Premier Village is the only all-villa resort on this stretch of My Khe. Every unit has a private plunge pool facing the ocean, which is a genuinely different kind of stay from a tower room, not just a nicer version of one. If you're not traveling with kids and privacy matters more than a lazy river, this is the pick.

The 30-Second Verdict

Who each hotel is actually for

Premier Village Da Nang
Best For

Couples, honeymoons, anyone who wants a private pool and doesn't need a kids club.

Wins
  • Private plunge pool in every villa
  • More privacy, quieter grounds
  • Separate living and sleeping areas
Tradeoff
  • Roughly double the Sheraton's rate
  • Thinner kids programming
Sheraton Grand Da Nang
Best For

Families, Marriott Bonvoy members, and anyone who wants resort energy over villa quiet.

Wins
  • Best kids club and waterslides on My Khe
  • Connecting rooms for families
  • About half the nightly rate
Tradeoff
  • Shared pools, no private plunge pool option
  • Busier, louder grounds in high season

Side by Side

The Pools

Premier Village
Sheraton Grand

Every Premier Village villa has its own plunge pool, so you're never sharing water with other guests. The Sheraton's pool is bigger and livelier, with a dedicated kids' section, but it's a shared resort pool at peak times.

Premier Village — for privacy

The Property

Premier Village
Sheraton Grand

Premier Village is low-rise villas spread across landscaped grounds, quieter and more spread out. The Sheraton is a conventional high-rise tower with a busier, more active resort feel and more food and beverage outlets in one place.

Tie — different by design

The Rooms

Premier Village
Sheraton Grand

Standard Sheraton rooms run from roughly 44 sqm with balconies on most upper floors. Every Premier Village unit is a standalone villa with separate living and sleeping zones, direct outdoor access, and no shared corridors.

Premier Village — for space and privacy
Which Should You Book?

Match the hotel to your trip

Book Premier Village If You Want:
  • A private plunge pool you don't share with anyone
  • A quiet, adults-oriented atmosphere
  • Extra space for a honeymoon or anniversary trip
  • AccorHotels ALL loyalty benefits
Book Sheraton Grand If You Want:
  • A strong kids club and waterslides
  • Connecting rooms for a family group
  • Lower cost per night for the same beach
  • Marriott Bonvoy points and status
Know Before You Book

The honest tradeoffs

Premier Village Da Nang
Watch Out For
  • Rack rate runs roughly double the Sheraton's
  • Kids club and family programming is limited compared to a family-first resort
  • Villas are spread across the grounds, more walking to restaurants and the lobby
Sheraton Grand Da Nang
Watch Out For
  • No private pool option at any room tier
  • Pool and lobby get busy with tour groups and families in peak season
  • Standard rooms are comfortable but not distinctive
Final Verdict

Which one should you actually book?

Premier Village is a one-idea hotel and the idea works: a private pool villa on a good beach, without the isolation penalty of a resort further out of town. If that's what you're coming for, book it and ask for a beachfront villa.

The Sheraton is the better hotel for families by almost every measure that matters to a family, and it costs roughly half as much. For a couple without kids who don't specifically want a private pool, the Sheraton is fine but Premier Village is the more interesting stay.

Our Pick: Premier Village Da Nang for couples, Sheraton Grand for families

Common Questions

Premier Village vs Sheraton Grand: FAQ

Is Premier Village worth the extra cost over Sheraton Grand?+

For couples who specifically want a private pool: yes. Premier Village is the only all-villa property on My Khe Beach and the private pool makes it a meaningfully different stay. For families or groups who will use the shared pool anyway, the Sheraton Grand offers better value and stronger kids facilities at roughly half the rate.
