Premier Village vs Sheraton Grand Da Nang

Premier Village's private villa concept versus Sheraton Grand's conventional luxury. Both on the southern beach strip, very different stays.

Premier Village for villa privacy. Sheraton Grand for the better family facilities.
Overview Location Rooms Amenities Best For Verdict

Hotel Overview

Premier Village Da Nang Resort

  • ★★★★★ All-villa resort
  • 109 private pool villas
  • My Khe Beach, south zone
  • Each villa has private pool and ocean view
  • Managed by AccorHotels
  • From ~$200/night

Sheraton Grand Da Nang Resort

  • ★★★★★ Full-service resort
  • 258 rooms in tower format
  • Truong Sa Road, My Khe Beach
  • Large pool + kids club, family focus
  • Marriott Bonvoy loyalty
  • From ~$110/night

Premier Village is the only all-villa resort on the main My Khe Beach strip. Every unit has a private plunge pool and faces the ocean. You get the privacy of a villa without the isolation of somewhere like the InterContinental. [VERIFY: villa count and pool configuration]

The Sheraton Grand is a conventional tower resort better suited to families. The kids infrastructure is the best on My Khe. At roughly half the price, the Sheraton is the obvious choice for anyone who doesn't specifically want a private villa.

Both properties are in the same resort zone on the southern end of My Khe Beach, close enough that location is not a meaningful differentiator. The Sheraton is slightly closer to the city, but neither hotel is somewhere you walk out of for dinner.

The private pool is Premier Village's entire value proposition. If that matters to you, it is worth the premium. If it doesn't, the Sheraton has better shared facilities including a stronger kids programme and a more active resort atmosphere.

Who Each Hotel Is Best For

Book Premier Village if…

  • Couples who want villa privacy without remote isolation
  • Honeymoons and anniversaries
  • Guests who find shared resort pools unappealing
  • AccorHotels ALL members at premium tier
  • Anyone who specifically wants a private pool on a good beach

Book Sheraton Grand if…

  • Families with children, best kids club on My Khe
  • Marriott Bonvoy members
  • Groups who want resort atmosphere and activity
  • Budget-conscious luxury travellers (half the Premier Village rate)
  • Guests who want ocean views from a tower room

Final Verdict

Premier Village is a one-idea hotel and the idea is good: private pool villa on a good beach. If that is what you are coming for, it delivers without the isolation penalty of somewhere like the InterContinental.

The Sheraton is a better family hotel by almost every measure. Kids club, connecting rooms, larger shared pool, lower price. For a couple, the Sheraton is adequate but Premier Village is the more interesting experience.

The price gap is real. Premier Village runs roughly double the Sheraton's rack rate. Whether a private pool justifies that difference is a personal call, but it is the only hotel in the My Khe zone where that option exists.

Premier Village vs Sheraton Grand: FAQ

Is Premier Village worth the extra cost over Sheraton Grand?+

For couples who specifically want a private pool: yes. Premier Village is the only all-villa property on My Khe Beach and the private pool makes it a meaningfully different stay. For families or groups who will use the shared pool anyway, the Sheraton Grand offers better value and stronger kids facilities at roughly half the rate.
