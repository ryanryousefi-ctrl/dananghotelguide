Editor's Pick

Stay Near My Khe / An Thuong

The easiest first-trip base

For most Korean travelers on a first trip, staying near My Khe / An Thuong removes a lot of friction. You can walk to the beach, to Korean BBQ and fried chicken, to cafes, convenience stores, and massage spots, and use Grab for everything else. Menus here are often in Korean, and staff frequently speak at least basic Korean.

The tradeoff: this area feels more tourist-oriented and international than Hai Chau or the city side. If you want a quieter, more local Da Nang, you'll need to go looking for it.

Da Nang Signature Pick

Hoi An Day Trip

Do not skip it

If you're flying from Korea all the way to Da Nang, Hoi An is too close and too easy to ignore. It's about 30 km south, a UNESCO World Heritage Ancient Town that's compact and walkable, lantern-lit streets, tailor shops, and riverside restaurants that photograph as well in person as they do online.

Even on a short trip, make time for at least an evening. A Grab or private car runs USD 10–25 each way, and organised day tours depart daily from Da Nang hotels.

Practical Basics

💳
Money

Vietnam runs on VND, roughly 1 KRW ≈ 18–20 VND. Hotels and larger restaurants take Korean Visa/Mastercard; street stalls are cash-only. Exchange at the airport or authorised booths, avoid street changers.

🚗
Grab

Southeast Asia's Kakao T equivalent. Works in English, takes card payment, a typical ride costs USD 2–5. Download the app before you arrive.

✈️
Airport

Da Nang International is close to the city and to My Khe. Direct flights from Seoul run 4.5–5 hours, comparable to a domestic long-haul in Korea.

💬
Language

Korean is widely understood around My Khe and An Thuong. Many hotel staff, servers, and tour operators speak basic to intermediate Korean.

☀️
Weather

Dry season March–September, hot and calm seas. Wet season October–January, heavy rain and occasional typhoons. Tet (February/early March) is the busiest Korean travel window.

🏖️
Beach Flags

Green means safe, yellow means caution, red means do not enter. Rip currents can be strong after rain. Don't swim after dark.

🙏
Etiquette

Cover shoulders and knees at pagodas. A small bow works as a greeting, similar to Korean custom. Tipping isn't mandatory but is appreciated.

🛡️
Safety

Da Nang is one of Vietnam's safest cities. Main risks are petty theft in busy markets and traffic, not violent crime. Use Grab over hailing random taxis.

Popular choices for Korean visitors

What Korean Travelers Tend to Like

Beach + Resort

My Khe and Non Nuoc beachfront resorts, priced well below equivalent stays in Jeju or Haeundae.

Cafes

Da Nang's modern cafe scene, especially around An Thuong and the Han River, is a natural fit.

Seafood

Open-air seafood restaurants along My Khe, pick from the tank, cooked to order, priced well below Korea.

Golf

Championship courses including BRG Da Nang and Montgomerie Links draw Korean golf groups specifically.

Shopping

Lotte Mart, the Vietnamese arm of the Korean chain, plus Vincom Plaza for familiar retail brands.

Family Travel

Ba Na Hills and family-oriented resorts cover a full day out with kids without much planning.

Eat, drink, shop

Food, Cafes & Shopping

Mi Quang

Da Nang's signature turmeric noodle dish with pork, shrimp, and peanuts. Order it once, early.

Bun Bo Hue

Spicy lemongrass beef noodle soup from nearby Hue, more intense than pho.

An Thuong Korean BBQ

Samgyeopsal, galbi, fried chicken and chimaek, clustered along An Thuong 4 and 8.

An Thuong Cafes

Rooftop and specialty coffee shops, many Korean-owned, dalgona coffee and bingsu included.

Han Market

One of Da Nang's oldest covered markets. Local produce, fabric, souvenirs, bargaining expected.

Lotte Mart / Vincom Plaza

Fixed-price retail, supermarkets and food courts if you want a break from bargaining.

Moving around the city

Getting Around

Grab for most rides
Airport My Khe Han River Son Tra Marble Mountains

Walk around My Khe and An Thuong, most of what you need is within a few blocks. Use Grab between areas, a typical cross-city ride runs USD 2–5. Scooter rental is common and cheap, but requires an international driving permit and comfort with Da Nang traffic, most first-time visitors are better off sticking to Grab.

Common Questions

Is Da Nang popular with Korean tourists?
Yes. Korean travelers consistently rank among the largest inbound tourist groups to Da Nang. Direct flights from Seoul, Busan, and Daegu, combined with affordable prices and short flight times, make it one of the most popular beach destinations for Koreans in Southeast Asia.
Are there Korean restaurants in Da Nang?
Yes. The An Thuong neighborhood near My Khe Beach has a dense concentration of Korean BBQ restaurants, Korean fried chicken spots, Korean cafes, and convenience stores. Korean-language menus and Korean-speaking staff are common throughout this area.
What hotels do Korean tourists prefer in Da Nang?
Korean visitors tend to favour beachfront resorts along My Khe Beach. Popular choices include the InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort, Hyatt Regency Da Nang, Furama Resort, and Four Points by Sheraton. Hotels near the An Thuong restaurant strip are also favoured for proximity to Korean dining and shops.
Do people speak Korean in Da Nang?
Korean is widely understood in the tourist areas of Da Nang, particularly around My Khe Beach and the An Thuong neighborhood. Many hotel staff, restaurant servers, and tour operators speak basic to intermediate Korean due to the sustained volume of Korean visitors.
Is Hoi An worth visiting from Da Nang?
Absolutely. Hoi An is 30 km south of Da Nang, a UNESCO World Heritage site with lantern-lit streets, riverside dining, and tailoring shops. A Grab or private car costs USD 10–25 each way and is well worth the journey.
