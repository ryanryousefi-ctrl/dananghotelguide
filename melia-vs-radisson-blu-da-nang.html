Hotel Comparison · Non Nuoc Beach · 2026

Melia vs Radisson Blu Danang

The two most underrated five-stars on Non Nuoc Beach — and the ones I'd actually recommend to someone who asks me which resort is the best value in Da Nang.

✎ Written by Ryan Yousefi · 📅 Last updated: April 2026 · ⏰ 8-min read
Overview Location Rooms Amenities Best For Verdict

Hotel Overview

Melia Danang Beach Resort

  • ★★★★★ Adults-focused resort
  • 168 rooms
  • Non Nuoc — south central stretch
  • 3 pools, strong design aesthetic
  • Adults-focused, design-led
  • From $140/night

Radisson Blu Resort Danang

  • ★★★★★ Beachfront resort
  • 282 rooms
  • Non Nuoc — central stretch
  • 4 pools, large beach frontage
  • Best overall value on the strip
  • From $120/night

If someone asks me which Non Nuoc resort has the best value in 2026, my answer is the Radisson Blu. Most people haven't heard of it, which is exactly why it's cheaper than the Hyatt and Marriott despite matching them on infrastructure. The Melia is the right alternative for anyone who finds big-resort energy exhausting — it's smaller, more considered in its design, and skews toward couples rather than families. These two are the intelligent choices on the Non Nuoc strip, which is why they deserve a direct comparison.

The Radisson Blu has four pools to the Melia's three, larger standard rooms, and better family infrastructure. The Melia fights back with a stronger design identity, the Yhi Spa which is genuinely one of the better resort spas in Da Nang, and an overall atmosphere that couples prefer. The Radisson Blu is the better resort on raw metrics; the Melia is the better resort if atmosphere and adult focus matter more than room count.

Who Each Hotel Is Best For

Choose Melia Danang Beach Resort if…

  • Couples who want an adults-focused environment
  • Design and aesthetic matter to you
  • You want a quieter beach position
  • Spa is a priority for your stay
  • MeliáPlus loyalty members
  • You find large resort energy draining

Choose Radisson Blu Resort Danang if…

  • You want the best five-star value on Non Nuoc
  • Room size matters — Radisson rooms are genuinely large
  • Travelling with kids (better programme)
  • You're not tied to a specific loyalty scheme
  • You want four pools and more F&B variety
  • You want to spend less without compromising on quality

Final Verdict

The Radisson Blu is the best value five-star on Non Nuoc Beach in 2026. That's the honest answer. Larger rooms, four pools, full family programme, and consistent service — at prices 20–30% below the Hyatt and Marriott. If you're not locked into a specific loyalty programme, there is no smarter booking on this strip.

The Melia is the right choice specifically if you want an adults-focused, design-led resort and the Radisson's family-friendly atmosphere isn't what you're after. The Yhi Spa is a genuine differentiator. Couples on honeymoon or anniversary trips who care about vibe over room count will be happier at the Melia.
