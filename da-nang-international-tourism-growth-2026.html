Da Nang Searches Up 281% in Russia as European Demand Hits Four-Year High

New data from Yandex and Agoda confirms Da Nang is the breakout international destination of 2026 - driven by surging Russian interest and back-to-back years leading Vietnam searches among European travelers.

The numbers are not close. In the first six months of 2026, Russian searches for Da Nang on Yandex increased by 281% compared to the same period in 2025. For context: overall searches for Vietnamese tourism on the platform grew 91% over the same period. Da Nang is pulling well above the country average - and it is not a fluke of a single month.

The category driving the biggest movement is searches for tourism activities in Da Nang specifically - those jumped 413%, accounting for about 12% of all Vietnam-related tourism searches on Yandex in the first half of the year. People are not just looking at Da Nang as a destination. They are looking at what to do there.

European Demand: Second Consecutive Year in the Top Group

The European picture is different in character but consistent in direction. According to Agoda's summer 2026 data, Vietnam ranked fourth among Asian destinations searched by European tourists - up one place from the same period in 2025. Da Nang sits in the group of Vietnam's most-searched cities for the second straight year.

It is worth noting what that sustained position means: Da Nang is not benefiting from a single promotional push or a news cycle. It has held its place in European search rankings across two consecutive summers, which suggests the interest is based on actual traveler experience and word of mouth, not marketing spend alone.

People are not just looking at Da Nang. They are looking at what to do there.

Bookings Are Following the Searches

Booking data from Yandex Travel backs up what the search numbers suggest. Hotel room bookings in Da Nang via the platform increased 155% in the first half of 2026 compared to the same period in 2025. Within that, Q2 2026 alone was 42% higher than Q1 2026 - meaning momentum is building through the year, not front-loaded into a January spike.

For hotels, the direction is clear. Russian travelers are converting search interest into actual reservations at a rate that was not visible in this market two years ago.

What the City Is Doing About It

Da Nang has been running active promotion in Russia since at least early 2026 - a city delegation met with Yandex representatives at Yandex headquarters in March as part of a formal tourism promotion program. That kind of direct partnership with platform operators, rather than relying on general country-level tourism campaigns, is part of why the search data for Da Nang specifically is outrunning Vietnam's national average.

On the platform side, Agoda is running multilingual promotion of Da Nang's offer - sea, resorts, nature, culture, cuisine - across European markets. From August 21 to 22, 2026, Da Nang is hosting around 150 content creators from multiple countries through a partnership with Klook, to generate direct content from the destination for international audiences.

What It Means for Travelers Booking Now

More international demand at this pace tends to have one predictable effect on hotel pricing: upward pressure, particularly on the better-reviewed properties. If you are planning a trip to Da Nang for late 2026 or into 2027, booking lead times that worked in 2024 may not be adequate at the same properties now.

The hotels that tend to fill first when a destination's profile rises internationally are the ones with the strongest review profiles and direct beach access. The InterContinental Sun Peninsula, Hyatt Regency, and Naman Retreat are the obvious candidates. The mid-range beach properties - A La Carte, TMS, Four Points - will absorb overflow demand and are worth watching for rate movement over the next two quarters.
