The legal landscape

Gambling in Vietnam

Gambling in Vietnam is tightly regulated by the government. For most of its modern history, all forms of gambling were illegal for Vietnamese citizens — and the law remains that way today. Casinos and gaming clubs operate legally, but exclusively for foreign nationals holding a valid passport. Vietnamese citizens are prohibited from entering unless they hold specific government permits introduced under a limited pilot programme.

The legal framework is set by Decree 03/2017/ND-CP, which governs casino operations in Vietnam. Venues must be licensed, located within designated tourism zones or international-standard hotels, and must strictly enforce the passport rule at the door. This makes Da Nang and the surrounding area — with its concentration of international-grade hotels and the Hoiana integrated resort — one of the most accessible legal gambling destinations in Southeast Asia for foreign visitors.

🌏
Who can enter?
Foreign passport holders only. Your passport is checked at the door of every venue — no exceptions. Vietnamese nationals require a special government permit (extremely rare) to enter casino premises.
📋
Legal framework
Vietnam Decree 03/2017/ND-CP governs casino licensing. All venues listed in this guide hold active government licences. Playing at unlicensed venues carries significant legal risk for both operators and players.
💵
Currency
Gambling at all licensed venues in Vietnam occurs in US Dollars (USD), not Vietnamese Dong. Bring USD cash or exchange at the venue — most major venues also accept credit/debit card buy-ins at the cage.
⚠️
Avoid illegal street gambling: Unlicensed card games or informal gambling operations occasionally operate near tourist areas. These are illegal, frequently involve scams, and carry serious legal consequences including detention. Use only the licensed venues listed in this guide.

Know before you go

Entry rules & what to bring

🛂
Passport Required
Every venue checks your passport at the door. No exceptions. A photocopy or phone photo is not accepted — bring the physical document. Hotel key cards do not substitute for passport entry.
💵
USD Cash
All gaming transactions occur in US Dollars. Bring USD in small to mid-denomination bills. Most venues have USD ATMs or a cage that accepts card buy-ins, but cash is preferred and saves on fees.
👔
Dress Code
Hoiana and hotel-based VIP clubs enforce smart casual as a minimum. Flip-flops and beachwear are not appropriate on casino floors. Open-toed sandals are typically fine. City clubs are more relaxed.
📵
Photography
Photography inside gaming floors is strictly prohibited at all venues. Security staff will ask you to stop immediately. Exterior and lobby photos are generally permitted.
🔞
Age Limit
Minimum age is 21 years old at all licensed venues in Vietnam. You may be asked to provide passport-verified age at the door or at the cage — carry your passport even if you look older.
🎰
Game Types
Da Nang's city clubs are e-gaming only — electronic slots and digital table games. For live dealer Baccarat, Roulette, Blackjack, and Poker, Hoiana is the only option in the area. Choose your venue accordingly.
🚐
Getting to Hoiana
Hoiana runs complimentary shuttle buses from Da Nang city and Hoi An. Grab taxis are also available. The resort is 45 minutes from Da Nang Airport and 15 minutes from Hoi An's Ancient Town.
🇻🇳
Vietnamese Citizens
Vietnamese nationals are not permitted to enter any casino or e-gaming club without a specific government-issued permit. This rule is enforced at every licensed venue. Do not attempt to assist locals to enter.
ℹ️
Hoiana vs. Da Nang clubs — which should you choose? Go to Hoiana if you want live table games (Baccarat, Poker, Roulette), a full resort atmosphere, and a proper night out. Choose a Da Nang city club if you want a quick, convenient session of electronic gaming, you're already staying near the beach, or you don't want to make the 45-minute trip south.

Play safely

Responsible gambling

Gambling is entertainment — it is not a reliable source of income, and the house always has a mathematical edge. All licensed venues in Vietnam are required to display responsible gambling information, and Hoiana in particular has formal self-exclusion and limit-setting programmes.

Before you play

  • Set a firm loss limit before you enter — in USD, not "one more machine"
  • Treat your gambling budget the same as a restaurant meal: money spent for enjoyment, not to recover
  • Never gamble with money you need for accommodation, flights, or essentials
  • Avoid gambling after drinking heavily — impaired judgement is the casino's best friend
  • Take breaks. Step outside, eat, and return with a clear head if you continue
  • Understand the game before you play. Ask staff to explain rules — they will always help
  • Baccarat has one of the lowest house edges in the casino — Banker bet is around 1.06%
  • Slots have higher house edges than table games — understand what you are playing
  • Self-exclusion programmes are available at Hoiana — ask at the cage if you need help
  • If gambling is affecting your life or travel, stop and seek support
International helplines: If you or someone you know is struggling with problem gambling, contact the National Problem Gambling Helpline (US): 1-800-522-4700 · GamCare (UK): 0808 8020 133 · Gambling Help Online (AU): 1800 858 858
