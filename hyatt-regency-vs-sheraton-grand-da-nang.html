Hyatt Regency vs Sheraton Grand Da Nang

The two biggest names on My Khe Beach go head to head. Same zone, similar rates, different experiences.

Sheraton for families. Hyatt for design and the pool.
Overview Location Rooms Amenities Best For Verdict

Hotel Overview

Hyatt Regency Da Nang Resort

  • ★★★★★ Luxury resort
  • 318 rooms across 23 buildings
  • Truong Sa Road, My Khe Beach
  • 5 outdoor pools including lap pool
  • World of Hyatt loyalty
  • From ~$120/night

Sheraton Grand Da Nang Resort

  • ★★★★★ Full-service resort
  • 258 rooms in tower format
  • Truong Sa Road, My Khe Beach
  • Large outdoor pool + kids pool
  • Marriott Bonvoy loyalty
  • From ~$110/night

These two properties are 400 metres apart on Truong Sa Road and compete for the same My Khe Beach customer. They are different hotels. The Hyatt is lower-rise, spread across pavilion-style buildings, and the architecture has more personality. [VERIFY: building count and layout]

The Sheraton is a tower with a conventional resort footprint, better for families with children because the kids club is genuinely good and the pool area is larger and more sheltered. If you're choosing between them, the right answer depends on who you're travelling with. See the full hotel ranking for context.

Neither property has a meaningful location advantage over the other. They sit on the same stretch of beach, equidistant from the airport and city. The beach here is wide, clean, and not crowded in shoulder season. Both hotels are too far from the Han River to walk anywhere interesting, so factor in Grab costs for evenings out.

The Sheraton's kids club and waterslide setup is the better family facility. The Hyatt has the better pool count and the Stillwater Spa has a solid reputation. Both have beach service. Neither is missing anything you'd expect at this price point.

Who Each Hotel Is Best For

Book Hyatt Regency if…

  • Couples who care about design and atmosphere
  • World of Hyatt members pursuing points or upgrades
  • Guests who want multiple pool options
  • Smaller groups, more intimate resort feel
  • Design-forward travellers who find tower hotels generic

Book Sheraton Grand if…

  • Families with children under 12, kids club is excellent
  • Marriott Bonvoy members
  • Guests wanting ocean views from a high floor
  • Larger groups or families needing connecting rooms
  • Vietnamese public holidays when the resort atmosphere suits families

Final Verdict

Skip it if you're a couple looking for atmosphere: the Hyatt will make you happier and the design difference is noticeable the moment you walk in.

Skip it if you have children under 12: the Sheraton's family infrastructure, kids club, waterslide, and pool layout is built for exactly this. The Hyatt is fine for kids but the Sheraton is engineered for them.

Loyalty programme members: straightforward choice. Bonvoy goes Sheraton, Hyatt goes Hyatt. If you're neutral on points, the Hyatt is the more interesting hotel.

Common Questions

Hyatt Regency vs Sheraton Grand: FAQ

Hyatt Regency or Sheraton Grand Da Nang — which is better?+

For couples: Hyatt Regency. The pavilion architecture, Stillwater Spa, and five-pool layout make it the more distinctive experience. For families with children: Sheraton Grand. The kids club, waterslide, and connecting room availability are purpose-built for travelling with under-12s.

Are Hyatt Regency and Sheraton Grand on the same beach?+

Yes. Both sit on Truong Sa Road on My Khe Beach, approximately 400 metres apart. The beach is the same stretch in both cases: wide, clean white sand, good surf in shoulder season. Neither has a location advantage.
