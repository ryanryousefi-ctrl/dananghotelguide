Da Nang Neighbourhood Guide

Where to Stay in
Da Nang

Da Nang is compact, 30 minutes across at its widest. But each neighbourhood delivers a different holiday.

This guide maps six distinct areas with honest pros, cons, and hotel picks.

Read the full guide →

Six distinct areas

Choose your base

Every area has a different character. The one that suits you depends on whether you want beach access, nightlife proximity, resort seclusion, or city walkability.

Interactive Map

Where Are These Areas?

Click any pin to explore each neighbourhood. All four zones sit within 25 minutes of each other.

Areas

My Khe Beach

Best for: Most travellers · Beach + city combo

The 30km urban beach strip. Direct sand access, the highest concentration of hotels, and the An Thuong restaurant and café scene within walking distance.

✓ Pros
Beach access · Dining scene · Central
✗ Cons
Busier peak season · Some tourist-trap restaurants
My Khe hotels →

Son Tra Peninsula

Best for: Luxury resort stays · Seclusion

The InterContinental Sun Peninsula's home, a protected nature reserve with clifftop resorts, jungle settings, and the most dramatic views in Central Vietnam.

✓ Pros
genuinely excellent resorts · Private beaches · Quiet
✗ Cons
20 min taxi to city · Higher prices
Son Tra resorts →

"The where-to-stay decision in Da Nang shapes everything: morning routine, how you eat, how much you spend on transport, and which version of the city you experience. My Khe is the beach life.

The Han River is the city life. Most visitors should pick one and stay there."

Han River & City Centre

Best for: Business · City explorers

Dragon Bridge, the night market, and Marble Mountains in one direction; Hoi An 30 minutes south. Best for travellers who want a city base with beach day-trips.

✓ Pros
Walkable city · Dragon Bridge · Lower rates
✗ Cons
15 min taxi to beach
Han River hotels →

Non Nuoc Beach

Best for: Families · Quieter beach

15km south of the city, adjacent to the Marble Mountains. Quieter and more spacious than My Khe.

Home to the Hyatt Regency and several family-focused resorts.

✓ Pros
Quieter beach · Space · Family resorts
✗ Cons
Further from dining scene
