Good to know

An Bang is 4km from the Ancient Town — every hotel provides bicycles or rents them cheaply. The ride through village lanes takes 25–35 minutes and is genuinely pleasant in the morning. For evenings, Grab motorbike costs 20,000–35,000 VND and takes 10 minutes. Late-night Grab availability can be patchy — arrange return transport before heading out.

Beach conditions

"An Bang has retained more sand than Cua Dai. March to September: good swimming. October to February: waves — excellent body surfing, less suitable for young children."

Getting around

An Bang to Old Town

4km separates An Bang Beach from the Ancient Town entrance. All options work; the bicycle is the most enjoyable.

🚲

Bicycle

Most hotels provide or rent bikes cheaply. Quiet lanes through rice paddies and village streets.

25–35 min · 50,000–100,000 VND/day rental

Honest assessment

An Bang vs Old Town stays

Choose An Bang if

  • Beach access is a priority
  • You want more space and greenery per dollar
  • You prefer a quieter, slower base
  • You're happy cycling or grabbing to Old Town
  • You're visiting March–September (calm sea)

Stay in Old Town instead if

  • You want to walk everywhere in the evenings
  • You're visiting Oct–Feb (rough sea months)
  • Transport cost over a week matters
  • You want more restaurant and cafe variety
  • Short stay (1–2 nights) with no time for beach days

South of Hoi An · 10km · 20 min

Common questions

An Bang Beach hotels — FAQ

Is An Bang Beach worth staying at in Hoi An?
Yes, if beach access is a priority. An Bang has held up better than Cua Dai (less erosion), good seafood restaurants, and a genuinely relaxed vibe distinct from the Ancient Town. It's 4km from Old Town, so factor in transport for evening visits. For beach-first travellers, it's the right base.
How far is An Bang Beach from Hoi An Old Town?
Approximately 4km northeast of the Ancient Town entrance. By Grab motorbike, 10 minutes, 20,000–35,000 VND. By bicycle, 25–35 minutes on quiet village roads — genuinely pleasant in the morning. Most beach resort properties run a shuttle service to the old town at set times.
Is Cua Dai or An Bang better in 2026?
An Bang is generally the better choice. Cua Dai has suffered significant beach erosion, with sea walls now protecting some hotel frontage where beach once stood. An Bang has retained more of its sand and has a livelier restaurant scene. Victoria Hoi An Beach Resort on Cua Dai still has good hotel facilities — it's the beach that's diminished, not the property.
Is An Bang Beach good for families?
Conditionally. In the calm season (March–September), the water is generally safe for older children. Young children should stay in the shallows. During the northeast monsoon (October–February), waves can be strong and the beach is not ideal for young kids. Hoi An Trails Resort is the most family-suited An Bang-area property, with large grounds and more space than the smaller boutique options.
