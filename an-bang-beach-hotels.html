Best Hotels in An Bang Beach

Coffee, sand, and noticeably less polish than the resort strips. That's the whole appeal.

Updated August 2026 · Independently selected

Why An Bang

Beach, Coffee, and Less Polish

An Bang is where to send someone who wants coffee shops and sand more than hotel lobbies. Casual restaurants, a real beach club scene, and a villa-rental market that runs alongside the hotels. It's younger, more neighborhood than resort, and noticeably less polished than Cua Dai a few kilometers south.

Editor's Pick

Shantira Beach Resort & Spa

An Bang Beach · Apartment-Style · $$

Apartment-style rooms with kitchens, right at the beach. Good for anyone who doesn't want to eat every meal in a hotel restaurant, or who's staying a week or longer.

It's the strongest all-round An Bang pick: real space, real kitchens, and still a short walk to the sand and the cafe strip.

Signature Pick

An Bang Seaside Village

An Bang Beach · Bungalow · $

Bungalow-style, homestay feel, a short walk to the sand. The pool is small, but the vibe is genuinely relaxed without the resort markup. This is the property that best represents what An Bang actually is.

Worth knowing

The Villa Rental Market

A meaningful share of An Bang accommodation runs outside the hotel booking system entirely: private villas rented by the week through owners or local agents, popular with families and longer stays. Hotels remain the simpler, more reliable option if you want live rates and instant confirmation, which is why they're what we feature here.
