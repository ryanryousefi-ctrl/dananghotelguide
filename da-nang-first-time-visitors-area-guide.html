Where to Stay in Da Nang
First Trip Edition

Don't overthink it. Most first-time visitors should stay at My Khe. Here's the full picture.

Editor's Top Pick

My Khe Beach
is the right call

Six kilometres of open sand, gentle surf, and a hotel strip that runs from proper budget guesthouses to Sheraton-level resort. That range is the point. Whatever your budget, you can land at My Khe and be on the beach in under two minutes.

The An Thuong restaurant district sits directly behind the hotels. Walk out your front door toward the ocean, or walk out the back toward 200 restaurants, bars, and cafes. First-time visitors who base here rarely feel like they're missing something. First-time visitors who base elsewhere often wish they were closer to the beach.

When in doubt, stay at My Khe. The rest of this guide exists for when My Khe genuinely isn't the right fit.

My Khe Beach

The main beach strip. Hotels run directly along the sand on Vo Nguyen Giap. Six kilometres of consistent beach, calm-ish surf suitable for swimming, and a mid-range to luxury hotel supply that covers most budgets above hostels. The An Thuong neighbourhood directly behind adds a dense restaurant and bar scene within walking distance. If you're only in Da Nang for a few days, this is where to be.

An Thuong

The neighbourhood directly behind My Khe Beach. Dense streets packed with restaurants, rooftop bars, cafes, and guesthouses. If you've seen any video of Da Nang street food or expat nightlife, it was probably filmed here. Staying in An Thuong proper means the beach is a 5-minute walk in one direction and 200 dinner options in every other direction. Ideal if you plan to eat out every night and want to walk to everything.

Han River / Hai Chau

The city proper. Dragon Bridge, the night market, Bach Dang waterfront, the covered central market. Hotels here skew toward business travellers and city-focused visitors. You're 15 minutes by Grab from the beach, which is fine if your trip is more city-explore than beach-sit. The Novotel Da Nang Premier Han River sits directly on the water and has Sky36, one of Da Nang's best rooftop bars, on top.

Non Nuoc Beach

The resort strip south of the Marble Mountains. The beach here is comparable to My Khe but quieter -- this stretch is dominated by full-scale resorts rather than a mix of hotels and guesthouses. Hyatt Regency, Furama, and the Marriott are the anchors. You're 20 minutes from An Thuong by Grab, which matters if you plan to eat out. If your trip is primarily resort-pool-beach with no city agenda, Non Nuoc is excellent and often better value than the comparable My Khe options.

South Son Tra

The southern entry point to Son Tra Peninsula, where the peninsula meets the city. Premier Village Da Nang Resort sits here -- private pool villas right on the rocks with a good beach. It's quieter than My Khe but not as isolated as going further up the peninsula. You're about 20-25 minutes from An Thuong by Grab. Works well for couples who want a quieter property without fully committing to the remoteness of the north end.

North Son Tra

The far end of Son Tra Peninsula. InterContinental Da Nang Sun Peninsula sits here, carved into a cliff above the South China Sea. It's deliberately isolated -- that's the product. Getting to An Thuong or the Han River takes 30-40 minutes by car, so plan meals on-property or account for the commute. This works as a honeymoon or anniversary stay, not as a base for someone who wants to explore the city. Naman Retreat (Non Nuoc-adjacent) is sometimes grouped here by geography but offers a similar contemplative vibe.

If My Khe isn't for you

My Khe suits most first-time visitors because it stacks beach, food, nightlife, and hotel variety in one walkable zone. But there are legitimate reasons to look elsewhere.

If your trip is primarily sightseeing with no strong beach agenda, stay Han River -- shorter Grab rides to the Dragon Bridge, the market, and the museum district. If you want a full resort experience where you barely leave the property, Non Nuoc is better value than My Khe's resort options. If you're celebrating something and budget isn't the main variable, InterContinental Sun Peninsula is in a category by itself.

What doesn't make sense for a first trip: North Son Tra as a general city base, or anything far south of Non Nuoc without a specific reason to be there.

First-Timer Logistics

Getting from the Airport

Da Nang Airport is 5-10 minutes from My Khe and 10-15 minutes from Han River. Grab is the easiest option -- expect 80,000-150,000 VND depending on destination. Official taxis are available but negotiate the fare before getting in, or insist on the meter. The drive to Non Nuoc is around 25-30 minutes.

Getting Around

Grab is reliable across all main zones. A My Khe to Han River ride costs 35,000-55,000 VND, which is under $3 USD. Grab to Non Nuoc from My Khe is 60,000-90,000 VND. You don't need a motorbike unless you're planning day trips to remote areas. Driver availability is good in all core zones including An Thuong and the Han River waterfront.

How Long to Stay

Three nights covers My Khe, one day trip to Hoi An, and a Son Tra excursion. Five nights lets you slow down, add Marble Mountains, and eat your way through An Thuong properly. If this is a Vietnam multi-city trip and Da Nang is a stop between Hue and Hoi An, two nights is doable -- stay My Khe to make the most of the limited time.

Day Trip to Hoi An

Hoi An is 30 km south, roughly 40-50 minutes by Grab (180,000-220,000 VND one way). A round trip with a few hours in the Old Town and a meal is completely doable from any Da Nang base area. Most visitors do it as a day trip from My Khe. Ask your hotel about shared shuttle services if you want a fixed-cost option -- most offer them for around 150,000-200,000 VND per person each way.
