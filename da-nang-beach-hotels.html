Best Beach Hotels in Da Nang

Beachfront resorts on Non Nuoc and Son Tra Peninsula, plus the best hotels on the My Khe strip. Independently selected.

Editor's Top Pick — Best Beach Resort in Da Nang

InterContinental Da Nang Sun Peninsula

Son Tra Peninsula · Luxury Resort · Private Beach

Nothing else in Da Nang is in the same conversation. The InterContinental sits on a private cove carved into Son Tra Peninsula, accessed by a funicular from reception. No road noise reaches it. The architecture cuts into the cliff face above the South China Sea, and the infinity pool view is the best of any hotel in the region. Three restaurants, 197 rooms, and a standard of service that the big Non Nuoc resorts don't come close to.

Skip it if you have young children (steep paths, no kids' club), you're budget-conscious, or you want to walk to restaurants. It's 25 minutes from the city. For those it suits, nothing in Da Nang compares.

Sheraton Grand Da Nang Resort

My An Beach · Full-Service Resort · 5-Star

The resort we'd send almost anyone to. Consistent, well-run, genuinely beachfront.

The most dependable resort on the Da Nang coast. Directly on My An Beach with a wide pool deck, multiple dining options, a full spa, and operational reliability that makes a holiday feel like a holiday. Rooms are large by Vietnamese resort standards. The beach here stays swimmable later into the afternoon than Non Nuoc, which loses sun behind the headland.

It lacks the wow factor of the InterContinental and the family infrastructure of the Hyatt. But as an all-round resort stay for couples who want comfort without drama, it consistently delivers.

Which Beach Area?

Da Nang's beachfront hotels span four distinct zones. Where you stay shapes the experience more than the hotel itself.

Son Tra Peninsula

Private & Secluded

InterContinental and Premier Village territory. No road noise, private coves, national park surroundings. 25 minutes from the city. Right for honeymooners and luxury travelers who won't leave the property.

My An Beach

Resort Strip Classic

Sheraton Grand and Furama. Wide beach, calmer sea than My Khe, resort-to-resort feel. 15 minutes from city. Right for couples wanting a proper resort vacation without total isolation.

Non Nuoc Beach

Full Resort Destination

Hyatt, Marriott, Melia, Radisson Blu, Sandy Beach. The most built-out resort zone, with the Marble Mountains as backdrop. Best family infrastructure. 20 minutes from city.

My Khe Strip

Urban Beach Access

M Hotel, TMS, SALA, CHICLAND. City hotels with beach proximity, not destination resorts. Walkable to An Thuong restaurants. Right for travelers who want beach access and city mobility.

Da Nang's original luxury resort, and still one of the best. Tropical garden grounds, wide beachfront, and a sense of space the newer resorts don't have. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best beach hotel in Da Nang?
The InterContinental Da Nang Sun Peninsula Resort is the best beach hotel in Da Nang for those who want a true luxury resort. It sits on a private cove on Son Tra Peninsula with no road noise, a private beach, and the highest service standard in the city. For a full-service resort on the main strip, the Sheraton Grand Da Nang is the most reliable all-round choice.
Which beach area is best for hotels in Da Nang?
Non Nuoc Beach has the largest concentration of full-service resort hotels and is the quietest, most resort-oriented stretch. My An Beach is calmer with a few large resorts. My Khe is the most urban beach - hotels have beach access but the strip is busy and commercial. Son Tra Peninsula is private and secluded but 25 minutes from the city centre.
Are Da Nang beach hotels good for families?
Yes. The Hyatt Regency Da Nang has the best kids' facilities of any resort on the strip - a dedicated kids' club, shallow pool zone, and Non Nuoc Beach which is safer for children than My Khe. The Sheraton Grand and Marriott Non Nuoc are also well set up for families. The InterContinental is not ideal for young children - steep paths and no kids' club.
What is the difference between My Khe and Non Nuoc Beach hotels?
My Khe Beach hotels are on an urban strip - busy, walkable, with restaurants and bars nearby, but the beach is crowded and the setting is not resort-like. Non Nuoc Beach hotels are full destination resorts with grounds, multiple pools, and a quieter beach. Non Nuoc is better for a resort stay; My Khe is better if you want city access with beach proximity.
When is the best time to visit Da Nang beach hotels?
February through August is the best time. The sea is calm, skies are clear, and beach conditions are excellent. September through November brings typhoon season and rough seas - beach hotels are significantly worse value during this period. December and January are dry but cooler and occasionally overcast.
Is it worth staying at a beach hotel vs city centre in Da Nang?
If your main goal is beach and pool, stay beachfront. The resort experience is significantly better than trying to access the beach from a city hotel. The beach strip is 15-20 minutes from the city centre by Grab, costing around 80,000-120,000 VND each way.
