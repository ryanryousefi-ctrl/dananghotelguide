Editor's Verdict

Where We'd Stay

One honest recommendation

Central My Khe is the right default for most first-time visitors. You are two minutes from the sand, An Thuong's restaurants are on your doorstep, and you never need a taxi in the evenings. For families or anyone wanting a true resort experience, the southern stretch — Sheraton Grand, Hyatt Regency, Pullman — is the better fit: larger pools, private beach clubs, more space. And for one trip in your life, if budget isn't the constraint, the InterContinental on Son Tra is something else entirely: a private bay, no neighbours, genuinely one of the best resorts in Asia.

North My Khe

Less crowded, 20-30% cheaper than the central strip. You are a short Grab from An Thuong. The beach here is noticeably quieter in the mornings.

Worth Knowing

You Don't Always Need "Beachfront"

Hotels one block behind Vo Nguyen Giap can be considerably cheaper, while the walk to My Khe Beach is often only 60-90 seconds. Because the beach is public, paying extra for the front row does not always make sense for budget and mid-range travellers. For luxury stays, the direct beach gate access starts to actually matter. Use accurate language when reading listings: "across from My Khe Beach" and "beachfront resort" are not the same thing.

Central My Khe and Non Nuoc are roughly 20 minutes apart by Grab. Choose your beach zone before booking.

Common Questions

Beach Hotels FAQ

What is the best beachfront hotel in Da Nang?

For ultra-luxury with real drama, the InterContinental on Son Tra is in a class of its own: private bay, no neighbours, genuinely one of the best resorts in Asia. For a five-star on My Khe, the Sheraton Grand or Pullman are the most consistent. For design and value in central My Khe, Chicland is the standout mid-range pick. For families, Hyatt Regency on the southern stretch is the most complete package.

Is My Khe Beach or Non Nuoc Beach better?

Neither is objectively better, they suit different trips. My Khe is better if you want to be in the mix: restaurants within walking distance, easy city access, more going on in the evenings. Non Nuoc is better if you want to slow down, you are travelling with young kids, or you are planning Hoi An day trips and want to be halfway there already. The sand quality on both is good. The difference is everything around the beach, not the beach itself.

What is the best budget beach hotel in Da Nang?

Hilton Garden Inn Da Nang is probably the best budget four-star directly on the beach right now: Hilton standards, real beach access, rates that consistently undercut comparable properties. Diamond Sea and Sala are solid mid-range picks in the central zone. And one useful trick: properties one block back from the sand in the An Thuong zone give you the same beach access at 25-35% less. The beach is public.

Can you swim at Da Nang beach year-round?

March through August is the main swimming season: calm water, good visibility, no issues. September through November is the northeast monsoon and it can get rough; red flags go up regularly and you should take them seriously. December through February is variable. The exception is the InterContinental's private bay on Son Tra, which is sheltered enough to be swimmable most of the year.

How far are the Non Nuoc resorts from Da Nang city centre?

Non Nuoc is about 13km south of the city centre, 20-25 minutes by Grab. Most guests factor in the ride. Hoi An is another 20 minutes further south, which makes Non Nuoc a reasonable base if you are splitting time between Da Nang and Hoi An — you are already halfway there.

Which Da Nang beach hotel is best for families?

Hyatt Regency Da Nang is the strongest family resort overall: four pools, a dedicated kids club, and the calmest water on the southern stretch. Premier Village works well for families who want private outdoor space — every villa has its own pool. The Pullman offers the most complete family resort experience without climbing to the very top of the price range.
