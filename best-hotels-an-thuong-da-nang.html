Why An Thuong?

An Thuong is where you stay when you want the easiest Da Nang vacation. Walk out the door and you have breakfast choices, specialty coffee, international restaurants, bars, massage shops, convenience stores and nightlife - all on foot. My Khe Beach is 5-15 minutes away depending on your hotel.

The neighborhood attracts longer-stay travelers, digital nomads, expats, and anyone who has been to Da Nang before and knows that the beachfront tower is only part of what makes the area work. If you're going to be in Da Nang for more than three nights, An Thuong almost always makes more sense as a base than a hotel directly on the beach road.

Common Questions

What is An Thuong in Da Nang?
An Thuong is the expat and local favorite neighborhood a few streets back from My Khe Beach. It has Da Nang's densest concentration of cafes, international restaurants, bars, spas and boutique hotels. Most things you want for a comfortable stay are walkable. My Khe Beach is 5-15 minutes on foot depending on your hotel.
How far is An Thuong from My Khe Beach?
Most An Thuong hotels are 5-15 minutes on foot from My Khe Beach. Hotels on the eastern edge of An Thuong can be closer to 3-5 minutes. Grab is always an option for the return trip in midday heat.
Is An Thuong good for families?
Yes. Relaxed neighborhood feel, family-friendly restaurants, walkable streets, and the beach is close. Some boutique hotels have pools. For families wanting convenience and beach access without paying beachfront rates, An Thuong is a strong choice.
Is An Thuong safe at night?
Yes. An Thuong is active and well-lit in the evenings. Bars, restaurants and cafes are open late. One of the most comfortable evening neighborhoods in Da Nang. The usual common-sense travel precautions apply.
Are An Thuong hotels cheaper than beachfront?
Generally yes. An Thuong boutique hotels tend to cost less than comparable quality on Vo Nguyen Giap. The tradeoff is the ocean is not outside your window - but it is a short walk away.
