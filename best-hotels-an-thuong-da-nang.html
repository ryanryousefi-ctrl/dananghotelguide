What is An Thuong?

An Thuong is a quiet grid of low-rise streets tucked between Vo Nguyen Giap (the main beach road) and 2/9 Street, roughly one block from My Khe Beach. The streets are numbered An Thuong 1 through 5, running perpendicular to the beach. The whole area is compact enough to walk end-to-end in 15 minutes.

Locals call it "foreigner street" - an informal label that reflects the high concentration of Western-style cafes, international restaurants, bars, and the type of boutique hotels that attract independent travelers. It is not exclusively a tourist enclave; there are Vietnamese families and businesses throughout. But the density of foreigner-oriented businesses is higher here than anywhere else in Da Nang, and the neighborhood reflects that mix.

The beach access is the starting point for most people's interest: 50-100 meters from Vo Nguyen Giap, which is the beach road. Hotels on the beach-facing side of An Thuong streets are a 3-5 minute walk from the sand. This is the tightest combination of walkable food and beach proximity in Da Nang at non-beachfront prices.

Why An Thuong works: You get the beach without paying beachfront rates, and you get Da Nang's best restaurant strip without the commute. The combination is hard to replicate elsewhere in the city. The trade-off is no resort grounds - An Thuong hotels are urban boutique properties, not full-service resorts.

The An Thuong Restaurant Strip

An Thuong 1-5 streets are collectively the best restaurant area in Da Nang for independent travelers. The density of good options is higher here than anywhere else in the city, the prices are reasonable, and the variety covers Vietnamese, seafood, Thai, Japanese, Indian, pizza, and cafes. Most places are open from mid-morning until 10pm or later.

This is not a tourist trap strip - Da Nang residents eat here regularly. You can find a genuinely excellent bowl of bun bo Hue for 40,000 VND alongside a well-made flat white at 50,000 VND and fresh seafood for 150,000-300,000 VND per person. The range covers every budget within the same short walk.

An Thuong 2 has the highest concentration of bars and gets louder on weekend nights. An Thuong 3 and 4 are calmer and have more restaurant-focused businesses. The morning market on An Thuong is active from 5-9am for fresh produce, meat, and local breakfast options if you are cooking or want a street breakfast.

Practical note: Most An Thuong restaurants do not take reservations and do not need them except on Vietnamese holidays. Walk in, pick a table, and order. The busiest time is 6:30-8pm. Outside that window, you will rarely wait for a table.

Local Perspective — Ryan Yousefi

Why I Keep Coming Back to An Thuong

I eat in An Thuong several times a week. It is where I go for a quick lunch, an evening meal with friends, and coffee when I do not want to ride across the city. The combination of quality and proximity - genuinely good food within a 5-minute walk - is something you do not get in most neighborhoods in Da Nang.

When friends visit and ask me where to base themselves, An Thuong is almost always my first recommendation if they have a normal to mid-range budget. A La Carte specifically - I point people there because the rooftop pool view is something they will not get anywhere else at that price point, and the walk to the restaurant strip is 2 minutes. Those two things together make a significant difference to the daily experience of a stay.

The one caveat I always give: if you are a light sleeper and you book a room facing An Thuong 2, you may notice bar noise on Friday and Saturday nights. Request a room on the calmer street side or on An Thuong 3-4. That small adjustment eliminates the only real noise issue in the neighborhood.

Noise Reality: What to Expect

An Thuong is not a noisy neighborhood by Vietnam standards. On weeknights it is genuinely quiet by 10-11pm. The morning beach road activity (vendors, motorbikes, beach rental operations) starts around 6-7am and is more active than the evenings - if you are a late sleeper who wants total silence until 9am, a room on the inland side of the hotel will help.

The exception is An Thuong 2 on Friday and Saturday nights. There are bars on this street that generate noise until midnight or later. Hotels that face this street directly will experience this. Hotels on An Thuong 3, 4, or 5 - and rooms on the upper floors or inland-facing sides of An Thuong 2-adjacent hotels - are noticeably quieter.

Booking tip for light sleepers: When booking any An Thuong hotel, contact the property and ask for a room away from An Thuong 2 (the bar strip end). Most hotels will accommodate this without issue. It is the single most useful room selection note in this neighborhood.

Beach vendor activity on My Khe in the mornings is audible from the beachside rooms of An Thuong hotels. This settles by 9am and is not a problem during the day. Motorcycles on Vo Nguyen Giap (the beach road) are constant but not unusually loud by Vietnamese city standards.

Common Questions

An Thuong Da Nang: FAQ

What is the An Thuong neighborhood in Da Nang?+

An Thuong is a compact grid of low-rise streets between the beach road (Vo Nguyen Giap) and 2/9 Street, roughly one block from My Khe Beach. An Thuong 1-5 streets run perpendicular to the beach and contain Da Nang's best restaurant strip, boutique hotels, cafes, and bars. Locals call it "foreigner street" due to the high concentration of Western-oriented businesses. It is Da Nang's best base for independent travelers who want beach proximity and walkable food.

Is An Thuong close to the beach?+

Yes. An Thuong is 50-100 meters from the beach road (Vo Nguyen Giap) and a 3-5 minute walk from My Khe Beach itself. Hotels on the beach-facing end of An Thuong streets have the closest access. It is not beachfront - you are not looking at the ocean from your room unless you are on a high floor - but you can walk to the beach in under 5 minutes from any An Thuong hotel. See the My Khe beach hotel guide for full beach comparisons.

Are there good restaurants in An Thuong?+

An Thuong has the best restaurant strip in Da Nang for independent travelers. An Thuong 1-5 streets have 50+ restaurants covering Vietnamese, seafood, Thai, Japanese, Indian, Western cafes, and bars. The food is genuinely good, prices are reasonable, and it is where local residents eat regularly - not just tourists. An Thuong 3 and 4 are the strongest streets for restaurants. An Thuong 2 skews more toward bars. See the dining guide for broader Da Nang restaurant coverage.

What is the best boutique hotel in An Thuong?+

A La Carte Da Nang Beach is the best overall boutique hotel in the An Thuong area. Rooftop infinity pool, ocean views, 5-minute walk to the beach, and right in the restaurant zone. Azura Da Nang is a strong second choice at a slightly lower price. Both give you the boutique experience with pool access at rates significantly below the large beachfront resorts. See the boutique hotels guide for a broader selection.

Is An Thuong safe?+

Yes. An Thuong is one of Da Nang's safer neighborhoods. It is a mixed residential and tourism area with consistent foot traffic in the evenings. The bar strip on An Thuong 2 gets lively on weekends - more people out, not a safety concern. Standard travel precautions apply: watch your belongings in crowded areas, do not leave valuables visible in vehicles. Da Nang has a low crime rate overall for a city of its size.
