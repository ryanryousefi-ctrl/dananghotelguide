The only Da Nang hotels with genuine lazy river facilities: a short list, but a good one.

By Ryan Yousefi · Updated May 2026

Lazy River vs Waterpark Hotels Ranked Seasonal Note FAQ

Lazy River vs Waterpark: The Honest Distinction

People searching for "Da Nang hotels with lazy river" often mean different things. Here's the actual landscape:

True lazy river: Hyatt Regency Da Nang is the only hotel with a genuine lazy river - a 120-metre continuous floating channel. Nothing else in Da Nang matches this specific facility.

Full waterpark: Sheraton Grand and Mikazuki have waterparks with slides, multiple pools, and dedicated children's areas. These are more entertainment-focused than the lazy river concept.

Large pool complex: Furama has a lagoon pool with a swim-through channel (similar lazy vibe, not a circuit). Pullman has a large main pool with beach access - good for adults who want pool time without the waterpark crowd.

Most hotels with serious water facilities are on Non Nuoc Beach, which is 20 minutes south of Da Nang city. If city access matters, factor in the location trade-off.

Hotels Ranked by Water Facilities

01
LAZY RIVER
Hyatt Regency Da Nang Resort & Spa
Non Nuoc Beach · 120m lazy river · 6 pools · World of Hyatt

The only true lazy river in Da Nang. The 120-metre floating channel at Hyatt Regency is well-maintained, runs alongside the beachfront pool complex, and is suitable for adults and older children.

Beyond the lazy river there are 6 pools total, including a main beachfront pool and children's pool. This is the best overall water experience at any Da Nang hotel.

Camp Hyatt makes it the clear top choice for families who want both a structured kids programme and serious pool facilities.

Book Hyatt Regency →
02
WATERPARK
Sheraton Grand Da Nang Resort
Non Nuoc Beach · Largest waterpark in Da Nang · Multiple slides

Sheraton Grand has the largest waterpark complex of any Da Nang hotel. Multiple slides, a large children's pool, a separate adults pool, and an overall aquatic area that keeps families entertained for a full day.

It's more slides-and-splash focused than the relaxed floating concept of Hyatt's lazy river - these are distinct types of experiences. For families who want waterpark entertainment rather than lazy river relaxation, Sheraton Grand delivers more.

The resort is also notably cheaper than Hyatt Regency, making it the best value waterpark option.

Book Sheraton Grand →
03
WATERPARK
Mikazuki Japanese Resorts & Spa Da Nang
Japanese-themed water complex · Indoor heated pool · Slides

Mikazuki has a Japanese-themed water complex with outdoor slides, multiple pools, and importantly an indoor heated pool - useful for the Da Nang cool season (November-March) when outdoor facilities are less appealing. The Japanese onsen concept also gives adults something separate from the children's waterpark areas.

Popular with East Asian families. Lower price than Hyatt or Sheraton.

For families who want indoor heated pool capability alongside slides, Mikazuki is the only option in Da Nang that covers this.

Book Mikazuki →
04
LAGOON POOL
Furama Resort Danang
My Khe Beach · Large lagoon pool · Swim-through channel

Furama's lagoon pool is not a lazy river - there's no motorised current and no circuit - but it has a swim-through channel section and is large enough to cover ground. For adults wanting a relaxed pool environment without the waterpark activity level, Furama's lagoon is beautiful and well-maintained.

The mature tropical gardens surrounding it add atmosphere that newer resorts lack. My Khe Beach position gives easier city access.

A good choice for adults who want pool relaxation alongside beach access.

Book Furama Resort →
05
LARGE POOL
Pullman Da Nang Beach Resort
My Khe Beach · Large adults pool · No lazy river or slides

Pullman has a large main pool but no lazy river and no waterpark slides. It's included here because it offers an excellent adult pool experience in a My Khe Beach setting, with the An Thuong restaurant strip within walking distance.

For adults who want a quality pool stay without the waterpark environment - and who want city access - Pullman delivers. Families expecting waterpark facilities should choose Hyatt, Sheraton, or Mikazuki instead.

Seasonal Note

Best months for outdoor water facilities: April through September. Da Nang's outdoor pools and waterparks are at their best during the warm, dry season.

Some outdoor facilities at certain hotels close or reduce hours during October-March when temperatures drop (to 18-24°C) and conditions are cooler.

Mikazuki's indoor heated pool is the exception - it operates year-round regardless of weather. All other facilities listed are primarily outdoor and best used May-September.

If you're visiting in the cooler months (November-February), verify with the hotel which water facilities will be operational during your stay.

Common Questions

Lazy River Hotels: FAQ

Which Da Nang hotel has the best waterpark?+

Sheraton Grand Da Nang Resort has the largest full waterpark of any Da Nang hotel, with multiple slides, children's pools, and a sprawling aquatic complex. Mikazuki Japanese Resorts has a Japanese-themed water complex with slides and an indoor heated pool.

Hyatt Regency has a 120m lazy river and 6 pools but is not a waterpark in the same sense.

Which lazy river is best for adults in Da Nang?+

Hyatt Regency Da Nang's 120m lazy river is the only true lazy river at any Da Nang hotel. It is well-maintained and suitable for adults and older children.

Furama's lagoon pool has a swim-through section that functions similarly for relaxed floating, and is set in beautiful tropical gardens.

Are waterpark facilities included in the room rate at Da Nang hotels?+

Yes - at Hyatt Regency, Sheraton Grand, Mikazuki, and Furama, pool and water facilities are included in the room rate. You do not pay extra to access the lazy river, waterpark slides, or pools.

Some premium facilities (cabanas, towel service) may have small charges but the core water facilities are included for hotel guests.
