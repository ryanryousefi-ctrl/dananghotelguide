Four Points by Sheraton Danang

4-star beachfrontMy Khe BeachFrom $80 a night

8.2 / 10 Editorial score

A four-star beach hotel that quietly out-cooks and out-drinks the five-stars on the same strip.

Nothing about the phrase "four-star beachfront hotel on My Khe" suggests one of the most talked-about breakfasts in Da Nang. But that is what is happening here, and it is the reason to book.

Guests who have also stayed at the Pullman and the Sheraton Grand say the breakfast at Four Points is better. One Pullman guest wrote it verbatim as a complaint about where they had chosen to stay instead. Take that for what it is worth, but the reputation is consistent enough to be worth knowing before you book.

Upstairs, the Horizon Bar runs a craft beer list of more than 100 varieties, heavy on locally brewed Vietnamese labels, alongside cocktails and bar food. That is not a normal amenity at this price point. For beer-focused travellers it is the main event.

Four Points sits in the honest-overperformer category. It does not try to be a resort, it knows exactly what it is, and it over-delivers in two specific areas no other hotel on My Khe Beach can match.

The Rooms

Comfortable, well-planned, forgettable

Spacious and modern, with large glass windows and a veranda facing the coastline. The Four Points Comfort Bed is a genuine brand strength and it gets praised consistently. Rooms run around 30 to 40 square metres, smaller than the resort competition, but well planned inside that footprint. Bathrooms are functional and maintained to a good standard.

The design is clean and contemporary without being distinctive. Upper-floor ocean-facing rooms give you real My Khe Beach views. Lower floors on the street side get road noise.

Which room would we choose? A high-floor ocean-facing room. The premium is modest and it solves the view and the noise question in one move. This is the version of the hotel worth paying for.

The Reason to Book It

The craft beer rooftop

The Horizon Bar is unusual for a hotel at this price point. More than 100 craft beers including locally brewed Vietnamese options, plus cocktails, wine, and bar food. "Biggest craft beer menu in Da Nang" turns up in multiple guest reviews.

The rooftop view holds up on its own merits. The beer list is the part that is genuinely the best in a Da Nang hotel, and no other beach hotel on My Khe is running anything close to it.

If that matters to you, it changes the calculation on where to stay in this bracket.

Beach, Pool & Location

Directly on the sand, twelve minutes from everything

Direct beachfront on My Khe gives veranda views of the coastline, with the sand a short walk from the building. The outdoor pool sits between the hotel and the beach. It is modest next to the five-star resort pools nearby, but clean and functional, and for a four-star at these rates that is the right trade. You are not paying resort prices for a resort-scale pool you may not use every day.

The airport is 7km out, roughly 12 minutes by Grab. Dragon Bridge is another 12 minutes. The My Khe restaurant strip is a 5-minute walk, with dozens of seafood grills and noodle shops in easy reach every night.

You are close enough to the city for easy exploring and directly on one of Da Nang's best urban beach stretches, without the premium the five-star resorts charge for the same fundamentals.

Food & Drink

Build your morning around breakfast

Breakfast is the standout. A genuinely wide spread across Vietnamese, Korean, Indian, and Japanese-leaning stations, with the live noodle station serving beef noodle soup as the specific highlight guests keep returning to. There is an omelette station, a fresh salad bar, pastries, and vegan and vegetarian options with Indian dishes built in rather than bolted on.

Do not skip it even if you are not usually a hotel-buffet person.

Dining beyond breakfast is limited. The restaurant runs a buffet format and you will be eating off-property most evenings, which given the neighbourhood is a feature rather than a problem. For dinner, walk the five minutes to the My Khe strip.

Who It Suits

Book it if, skip it if

Marriott Bonvoy members who want points-earning at a beach hotel without paying Sheraton Grand or Marriott Resort rates are the clearest fit. It is also a solid redemption option. Value-focused travellers who want direct My Khe access, a reliable four-star room, and a real breakfast without resort pricing do well here. Beer enthusiasts get a bonus nobody else on the strip offers.

Skip it if you want resort-scale kids' facilities, or if you are a couple after a quiet luxury escape. The five-star properties nearby serve both better. This hotel is built around reliability and value, not indulgence.

The Tradeoff

What you give up

Rooms lean business-hotel generic. Comfortable and well equipped, forgettable in character. The pool is modest compared to the five-star beachfront rivals on the same strip. Street noise reaches the lower floors on the road side, so request a high floor facing the water.

On-site dinner is limited to a buffet, so you are eating off-property most nights. And the immediate beach strip runs busier and more commercialised than the quieter southern stretches of My Khe.

Common questions

Is Four Points by Sheraton Danang worth staying at?

Where is it located?
