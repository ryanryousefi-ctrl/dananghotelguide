Da Nang Marriott Resort & Spa Review

RY
Ryan Yousefi
Da Nang Hotel Guide · Last updated March 2026 · ★★★★★ · From $170/night
BonvoyCouplesFamiliesBeachfront

The Da Nang Marriott has figured out something that most resorts this size get wrong — it delivers the full five-star resort experience without feeling like an airport terminal.

The Marriott Resort sits on Non Nuoc Beach with 200+ rooms and feels considerably more intimate than that room count suggests. The design uses low-rise villa buildings arranged through tropical gardens rather than a single tower block, which is the difference between a resort that works and one that processes guests.

I've stayed here twice — once in a garden-view room in low season and once in a beach villa in peak. The garden room was a surprise: quieter, more private, and better value than I'd expected. The beach villa was exceptional. The private pool looking directly at the South China Sea at 6am is the kind of experience that justifies the premium.

The operational quality is consistently high. Service at the Marriott is the best-executed of the Non Nuoc resorts — not warmer than the Hyatt (they're comparable) but more precise. Things happen when they're supposed to.

Location & Getting There

7 Truong Sa Street, Ngu Hanh Son District — Non Nuoc Beach, 13–14km from Da Nang city centre. Same resort cluster as the Hyatt and Sheraton Grand. Airport is 20–22 minutes. Hoi An is 30–35 minutes south. Marble Mountains are 12–15 minutes by Grab.

The Non Nuoc beach position means you're committed to resort life for most of your stay. City dining requires a Grab both ways (80,000–120,000 VND each direction) which adds up over a long stay. What you get in exchange: the best beach quality in Da Nang — wider, calmer, and less crowded than My Khe — and resort infrastructure that means you don't need to leave.

An Thuong restaurant district is 18–20 minutes north. Worth the trip for dinner two or three nights during a week-long stay. The beach bar and restaurants on property cover the rest adequately.

The Rooms

Room categories range from garden-view rooms through ocean-view to the beach villas with private pools. Standard garden-view rooms are underestimated — quieter than ocean-facing rooms, with direct access to the garden and pool corridor, and meaningfully cheaper. Ocean-view rooms on upper floors have proper sea views. The villa category is the product that sets the Marriott apart.

The beach villas have private gardens with plunge pools looking toward the ocean. Designed for couples and honeymooners, they deliver what they promise: genuine privacy, direct beach access, and a setting that makes other accommodation choices feel like settling. The cost is real ($400–700/night depending on season) but the experience justifies it for the right trip.

Standard rooms are 50–55sqm with proper Marriott quality: good beds, working blackout curtains, powerful AC, above-average bathroom fittings. The brand consistency that Bonvoy members rely on is present throughout. Rates from $170–250/night for ocean view rooms in shoulder season.

Pool, Beach & Facilities

The main pool runs along the beachfront — a proper resort pool designed for genuine swimming and relaxation rather than just Instagram photography. Pool attendants are present and attentive without being intrusive. Loungers are available into mid-morning even in peak season, which is a management discipline that not all Non Nuoc resorts maintain.

Non Nuoc Beach directly in front is the best beach access of any Da Nang resort. Wide, clean, maintained by the resort, lifeguarded. Waves increase from June but swimming is generally reliable until September. The Marriott's beach service is comprehensive — attendants, sunbeds, towels, and drinks service that comes to you.

Spa — Quan Spa — is a serious spa operation with extensive treatment menus and therapists who've been trained to a proper standard. Pre-booking is recommended in peak season. Kids' club is supervised and runs a proper activity programme rather than an unsupervised playroom.

Food & Drink

Four dining outlets and all four are better than the resort-restaurant average. Feast is the all-day dining restaurant and breakfast operation — the breakfast quality is among the best in the Non Nuoc cluster, with made-to-order stations and a Vietnamese morning food section that serves proper pho and banh cuon. Plan a slow morning around it.

Fireplace restaurant is the signature dinner venue — the Da Nang Marriott's version of a destination kitchen, with a menu that focuses on live-fire cooking and quality ingredients. Worth booking for at least one evening. Prices run $30–50pp, which is elevated for Da Nang but reasonable for the quality.

The beach bar and pool bar cover the daytime F&B needs effectively — cold drinks service comes to you rather than requiring return trips to a bar, which is the operational standard you'd expect and don't always get.

The beach villa with a private pool looking at the South China Sea — there's a version of a Da Nang trip that is entirely defined by that one decision.

The Good Stuff

  • Best villa-with-private-pool category on Non Nuoc Beach
  • Beach service and pool management consistently the best-run of the Non Nuoc cluster
  • Marriott Bonvoy — strong elite status benefits and Fireplace restaurant credit options
  • Non Nuoc Beach position — widest and calmest beach in Da Nang
  • Feast breakfast is genuinely excellent — proper made-to-order Vietnamese and international spread

Watch Out For

  • 13–14km from city — evening city dining requires Grab both ways
  • Villa category pricing is premium — standard rooms are the better value play
  • Peak season rates for ocean-view rooms approach InterContinental pricing without the IC's unique setting
  • Beach and pool can get busy in July–August peak

Who Should Stay Here

Marriott Bonvoy members with a points balance to redeem, couples looking for a villa-category property with genuine spa facilities, and anyone who wants a polished large resort with strong F&B.
