Da Nang Marriott Resort & Spa Review

RY
Ryan Yousefi
Da Nang Hotel Guide · Last updated March 2026 · ★★★★★ · From $170/night
BonvoyCouplesFamiliesBeachfront

The Marriott is the hotel I keep recommending to people who ask for 'the best reliable choice in Da Nang' — not the most dramatic, not the most distinctive, but the one that consistently doesn't let you down.

Non Nuoc Beach, neighbours with the Hyatt Regency. Same beautiful stretch of sand, same 12km distance from the city, similar rate range. So what makes the Marriott the Marriott?

For me, it's the operational consistency. I've stayed here three times across different seasons, and every time the check-in is smooth, the room is ready, the breakfast is exactly what it was last time, and the staff know how to handle requests without making them feel like requests. It doesn't surprise you — but it never disappoints you either, which is actually harder to pull off than it sounds.

If you're a Bonvoy member, this is almost certainly where you should be staying in Da Nang. The upgrade treatment at Platinum level is genuinely generous here — I've been moved from a standard room to a beach villa category on availability more than once. That changes the value calculation significantly.

Location & Getting There

Non Nuoc Beach is the better beach in Da Nang — wider, cleaner, quieter than My Khe — and the Marriott sits directly on it. You're 12km south of the city centre, which means taxi costs if you're going out for dinner (80,000–120,000 VND each way by Grab). The Marble Mountains are visible from the beach, reachable in 10 minutes. Hoi An is 30 minutes south. Airport runs 18–22 minutes.

The Ngu Hanh Son district around the resort has improved in recent years — there are more independent restaurants and cafés within a short Grab ride than there were a few years back, which reduces the resort-dependency slightly.

The Rooms

Rooms are reliable Marriott: well-executed, consistent, no bad surprises. Standard Deluxe runs around 50sqm, furnished in contemporary neutral tones. The Ocean View upgrade is worth it for stays of more than two nights. Beds are proper quality. Blackout curtains work. Bathrooms have soaking tub and rain shower in most categories. Balconies are good-sized on the sea-facing categories.

Mid-season rates start around $170/night. The gap to the Hyatt Regency next door is smaller than you'd expect — compare availability before committing to either.

Pool, Beach & Facilities

Three pools including a main infinity pool facing the beach. A quieter adult pool at the back provides escape when families fill the main pool in peak season. Beach attendant service is attentive — loungers, umbrellas, towel refresh without you having to ask. The beach itself is good: same Non Nuoc sand as the Hyatt, same quality beach service, slightly smaller frontage.

Food & Drink

Fireplace is the main restaurant and handles both Vietnamese and Western menus competently. Breakfast buffet is extensive and the weekend brunch draws both hotel guests and Da Nang locals — that's usually a good sign. The pool bar and beach bar serve food through the day at reasonable prices for a five-star property. The afternoon tea setup is popular and priced fairly for the quality.

The dependable choice at Non Nuoc. If you're a Bonvoy member it's straightforward. If you're not, compare with the Hyatt Regency next door before you commit — similar price, different facilities profile.

The Good Stuff

  • Consistent Bonvoy service — upgrades at Platinum are genuinely generous
  • Non Nuoc beach position is excellent
  • Breakfast quality is reliably high
  • Good value for Bonvoy members stacking points and benefits
  • Reliable without rough edges — everything just works

Worth Knowing

  • Rooms are well-executed but not particularly distinctive
  • Kids' club is seasonal and less comprehensive than the Hyatt's
  • Food quality is good but rarely memorable — go off-resort a few evenings
