Best Areas in Da Nang for Families

Where to stay for beaches, pools, easier days and fewer logistical headaches with kids.

Updated August 2026 · Independently selected · We went. We picked.

Editor's Top Pick

Sheraton Grand Danang Resort

Non Nuoc · Full-service family resort · Best overall

Families usually have one big decision: stay where you can walk out for dinner, or book a resort good enough that nobody wants to leave. Sheraton Grand is the second kind — and it delivers. The pool is genuinely large, the beach is direct, and the property has enough room that kids are not constantly underfoot of other guests.

The Non Nuoc location is quieter than central My Khe, which matters more than it sounds when you are managing small children. Grab gets you to An Thuong restaurants in 15 minutes when you need a change of scene. For a family trip where the resort itself is the vacation, this is the easy top pick.

Large pool Direct beach Non Nuoc Family facilities ~15 min Grab to city

A Weekend at Da Nang Mikazuki

Northwest Da Nang · Water park resort · Family weekend experience

Most families visit Mikazuki for a day. The smarter move is staying one or two nights. The water park is the attraction — slides, pools, wave areas — and having it outside your room door means two full days of it instead of a rushed afternoon. When the weather turns, Mikazuki also has indoor options, which makes it unusually rain-proof for Da Nang.

This is not a beach resort. It is a destination resort where the water park is the point. For families with kids who want to go full-throttle for a weekend, nothing in Da Nang competes with it.

Good to Know With Kids

Grab
Works well for most family trips. GrabCar seats four. Book GrabCar XL for larger groups or if you have a stroller.
Car Seats
Not standard in Grab or taxis. Bring a travel car seat if you have toddlers. Private car hire through hotels can sometimes arrange one — ask ahead.
Heat
Peak heat is 11am-3pm. Plan beach and outdoor time early. Resort pools are the smart option during the hottest hours.
Naps
Resort-heavy itineraries make nap logistics easier. Non Nuoc means no need to Grab back from the beach — the room is always close.
Food
My Khe and An Thuong have the easiest variety for families: Vietnamese, Western, Japanese, Korean, bakeries, supermarkets. Non Nuoc relies more on resort dining.
Rain
Mikazuki is the strongest wet-weather option. Indoor water park means rain doesn't end the day. Beach resorts are more exposed when weather turns.

Common Questions

Is Non Nuoc or My Khe better for families?
Non Nuoc for young kids — calmer water, resort infrastructure, no traffic crossing to reach the beach. My Khe for families with older kids and teens who want restaurants, walkability and city access. If your goal is a resort where nobody needs to leave, Non Nuoc. If you want to explore every day, My Khe.
What is the best family resort in Da Nang?
Sheraton Grand Danang Resort is our top overall pick. For a water-park-focused weekend, Da Nang Mikazuki is the most kid-specific experience in the city. Hyatt Regency Danang is the strongest pick for families wanting a full-service kids club with structured activities.
Is Mikazuki worth staying overnight?
Yes, for families with young kids. Staying one or two nights means two full days at the water park without the commute, plus evening access to pools and resort facilities. Works especially well as a weekend break within a longer Da Nang trip.
Is Da Nang safe for children to swim?
Generally yes, but conditions vary. Non Nuoc has calmer surf and is safer for young children. My Khe has stronger waves especially October to April — check the flag system before letting young kids in the water. Resort pools are always the safest option for toddlers.
Do Da Nang resorts have kids clubs?
Hyatt Regency has the strongest kids club (Camp Hyatt, ages 4-12). Sheraton Grand and Furama both have kids facilities. Mikazuki replaces a traditional kids club with a full water park. Most clubs operate 9am-5pm with age minimums around 4 years.
