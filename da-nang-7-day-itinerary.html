Da Nang in 7 Days

Enough time to actually slow down.

Updated August 2026 · By Ryan Yousefi

Is this enough time?

Best for slower travel, families, real beach time, an overnight in Hoi An, an optional Hue day trip, and actual downtime. Three to four days covers the headline attractions. The extra days buy the slower pleasures: a full morning on the beach with nowhere to be, an early ride to Son Tra before the heat builds, a long lunch that becomes dinner in Hoi An.

Editor's Pick

Slow Down

The Point of Seven Days

Do not turn seven days into seven excursions. Three to four days already covers Da Nang's headline sights. Use the extra time for beach, cafes, and food, not another attraction. A day with no schedule is not a wasted day.

Beach + cafes No back-to-back big days Real downtime

Signature Pick

Overnight in Hoi An

Day 5

With seven days, there's little reason to experience Hoi An only as a day trip. Hoi An's best qualities, the lantern-lit Ancient Town after dark and the streets before 8am, are only accessible with an overnight stay. One night adds the evening atmosphere; two allows An Bang Beach and a cooking class.

Day 1
Arrival + My Khe Orientation
Easy Arrival Beach
Arrival
Land at Da Nang Airport (DAD), buy a Viettel SIM at arrivals (150,000-200,000 VND), Grab to your hotel (60,000-90,000 VND to My Khe). Check in or drop luggage.
Morning
An Thuong street for breakfast, banh mi from a street cart (25,000-35,000 VND) and a ca phe sua da at any cafe on An Thuong 2 or 3.
Afternoon
Walk to My Khe Beach, 5 minutes from most An Thuong hotels. Check the flag colour before swimming. Sun loungers from your hotel or beach operators (30,000-50,000 VND). Deliberately light, acclimatisation, not sightseeing.
Evening
Walk An Thuong as the street comes alive at dusk. Dinner at a mid-range Vietnamese restaurant, try mi Quang, Da Nang's signature dish, if you haven't already. Walk the beachfront promenade after dinner. Early night, tomorrow is active.
Why Today Works

You've just arrived. The goal is acclimatisation, not sightseeing. One good meal, a beach walk, and an early night sets up the rest of the week properly.

Day 2
Marble Mountains + Non Nuoc Beach
Moderate Culture Beach
7:30 AM
Grab to Marble Mountains (80,000-110,000 VND from My Khe, 22 minutes). Arrive at opening.
Morning
Entrance ticket (40,000 VND) and elevator (15,000 VND). Route: elevator up, Linh Ung Pagoda, Huyen Khong Cave (morning light through the skylights is the main event), Vong Giang Dai summit viewpoint, Linh Nham Cave, walk down. Allow 3 hours.
Afternoon
Walk 500m east to Non Nuoc Beach, quieter than My Khe, long stretch of sand backed by casuarina trees. Swim, lunch at a beachside cafe (bun bo Hue or com ga, 60,000-100,000 VND), unhurried afternoon on the sand. Grab back to My Khe (80,000-110,000 VND) by 4-5pm.
Evening
Dragon Bridge evening walk, 10 minutes by Grab from My Khe (50,000-70,000 VND). Walk the Han River promenade, lit beautifully after dark. Dinner riverside. Saturday or Sunday: stay for the fire and water show at 9pm, free, from the bridge banks.
Why Today Works

Culture in the morning while it's cool, beach in the afternoon once the heat sets in, river walk in the evening once it cools again. The day follows the temperature, not the other way around.

Day 3
Ba Na Hills, Full Day
Big Day Out Early Start Full Day
7:00 AM
Leave your hotel. Grab to Ba Na Hills, 35-45 minutes (250,000-420,000 VND one way).
Morning
Tickets at the gate (750,000-1,100,000 VND depending on age and season). Board the cable car early, queues are minimal before 9am and the views to the forest canopy and coast are exceptional in the morning clarity. Head directly to the Golden Bridge before other visitors arrive.
Afternoon
After the Golden Bridge, explore at your own pace, Fantasy Park rides, the French Village, Linh Ung Pagoda on the summit, the gardens. Lunch on site (overpriced but expect it, 150,000-300,000 VND per person, acceptable quality). The afternoon is when crowds build at ride attractions, use this time for the quieter temple and garden areas.
Evening
Leave by 5-6pm, cable car queues build near closing, factor this in. Grab back to Da Nang. Low-effort dinner, a local pho shop or Grab Food delivery is entirely acceptable after a full Ba Na Hills day.
Why Today Works

Ba Na Hills is a big day out. Going early and hitting the Golden Bridge first is the single biggest quality difference between a good visit and a crowded one.

⚠️
Sequencing Note

Days 3 and 5 (Ba Na Hills and Hoi An) are both high-activity, high-spend days. If your schedule is flexible, avoid placing them back-to-back, Day 4 as a recovery day between them works well.

Day 4
Son Tra Peninsula + Seafood Dinner
Easy Nature Motorbike Seafood
7:00 AM
Rent a motorbike from your hotel or a nearby hire shop (120,000-150,000 VND/day). Leave by 7am, the 15-minute ride from My Khe to the base of the peninsula is straightforward.
Morning
The summit road through protected forest rises 693m above sea level, ocean views both sides. Stop at the Lady Buddha pagoda (free entry, 67m statue overlooking the bay). Allow 3 hours for the full circuit at a relaxed pace.
Afternoon
Back to My Khe for a long beach afternoon, this is the relaxation day. Swim, read, rent a sun lounger, work through the Vietnamese coffee menu at a beachside cafe. If you want more activity, Bai But beach on the south side of Son Tra is quieter than My Khe with good snorkelling.
Evening
Da Nang seafood dinner, the highlight meal of the week. Head to a local seafood restaurant away from the tourist strip, the Bac My An area south of My Khe has excellent options. Order grilled tiger prawns, steamed whole crab, garlic clams, morning glory with garlic. Budget 300,000-600,000 VND per person.
Why Today Works

No agenda beyond recovery from Ba Na Hills and one genuinely great meal. This is what the extra days of a 7-day trip are for.

🦐
Worth Knowing

This is what fresh South China Sea seafood actually tastes like, meaningfully better than tourist-strip equivalents at three times the price.

Day 5
Hoi An Old Town, Full Day
Full Day UNESCO Food Overnight Option
7:30 AM
Grab to Hoi An Ancient Town, 40 minutes, 220,000-300,000 VND. Arrive by 8-8:30am, before the main tour groups.
Morning
Ancient Town ticket (120,000 VND, covers 5 heritage sites). Walk the old streets while quiet: Tran Phu, Nguyen Thai Hoc, the Japanese Covered Bridge. Breakfast at a riverside cafe, banh mi Phuong is 2 minutes from the Ancient Town and widely considered the best banh mi in the world (40,000-60,000 VND).
Afternoon
Covered market, tailor shops (allow 2-3 days for tailored clothing, not practical on a day trip), Thu Bon riverside. Lunch: Cao Lau (100,000-180,000 VND) and White Rose dumplings (70,000-120,000 VND), unique to Hoi An. By 2-3pm streets peak in crowd density, move to An Bang Beach (4km, 60,000 VND Grab) for respite if needed.
Evening
Stay for the evening if you can. Hoi An after dark with paper lanterns lit over the Thu Bon River is genuinely beautiful and worth the late return. Dinner riverside (200,000-400,000 VND per person). Grab back to Da Nang by 9:30-10pm, or book one night in Hoi An and return Day 6 morning, the overnight adds the dawn Ancient Town experience, the best version of the place.
Why Today Works

This is the signature day of a 7-day trip. Nowhere else on this itinerary rewards an overnight stay as much as Hoi An does.

Day 6
Relaxation Day: Cafe, Spa, Beach
Low Pace Spa Beach
Morning
No agenda before 9am. Late breakfast at a cafe, 43 Factory Coffee Roaster or Cong Caphe on An Thuong. Order the filter coffee, read, watch the street. Then walk or Grab to My Khe for a beach morning. This is the day with no schedule, resist the urge to add another attraction.
Afternoon
Spa afternoon. Da Nang has excellent massage and spa options at prices well below Western equivalents, a 90-minute traditional Vietnamese massage at a reputable spa runs 250,000-450,000 VND. Book in advance for the better properties. Afterward, back to the beach or a slow walk through the Cham Museum (60,000 VND) if you haven't been, one of Vietnam's most important and most undervisited museums.
Evening
Dinner at a rooftop restaurant or one of the better An Thuong international options, the meal for something slightly more upscale than the rest of the week. Budget 300,000-600,000 VND per person. Nightcap at a rooftop bar overlooking the beach or city. Early night if departure is tomorrow.
Why Today Works

This is the day the extra time is for. Resist the urge to add another attraction, that's the entire point of having it.

Day 7
Flexible Buffer: Hue or Final Beach Day
Flexible Hue Option Departure
Option A: Hue
Leave by 7am. Private car via the Hai Van Pass (100km, 2 hours scenic) or tunnel (1h 40min faster). In Hue: Imperial Citadel (150,000 VND entrance), Thien Mu Pagoda (free), riverside lunch (bun bo Hue, the dish's origin city). Return by 7pm via tunnel. Cost: 800,000-1,200,000 VND for a private car, shared tours from 350,000 VND per person.
Option B: Final Beach
If Day 7 is departure day, use the morning for a final My Khe swim, a slow An Thuong breakfast, and last-minute shopping at Han Market. Han Market has the best concentration of local produce, Vietnamese coffee beans, lacquerware, and silk at reasonable pre-negotiation prices. Build in 30 minutes extra for airport queues during peak season.
Departure
Da Nang Airport (DAD) is 5km from My Khe, 12 minutes by Grab (60,000-90,000 VND). International check-in opens 3 hours before departure, domestic 2 hours. T1 is domestic, T2 is international, confirm your terminal on your boarding pass.
Why Today Works

Genuinely flexible by design. Take the Hue option if historical sites interest you and you have the energy for one more early start. Otherwise, a slow final morning is exactly what a 7-day trip should end with.

Where to base yourself

Best Areas for 7 Days

My Khe Beach (An Thuong area) is the optimal base for all 7 nights. It places you on the beach, within walking distance of the best cafe and restaurant strip, and within easy Grab reach of the airport and every attraction on this itinerary. Splitting between Da Nang and one Hoi An night (Day 5) adds the overnight Ancient Town experience without complicating logistics.

For luxury travellers, the Son Tra Peninsula properties, the InterContinental Sun Peninsula in particular, offer a qualitatively different experience: more secluded, more resort-like, at the cost of a slightly longer Grab to everything. Worth considering if the resort experience matters more than convenience.

If it rains

Rainy Season Alternative

Restructure the week around indoor-friendly and rain-resilient activities. The cultural attractions work well in any weather.

Days 1-2
Da Nang cultural base: Cham Museum, Marble Mountains (caves are fine in rain), Dragon Bridge, An Thuong cafe culture. Beach if conditions allow.
Day 3
Ba Na Hills: the French Village and indoor attractions work well in overcast or light rain, mist on the mountain can be atmospheric. Avoid if heavy rain or thunderstorms are forecast.
Days 4-5
Hoi An, 2-night stay: the ancient town streets, cooking classes, and tailor visits are all weather-independent. An Bang Beach is less of a draw, spend more time on food, heritage, and shopping.
Day 6
Hue day trip: the Imperial Citadel and pagodas are excellent in any weather. The Hai Van Pass route is less scenic in heavy cloud but still worth it in light rain.
Day 7
Flex or departure: cooking class, market shopping, or a final beach morning if conditions have improved.
Common questions

7-Day Itinerary FAQ

Is 7 days too long in Da Nang?

Not at all. Three to four days covers the main attractions. The remaining days allow for Hoi An overnight, a Son Tra morning, and optionally a Hue day trip, without a 3-night sprint through the highlights. Under 5 days, the itinerary compresses significantly.

Can you do Hue in one day from Da Nang?

Yes, with an early start. Hue is 100km north, about 2 hours by private car via the Hai Van Pass, or 1h 40min via tunnel. Covers the Citadel, Thien Mu Pagoda, and lunch. Leave by 7am, return by 7-8pm. Private car costs 800,000-1,200,000 VND.

Is Hoi An worth staying overnight?

Yes, if your schedule allows. The lantern-lit Ancient Town after dark and the streets before 8am are only accessible with an overnight stay. One night is the minimum for the evening atmosphere. If all 7 nights are already booked in Da Nang, Hoi An still works as a day trip.

What is the best time of year for this 7-day itinerary?

March to May: warm, sunny, low-to-medium crowds, hotel prices near annual minimums. June to August: best beach weather, higher temperatures and peak crowds. December to February: dry, cooler, good for sightseeing. Avoid October and November, the rainy season affects several outdoor days.

Is Da Nang expensive for a week?

Moderately affordable. A mid-range 7-night trip (4-star My Khe hotel, mix of local and tourist restaurants, Grab transport, main activities) runs approximately $840-1,210 per person excluding flights. Budget travellers can do it for $500-750. Luxury travellers should budget $1,560-3,000. Ba Na Hills tickets are the single biggest line item.
