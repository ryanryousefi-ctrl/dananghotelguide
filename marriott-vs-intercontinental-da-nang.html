Marriott vs InterContinental Da Nang

The big Marriott on Non Nuoc Beach versus the InterContinental on Son Tra Peninsula. Two different versions of Da Nang luxury.

InterContinental if budget is not the main constraint. Marriott if you are travelling with family.
Overview Location Rooms Amenities Best For Verdict

Hotel Overview

Da Nang Marriott Resort & Spa

  • ★★★★★ Large luxury resort
  • 200 rooms and villas
  • Non Nuoc Beach, south My Khe
  • Infinity pool, private beach
  • Marriott Bonvoy loyalty
  • From ~$150/night

InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort

  • ★★★★★ IHG luxury resort
  • 197 rooms and villas
  • Bai Bac, Son Tra Peninsula
  • La Maison 1888 (MICHELIN Star)
  • IHG One Rewards loyalty
  • From ~$200/night

These are the two properties that compete for the "most impressive Da Nang hotel" headline, and they genuinely are competing for different customers. The Marriott is a large conventional luxury resort on Non Nuoc Beach with everything in place: villas, multiple pools, a spa, and easy beach access. [VERIFY: exact room and villa count]

The InterContinental is architecturally different and sits on Son Tra Peninsula, 20 minutes from the main beach strip. The drama of the setting is real. La Maison 1888 is a legitimate MICHELIN-starred restaurant in the building. If money is not the main decision factor and you want the more memorable hotel, it's the InterContinental. The InterContinental's isolation is both its main selling point and its only real drawback. You are on a headland, and getting anywhere requires a taxi or the hotel shuttle. If you want to walk to dinner or explore the city, you are better placed at the Marriott on Non Nuoc. If the point of the trip is the resort itself, the isolation is fine.

The InterContinental's hillside layout means the resort is spread vertically. There is a funicular. Guests with mobility issues or small children should factor this in. The Marriott's flat layout is more conventional and easier to navigate.

La Maison 1888 is the single best restaurant attached to any hotel in Da Nang. If a MICHELIN dinner is on your itinerary, this is where you're staying. The Marriott's dining is solid but not distinctive. The Marriott wins on family infrastructure by a significant margin.

Who Each Hotel Is Best For

Book Marriott Da Nang if…

  • Families with children (kids club, flat layout)
  • Marriott Bonvoy members
  • Guests wanting Non Nuoc Beach's long surf beach
  • Those who want to day-trip to Hoi An and Marble Mountains (closer)
  • Travellers who value easier city access

Book InterContinental if…

  • Couples or adults-only trips
  • Special occasions where the setting matters
  • Guests who want La Maison 1888 on the doorstep
  • Those who want genuine seclusion, not a resort zone
  • IHG One Rewards members at the top tier

Final Verdict

The InterContinental is one of the best resort experiences in Southeast Asia. That sentence is not marketing copy, it is a genuine position. The architecture, the setting, La Maison 1888, the cliff-edge pool — it is doing things the Marriott is not.

The Marriott is the better family hotel. The layout is easier with children, the kids club is real, and the price difference can be used on Hoi An day trips and dinners at An Thuong.

If budget is flexible and you are travelling as a couple or adult group, the InterContinental is the correct choice. It is the one hotel in Da Nang that guests specifically come back to book again.

Common Questions

Marriott Da Nang vs InterContinental: FAQ

Is the InterContinental Da Nang worth the extra cost over Marriott?+

For couples and adults: yes. The setting on Son Tra Peninsula is genuinely dramatic, La Maison 1888 is one of Vietnam's best restaurants, and the cliff-edge pool is exceptional. For families with young children: the Marriott's flat layout, kids club, and Non Nuoc Beach location makes more practical sense.

Is the InterContinental Da Nang hard to get to?+

It is about 20 minutes from the airport and city centre by taxi. The Peninsula road is winding. The hotel runs a complimentary city transfer for dinner guests at La Maison 1888. It is not inconvenient if you are staying in the resort, but if you plan to go out every evening the location will cost you in taxi time.
