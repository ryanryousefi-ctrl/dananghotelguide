Editor's top pick — Luxury

Four Seasons Resort The Nam Hai

The finest beach resort near Hoi An

100 pool villas on a private stretch of Ha My Beach, 10km north of the Ancient Town. Three pools, two restaurants with a quality standard the town itself can't match, and service that's consistent in a way that takes years to build. Not cheap. Worth it if a beach resort is what you're actually here for. For Old Town access, you take a 20-minute private transfer or taxi — treat it as a day trip.

Ultra-luxury — 10km south

Hoiana — the resort enclave

Hoiana is not inside Hoi An. It's a purpose-built luxury resort destination 10km south on a 1km stretch of private beach, with an 18-hole Robert Trent Jones Jr. golf course. Rosewood and Hoiana Hotel & Suites are the two properties. Both share the beach and golf. Neither is a budget option. Both are genuinely excellent.

Ultra-luxury · Premier property

Rosewood Hoi An

120 rooms and residences, multiple restaurants, three pools, and Rosewood's signature service delivery. The golf course adjoinment and beach proximity make this a full-stay destination rather than a base for exploration. Rates start around $400 and reflect where you are.

Luxury · Second Hoiana property

Hoiana Hotel & Suites

Shares the beach and golf course access with Rosewood but at 30–40% lower rates. Suite-format rooms with pool or ocean views. The right choice if you want the Hoiana experience without the Rosewood price tag. Both are well above the standard of anything inside Hoi An town.

Common questions

Best hotels in Hoi An — FAQ

What is the best hotel in Hoi An?
Four Seasons Resort The Nam Hai is the best beach resort. For Old Town and riverside, Anantara Hoi An Resort is the consistently excellent choice. Best value is Hoi An Chic Hotel. At the ultra-luxury end, Rosewood Hoi An at Hoiana has no real rival in Quang Nam province.
What is the best area to stay in Hoi An?
The riverside area is the best overall base for most visitors: walkable to Old Town, quieter at night, better pools and gardens than staying inside the Ancient Town itself. An Bang Beach is better for beach-focused trips. Hoiana is for luxury resort guests who will use the Old Town as a day trip.
Is Four Seasons Hoi An worth the price?
Yes, if you are staying 3+ nights and intend to use the resort. Private beach, three pools, 100 villas each with its own pool, and food and beverage quality that is well above what you find in the town. For a 1-night stay on a big budget, Rosewood at Hoiana offers a similar level with more on-site activities.
What are the best budget hotels in Hoi An?
Hoi An Chic Hotel offers the best budget-to-quality ratio near the Ancient Town from around $45/night. Phu Thinh Boutique and Little Hoi An are consistent in the $40–55 range. For the An Bang beach area, The Linh An Bang Beach is the best budget option. All have pools.
Should I stay at Rosewood or Four Seasons in Hoi An?
If golf is important or you want a more active resort with more on-site options, Rosewood Hoi An at Hoiana. If private beach and pool-villa quality is the priority, Four Seasons Nam Hai. Both are around $400–700/night and deliver at that level. Hoiana is 10km south of Old Town; Four Seasons is 10km north — factor in which direction suits your itinerary.
