Best Hotels in Hoi An
(Curated 2026 Guide)

Hoi An is one of the most photogenic towns in Southeast Asia and one of the easiest to overpay in. This is a curated selection — not a paid ranking.

Every property here has been walked, assessed, and chosen on merit: position relative to the Ancient Town, pool and room quality, and genuine value within its category. The guide covers the full spectrum, from the UNESCO lantern-lit guesthouses to the sprawling beach resorts on An Bang, 4km outside town.

Luxury Resorts near Hoi An

3 properties

The top-end Hoi An properties sit along Ha My and Cua Dai beaches, north and south of the Ancient Town respectively. Distance from the Old Town is 8–15km — not walkable — but every luxury property runs shuttle services and the journey by private transfer is scenic.

The Four Seasons Nam Hai is the unambiguous benchmark for central Vietnam; the Anantara sits closer to town and earns its rate with a genuinely beautiful riverside position.

Good to Know — Riverside Hotels

The riverside zone roughly spans 1.5km from the Ancient Town entrance south to Cam Nam Bridge. Properties closer to the entrance (Anantara, Historic Hotel) have the strongest Old Town walkability. Properties further south (Silk Marina, Bellerive, Soluna) are quieter at night but require a 15–20 minute walk.

Pool access matters in summer. Hotels with river views often have smaller pools or pools positioned away from the river. Ask the hotel specifically: "Is the pool visible from the river side?" before booking based on photos.

An Bang Beach Hotels

6 properties

An Bang Beach sits 4km northeast of the Ancient Town — close enough for easy access, far enough to feel genuinely separate. The beach itself is significantly better than Cua Dai: wider, cleaner, and with a restaurant strip that has become one of the best stretches of coast in central Vietnam. Most properties here are smaller and more informal than the Da Nang resort belt, which is the point.

An Bang Beach Practical Notes

The beach clubs at An Bang (Soul Kitchen, La Plage, An Bang Beach Shack) are open to non-guests and are generally better than hotel restaurants for lunch and afternoon drinks. Renting sun loungers and walking to a beach club from your hotel is standard practice here.

Motorbike rental to the Ancient Town runs 100,000–150,000 VND/day. Grab bikes cost 40,000–80,000 VND each way. Factor this in if you're planning multiple evening trips into the old quarter.

Where to Stay in Hoi An

The question isn't just which hotel, it's which part of Hoi An. Here's how each area plays out in practice.

Ancient Town & Riverside
Most walkable · No pool

The narrow lanes of the UNESCO-listed Ancient Town are pedestrian-only at certain hours. Staying here or in the immediate riverside zone means you walk to everything — the tailor shops, the lantern market, the best restaurants.

The trade-off is typically no pool and smaller rooms for the money.

Walk to Ancient Town2–10 min
Typical room rate$35–$180
Best forFirst-timers, short stays
An Bang Beach
Best beach · Easy Grab to town

An Bang is 4km from the old town — five minutes by Grab, 15–20 minutes by bicycle. The beach itself is the best near Hoi An: wide, largely un-commercialised, and backed by a run of excellent seafood restaurants.

The right choice if you want a genuine beach holiday with Hoi An day trips.

Distance to Ancient Town4 km
Typical room rate$55–$140
Best forBeach lovers, longer stays
Resort Zone (Ha My / Dien Duong)
Luxury isolation · Shuttle dependent

The Four Seasons Nam Hai and a handful of resort properties sit on Ha My Beach north of An Bang, 10–15km from the Ancient Town. You are here for the resort experience and the beach — not for proximity to the old town. Shuttles are provided but the dependency is real.

Distance to Ancient Town10–15 km
Typical room rate$180–$900
Best forLuxury, honeymoon, families
Hoiana Resort Zone
Integrated resort · Casino · Golf

Hoiana is 10km south of the Ancient Town, 20 minutes by Grab. A separate destination rather than a Hoi An base: casino, three golf courses (Robert Trent Jones Jr.), private beach. Rosewood is the standout property; Hoiana Hotel & Suites is the accessible option.

Da Nang vs Hoi An

Hoi An is 30 minutes south of Da Nang Airport. Most visitors fly into Da Nang and transfer to Hoi An by taxi ($12–15).

Staying in Da Nang gives you beach access; staying in Hoi An gives you the Ancient Town on your doorstep.

Frequently Asked

Hoi An Hotels FAQ

Is it better to stay inside the Ancient Town or outside?

For most first-time visitors, staying within a 10-minute walk of the Ancient Town gives you the best experience — you can walk to the lantern-lit streets at night and reach tailors without a taxi. The trade-off is no pool and higher prices per room quality.

If you are coming for the beach, An Bang (4km away) makes more sense. The riverside boutique strip is the sweet spot for most: rooftop pools, greenery, and walkability.

How much do hotels cost in Hoi An?

Budget guesthouses inside the Ancient Town run $20–45/night. Well-run boutique hotels on the riverside start around $55–90.

Mid-range resort properties with pools are $90–160. The Four Seasons Nam Hai sits at $450–900 in peak season.

Unlike Da Nang, beach properties aren't dramatically more expensive than inland — the premium is really about pool quality and room size.

When is the best time to visit Hoi An?

February through May is broadly the best window — the northeast monsoon has passed, temperatures are mild (24–28°C), and the post-Tet crowds have thinned. June through August is hot and humid but reliably sunny; this is peak beach season for An Bang.

October is statistically the wettest month and flooding in the Ancient Town is a genuine risk in October and November. December and January are cooler and can have overcast stretches, but are often pleasant.

Can you walk from Hoi An hotels to the Ancient Town?

Most riverside and central boutique hotels are genuinely walkable to the Ancient Town entrance — typically 5–15 minutes on foot. The Ancient Town itself is pedestrian-only during certain hours.

Beach hotels at An Bang or Cua Dai are 4–6km from the old town; most resorts provide a shuttle and Grab is cheap and available.
