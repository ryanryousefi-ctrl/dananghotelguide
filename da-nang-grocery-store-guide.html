Da Nang Grocery Store Guide

Where to buy everything from fresh seafood and Vietnamese staples to imported cheese, snacks and bulk household supplies.

Six stores. Three clear winners.

Editor's Pick
Editor's Pick
Lotte Mart
Best overall

Lotte Mart is where we'd send a first-time visitor who wants to make one grocery trip and be done. Exceptional fresh counters, a real butcher, live seafood, a proper bakery, a wide imported section, and every Vietnamese staple you need. The food court matters: you can shop, pick up imported goods, get fresh meat and seafood, then eat lunch without leaving the building.

If you only visit one supermarket in Da Nang, make it Lotte Mart.
MegaMart is cheaper. Winmart is often more convenient. Neither offers quite the same all-around experience.
Da Nang Signature Pick
Winmart
The everyday supermarket

Winmart is the store you're most likely to actually use regularly. Multiple locations, excellent Vietnamese staples, meat, seafood, dairy, produce, household goods, and reasonable prices with a solid imported selection on top. The Vincom Plaza branch is the standout, covering more categories and SKUs than most travelers expect.

For normal week-to-week shopping, Winmart just works.
Imported specialty products are better elsewhere, and smaller Winmart locations aren't as comprehensive.
Value Pick
MegaMart
The Costco of Da Nang

Huge warehouse, enormous selection, bulk goods across groceries, produce, meat, seafood, imported products, household supplies, and cases of drinks. Strong unit pricing throughout. This is where you go when you need everything, and a lot of it. Best for families, apartments, villas, long stays, and expats doing a big weekly shop.

Common Questions

Where do expats shop for groceries in Da Nang?
Most long-term expats use two or three stores depending on their needs. Jollymart is the default for hard-to-find imported goods. Moonmilk handles fresh fruit reliably. Winmart, particularly the Vincom location, works for everyday Vietnamese staples. For bulk buying, MegaMart is the Costco equivalent. Lotte Mart is many expats' one-stop shop when they want fresh food, imported items, and a food court meal in a single trip.
Can I find Western or imported food in Da Nang?
Yes, with varying selection by store. Jollymart has the best curated range of imported goods. Lotte Mart carries a reasonable import section. MegaMart has good bulk imported staples. Full Market and Winmart have very limited imported ranges. Selection is narrower than Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi, specific brands may not be available.
Is there a grocery store near the beach hotels in Da Nang?
Yes, Full Market operates multiple locations and is the most convenient option for beach-area hotel guests needing snacks or basic supplies. Winmart has locations accessible from the main beach corridor. Lotte Mart is a 15–25 minute Grab ride from the main beach strip.
What is the best grocery store in Da Nang for fresh produce?
Moonmilk has the best fresh fruit and vegetable selection, well-maintained with good turnover. Lotte Mart's fresh section is the most complete if you want fresh produce, meat, and seafood in one location. Traditional local markets remain the best option for ultra-fresh, competitively priced produce.
Do Da Nang grocery stores accept credit cards?
Lotte Mart, Winmart (larger locations), MegaMart, and Jollymart all accept Visa and Mastercard. Full Market locations are typically cash only or accept Vietnamese QR payment. Moonmilk varies by location. Carry sufficient VND cash as a backup.
