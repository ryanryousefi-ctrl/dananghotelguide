Marriott Resort vs Sheraton Grand Da Nang

Same stretch of Non Nuoc Beach, same Marriott Bonvoy program, very different resorts. One is built for kids; the other is built for quiet.

Non Nuoc Beach

Why This One's a Split Decision

These two sit close enough on Non Nuoc Beach that location breaks even. The Sheraton is the largest hotel in Da Nang at 785 rooms with a genuine waterpark; the Marriott is smaller, quieter, and more spa-focused. Traveling with kids, book the Sheraton. Traveling as a couple, book the Marriott.

The 30-Second Verdict

Who each hotel is actually built for

Da Nang Marriott Resort & Spa
Best for: couples, spa-focused trips, guests who want a calmer version of Non Nuoc Beach.
Wins
  • Stronger, more relaxed spa programme
  • Quieter pool areas and public spaces
  • Well-reviewed breakfast
Tradeoff
  • No dedicated water attraction for kids
  • Fewer dining outlets on-site
Sheraton Grand Danang Resort
Best for: families, groups wanting more dining variety, guests who want the biggest resort in the city.
Wins
  • Real water complex: slides, splash zone, lazy river
  • Multiple restaurants under one roof
  • Often the cheaper of the two
Tradeoff
  • 785 rooms means more foot traffic and noise
  • Busy and loud during school holidays

The Properties
Marriott
Sheraton Grand

The Marriott is contained and quiet, roughly 200 rooms and villas. The Sheraton Grand is a genuine mega-resort at 785 rooms, the largest hotel in Da Nang by a wide margin. That scale shows in the crowd, not just the buildings.

Depends on Preference
The Pools
Marriott
Sheraton Grand

Marriott's pool complex is calmer and adult-focused. Sheraton Grand's water complex has slides, a splash zone, and a lazy river, one of the best family water setups on this stretch of coast.

Sheraton Grand
Decide

Which should you book?

Book Marriott Resort If You Want:
  • A quieter version of Non Nuoc Beach
  • A stronger, more relaxed spa program
  • Fewer crowds in the pool and public areas
  • A couples or adults-only trip
  • A well-reviewed breakfast without the crowd
Book Sheraton Grand If You Want:
  • A real water complex for kids: slides, splash zone, lazy river
  • More restaurants and dining variety on-site
  • The largest resort in Da Nang experience
  • A slightly lower nightly rate on average
  • Club lounge access at a bigger property
Know Before You Book

The honest tradeoffs

Da Nang Marriott
Watch Out For
  • No dedicated water attraction, kids will get bored of the standard pool faster
  • Fewer dining outlets than the Sheraton
  • Runs slightly higher rates for a comparable star rating
Sheraton Grand Danang
Watch Out For
  • 785 rooms means real crowds at the pool and breakfast during peak season
  • Loud and busy during Vietnamese and Korean school holidays
  • Spa is solid but not the focus the way it is at the Marriott
Final Verdict

Same beach, opposite atmospheres

Travelling with kids, book the Sheraton Grand. The water complex is genuinely excellent and nothing else on Non Nuoc Beach comes close for family entertainment. You'll also likely pay a bit less for the extra scale.

Travelling as a couple or without children, book the Marriott. The quieter pool areas, stronger spa program, and calmer public spaces make for a better adult stay, and the price difference is small enough not to matter.

Our Pick: Sheraton Grand for families, Marriott for couples
