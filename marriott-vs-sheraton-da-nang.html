Marriott Resort vs Sheraton Grand Da Nang

Two Bonvoy properties, same stretch of Non Nuoc Beach, but one is built for families and one for quieter stays. The decision is simpler than it looks once you know what each does best.

✎ Written by Ryan Yousefi · 📅 Last updated: May 2026 · ⏰ 8-min read

Hotel Overview

Da Nang Marriott Resort & Spa

  • ★★★★★ Resort & Spa
  • ~200 rooms + villas
  • Non Nuoc Beach, south Da Nang
  • Strong spa programme
  • Adult and couple-oriented atmosphere
  • Good breakfast included on most rates
  • From $150/night

Sheraton Grand Da Nang Resort

  • ★★★★★ Resort - largest in Da Nang
  • 785 rooms (massive scale)
  • Non Nuoc Beach, adjacent to Marriott
  • Water complex with kids' facilities
  • Strongly family-oriented
  • Multiple restaurants on-site
  • From $110/night

These two hotels sit close to each other on Non Nuoc Beach and share the same Marriott Bonvoy loyalty programme. The differences are primarily in scale, atmosphere, and target audience.

Sheraton Grand is a genuine mega-resort - at 785 rooms it's the largest hotel in Da Nang by a significant margin. That scale means more facilities but also a busier, louder atmosphere.

The Marriott Resort is more contained, spa-focused, and better calibrated for adults who want a quieter experience.

Both offer solid Non Nuoc beach access, competitive Bonvoy earning, and that Non Nuoc advantage of being roughly 35 minutes from Hoi An. The choice really comes down to whether you're travelling with kids or not.

Neither has a meaningful location advantage over the other. The decision should be made entirely on the basis of facilities and atmosphere.

Rooms & Suites

Marriott Resort, Rooms

  • Contemporary resort design throughout
  • Ocean-view rooms available
  • Pool villas with private plunge pools
  • Quieter building layout
  • Good size - standard rooms well-proportioned
  • Marriott Bonvoy suite upgrades when available

Sheraton Grand, Rooms

  • 785 rooms across a large complex
  • Varied room types and categories
  • Family room options and interconnecting layouts
  • Club floor with lounge access available
  • Ocean-facing and pool-facing options
  • Sheraton Bonvoy suite upgrade benefits

Room quality is competitive at both properties. The Marriott Resort rooms benefit from a quieter environment - with fewer guests overall, the corridors and common areas feel calmer.

At Sheraton Grand, with 785 rooms, the scale means more foot traffic and a livelier atmosphere through the property. Families will appreciate the Sheraton's connecting room options; couples will likely prefer the Marriott's quieter configuration.

The Sheraton Grand's water complex is genuinely impressive and one of the best family water facilities in Da Nang. Kids of almost any age will be occupied for hours.

That is the Sheraton's single clearest advantage. The Marriott counters with a stronger spa programme and a more refined overall atmosphere that adults tend to prefer.

On dining, Sheraton Grand's size means more options under one roof. The Marriott's restaurant quality is solid and the breakfast particularly well-reviewed.

Neither hotel requires you to eat out, though both benefit from being a Grab ride from Hoi An's restaurant scene.

Marriott Bonvoy Loyalty

Both hotels earn and redeem Marriott Bonvoy points, so loyalty members can use either without compromising their programme. A few practical notes:

  • Earn rates are based on spend, not nights - higher rack rates at the Marriott may generate marginally more points per stay
  • Elite status benefits (upgrades, late checkout, lounge access) apply at both properties
  • Points redemption is available at both - check award availability before booking
  • Titanium and Ambassador members will find both hotels attentive to status recognition

If Bonvoy points earning is a primary consideration, both hotels work equally well for the programme. Pick based on the experience you want, not the loyalty maths.

Who Each Hotel Is Best For

Choose Marriott Resort Da Nang if…

  • You're a couple or travelling without children
  • Spa time is a priority
  • You prefer quieter pool areas
  • Smaller-scale resort atmosphere appeals
  • Good breakfast matters for your routine
  • You want less crowded public spaces

Choose Sheraton Grand Da Nang if…

  • You're travelling with kids - the water complex is exceptional
  • More dining options under one roof is a priority
  • You want the largest resort in Da Nang experience
  • Budget matters - Sheraton often runs slightly cheaper
  • Club lounge access appeals at a larger property
  • You don't mind a busier atmosphere during peak periods

Final Verdict

The decision is actually straightforward: travelling with kids, choose the Sheraton Grand. The water complex is genuinely excellent and will make your trip for children of almost any age.

No other Non Nuoc hotel comes close to matching it for family entertainment.

Travelling as a couple or solo, choose the Marriott Resort. The quieter atmosphere, stronger spa, and more refined experience make for a better adult stay.

You'll pay slightly more on average but the environment justifies it.

Both earn Bonvoy points, both have the same Non Nuoc beach access, and both are competent five-star hotels. The overlap ends there.

Common Questions

Marriott vs Sheraton Grand: FAQ

Which earns more Marriott Bonvoy points?+

Both properties earn Marriott Bonvoy points at similar rates since they're both part of the Bonvoy programme. Earn rates are based on spend, so the Marriott Resort - which typically runs slightly higher rates - may generate marginally more points per night.

Elite status benefits and suite upgrades apply at both properties.

Is the Sheraton Grand good for adults without kids?+

Yes, adults without kids can enjoy the Sheraton Grand - it has good restaurants, a solid spa, and direct Non Nuoc beach access. However, the hotel is built around families and the water complex is its main draw.

During school holidays the property gets busy and loud. If you want a quieter atmosphere, the Marriott Resort is the better adult-focused choice on the same stretch of beach.

Which has the better pool?+

The Sheraton Grand has the more impressive pool complex - it includes a large water park-style area with slides and splash zones that makes it exceptional for families with kids. The Marriott Resort has a more refined pool setup with a calmer atmosphere for adults.

If pool-as-entertainment is the priority, Sheraton wins. If pool-as-relaxation is the priority, Marriott wins.
