Da Nang vs Hoi An

Beach city or lantern town? Here's the simple answer and when you should stay in both.

Seconds, not paragraphs

The Short Answer

Choose Da Nang
Easier base

Beach, convenience, nightlife, airport access, and the larger hotel selection. The practical choice.

Choose Hoi An
More atmosphere

Walkable Ancient Town, history, food, and evenings that feel like nowhere else in Vietnam.

Best trip
Do both

Stay in Da Nang and spend real time in Hoi An too. Most travelers shouldn't have to pick.

Editor's Pick

Da Nang

Best Base for Most Travelers

Da Nang is the easier overall base for most travelers. You get beach, an airport 15 minutes from most hotels, Grab that works everywhere, a much larger hotel selection at every price point, a real food and nightlife scene, and easier logistics for day trips to Marble Mountains, Ba Na Hills, Hai Van Pass, and Hoi An itself.

The tradeoff is real: Da Nang doesn't have Hoi An's Ancient Town atmosphere or the walkable, lantern-lit feel that makes Hoi An distinctive. If that's the reason you're visiting Central Vietnam, keep reading.

Do Both

Stay in Da Nang, Experience Hoi An

This isn't really an either/or choice unless your time is extremely limited. Da Nang and Hoi An sit 25km apart, about 30 minutes by Grab, close enough that the strongest trip usually combines both rather than picking one.

With 2 days: Da Nang plus a Hoi An evening. With 3–4 days: Da Nang as your base plus a full Hoi An day. With 5+ days: consider splitting your nights between the two.

Who Should Choose Which

Choose Da Nang
The practical base

Best for first-time visitors, families, beach travelers, and anyone who wants nightlife and airport convenience close at hand. Da Nang has the larger hotel choice, works well for longer stays, and suits digital nomads who need reliable infrastructure day to day.

Choose Hoi An
The atmosphere trip

Best for couples, photographers, and travelers who want to walk everywhere and be in a smaller destination. Hoi An rewards evenings spent in town, not day trips. If history and atmosphere matter more than convenience, this is where to base.

Common Questions

Should I stay in Da Nang or Hoi An?
Stay in Da Nang if you want beach access, nightlife, more accommodation options at every price point, and easier logistics. Stay in Hoi An if the Ancient Town experience is the main reason you're visiting Central Vietnam. Both cities work for a week; most people do 2–3 nights Da Nang and 3–4 nights Hoi An, or vice versa.
Can you do Da Nang and Hoi An in the same trip?
Yes, and most people do. It's 25km between cities, 30–40 minutes by Grab (200,000–300,000 VND). A week-long trip works well as 3 nights one city and 4 nights the other. The most common mistake is shuttling back and forth daily instead of picking a base and taking one or two day trips.
Which city has better beaches, Da Nang or Hoi An?
Da Nang's My Khe Beach is longer and more developed, with more beach-bar infrastructure. Hoi An's An Bang Beach is a better local beach experience — less developed, better restaurants behind it, generally less crowded. Cua Dai Beach near Hoi An has lost beach to erosion and is no longer a reason to base in Hoi An.
Is Hoi An worth visiting from Da Nang?
Yes, as a day trip or an overnight. The Ancient Town at dusk, when tour groups leave and the lanterns come on, is one of the most distinctive experiences in Central Vietnam. A day trip from Da Nang misses the morning, which is the best time in Hoi An. If you have 5+ nights in the region, spend at least 2 nights in Hoi An rather than commuting from Da Nang.
