Comparison Guide · Updated 2026

Da Nang
vs Hoi An

Where to base yourself, how to split your time, and the honest answer to which city wins on beach, value, and atmosphere. Plus: why Hoiana changes the calculation for luxury travellers.

Home › Da Nang vs Hoi An

Da Nang vs. Hoi An isn't a competition. It's a family affair. These two cities exist as brother and sister -- dragon and lantern. They were never meant to be pitted against each other.

Hoi An is the older sibling -- the one with memories richer than money can buy. Ancient streets, tailors who know your name, a riverfront that hasn't changed in a hundred years. Da Nang is the younger one: the hot-shot, the popular kid, the one with the beach and the rooftops and the YouTube travel montages that make everyone book a flight.

This isn't a choice. You need to do both. They're a short Grab ride from each other -- 30 minutes, give or take. The only real question is which order you want your paradise in.

Quick decision

Which city for you?

25km apart, 30 minutes by Grab. Most people do both. The question is which one to base in.

Stay in Da Nang if...

City first, Ancient Town optional

  • You want beach plus nightlife plus restaurants
  • You're here for a week and want more things to do
  • You value accommodation choice and transport ease
  • You plan to visit Hoi An as a day trip (30–40 min each way)
  • You're a digital nomad or on a longer stay

Stay in Hoi An if...

Ancient Town is the whole point

  • The UNESCO town experience is why you came
  • You want to be there at dawn and evening, not as a day-tripper
  • A slower pace and boutique hotel is your preference
  • 3–4 nights is all you have in Central Vietnam
  • You want An Bang Beach access plus Old Town

Do both if...

One trip, two cities

  • You have 7+ nights in Central Vietnam
  • Split 3 nights Da Nang, 4 nights Hoi An — or reverse
  • Grab between them costs 200,000–300,000 VND
  • Don't shuttle back and forth — pick a base and day-trip

The real difference

"Da Nang is a city with a beach. Hoi An is an Ancient Town that happens to have one nearby. They feel completely different."

The third option

Hoiana — neither city

Hoiana is a luxury resort enclave 10km south of Hoi An Ancient Town. Rosewood Hoi An and Hoiana Hotel & Suites occupy a private beach stretch with a Robert Trent Jones Jr. golf course. From here, both Da Nang and Hoi An are accessible by taxi. For luxury travellers, it sidesteps the whole Da Nang vs Hoi An debate: you stay at Hoiana, and you visit both cities as excursions.

Common questions

Da Nang vs Hoi An — FAQ

Should I stay in Da Nang or Hoi An?
Stay in Da Nang if you want beach access, nightlife, more accommodation options at every price point, and easier logistics. Stay in Hoi An if the Ancient Town experience is the main reason you're visiting Central Vietnam and you want to be in the middle of it. Both cities work for a week; most people do 2–3 nights Da Nang and 3–4 nights Hoi An, or vice versa.
Can you do Da Nang and Hoi An in the same trip?
Yes, and most people do. It's 25km between cities, 30–40 minutes by Grab (200,000–300,000 VND). A week-long trip works well as 3 nights one city and 4 nights the other. The most common mistake is shuttling back and forth daily instead of picking a base and taking one or two day trips.
Which city has better beaches, Da Nang or Hoi An?
Da Nang's My Khe Beach is longer and more developed, with more beach-bar infrastructure. Hoi An's An Bang Beach is better as a local beach experience — less developed, better restaurants behind it, and generally less crowded. Cua Dai Beach near Hoi An has lost beach to erosion and is no longer recommended as a reason to base in Hoi An. Four Seasons Nam Hai's private beach is the best in the area.
Is Hoi An worth visiting from Da Nang?
Yes — as a day trip or an overnight. The Ancient Town at dusk, when tour groups leave and the lanterns come on, is one of the most distinctive experiences in Central Vietnam. A day trip from Da Nang misses the morning, which is the best time in Hoi An. If you have 5+ nights in the region, spend at least 2 nights in Hoi An rather than commuting from Da Nang.
What is Hoiana and how is it different from Hoi An?
Hoiana is a private luxury resort enclave 10km south of Hoi An Ancient Town. It's home to Rosewood Hoi An and Hoiana Hotel & Suites — two five-star properties sharing a private beach and 18-hole golf course. It has nothing to do with the UNESCO Ancient Town; you visit Hoi An from there as a day trip. It's the answer to the Da Nang vs Hoi An question if your budget allows for $300+ per night.
