Da Nang vs Hoi An: Where Should You Stay?

30km apart. 40 minutes by Grab. Very different experiences. Here's how to choose.

Updated 2026· By Ryan Yousefi· Local resident since 2021

The short version

Da Nang
Best for beach & convenience

International airport, 20km of beach, more hotel choice, lower prices, Hoi An 40 minutes away.

Hoi An
Best for atmosphere & walking

UNESCO Ancient Town, walkable streets, lantern evenings, world-class food, intimate boutique hotels.

Best trip
Split your stay

3 nights Da Nang + 2 nights Hoi An. You get both without giving up either.

Editor's verdict

The honest answer depends on what you're optimising for

Most people who ask this question are trying to decide between beach infrastructure and cultural depth. Da Nang wins the first; Hoi An wins the second — and they're 40 minutes apart, so the choice isn't permanent.

If I had to pick one: base in Da Nang, spend a full day in Hoi An. The Ancient Town is genuinely better as an experience than as an extended stay — once you've seen the streets by lantern light, wandered the market, and eaten well, most people feel the atmosphere has been absorbed. You don't need a week of it.

The exception: couples on a slow trip who want to actually live in the Ancient Town atmosphere. Two nights in Hoi An changes how you see the place.

— Ryan Yousefi, Da Nang resident since 2021

The hotel experience

Da Nang hotels

Modern resort hotels dominate: InterContinental, Hyatt, Marriott, Sheraton along Son Tra and My Khe. Large pools, full resort amenities, direct beach access. The competition keeps prices honest.

What Da Nang doesn't offer: the intimate boutique stays or the feeling of sleeping inside a UNESCO heritage zone.

Hoi An hotels

Boutique properties define this market: colonial courtyards, garden pools, riverside terraces. Anantara on the Thu Bon, Four Seasons at Ha My Beach. Moire (Vignette/IHG), Almanity, Silk Marina — each has a distinct character.

The Ancient Town premium is real — you pay for proximity to the heritage zone, not just the room.

There is a third option

Hoiana — the integrated resort, 20 min south

Neither Da Nang city nor Hoi An Ancient Town — Hoiana is a 1,000-hectare integrated resort complex on the coast 10km south of Hoi An. If you want golf, a casino, private beach access, and a full resort environment, this is a distinct third option.

Three Robert Trent Jones Jr. golf courses, a casino for foreign passport holders, Rosewood Hoi An at $400–700/night, and Hoiana Hotel & Suites at $130–200/night as the accessible entry point. None of this is about walking Ancient Town streets at lantern hour.

Common questions

Da Nang vs Hoi An: FAQ

Should I stay in Da Nang or Hoi An? +
It depends on your priorities. Da Nang wins on beach, transport, and budget. Hoi An wins on atmosphere, walkability, and food. Most travellers with 5+ nights find a 3+2 split gives the optimal experience of both. If you only have 2–3 nights, pick the experience you most want and day-trip to the other.
Is Da Nang cheaper than Hoi An? +
Yes, modestly. Mid-range hotels in Da Nang run 10–25% cheaper than equivalent quality in Hoi An's Ancient Town zone. Street food and local restaurants are similar prices in both cities. Transport inside Hoi An is slightly more expensive (tuk-tuks, bike hire to An Bang). The biggest cost difference: Da Nang's beach is free and walkable; Hoi An's beach costs transport.
Can you do Hoi An as a day trip from Da Nang? +
Yes, easily. Hoi An is 30km south, 40 minutes by Grab (220,000–280,000 VND one way). Leave by 7:30am to see the Ancient Town before tour groups arrive. Wander on foot until noon, eat at Morning Glory or Banh Mi Phuong, and return mid-afternoon. For the lantern atmosphere, add one overnight in Hoi An.
Which city has the better beach? +
Da Nang, significantly. My Khe is 20km of consistent beach with reliable cleanliness Feb–Aug and surf season Oct–Feb. Hoi An's An Bang is pleasant but 4km from the Old Town (requires transport) and shorter. Cua Dai Beach has suffered serious erosion since 2014 and is considerably narrower than it once was.
What is Hoiana and is it worth considering? +
Hoiana is a 1,000-hectare integrated resort 10km south of Hoi An — separate from both the Ancient Town and Da Nang. It has golf (three Robert Trent Jones Jr. courses), a casino for foreign passport holders, private beach access, Rosewood Hoi An ($400–700/night), and the more accessible Hoiana Hotel & Suites ($130–200/night). Worth considering if resort facilities — golf, casino, beach — are a priority rather than cultural exploration.

The verdict

Do both. 30km apart. 40 minutes.

Da Nang wins on practicality. Hoi An wins on soul. Neither one needs to come at the expense of the other. Three nights in Da Nang and two in Hoi An is the baseline for a first-time trip to central Vietnam.
