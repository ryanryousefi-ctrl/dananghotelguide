Hotel Comparison

Furama vs Pullman

Da Nang's original five-star with mature gardens and real character, against a modern Accor resort with a quieter beach position. Which you prefer says a lot about how you travel.

Hotel A
Furama Resort Danang
Hotel B
Pullman Danang Beach Resort
VS
Editor's Pick
Furama Resort Danang
Why It Wins

The lagoon pool with mature palms and tropical landscaping is more photogenic than any rectangular resort pool in Da Nang, and La Maison 1888 is the most serious restaurant attached to any hotel in the city. Furama has more soul than almost anything else at this price point. Pullman is the safer, more modern booking, but it won't surprise you the way Furama can.

The 30-Second Verdict

Quick read before you scroll further

Furama Resort Danang
Best For

Travelers who value character and atmosphere, families wanting garden space, serious diners

Wins
  • Lagoon pool with mature tropical gardens
  • La Maison 1888, the city's most serious hotel restaurant
  • Closer to An Thuong restaurants, ~8 min Grab
Tradeoff
  • Older infrastructure in places, opened 1997
  • No hotel loyalty programme benefits
Pullman Danang Beach Resort
Best For

Accor ALL members, travelers who want modern infrastructure, closer Hoi An access

Wins
  • Newer, cleaner contemporary design
  • Quieter Non Nuoc beach, closer to Hoi An
  • Accor ALL loyalty programme
Tradeoff
  • Standard resort landscaping, no garden character
  • ~18 min Grab to An Thuong restaurants
  • Fewer dining outlets than Furama

Which Should You Book

Match it to your trip

Book Furama If You Want:
  • Mature gardens and a genuinely photogenic pool
  • La Maison 1888 or serious on-site dining
  • Walkable-ish access to An Thuong restaurants
  • Character over a slick modern aesthetic
Book Pullman If You Want:
  • Modern, clean contemporary design
  • A quieter beach and closer Hoi An access
  • Accor ALL loyalty points
  • Ocean-view rooms as a priority
Know Before You Book

The honest tradeoffs

Furama Resort Danang
Tradeoffs
  • Opened 1997, some infrastructure shows its age
  • No hotel loyalty programme, feels independent
  • ~45 min to Hoi An, further than Pullman
Pullman Danang Beach Resort
Tradeoffs
  • Standard resort landscaping, no garden character
  • ~18 min Grab to An Thuong restaurants
  • Fewer F&B outlets than Furama
Final Verdict

Which one should you actually book?

These two suit genuinely different travelers, but if you care about atmosphere, the look of your pool photos, and having a good dinner on-site, book Furama. It has more soul than almost anything else in Da Nang at this price point, and the My Khe position keeps An Thuong's restaurants a short Grab away.

Book Pullman if you want clean modern infrastructure, a quieter beach, better Hoi An access, and Accor loyalty benefits. It won't surprise or delight you the way Furama might, but it won't disappoint you either.

Our Pick: Furama Resort Danang
