Pullman Danang Beach Resort

“The open-lobby pool and sea view, and excellent suite bathrooms.”

Our Take

The Pullman Danang has an open-lobby concept, the entrance looks straight through to the infinity pool, and through that to the ocean.

It's a good first sight.

You arrive, you see the pool, you see the sea beyond it, and whatever travel stress you were carrying drops a notch.

On hot days, some guests migrate to the business center where the air conditioning is more aggressive, which tells you something about the trade-off: beautiful but warm.

Staff greet you with welcome drinks and take the bags, and the reception team has a specific kind of warmth that doesn't feel scripted, one staff member, a guest relations manager named Ms Ny, gets mentioned by name in review after review, someone who made guests feel like valued family members.

That kind of reputation doesn't come from training manuals alone.

Suites are generous, one junior bedroom, one master, direct sea views from every room, a proper bathtub and separate shower in the bathroom.

The word elegant comes up often.

The overall design is contemporary without being cold, warm neutrals, balconies that work, a bed you sink into.

Standard rooms are smaller, around 40 square metres, clean and functional with balconies, but they're not the point of the Pullman.

The suites are the point, and if the budget allows, the upgrade makes a meaningful difference.

The Rooms

The Rooms

Suites are the clear standout, one junior bedroom, one master, direct sea views from every room, and a bathroom good enough that one guest admitted it made them reconsider whether they needed to go outside. Standard rooms run around 40 square metres, clean and functional with working balconies, but they don't carry the same design weight as the suites. The contemporary warm-neutral palette works throughout, and the beds are genuinely comfortable across categories.

Which room would we choose? A suite, without hesitation. The bathroom alone justifies the upgrade, and the sea view from every room in that category beats what standard rooms offer.

The Reason to Book It

The Open Lobby & Pool

The open-lobby concept, entrance to infinity pool to ocean in one sightline, is the resort's signature moment and the reason it makes a strong first impression before you've even checked in. It's deliberately airy, which is beautiful but genuinely warm on hot days, some guests retreat to the business centre for stronger air conditioning. The tradeoff is honest and worth knowing: gorgeous architecture, imperfect climate control.

Pool + Beach

Pool + Beach

Here's where honesty matters: the pool is smaller than you'd expect for a resort of this size, and when the resort is full in peak season, sunbeds run out. Multiple guests have mentioned this specifically, it's a recurring pattern, not a one-off. The beach directly in front is the overflow, and it's excellent, wide and clean with full service, but if the plan is to spend most of the day on a lounger at the pool, know the competition for those loungers is real in December and January. Off-peak, shoulder season and May through August, the pool area is spacious and relaxed, this is a non-issue.

Food + Breakfast

Food + Drink

The breakfast buffet is large, biggest breakfast buffet we've ever seen is an actual guest quote, which tells you something about the ambition. Asian hot dishes, fruit, pastry, a good selection of everything. Some guests find it exceptional, others note that the Four Points Sheraton nearby runs a stronger breakfast, a cross-hotel comparison that keeps appearing in reviews. This one is good. Whether it's great depends on what you're comparing it to.

Who It's For

Families, Couples & Who It Suits

Couples and Accor ALL members get the clearest value here, a suite with a genuinely excellent bathroom, reliable service, and easy walking access to My Khe's restaurant strip. Families do fine too, standard rooms are functional and the beach is wide and full-service, but the smaller pool and peak-season sunbed shortage are worth factoring in if pool time is the centerpiece of the trip. It's not trying to be the InterContinental or Naman Retreat, and it doesn't need to be, what it does, dependable luxury on a workable budget, it does consistently well.

What We Like

What We Like

The open lobby

A direct sightline from entrance to pool to sea makes for a genuinely strong first impression, deliberately airy design.

Suite bathrooms

Genuinely excellent, a proper bathtub and separate shower, well above the category average for the price point.

Ms Ny and the reception team

A guest relations manager who gets mentioned by name in review after review for making guests feel like valued family members.

Accor ALL value

A legitimate earning property at a quality level where points add up fast for status or redemption.

The Tradeoff

The pool is smaller than the resort's scale suggests, and sunbeds get genuinely scarce in peak season, this is a recurring complaint across multiple guest reviews, not a one-off. The open lobby is beautiful but warm, guests seeking stronger air conditioning retreat to the business centre. Breakfast is good but trails the nearby Four Points Sheraton in direct comparisons guests keep making. Design overall is competent rather than distinctive, there's no architectural showpiece here, just consistent delivery.

Location

Location

My Khe Beach, with the restaurant strip 5-10 minutes on foot, seafood grills, noodle shops, and good local dining within easy reach. The airport is 15 minutes, and Hoi An is 35 minutes by car. The Pullman works well as a base for exploring Da Nang without being so isolated that a taxi is required for everything.

Restaurants: 5-10 min walkAirport: 15 min
Would We Stay Here

YES

In a suite, yes. The Pullman gives a reliable, well-staffed beachfront hotel with a good breakfast and easy access to the city's restaurant scene. It's not trying to be the InterContinental or Naman Retreat, and it doesn't need to be. What it does, dependable luxury on a workable budget, it does consistently well. That's enough.

Common Questions

Pullman Danang Beach Resort: FAQ

Is Pullman Danang Beach Resort worth staying at in Da Nang?

Yes, particularly for Accor loyalty members. The Pullman delivers a well-managed resort with direct beach access, good pool facilities, and reliable Accor service standards.

How much does Pullman Danang Beach Resort cost per night?

Rates typically range from $145–$190/night in low season, rising to $240–$360/night during peak December to February.

Where is Pullman Danang Beach Resort located in Da Nang?

Located on My Khe Beach, 8 km from Dragon Bridge and 10 km from Da Nang Airport.
