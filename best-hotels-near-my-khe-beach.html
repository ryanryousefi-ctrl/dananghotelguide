The beachfront towers, design hotels and An Thuong stays we'd actually book near Da Nang's best-known beach.

Editor's Top Pick

TMS Hotel Da Nang Beach

My Khe · Vo Nguyen Giap · Upscale · $$$

TMS makes its case upstairs. The rooftop infinity pool has the sort of uninterrupted My Khe view people imagine when they book a Da Nang beach trip - ocean in every direction, the hotel strip below, horizon beyond. Rooms are well-sized and modern with ocean-facing balconies. It sits directly on Vo Nguyen Giap, the beachfront road, which means the sand is a few steps across.

This is our pick for most first-time visitors asking where to stay near My Khe. Strong location, strong rooftop, genuinely good value for the quality. The beach, An Thuong, cafes and restaurants are all walkable from here.

CHICLAND Hotel

My Khe · Vo Nguyen Giap · Boutique · $$$

Every other hotel on this stretch is a glass tower. CHICLAND is the one covered in greenery. Designed by Vo Trong Nghia - Vietnam's best-known sustainable architect - it wraps the building in plants and bamboo screens that actually work as shade and character. It has become one of the most photographed hotels in Da Nang for good reason.

The rooftop pool with ocean views is excellent. Rooms have the same considered design sensibility. It sits on Vo Nguyen Giap directly across from the beach. If you want something that feels genuinely Da Nang rather than generic Asian coastal resort, CHICLAND is it.

Why Stay Near My Khe?

My Khe is the easiest base for most first-time Da Nang leisure travelers. The beach is long, flat, swimmable and right there. An Thuong - the cafe and restaurant strip behind the beach - has everything you need walking distance. Airport Grab takes 10-15 minutes. Hoi An is 35 minutes by car. The whole setup is simple.

The practical case: you wake up, walk to the beach, have coffee on An Thuong, eat seafood at night, and use Grab for anything further afield. It's not complicated - and that's the point.

Common Questions

Which area is best for first-time visitors?
For most first-time leisure visitors, My Khe beachfront or An Thuong gives the most convenient experience. My Khe puts the beach at your door. An Thuong puts cafes, restaurants and nightlife at your door. Both are within easy walking distance of each other.
Are My Khe hotels expensive?
My Khe has a wide range. Mid-range options like Muong Thanh Luxury and Paris Deli offer good value. Upscale options like TMS and CHICLAND are reasonably priced by international standards. Luxury (Sheraton Grand, Risemount) costs more but remains cheaper than comparable beach properties in Thailand or Bali.
How far is My Khe from the airport?
About 4-5km. A Grab takes 10-15 minutes and costs approximately 80,000-120,000 VND.
What is An Thuong and why do people stay there?
An Thuong is the expat and restaurant strip a few streets back from My Khe Beach. It has the densest concentration of cafes, bars, restaurants and long-stay apartments in Da Nang. Many travelers prefer it because you can walk everywhere for food and nightlife while the beach is still only a few minutes on foot.
Is My Khe Beach good for swimming?
Yes - one of Vietnam's most swimmable urban beaches. Wide, sandy, lifeguards in season. Red flags mean no swimming. Be aware of strong rip currents during October-November typhoon season.
