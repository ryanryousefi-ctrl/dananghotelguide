Which Stretch of My Khe is Right For You?

My Khe is 8 kilometers of beach running south from the Da Nang port area. The northern end starts at Nguyen Tat Thanh street - less developed, quieter, and further from the tourist core.

The central stretch, anchored by the An Thuong neighborhood off Vo Nguyen Giap, is where most visitors should base themselves. The southern end fades into Non Nuoc - a different resort zone with a different vibe entirely.

The stretch you pick determines how your day-to-day feels. Central My Khe means you can walk to food.

Northern My Khe means you ride Grab everywhere. Southern My Khe means you are effectively in a resort zone with minimal city access without a vehicle.

Walkability: What You Can Actually Reach on Foot

An Thuong food street is the most important walkability factor for My Khe hotels. From central My Khe hotels (A La Carte, TMS, Sheraton Grand area), An Thuong 1 through 5 streets are a 5-10 minute walk inland.

These streets have 50+ restaurants including some of Da Nang's best Vietnamese food, fresh seafood spots, Western cafes, and bars. Prices are reasonable.

Most places are open for lunch and dinner. This is where I eat several times a week.

The nearest convenience stores - Circle K, GS25, Family Mart - are scattered through the An Thuong area, a short walk from central My Khe hotels. Morning coffee and bahn mi options are within the same radius.

Northern My Khe food reality: If you stay at Pullman or Fusion Suites, budget for Grab rides at every meal outside the hotel. The nearest restaurant cluster is a 10-15 minute ride away.

Not a dealbreaker, but factor it into your budget and time planning. Grab rides cost 40,000-80,000 VND each way.

For access to the Han River area, Dragon Bridge, and the city center, a Grab from any My Khe hotel takes 10-15 minutes and costs 40,000-70,000 VND. My Khe is close to the city - this is not a long commute by any measure.

Which Stretch I Would Pick and Why

For a first visit to Da Nang, I would always choose central My Khe around An Thuong. The walkability to food changes the whole trip.

Being able to walk out at 7pm and pick from 50 restaurants within a few streets - Vietnamese, seafood, Thai, pizza, whatever - is genuinely useful. It means you eat better, spend less, and discover more.

For a second or third visit, or for anyone specifically wanting quiet and less city energy, I would look at northern My Khe or make the jump to Non Nuoc entirely. The quieter beach sections around Pullman have noticeably less activity.

You hear fewer vendors, fewer motorbikes, and the water feels more like you have it to yourself.

What I would not do: book a northern My Khe hotel expecting the convenience of central My Khe, or book central My Khe expecting the quiet of Non Nuoc. Pick based on what you actually want from the area, not based on which hotel has the best pool photos.

My Khe Beach: Honest Assessment

My Khe is a good beach. Fine white sand, wide shoreline, and generally clean water.

It earned its "one of the most beautiful beaches in the planet" designation from Forbes in the 1990s, which is still quoted in every tourist brochure despite being 30 years old.

The wave situation varies significantly by season. February through April brings consistent swell - the waves are strong enough for beginner surfing and require care for casual swimmers.

The flag system is actively enforced: green means safe, yellow means caution, red means no swimming. Lifeguards are present at the main stretches.

May through September is calmer and more suitable for families.

Beach vendors operate on My Khe, particularly on the central stretch. Morning vendor activity is the most active.

By 9am on weekends, you will have people offering fruit, drinks, and corn. This is not oppressive - you can decline and be left alone - but it is different from a resort beach with enforced no-vendor zones.

Non Nuoc resorts have cleaner, more controlled beach frontage if that matters to you.

Family swimming note: My Khe is best for families May through September when waves are calmer. Feb-Apr is surfing season - the waves are strong and the flag system will sometimes restrict swimming.

Always check the flag before entering the water regardless of the time of year.

My Khe vs Non Nuoc: Who Should Stay Where

Stay on My Khe if: you want walkable food access, you are on a first visit to Da Nang and want city access alongside the beach, you have a moderate budget and want value for money, or you prefer an active beach atmosphere over a contained resort setting.

Consider Non Nuoc instead if: you want a fully contained resort experience with no need to leave the grounds, you are prioritizing maximum peace and quiet, you want the larger resort complexes (Hyatt, Naman, Fusion Maia), or you are on a honeymoon or couples retreat where a beach club atmosphere is preferred over a city-adjacent beach.

My Khe is not a second-tier option to Non Nuoc - they serve different traveler types. A couple who wants to eat well across Da Nang's restaurant scene while having beach time each day will be happier on My Khe.

A couple who wants to barely leave the resort and treat the property as a retreat will be happier at Non Nuoc.

Common Questions

My Khe Beach Hotels: FAQ

Which part of My Khe Beach is best to stay on?+

Central My Khe - the stretch around Vo Nguyen Giap and the An Thuong neighborhood - is the best choice for first-time visitors. You get beach access, walkable restaurants on An Thuong food street, and hotels at every price point.

Northern My Khe (toward Pullman) is quieter but requires Grab for food. Southern My Khe transitions into the Non Nuoc resort zone, which is a different experience entirely.

See the full where-to-stay guide for a broader comparison.

Is My Khe Beach safe for swimming?+

Generally yes, but with seasonal caveats. Da Nang runs a flag warning system on My Khe: green means safe, yellow means caution, red means no swimming.

Feb-Apr brings strong waves and rip currents - good for surfers, requires care for families. May through September is calmer and more suitable for casual swimmers.

Always swim between the flags and respect lifeguard instructions.

Are there restaurants walking distance from My Khe hotels?+

It depends on the stretch. Central My Khe hotels near An Thuong are within 5-10 minutes walk of 50+ restaurants on An Thuong 1-5 streets - Vietnamese, seafood, international, and cafes.

Northern My Khe hotels like Pullman and Fusion Suites are isolated and you will need a Grab ride (5-10 minutes, 40,000-80,000 VND) to reach food. Read the An Thuong neighborhood guide for more detail on the food scene.

Is My Khe or Non Nuoc better for families?+

Non Nuoc suits families who want a contained resort experience. The beach is slightly calmer, and larger resorts like Hyatt Regency have better kids' facilities.

My Khe suits families who want city access alongside the beach - walkable restaurants, An Thuong food street, and more flexibility. For the beach itself, both are fine for families May-September.

See the Non Nuoc guide for a full comparison.

How far is My Khe from the city center?+

My Khe runs roughly parallel to the Han River city center, about 3-4 kilometers away. From central My Khe hotels, a Grab to Bach Dang riverside walk, Con Market, or the Dragon Bridge area takes 10-15 minutes and costs 40,000-60,000 VND.

My Khe is genuinely close to the city. It is not a remote resort location - you can easily do both beach and city on the same day.
