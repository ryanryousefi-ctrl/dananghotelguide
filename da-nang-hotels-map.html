Area 01 · Beachfront

My Khe Beach

Best for first-time visitors

Stay here if you want to walk downstairs in flip-flops and be on the sand in two minutes. The strip runs along Vo Nguyen Giap, and almost every tower on it faces the water.

The trade-off is that the strip itself is hotels and tourist restaurants. Dinner worth walking to is a few streets back in An Thuong.

Area 02 · Neighbourhood

An Thuong & My An

Best for food, cafes and longer stays

An Thuong makes more sense than the beachfront if cafes, restaurants and walking matter more to you than an uninterrupted sea view. It is the grid of streets 400 to 800 metres behind the sand, and it is where most of us who live here actually eat.

Hotels are smaller and cheaper. You give up the view and gain a neighbourhood.

Area 03 · City

Han River & Hai Chau

Best city base

This is the working city. Bach Dang runs along the water, the Dragon Bridge sits at the end of it, and the promenade fills up every evening with locals rather than tourists.

Rooms cost less here than on the beach for the same standard. The beach is 8 to 12 minutes away by Grab, which is close enough that you are not cut off, and far enough that you will not go daily.

Area 04 · Peninsula

Son Tra Peninsula

Best luxury escape

A protected jungle headland north of the city with monkeys in the trees and two resorts on it. Nothing else. Staying here does not feel like staying in Da Nang at all.

Be honest with yourself about the isolation. It is 20 to 25 minutes back to town on a winding road, so you will eat where you sleep. Book it if that sounds good, not if it sounds tolerable.

Area 05 · Resort coast

Non Nuoc Beach & Ngu Hanh Son

Best resort area

South of the Marble Mountains the coast opens out and the towers stop. This is where the big resorts sit on wide, quiet sand with room for lazy rivers and kids clubs.

It is also the smart base for a Hoi An trip, roughly 20 minutes south. Do not book it expecting to walk anywhere for dinner, because you will not.

Here for Something Specific?

Stay Near What You Came For

Four landmarks people plan trips around, and the smartest hotel area for each.

Stay: Han River / Hai Chau Dragon Bridge Walkable from the western riverfront. The fire show runs weekend nights at 21:00. Stay: My Khe or An Thuong My Khe Beach Beachfront towers put you on the sand. An Thuong puts you five minutes back for less. Stay: Non Nuoc / Ngu Hanh Son Marble Mountains The Non Nuoc resorts sit at the base of them. Everywhere else is a 20 minute drive. Stay: Son Tra, or anywhere Lady Buddha & Son Tra Worth a half day from any area. Only worth sleeping up here if isolation is the point.
Common Questions

Picking an Area, Answered

Which area of Da Nang should I stay in?

For a first trip, stay on My Khe Beach. You walk downstairs and you are on the sand, and the hotel choice runs from budget to five star. If restaurants and cafes matter more than a sea view, stay in An Thuong instead, two or three streets back. For city access and nightlife pick the Han River in Hai Chau, for a resort holiday pick Non Nuoc, and for isolation pick Son Tra.

What is the difference between My Khe and An Thuong?

My Khe is the beachfront strip along Vo Nguyen Giap, mostly towers with sea views. An Thuong is the grid of streets behind it, roughly 400 to 800 metres from the sand, with the city's densest run of cafes, bars and independent restaurants and mostly smaller boutique hotels. An Thuong is cheaper, more walkable and better for long stays. My Khe wins if you want to see the water from bed. Our An Thuong hotel guide covers that area in full.

Where are Da Nang's luxury resorts located?

They split into two clusters. Son Tra Peninsula holds the InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort and Premier Village, both isolated and dramatic. Non Nuoc Beach, south towards the Marble Mountains, holds the Hyatt Regency, the Da Nang Marriott, Naman Retreat and TIA Wellness, which are larger, flatter and better for families. See the luxury hotels guide for the full ranking.

How far is My Khe Beach from the Han River and Dragon Bridge?

Around 3 to 4 km, which is 8 to 12 minutes in a Grab car outside rush hour. Nothing in central Da Nang is genuinely far from anything else, so the choice between beach and river is about what you want outside your door in the evening, not about travel time.

Is Non Nuoc Beach too far from Da Nang city?

Non Nuoc sits about 20 to 25 minutes south of the Han River and roughly 20 minutes north of Hoi An, so it is a poor base for city evenings and a good base for a resort holiday with a Hoi An day trip. Book it if you plan to spend most of your time on the property.

Which area is cheapest?

An Thuong and the Han River both undercut the beachfront for the same standard of room, because you are paying for a location rather than a view. The budget guide has specific properties, and our cheap hotels list maps them by area.
