Sanouva Da Nang Hotel

Mid-Range City Hotel · Hai Chau District

A Han River-area city hotel with daily afternoon tea thrown in and a quietly strong review record.

A note on how this page was built: we have not personally stayed at the Sanouva Da Nang Hotel. This profile is compiled from the hotel's published amenities and the pattern of verified guest reviews on major booking platforms, not a firsthand inspection. Where we're not confident in a detail, we've left it out rather than guess. Treat this as a research-backed overview to help you decide whether to look closer, not a substitute for reading recent guest reviews yourself before booking.

What the Listings Show

Sanouva Da Nang Hotel sits in Hai Chau District, Da Nang's city center, within walking distance of the Han River and roughly 2-3 km from My Khe Beach. It's a mid-range city hotel, not a beach resort, and it's positioned that way in every listing: the pitch is a comfortable, well-located base for getting around the city, not a beachfront stay.

Guest review scores are consistently strong for the price tier, with couples in particular rating the location highly. The hotel includes daily afternoon tea as a standard inclusion, something most city hotels at this rate don't bother with, and it shows up repeatedly in guest feedback as a small but appreciated touch.

On-site facilities listed include a fitness center, massage and sauna services, and a restaurant serving a daily breakfast buffet with a mix of Vietnamese and international dishes. Rooms are described as straightforward and modern: flat-screen TV, electric kettle, minibar, private bathroom with shower.

Who It's Likely Right For

Who It's Likely Right For

Based on the location and price point, this reads as a sensible pick for travelers who want to be based in the city center near the Han River, close to Dragon Bridge and the riverside restaurant strip, rather than parked on a beach resort strip. It's a reasonable fit for business travelers, couples on a city-focused trip, or anyone splitting a longer Da Nang stay between a few nights downtown and a few nights at the beach.

Who should look elsewhere: anyone whose trip is built around beach time. My Khe Beach is about 2.9 km away, roughly a 10-15 minute taxi or Grab ride depending on traffic, not a walk, and this hotel isn't trying to be a resort. If direct beach access is non-negotiable, a My Khe or Non Nuoc property makes more sense.

The Honest Gap in This Page

We can tell you what the hotel advertises and what guest review patterns suggest. We can't yet tell you what the rooms actually smell like, whether road noise reaches the upper floors, or whether the breakfast buffet lives up to the "afternoon tea included" reputation on a crowded Saturday. Those are the kinds of details that only come from an actual stay, and we'd rather say that plainly than invent them.

Location

Location

Hai Chau District, Da Nang's city center, close to the Han River and Dragon Bridge. My Khe Beach is about 2.9 km away, roughly a 10-15 minute taxi or Grab ride rather than a walk. Da Nang International Airport is a short drive. For travelers splitting time between the city and the beach, this location works; for a pure beach trip, it isn't the right base.
