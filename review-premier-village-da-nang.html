Premier Village Da Nang Resort Review

RY
Ryan Yousefi
Da Nang Hotel Guide · Last updated March 2026 · ★★★★★ · From $380/night
Private VillasFamiliesBeachfrontMost Space

Premier Village is Da Nang's private villa resort — the property where every room is a two-storey villa with a private pool looking at the South China Sea, and where 'going to the pool' means walking ten steps from your bedroom.

Premier Village Da Nang is on Son Tra Peninsula — not on My Khe or Non Nuoc, but on the rocky coastal headland that separates the two. The position gives it something unique: a villa development built on natural terrain with sea views that aren't interrupted by neighbouring resort towers.

Every room category is a villa. There are no standard rooms, no hotel corridors, no shared pool areas. You check into a private two-storey villa with your own pool, your own outdoor space, and a sea view that you don't share. That's the product, clearly defined.

The InterContinental takes the 'exclusive resort on Son Tra' concept in one direction — elevated, remote, funicular, jungle. Premier Village takes it in another: more accessible, pool-villa format, family-capable. Both are the right answer for different travellers.

Location & Getting There

Son Tra Peninsula, Da Nang — on the coastal road that wraps around the peninsula between the city's northern beach and the far headland. 8–10km from Da Nang city centre — closer than Non Nuoc, further than My Khe. Airport is 15–18 minutes. The Marble Mountains and Hoi An are further south across the city, requiring 35–50 minutes by Grab.

Son Tra Peninsula itself is one of Da Nang's best day-trip destinations — the monkey forest, the Lady Buddha statue, the coastal road drive. Premier Village guests have this essentially on their doorstep. The drive along the peninsula road from the hotel is one of the more scenic commutes available from any Da Nang hotel.

Beach access is a private cove below the villa development — not the long flat My Khe or Non Nuoc stretches, but a natural cove protected by the peninsula's geography. Calmer water than either main beach and genuinely private in a way that My Khe and Non Nuoc can't match.

The Rooms

Every unit is a villa — two-storey format with ground-floor living space and first-floor bedrooms, each with a private plunge or lap pool. The size range starts at around 200sqm for the smallest category and increases through the family villa configurations to the largest units. By any standard, the space is exceptional.

Design is contemporary Vietnamese — clean lines, natural materials, sea-facing orientation throughout. The private pools are properly maintained and genuinely swimmable rather than decorative plunge pools. The two-storey format gives genuine separation between living and sleeping areas, which makes the villas work for families as well as couples.

Rates from $380–600/night in shoulder season for standard villas, rising to $700–1,000+ in peak. This is premium pricing — more expensive per night than the Hyatt or Sheraton Grand's standard rooms. The comparison is not to those hotels' standard rooms but to their villa categories, where Premier Village competes directly and often wins on space and privacy.

Pool, Beach & Facilities

Private pool per villa — this is the fundamental differentiator. No shared pool infrastructure, no early-morning lounger competition, no other guests in your pool at 7am. The pool is yours for the duration of your stay. For travellers who have stayed at a resort with a shared pool and found it a compromise, Premier Village removes the compromise.

The cove beach below the development is private. Access is via a path from the villa level. The cove has calmer water than the main Da Nang beaches due to the peninsula's protection from open-sea swell. Beach facilities are available on the cove.

Shared facilities include a larger resort pool, spa, and restaurant — these supplement the private villa pools rather than replacing them. Spa quality is at the upper Da Nang end.

Food & Drink

Restaurant operates at a level appropriate to the villa rate — quality ingredients, properly executed menu, sea-view setting. For a property at this price point, the restaurant needs to do more than convenience feeding, and it does.

The villa format means you can have meals served at your private pool or outdoor dining area on request — this is the room service model that makes more sense at a villa property than at a standard hotel. Breakfast at your pool deck rather than at a buffet restaurant is the right way to use Premier Village.

Son Tra Peninsula location means outside dining options are limited compared to My Khe or city-centre hotels. The resort restaurant handles most meals for most guests. For specific evenings requiring city dining, a 15-minute Grab into Da Nang is achievable.

Every room is a two-storey villa with its own pool. Da Nang has nowhere else where that's the standard product.

The Good Stuff

  • Every room is a private pool villa — no shared resort pool, no lounger competition
  • Son Tra Peninsula position gives access to monkey forest, Lady Buddha, and coastal road drives
  • Private cove beach calmer than My Khe or Non Nuoc
  • Two-storey villa format works for families as well as couples
  • Closest major villa resort to Da Nang city centre

Watch Out For

  • Premium villa rates — significantly more expensive than hotel-format Non Nuoc resorts
  • No long flat beach access — cove only, not suitable for watersports or long beach walks
  • Son Tra peninsula means Hoi An and Non Nuoc day trips require crossing the city
  • Limited external dining options in the peninsula location

Who Should Stay Here

Families who want genuine villa privacy with hotel services. Groups and multi-family bookings. Anyone for whom the standard hotel room format doesn't provide the space and separation they want. Wrong choice for anyone who wants easy city access or plans to eat out regularly.
