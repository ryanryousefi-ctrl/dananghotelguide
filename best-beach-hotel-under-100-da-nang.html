Da Nang Beach Hotels Under $100: What to Know

Da Nang is one of the better value beach destinations in Southeast Asia. At the $70-100/night mark you can get a clean, comfortable room at a well-run 3-4 star property with pool access and easy beach access. What you won't get at this price is direct sand-to-lobby access, which most of the five-star resorts on Non Nuoc and My Khe have.

Most of the budget-friendly beach hotels in Da Nang are located along My Khe Beach, typically on the Vo Nguyen Giap road that runs parallel to the beach. The road is not wide and crossing is easy - "beach-adjacent" at My Khe genuinely means a 2-minute walk to the sand.

Seasonal pricing note: June-July DIFF fireworks festival adds 40-60% to rates across all properties. If your dates overlap with the festival (usually late June into July), expect prices to push well above $100. The cheapest months are October-November and January-February.

Best Picks Ranked

01
A La Carte Da Nang Beach
My Khe Beach · Rooftop infinity pool · Younger crowd
$70-100/night

A La Carte is the most talked-about budget-to-mid hotel on My Khe for good reason. The rooftop infinity pool is genuinely impressive - sweeping views east over the South China Sea and west to the Marble Mountains. The hotel is across the road from the beach (not on the sand directly), but the 360-degree rooftop is the draw. Room quality is solid for the price, the crowd trends younger and social, and the location puts you near the An Thuong restaurant strip. This is the top pick in this category.

Muong Thanh Luxury Da Nang
My Khe adjacent · Vietnamese chain · Direct beach area access

Booking Tips

Cheapest months: October-November (low season, some rain in Oct) and January-February (post-Tet quiet period). Rates at all these properties can drop 20-30% below mid-season averages.

Avoid for budget travel: June-July. The Da Nang International Fireworks Festival (DIFF) runs late June into July and hotel rates spike 40-60% across all categories. Even budget hotels exceed their normal pricing.

Common Questions

Beach Hotels Under $100: FAQ

Can you find beachfront hotels under $100 in Da Nang?+

Yes, but be precise about what "beachfront" means. Most hotels under $100 in Da Nang are beach-adjacent (across the road, or within 5 minutes walk) rather than sand-direct. True beachfront at this price is limited to Vietnamese-chain hotels like Muong Thanh. A La Carte and Fusion Suites are across the road from My Khe but have excellent pool experiences at height.

What are the cheapest months for beach hotels in Da Nang?+

October-November and January-February are the cheapest months for Da Nang beach hotels. October-November are low season (October can have rain). January-February post-Tet sees good rates before the spring surge. Avoid June-July when the DIFF fireworks festival adds 40-60% to rates across all properties.

What does $70 per night actually get you in Da Nang?+

At $70/night in Da Nang you can expect: a clean, air-conditioned room at a 3-4 star property, rooftop or main pool access, breakfast usually included, beach access within 5-10 minutes walk. You won't get direct sand-to-room beach access, large resort grounds, or 5-star service. But Da Nang is good value at this level compared to equivalent Asian beach destinations like Phuket or Bali.
