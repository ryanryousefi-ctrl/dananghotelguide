Azura Da Nang Review

RY
Ryan Yousefi
Da Nang Hotel Guide · Last updated March 2026 · ★★★★ · From $90/night
BoutiqueAdultsPersonal ServiceValue

Azura is My Khe's quiet overachiever — a boutique hotel that consistently delivers better than the category it occupies, mostly because it's small enough to actually care.

The hotel has about 80 rooms which means a staff-to-guest ratio the big resorts can't match. On check-in I was addressed by name at breakfast the following morning. That's not unusual at a place this size but it matters — a week at a large anonymous resort where no one knows you is a different experience to a week somewhere that notices when you come and go.

The location is the other argument for Azura: direct beach access, An Thuong restaurants within walking distance, city close enough that you're not taxi-dependent for every errand. It occupies the same My Khe strip position as the A La Carte and Wyndham but at a slightly lower price point and with less infrastructure.

The trade-off is real: smaller pool, no rooftop infinity spectacle, no extensive spa facilities. What you get instead is personal service and a calm atmosphere that the bigger My Khe properties don't offer. Which matters depends entirely on how you plan to spend your time.

Location & Getting There

My Khe Beach, Ngu Hanh Son — beach directly across the road, An Thuong restaurant district 5 minutes on foot. Airport is 12–14 minutes by Grab. Da Nang city centre is 8–10 minutes. Non Nuoc's big resort strip is 20 minutes south. All the numbers are slightly better than the Non Nuoc properties because you're sitting closer to the centre.

The An Thuong walking advantage is the same one the A La Carte trades on — you can go to dinner without calling a Grab every time. Da Nang's best independent restaurants are walkable: Nen, Madame Lan, the My Khe seafood strip along the beach road. After a few nights this starts to feel like living in the city rather than visiting a resort enclave.

For day trips: Marble Mountains 15 minutes by Grab, Hoi An 35–40 minutes. The central My Khe position means you don't lose an hour of your day every time you want to be somewhere else.

The Rooms

Rooms at 40–50sqm — contemporary, clean, properly maintained. The design uses Vietnamese materials without overdoing the ethnic-décor effect that some properties lean on too hard. Warm tones, natural textures, nothing offensive. Sea-view rooms on upper floors have proper My Khe Beach views and balconies that are actually usable rather than symbolic ledges.

Bathrooms are modern and above-average for the tier. Beds are genuinely comfortable — Azura uses better mattresses than most comparable My Khe properties. AC is powerful and quiet. Blackout curtains actually block light, which matters on a beach where sunrise comes early. These small functional details add up over a week-long stay.

The attention to maintenance is the thing that distinguishes Azura from properties at similar prices on this strip. Room condition stays consistent rather than deteriorating mid-week. That's a management attitude rather than a renovation investment.

Rates run $90–115/night in shoulder season. Peak July–August pushes $130–160. The value proposition holds most strongly in March–June when the weather is best and prices haven't hit summer peak.

Pool, Beach & Facilities

The pool is small — appropriate for an 80-room boutique — and as a result it's rarely crowded. In peak season when the A La Carte's rooftop fills up and the Wyndham's pool has queue systems for loungers, Azura's pool has space. That uncrowded quality is worth something in July.

Beach access is direct across Vo Nguyen Giap. My Khe here is well-maintained — municipal beach cleaning happens regularly, lifeguards are present during swimming hours in season. Waves pick up from June, which makes the pool the better swimming choice from mid-summer onward.

The spa is the genuine differentiator. Therapist quality is consistently above comparable hotels on this strip — this comes up in every review cluster and from every expat I've talked to who's stayed here. A 90-minute treatment in the 900,000–1,200,000 VND range is what you do at Azura that you wouldn't necessarily do at the A La Carte next door. Book ahead in peak season; the treatment rooms fill up.

Food & Drink

Restaurant handles breakfast and dinner competently. Breakfast has a solid Vietnamese station — proper pho and banh mi alongside the standard international spread. It's not the elaborate buffet of the five-star resorts, but at Azura's price point the breakfast quality is above expectations.

For dinner, An Thuong proximity makes the in-house restaurant feel optional rather than essential. You're genuinely within walking distance of better food: Nen and Madame Lan for serious Vietnamese, the My Khe seafood strip for casual grilled fish at 300,000–500,000 VND for two. The hotel restaurant works for nights when you can't be bothered leaving, which is a different standard than 'destination dining'.

The bar is quiet and pleasant — a good place to end an evening if you've had dinner elsewhere. Prices are fair for a hotel bar rather than tourist-strip inflated.

The spa and the personal service are Azura's real product. The beach and location come standard on this strip — the human-scale attention doesn't.

The Good Stuff

  • Boutique scale delivers genuinely personal service — staff know you by day two
  • Spa therapist quality consistently above comparable My Khe properties
  • An Thuong restaurant district walkable — best food access on My Khe
  • Rooms well-maintained and above-average for the tier
  • Uncrowded pool atmosphere even in peak season

Watch Out For

  • Small pool and limited facilities vs bigger My Khe resorts
  • No kids' club or family infrastructure
  • In-house dinner dining functional rather than interesting
  • Fewer room categories and less upgrade path than larger properties

Who Should Stay Here

Couples and adult travellers who want a quieter boutique beach property with good spa access and An Thuong restaurant convenience. Not the choice for guests who specifically want the A La Carte's rooftop view or the Wyndham's apartment-style kitchen format.
