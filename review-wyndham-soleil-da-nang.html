Wyndham Soleil Da Nang Review

RY
Ryan Yousefi
Da Nang Hotel Guide · Last updated March 2026 · ★★★★ · From $110/night
Condo HotelApartment FormatLonger StaysKitchen Access

The Wyndham Soleil is technically a hotel but staying here feels more like renting a contemporary Da Nang apartment that someone has stocked with good furniture and then positioned on My Khe Beach.

The all-suite format means every room has a proper living area, a kitchen, and laundry facilities. The studio-to-two-bedroom range accommodates couples, solo travellers, and small families without any of the categories feeling like a compromise.

It's on My Khe Beach at 216 Vo Nguyen Giap — the An Thuong area, same position as the A La Carte, with the same proximity to Da Nang's best independent restaurant strip. The design leans contemporary residential rather than resort: clean lines, proper kitchen equipment, the kind of apartment-style aesthetic that works well for stays of five nights or more.

The comparison to the A La Carte is inevitable given the location. Both are all-suite, both are in An Thuong proximity, both charge around $80–95/night. The Wyndham is slightly more apartment-feel and slightly less hotel-amenity-feel. Which you prefer depends on whether you want a hotel that happens to have kitchens, or an apartment that happens to have hotel services.

Location & Getting There

📷 Photo coming soon — location and surroundings

216 Vo Nguyen Giap, My Khe Beach — central strip in the An Thuong area. Beach across the road. An Thuong restaurants and cafés within 5-minute walk. Airport 12–14 minutes by Grab. City centre 8–10 minutes.

Same location fundamentals as the A La Carte — the An Thuong position is the main argument for this part of My Khe over the quieter Non Nuoc resorts. If you plan to eat out regularly, this location delivers access that the Non Nuoc strip can't match without taxi costs.

The Rooms

📷 Photo coming soon — room interior

Studio, one-bedroom, and two-bedroom suite configurations. Every category has a full kitchen — not a kitchenette, an actual kitchen with hob, refrigerator, oven or microwave, and proper utensils. The one-bedroom and two-bedroom configurations are a genuine alternative to booking two hotel rooms for a couple travelling together or a small family.

Design is contemporary residential — quality furniture, clean layouts, proper storage. The rooms feel like a well-appointed serviced apartment more than a hotel room. Balconies are functional. Upper-floor ocean-facing rooms have good sea views.

Rates from around $80–95/night in shoulder season. The two-bedroom configuration at $120–140/night is notably competitive for families or two couples travelling together.

Pool, Beach & Facilities

📷 Photo coming soon — pool and beach

Pool and beach access are provided — the pool is not the headline feature but is maintained and functional. The beach across Vo Nguyen Giap is central My Khe.

The main reasons to be here are the suite format, the kitchen, and the An Thuong proximity. The pool and beach are a given in this location, not a differentiator.

Food & Drink

📷 Photo coming soon — restaurant or bar

Breakfast is served but the kitchen in the suite is also functional — many guests use both. The An Thuong proximity means the bakeries, cafés, and breakfast spots of Da Nang's best area are a 5-minute walk. Vietnamese street breakfast from An Thuong's café strip is better than any hotel buffet.

For dinner, An Thuong is outside the door. The Wyndham's own restaurant does dinner but it's not the reason to choose this hotel — the reason is location and suite format.

The best choice for longer stays — real kitchen, real apartment space, and the best restaurant access of any beach property in Da Nang.

The Good Stuff

  • Full kitchen in every suite — genuinely useful for stays of 5+ nights
  • One and two-bedroom configurations competitive for families or groups
  • An Thuong location — same restaurant access advantage as the A La Carte
  • Apartment-style residential feel rather than hotel feel
  • Good value for the suite format and beach position

Worth Knowing

  • More apartment than hotel — guests who want resort amenities should look elsewhere
  • Beach is across the road, not direct access
  • Pool and facilities are functional, not distinctive

Who Should Stay Here

Longer-stay visitors who want the comfort of a proper apartment with hotel services. Families and groups of two couples who want bedroom separation at a competitive rate. Anyone who will use the kitchen and values having An Thuong's restaurant strip on foot.
