Mandila Beach vs Stella Maris Da Nang

Two mid-tier My Khe hotels with different personalities. One is built for families, the other leans boutique for couples.

Hotel A

Mandila Beach Hotel

VS

Hotel B

Stella Maris Da Nang

Editor's Pick

Mandila Beach Hotel

Why It Wins

Stella Maris has the better pool and the newer interiors. But Mandila is the safer, more proven booking for most travelers in this price tier: a larger property, a bigger ground-level pool that actually works for kids, and a longer track record of consistent reviews. Stella Maris pulls ahead specifically for couples who want a smaller, design-led stay.

A note on the photography above: we don't have a verified photo set for either specific property, so it shows the general My Khe Beach hotel strip rather than the exact buildings. The comparison below is based on published specs and guest-review patterns instead of a side-by-side photo match.

The 30-Second Verdict

Proven and practical versus small and design-led

Mandila Beach Hotel

Best For

Families and groups who want an established, larger beachfront hotel with reliable reviews

Wins

  • Larger ground-level pool, better for kids
  • More room categories and interconnecting options
  • Longer track record, more consistent reviews

Tradeoff

  • Functional 4-star design, nothing distinctive
  • Larger property feels less personal

Stella Maris Da Nang

Best For

Couples and solo travelers who want a smaller, newer, design-forward stay with a rooftop pool

Wins

  • Rooftop pool with beach views
  • Newer property, contemporary interiors
  • Smaller, quieter, more intimate feel

Tradeoff

  • Fewer rooms and room-category options
  • Less proven for families needing connecting rooms

Which Should You Book?

Match the hotel to your trip

Book Mandila Beach If You Want:

  • A larger pool that actually works for kids
  • More room categories, including interconnecting options
  • An established property with a longer, more consistent review history
  • Standard 4-star comfort at a fair rate

Book Stella Maris If You Want:

  • A rooftop pool with beach views
  • Newer, more design-forward interiors
  • A smaller, quieter property over a big hotel corridor
  • A stay built around a couple's trip, not a family one
Know Before You Book

The honest tradeoffs

Mandila Beach Hotel

Watch Out For
  • Functional 4-star design, don't expect a design statement
  • Larger property can feel more generic than boutique alternatives

Stella Maris Da Nang

Watch Out For
  • Fewer rooms means less flexibility if you need specific categories
  • Newer property with a shorter review track record

Final Verdict

Which one should you actually book?

If you're traveling with kids or want the safer, more proven option in this price tier, book Mandila. The bigger ground-level pool and wider room selection do more for a family stay than a rooftop view.

If you're a couple who wants a smaller, newer property with a rooftop pool and more design personality, Stella Maris is the better call, often at a similar or slightly lower rate.

Neither is a splurge stay. If your budget stretches further, Furama Resort a short distance away adds genuine five-star character for a modest premium.

Common Questions

Mandila Beach vs Stella Maris: FAQ

Is Mandila Beach or Stella Maris better in Da Nang?+

Mandila Beach is more family-suitable with a larger pool and established track record. Stella Maris is better for couples who want a rooftop pool, design-forward interiors, and a more intimate property.

Both offer good value on My Khe Beach at similar price points.

Is My Khe Beach the right choice for my trip?+

My Khe is the most central beach area, closest to Da Nang city restaurants and shops. Non Nuoc beach is quieter and closer to Hoi An.
