Mandila Beach Hotel vs Stella Maris Da Nang

Two upper-mid My Khe Beach hotels with very different personalities. Mandila is a proven beachfront with pool and family value. Stella Maris brings design and a quieter, more intimate feel.

✎ Written by Ryan Yousefi · 📅 Last updated: May 2026 · ⏰ 7-min read
Overview Location Rooms Amenities Best For Verdict

Hotel Overview

Mandila Beach Hotel Da Nang

  • ★★★★ Established beachfront hotel
  • Large property with multiple floors
  • My Khe Beach — central strip
  • Pool + direct beach access
  • Consistently solid review scores
  • From $70/night

Stella Maris Da Nang

  • ★★★★ Boutique-style beachfront hotel
  • Smaller, more design-forward property
  • My Khe Beach — central-north
  • Rooftop pool + beach access
  • Newer property with contemporary design
  • From $65/night

Both hotels occupy the popular mid-tier of My Khe Beach — priced between the basic guesthouses and the full five-star resorts. Mandila is the more established option with a larger footprint and family-friendly setup. Stella Maris is newer, has a rooftop pool, and leans into the boutique feel that younger travellers and couples often prefer.

Location is essentially equal. Both hotels sit on the My Khe strip with the same core advantages: close to the airport, close to An Thuong restaurants, and reasonably positioned for Hoi An day trips. The My Khe beach strip itself is lively with local restaurants and cafes within walking distance of both properties.

Rooms & Style

Mandila Beach — Rooms

  • Standard rooms comfortable and clean
  • Ocean-view rooms worth paying for
  • Functional 4-star design — nothing unexpected
  • Good for families with interconnecting options
  • Balconies on upper floors
  • Larger property means more room variety

Stella Maris — Rooms

  • Rooms with contemporary, design-led interiors
  • Rooftop access from upper floors
  • Better suited to couples and solo travellers
  • Newer property — facilities in good condition
  • Fewer rooms — quieter corridor experience
  • Check ocean-facing room types at booking

Stella Maris's rooftop pool is a genuine advantage — the views over the beach from an elevated position make it more enjoyable as a destination in itself. Mandila's ground-level pool is larger and more practical for families with children. Both have beach access and comparable F&B setups for this price tier.

Who Each Hotel Is Best For

Choose Mandila Beach Hotel if…

  • Travelling as a family or group
  • Ground-level pool is preferred for children
  • You want a proven, established beachfront
  • Reliability and consistency matter
  • You want more room category options
  • Standard 4-star comfort at a fair rate

Choose Stella Maris Da Nang if…

  • Travelling as a couple
  • Rooftop pool with views appeals
  • Design and aesthetics matter to you
  • Smaller, quieter property is preferred
  • Newer property condition is a factor
  • You find larger hotels generic and impersonal

Final Verdict

For families: Mandila is the sensible choice. The ground-level pool, larger room variety, and established track record make it the more dependable option for groups travelling with children.

For couples: Stella Maris edges ahead — the rooftop pool and bar, the newer design-led interiors, and the boutique atmosphere give it a personality that Mandila lacks. It's the more memorable stay at a similar or lower rate.

Common Questions

Mandila Beach vs Stella Maris: FAQ

Is Mandila Beach or Stella Maris better in Da Nang?+

Mandila Beach is more family-suitable with a larger pool and established track record. Stella Maris is better for couples who want a rooftop pool, design-forward interiors, and a more intimate property. Both offer good value on My Khe Beach at similar price points.

Is My Khe Beach the right choice for my trip?+

My Khe is the most central beach area, closest to Da Nang city restaurants and shops. Non Nuoc beach is quieter and closer to Hoi An. See our where to stay guide to compare beach areas and decide which suits your trip.
