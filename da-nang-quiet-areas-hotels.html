Editor's Area Pick

South Son Tra

Best balance of quiet and convenience

South Son Tra sits close enough to Da Nang to stay practical, far enough from the busiest My Khe and An Thuong energy to feel noticeably calmer. You get beach access, quieter stretches of coastline, and a real reduction in the noise and movement that makes central Da Nang feel relentless in high season.

Grab rides into the city core take around 15 minutes. You are not cut off. But you are far enough from the main hotel strip that the evening energy does not follow you back to the room. For most travelers who want quiet without full resort isolation, South Son Tra is the right call.

North Son Tra

Most secluded · True coastal escape

This is where Da Nang starts to feel less like a city and more like a coastal escape. The Son Tra Peninsula pushes north into the sea, and the resorts up here sit in genuine nature: jungle, mountain, private bays. The InterContinental Sun Peninsula is the obvious anchor.

The tradeoff is real: you are choosing peace over convenience. Walk-out dining is not happening. The city is 20-30 minutes by car. If you want An Thuong restaurants for dinner, budget the Grab. For couples and anyone whose goal is an actual escape rather than a city-base with a quieter address, North Son Tra delivers that better than anywhere else near Da Nang.

Peninsula nature Private bays High seclusion 20-30 min to city Luxury resort zone
Da Nang Signature Area Pick
Editor's Hotel Pick

Sandy Beach Non Nuoc Resort

Non Nuoc · Beachfront resort · Best quiet-hotel value

Sandy Beach works because it gives you what quiet travelers actually want: sand, space and enough distance from central Da Nang that the city stops following you back to the hotel. The Non Nuoc location is genuinely removed from the My Khe hotel strip noise, not a marginal upgrade, a real change in atmosphere.

The resort has enough infrastructure to stay put for a day or two without going anywhere. Pool, direct beach, grounds. If your goal is to get away from Da Nang's noise without paying InterContinental money, Sandy Beach is the compromise that makes sense. It is not the fanciest resort on this page. That is precisely the point.

Common Questions

Which area of Da Nang is quietest?
North Son Tra is the most secluded, the InterContinental peninsula resort sits here. For balance of quiet and convenience, South Son Tra works better. Non Nuoc gives you resort space and a wide beach farther south.
How far is Non Nuoc from Da Nang city centre?
Non Nuoc beach is roughly 8-12 km south of central Da Nang. A Grab typically takes 15-25 minutes depending on traffic. Further than An Thuong but the resort corridor is significantly quieter.
Is the InterContinental Da Nang far from everything?
Yes. That is largely the point. The InterContinental Sun Peninsula sits on a private peninsula in North Son Tra. You are 20-30 minutes by car from An Thuong and central Da Nang. It functions as a destination resort, not a city-base hotel.
Are there quiet hotels closer to central Da Nang?
Hilton Garden Inn and Peninsula Hotel Danang both sit closer to the city edge with a quieter feel than the central My Khe hotel strip. They are not secluded resorts but noticeably calmer than a midrange tower on Vo Nguyen Giap.
Is Sandy Beach Non Nuoc Resort good value?
Yes. Sandy Beach is the strongest quiet-hotel value on this page. Beachfront, resort grounds, pool, genuinely away from the city's noise, without InterContinental or Hyatt pricing. The best starting point for travelers who want downtime without a luxury budget.
