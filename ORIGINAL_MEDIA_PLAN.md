## Why This Matters for AdSense

Google's E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) framework rewards demonstrated real-world experience. Original photos taken in Da Nang by the site's author are the single strongest signal that the content comes from someone who was actually there — not AI generation or content farming.

## Priority Pages for Original Photography

### Tier 1 — Highest Impact (do first)

**1. `marble-mountains-da-nang.html`**
- Photos needed: cave interiors, views from the summit, the cave entrances, the pagoda, the staircase views
- Why: The audit found this page scores 0 on personal signals. A photo taken from the actual site would immediately differentiate it from AI content.
- Caption opportunity: "View from the Am Phu cave entrance, Marble Mountains, April 2026"

**2. `dining.html` and `da-nang-food-guide.html`**
- Photos needed: bun bo shop (Hung Vuong area), banh mi cart on My Khe, An Thuong street cafes, night market stalls
- Why: Food photography is the highest-trust signal for dining content. Stock photos of Vietnamese food are immediately recognisable as stock.

**3. `da-nang-first-time-visitors.html`**
- Photos needed: My Khe beach (with the actual hotel strip visible), An Thuong street, Han River at dusk, Dragon Bridge
- Why: This is the highest-traffic entry page and has no imagery that's identifiably from Da Nang vs. generic beach stock.

**4. `my-khe-beach-da-nang.html`**
- Photos needed: the beach at different times of day, the hotel roofline from the sand, the surf at different seasons
- Why: The page discusses beach safety and specific stretches — original photos showing the actual conditions would be highly valuable.

### Tier 2 — High Impact

**5. `da-nang-budget-guide.html`**
- Photos needed: a local pho bowl with price visible, Grab app on a phone, market stalls, local cafe interior
- Why: Budget content with real local prices is most convincing when it shows what those prices actually buy.

**6. `dragon-bridge-da-nang.html`**
- Photos needed: bridge at night during the fire/water show, crowd perspective
- Why: Any Da Nang resident can photograph this on a Saturday night. Not doing so is a missed opportunity.

**7. `ba-na-hills-guide.html`**
- Photos needed: Golden Bridge approach, cable car view, French Village
- Why: Ba Na Hills is one of the most-photographed sites in Vietnam. Original photos from an actual visit would be immediately distinguishable from press-supplied images.

**8. `son-tra-peninsula-da-nang.html`**
- Photos needed: Lady Buddha, monkey sightings (douc langurs), viewpoint over Da Nang Bay
- Why: The douc langur (red-shanked monkey) is one of the most photographed and distinctive features of Son Tra. A personal photo of one would be extremely strong E-E-A-T evidence.

## Technical Requirements for Site Integration

Images should be:
- Saved to `images/` directory with descriptive filenames (e.g., `marble-mountains-am-phu-cave-entrance.jpg`)
- Compressed to under 200KB for web display
- Width 1200px or 800px depending on use
- Tagged with `alt` text that includes the location and what's shown
- Captioned with date and location for credibility

Replace Unsplash stock images on Tier 1 pages first.
