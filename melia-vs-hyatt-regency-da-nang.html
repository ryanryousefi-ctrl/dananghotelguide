Melia Da Nang vs Hyatt Regency

Same Non Nuoc beach, very different price points. Melia is the design-forward value play; Hyatt Regency is the more complete, more expensive resort.

Non Nuoc Beach
Melia Danang Beach Resort
VS
Non Nuoc Beach
Hyatt Regency Danang
Editor's Pick
Melia Danang Beach Resort
Why It Wins

Melia typically runs 20-30% cheaper than the Hyatt Regency for the same beach and a genuine five-star room. Upgrade to The Level, its adults-only floor with a private lounge, and it beats the Hyatt on atmosphere for couples at a fraction of the price. The Hyatt only pulls ahead if you need more pools or a structured kids program.

The 30-Second Verdict

Who each hotel is actually built for

Melia Danang Beach Resort
Best for: value-conscious couples, design-focused travelers, anyone not tied to a specific loyalty program.
Wins
  • Runs 20-30% cheaper on comparable dates
  • The Level: a genuine adults-only floor with private lounge
  • More distinctive design aesthetic
Tradeoff
  • Only 2 pools versus the Hyatt's 5
  • Thinner loyalty program (Melia Rewards) than World of Hyatt
Hyatt Regency Danang
Best for: families with young kids, World of Hyatt members, guests who want the most complete resort on Non Nuoc.
Wins
  • 5 pools including a 100m beachfront pool
  • Camp Hyatt, a structured age-specific kids programme
  • Larger standard rooms (48 sqm vs Melia's ~35 sqm)
Tradeoff
  • Meaningfully more expensive for a comparable stay
  • No adults-only floor or equivalent to The Level

Side by Side

The photo comparison

The Properties

Melia leans into a bolder, more distinctive design aesthetic. The Hyatt is more conventional five-star, polished but less memorable to look at. Both sit on the same stretch of Non Nuoc Beach.

Hyatt Regency
Decide

Which should you book?

Book Melia If You Want:
  • A genuine five-star resort at a noticeably lower rate
  • The Level: an adults-only floor with a private lounge
  • A more distinctive, design-forward hotel
  • A smaller, more intimate resort feel
  • A couples trip without the Hyatt price tag
Book Hyatt Regency If You Want:
  • 5 pools, including a 100m beachfront pool
  • Camp Hyatt's structured kids programme
  • World of Hyatt points or elite status
  • Larger standard rooms (48 sqm+)
  • The most complete resort on Non Nuoc, budget aside
Know Before You Book

The honest tradeoffs

Melia Danang
Watch Out For
  • Only 2 pools; less to do on-site than the Hyatt on a rainy day
  • Melia Rewards is a much thinner loyalty program than World of Hyatt
  • Kids facilities exist but aren't the focus
Hyatt Regency Danang
Watch Out For
  • Meaningfully pricier than Melia for a comparable room
  • No adults-only floor or quiet-zone equivalent to The Level
  • Larger resort scale means less of an intimate feel
Final Verdict

Value wins unless you need the extras

If the price gap on your dates is more than about $40 a night, book Melia. You get the same beach, a genuinely well-designed room, and if you're a couple, The Level upgrade beats anything the Hyatt offers at a similar price. Melia is the better deal for most travelers who aren't loyalty-committed.

Book the Hyatt Regency if you're traveling with young kids and want Camp Hyatt, if 5 pools genuinely matters to how you'll spend your days, or if you're a World of Hyatt member who wants the points and status recognition. Those are real reasons to pay more, not just brand preference.

Our Pick: Melia Danang Beach Resort
