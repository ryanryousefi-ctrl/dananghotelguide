Hotel Overview

Melia Da Nang Beach Resort

  • ★★★★★ Design-forward resort
  • Melia Hotels International
  • Non Nuoc, south Da Nang
  • 2 pools + beachfront access
  • The Level (adults-only premium floor)
  • From $110/night

Hyatt Regency Da Nang Resort & Spa

  • ★★★★★ Full-service resort
  • World of Hyatt
  • Non Nuoc, south Da Nang
  • 5 pools including 100m beachfront pool
  • Camp Hyatt kids programme
  • From $150/night

The clearest difference between these two hotels is price, Melia often runs around 20–30% less than the Hyatt Regency for comparable dates. At that discount, Melia represents exceptional value for a genuine five-star on Non Nuoc beach.

The Hyatt is the more complete resort, more pools, better kids programme, stronger loyalty benefits, but you pay for it.

Rooms & The Level

Melia Da Nang, Rooms

  • Rooms from ~35 sqm with modern design
  • "The Level", adults-only premium floor with exclusive lounge
  • Ocean view rooms on upper floors
  • Melia brand design aesthetic, distinctive
  • Good value base category
  • Suite upgrade path available

Hyatt Regency, Rooms

  • Standard rooms from 48 sqm
  • Regency Club lounge access
  • Ocean-view rooms with direct sea views
  • Pool villas with private plunge pools
  • World of Hyatt upgrade eligibility
  • Larger standard room size

Melia's "The Level" is a genuine differentiator, a premium floor with an adults-only focus and exclusive lounge access that makes it a compelling couples resort within the wider family-friendly property. If you're a couple and can upgrade to The Level, Melia competes very favourably with the Hyatt at a meaningful price saving.

Hyatt clearly wins on pool count. Melia's unique advantage is The Level adults-only floor, something Hyatt doesn't offer.

Both spas are well-regarded. Hyatt's Camp Hyatt programme is better structured for families with children.

If the pool count is important to you, Hyatt is the stronger choice; if you're a couple seeking a premium resort at a lower rate, Melia is compelling.

Who Each Hotel Is Best For

Choose Melia Da Nang if…

  • Budget is a consideration, often ~30% cheaper than Hyatt
  • You're a couple and The Level adults-only upgrade appeals
  • Distinctive design matters more than conventional luxury
  • Melia Rewards membership
  • You want a beach resort without the full-service price tag
  • Smaller, more intimate resort feel preferred

Choose Hyatt Regency Da Nang if…

  • World of Hyatt membership (points, upgrades)
  • 5 pools is important for your stay
  • Camp Hyatt for children is a priority
  • You want the most complete resort on Non Nuoc
  • Large beachfront pool matters
  • Budget is not a constraint

Final Verdict

The value case for Melia is strong. If the price difference on your dates is more than ~$40/night, Melia on The Level is a hard deal to turn down, you get the same beach, a genuinely luxurious room, a distinctive design hotel, and an adults-only floor that the Hyatt can't match.

For families with children: the Hyatt Regency is the better call. Camp Hyatt is a more structured kids programme, and 5 pools gives the family more options throughout the day.

For World of Hyatt members: the loyalty maths will usually favour the Hyatt. Melia Rewards is a thinner programme globally.

If you're not loyalty-committed, compare the nightly rates and let the gap decide.

Common Questions

Melia vs Hyatt Regency Da Nang: FAQ

Is Melia or Hyatt Regency better in Da Nang?+

Hyatt Regency is the more complete luxury resort, 5 pools, Camp Hyatt kids programme, larger rooms, and World of Hyatt loyalty. Melia is the better value pick and has "The Level" adults-only floor that couples find attractive.

If budget matters, Melia; if you want the best resort on Non Nuoc, Hyatt.

Is Melia Da Nang good for couples?+

Yes, particularly with The Level upgrade, which provides an adults-only floor with an exclusive lounge. For couples who don't need a kids programme or extensive pool count, Melia at a lower rate is an excellent choice.
