How to check Da Nang hotel prices for your dates

We cannot show you a live rate; only a booking site can. Here is how to use one well: what to compare, what to read, and what to ignore.

The short answer

Pick your dates first, then compare the same room type across two or three hotels, then read the cancellation terms before you choose. Rates change with season, weekday, room category and demand, so a price you saw last month tells you little about your dates.

A six-step check

  • Fix your dates. Even a one-night shift can change the rate, particularly around weekends and holidays.
  • Compare the same room type. Compare like with like: a standard room at one hotel against a standard room at another, not against a suite.
  • Look at room categories. Views, bed configuration and club-lounge access can change the value of a stay; see our lounge guide for what club access involves.
  • Read the cancellation terms on the exact rate you are choosing; terms differ between rates at the same hotel.
  • Check what the price includes. Our price index compares nightly rates before taxes and resort fees, which can add roughly 10 to 20 percent; confirm the total on the booking page.
  • Shortlist two or three hotels and compare them side by side in our comparison tool, then check each one's rate.

Planning

When should you book your Da Nang hotel?

There is no single best day to book. What matters is which demand pattern your dates fall into. These patterns come from our own monthly price index and best-time guide; they describe typical seasons, not current rates.

Summer beach season (about May to August)

Vietnamese domestic demand peaks and beachfront resorts are busiest. Decide earlier if you need a specific resort or room type.

Tet (late January to mid-February)

Our index shows the highest rates of the year around Lunar New Year. Check Tet hotel advice and compare early if you are travelling then.

Rainy season (about September to November)

Rain and typhoon risk are highest in October and November and rates are typically lower. Choose hotels with good indoor options and keep plans flexible.

Resort or city hotel?

Resort stays depend on pools, beach and room type, so compare room categories. City hotels are easier to swap if plans change.

Because rates depend on dates, room type and demand, the only reliable way to know the price for your trip is to check your actual dates.

Common Questions

Da Nang Airport Hotels: FAQ

How far is Da Nang Airport from My Khe Beach?+

Da Nang Airport to My Khe Beach runs about 10-15 minutes by taxi or Grab, depending on traffic and which stretch of beach you're headed to. The My Khe strip hotels (A La Carte, and Pullman on the southern Bac My An stretch) are roughly 13-15 minutes by road without traffic (routing estimate).

Grab fares typically run $3-6, so there's no real cost penalty for choosing the beach over an airport-adjacent hotel.

Is there a free airport shuttle from Da Nang hotels?+

Most Da Nang hotels don't offer free airport shuttles. Some five-star properties run paid transfers at $15-25.

For most travelers, Grab (Vietnam's equivalent of Uber) is the more efficient option: fixed-price rides from the airport to any hotel in 10-20 minutes for $3-8. See our transport guide for full details.

Which hotel is closest to Da Nang Airport?+

City centre hotels on the Han River sit closest to Da Nang Airport, around 13-15 minutes by road without traffic (routing estimate). Novotel Da Nang Premier Han River and Grand Mercure Da Nang are among the closest major hotels.

But since even the furthest city or beach hotels are only 15-20 minutes away, airport proximity rarely settles anything on its own in Da Nang. Choose based on beach versus city preference, and save the closest-hotel question for layovers and early departures.
