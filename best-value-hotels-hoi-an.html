Good to know

Hoi An genuinely delivers mid-range value that's hard to find elsewhere in Vietnam's tourist circuit. A boutique hotel with a pool and breakfast on the river for $60–90 per night puts you within walking distance of the Ancient Town. The luxury tier (Anantara, Four Seasons) starts at $200+ — there's a clean gap and these properties fill it well.

Common questions

Value hotels Hoi An — FAQ

What's the best value hotel in Hoi An for couples?
Hoi An Chic Hotel for Old Town proximity and design at ~$80. Phu Thinh Boutique Resort if you want riverside and a pool closer to the Ancient Town. For beach-first couples, Sol An Bang at ~$85 or The Linh Seaside Villa at ~$65 are both strong picks.
Is it worth spending more on Anantara in Hoi An?
Yes, if you want the best service and pool experience in the walkable zone. Anantara is roughly double the price of Hoi An Chic or La Siesta, and it earns that gap. But the mid-range options genuinely deliver — the value gap in Hoi An isn't as stark as in some other destinations.
Is Hoi An or Da Nang cheaper for hotels?
They're close at budget and mid-range levels, but Hoi An often delivers better value at the mid-range ($60–100) tier. You get boutique quality, pool access, and walkability to the Ancient Town. See the Hoi An vs Da Nang cost comparison for a full breakdown.
