Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to dananghotelguide.com

Information we collect

Da Nang Hotel Guide is a static editorial website. We do not run a user registration system, collect personal data directly, or store any information on our own servers beyond standard web hosting logs (IP address, browser, pages viewed) which are automatically purged after 30 days.

If you contact us by email, we will retain your message and email address only as long as necessary to respond.

Affiliate links and third-party services

This site participates in the Booking.com affiliate programme. When you click a booking link and visit Booking.com, Booking.com's own privacy policy applies.

Booking.com may set cookies on your device when you arrive at their site. We do not receive any personal data from Booking.com about users who click our links.

We use Google Fonts, which is loaded from Google's servers. When your browser requests these fonts, Google's servers log your IP address as part of standard HTTP logging.

See Google's privacy policy for details.

Some images are served via Unsplash, videos via YouTube, and map tiles via OpenStreetMap, Esri and CARTO. These services receive your IP address when they deliver content.

Analytics and advertising

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used: which pages are read and which hotel booking buttons are clicked, together with your device type (mobile, tablet or desktop). We use Google AdSense to show ads. Some pages also use Ahrefs Web Analytics, which its provider describes as cookieless. We do not use other advertising pixels.

Cookies

This site uses these cookies and similar technologies:

  • Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_0T1H4G2N80, lasting up to two years) to measure visits and button clicks.
  • Google AdSense may set advertising cookies to serve and measure ads.
  • Booking.com and the CJ Affiliate network may set cookies when you follow a booking link, so a booking can be credited to us. A CJ script on our pages also reports page views to CJ.
  • YouTube, Google Maps and Google Fonts may set cookies or log your IP address when they load.

Your choice. A banner on your first visit lets you accept or reject Google Analytics and Google advertising cookies. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Vietnam these stay off until you accept. Elsewhere they are on unless you reject. You can change your choice at any time with “Privacy settings” in the footer. Refusing does not affect how the site works. Cookies set by sites you visit through our links (Booking.com, CJ) or embeds (YouTube) follow those services’ own policies and are not controlled by this choice. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Your rights

If you are an EU resident and believe we hold personal data about you (e.g. from email correspondence), you have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of that data. Contact us at hello@dananghotelguide.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.
