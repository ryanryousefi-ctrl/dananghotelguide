Check Your Airline

The airport isn't a round-the-clock operation, it generally runs through the day and evening rather than overnight. If you're on a very early or very late flight, check your airline or the terminal directly for current hours.

Terminals

Which Terminal Do You Need?

Two terminals, connected by a short covered walkway. Getting this backwards on departure day is the one mistake worth avoiding.

Terminal 1
Domestic

Flights within Vietnam, Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, Phu Quoc, Nha Trang and other domestic routes. Smaller and simpler than T2.

Terminal 2
International

Flights arriving from overseas. This is where immigration, the SIM counters, and currency exchange are.

Getting Between Them
A covered walkway connects the two terminals, roughly a five-minute walk. If you're connecting from an international arrival to a domestic flight (or the reverse), follow the terminal signage rather than going back out to the main road. Confirm your terminal on your boarding pass before you leave for the airport, especially on departure day when you're cutting it close.
Immigration

Passport Control

Not complicated, just occasionally slow.

Have your passport out and ready, along with your visa or e-visa paperwork if you need one. Follow the signage to the correct line and have your arrival card details in order before you reach the counter. Wait times vary a lot depending on how many international flights land around the same time as yours, there's no fixed number worth promising you.

Local Tip
If several international flights land in the same window, the queue can shift fast, both directions. A quiet counter can back up in ten minutes if two wide-bodies land together. Nothing to do about it except have your documents ready so you're not the one holding up the line.

Getting To Your Hotel

Grab From Da Nang Airport

If you're staying near My Khe, An Thuong, or the Han River, Grab is usually the simple answer.

Don't book a car while you're still wandering around baggage claim. Get outside, find the pickup area, then order. Da Nang Airport has a designated ride-hailing zone separate from the taxi rank, signed for app-based pickups, follow that signage rather than the general taxi queue.

Cash

Do You Need Cash At The Airport?

Probably not much, immediately, if your cards and data are working. Grab can run on a card in the app, and plenty of places in the city take cards or QR payment. That said, having some Vietnamese dong on you for the first day, a bottle of water, a quick meal, is still worth doing.

Currency

Currency Exchange

Exchange counters in the arrivals hall are convenient, open for every flight, and fine for changing a small amount to get through your first few hours. Rates elsewhere in the city can be better, so there's no need to change a large sum right at the airport. See our money exchange guide for where locals actually change money.

Luggage

Baggage + Oversized Items

Paid luggage storage is available near the international terminal, priced per bag per day, useful if you're landing and heading straight to Hoi An or the beach for a few hours before checking in anywhere. If you're travelling with surfboards, golf bags, or other oversized items, confirm handling with your airline before you fly rather than assuming standard drop-off applies.

Food

Need Coffee Or Food?

A few cafes and quick-service spots are available in both terminals, along with a handful of options in the public area before security. Nothing worth planning your trip around, but enough to get a coffee or a quick meal while you wait. If you land hungry and want something better, save it for the city, the food scene there is a big part of why people visit Da Nang in the first place.

Timing

Landing After 10pm?

Grab still works and is generally the easiest option late at night, though driver availability can thin out compared to daytime. Confirm your hotel's front desk hours before you land if you're arriving very late, some smaller properties run a night bell rather than a 24-hour desk. Food options narrow once you're in the city, but GrabFood can be useful once you've checked in if the kitchen at your hotel is closed. See the nightlife guide for what's still open late, and the Grab guide for delivery details.

Data matters more, not less, at night. Have your eSIM active before you land so you're not fumbling with a physical SIM counter in an emptier terminal at midnight.

Timing

Arriving At 6am?

Don't assume early check-in. Ask your hotel about storing luggage while your room is being prepared, most will hold bags without complaint. Go find breakfast, coffee is easy to find near most hotel clusters, and take a walk along My Khe while the beach is still quiet. Check-in usually opens early-to-mid afternoon, so plan your first few hours around that rather than around your flight time.

Don't Do This

Common Mistakes

Book Grab Before You Know Your Pickup Point
Confirm you're standing at the actual pickup zone first, then book. A pin set from inside the terminal can send your driver to the wrong side of the building.
Get Into The Wrong Car
Match the plate and driver name in the app before you get in, every time, even when you're tired.
Assume Hoi An Is A Quick City Ride
It's a proper transfer, not a hop across town. Budget real time for it and consider a private car if you're arriving late or with a family.
Wait Until Landing To Figure Out Data
Install your eSIM before the flight. You want data working the second you're off the plane, not after you've found a counter.
Forget Hotel Name/Address
Save it in your phone and have it written down as backup. Drivers appreciate the exact address, not just a neighbourhood name.
Assume Early Check-In
Rooms usually aren't ready before early-to-mid afternoon. Plan breakfast and a walk instead of showing up expecting keys at 7am.
Keep All Important Documents Only Online
Have a backup, a photo saved offline or a printed copy, in case your phone dies or you land without signal.

Common Questions

Da Nang Airport FAQ

How far is Da Nang Airport from the city and beaches?
About 3km from the city centre and 5km from My Khe Beach, one of the closest major airports to a beach district in Southeast Asia. A Grab to My Khe takes roughly 10-15 minutes. Hoi An is farther out, around 40-50 minutes.
Should I use Grab or a taxi from Da Nang Airport?
Grab is the simple answer for most arrivals. The price is fixed before you confirm and the pickup zone is a short, clearly signed walk from arrivals. If you need a walk-up option, use a metered taxi from the official rank rather than anyone approaching you inside the terminal.
How do I get data at Da Nang Airport?
Install an Airalo eSIM before you fly so you land already connected, or buy a physical SIM at one of the carrier counters in arrivals. Either way, get data sorted before you try to book a Grab.
Is Da Nang Airport open 24 hours?
No. It's not a round-the-clock facility, it generally runs through the day and evening rather than overnight. Check your airline or the airport directly if you're on a very early or very late flight.
Which terminal do I need at Da Nang Airport?
Terminal 2 (T2) handles international flights, Terminal 1 (T1) handles domestic flights within Vietnam. They're connected by a short covered walkway, roughly a five-minute walk between them.
Is there luggage storage at Da Nang Airport?
Yes, paid luggage storage is available near the international terminal, useful if you're landing and heading straight out to Hoi An or the beach before checking into a hotel.
Can I exchange currency at Da Nang Airport?
Yes, exchange counters are in the arrivals hall and are fine for changing a small amount to cover your first few hours. Rates elsewhere in the city can be better, see our money exchange guide for details.
