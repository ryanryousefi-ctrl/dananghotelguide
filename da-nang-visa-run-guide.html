Editor's Pick

Fly to Hong Kong

Turn the visa run into a vacation

Hong Kong is our favorite visa run from Da Nang. Flights can sometimes land in the same general bargain territory as Bangkok, so compare both before automatically choosing Thailand. For U.S. citizens in particular, Hong Kong can be remarkably straightforward for a short visit when current entry requirements are met.

The airport experience is a huge part of the appeal. Hong Kong International is one of the most efficient airports we've used, the Airport Express train runs on a near-99% on-time record and gets you into the city in about 24 minutes. Once you're through: Uber is easy, public transport is excellent, hotels exist across a wide range of budgets, and food and nightlife are everywhere. Unlike a border run, you're somewhere you might actually want to spend a couple of nights.

If you have to leave the country anyway, go somewhere fun.
The Catch

Hong Kong can be inexpensive to reach and expensive once you're there. Hotel prices vary enormously, rooms can be small, and food and drinks cost considerably more than Da Nang. Compare the total trip cost, not just the airfare.

Common mistakes

What Can Go Wrong

Wrong e-visa information

Name, passport, or date mistakes on your e-visa application can become major problems at the border.

Wrong entry point

Make sure your visa is valid for the specific entry point you're using where applicable.

Visa not ready

Don't assume an application will be approved simply because your flight home is tomorrow. Apply early.

Passport problems

Check validity and condition well before booking anything. Airlines will deny boarding over this.

Overstay

Don't treat this guide as a fix for an existing overstay. Get proper assistance directly.

Booking too tight

Leave yourself margin. Border wait times and processing delays happen.

Common Questions

What is the nearest visa run destination from Da Nang?
The nearest land border is the Lao Bao / Dansavanh crossing into Laos, approximately 4–5 hours by road. For flight-based runs, Hong Kong, Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur, and Singapore are all reachable direct from Da Nang. The right option depends on your visa type, budget, and how quickly you need to return.
Is Lynn Visa in Da Nang reliable?
Lynn Visa is a well-known, Da Nang-based visa assistance agency frequently referenced in expat forums and digital nomad communities. They assist with e-visa applications, organized visa runs, and related paperwork. As with any third-party agency, confirm current services, fees, and turnaround times directly before engaging them, and never hand over your passport without a written receipt. We do not endorse or guarantee any specific agency.
Can you re-enter Vietnam the same day after a visa run?
Same-day re-entry happens regularly at land borders and can be done in a single long day. Immigration officers have discretion to question very rapid or frequent re-entries. For flight-based runs, a minimum overnight is practically unavoidable given flight schedules.
How much does a Vietnam e-visa cost?
The government fee is currently USD 25 per application, paid on the official government portal. This fee is non-refundable. Unofficial third-party websites often charge significantly more for the same service, always apply directly through the official portal.
What happens if you overstay your Vietnam visa?
Overstaying carries real consequences: fines on departure, a formal record against your entry history, and potential re-entry bans. If your visa is approaching expiry, act before it expires. This guide is not a fix for an existing overstay, seek proper assistance directly.
