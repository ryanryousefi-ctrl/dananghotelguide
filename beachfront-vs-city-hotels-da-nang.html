Beachfront vs City Hotels

My Khe and Non Nuoc beach resorts against Han River city hotels. One wins on access to the sea, the other wins on price and walking distance to the actual city.

Category A
Beachfront Hotels
Category B
City Hotels

Editor's Pick
Beachfront Hotels
Why It Wins

Walking to the beach versus Grabbing to it changes the whole trip. Beach hotels run 20-40% more, but that premium buys spontaneous swims, better pools, and resort facilities city hotels just don't have room for. Unless you've got a specific reason to stay in the city, book the beach.

The 30-Second Verdict

Quick read before you scroll further

Beachfront Hotels (My Khe / Non Nuoc)
Best For

Leisure trips of 3+ nights, couples, families, anyone who came to Da Nang for the beach

Wins
  • Walk-out beach access, no Grab required
  • Larger resort pools, often lagoon-style
  • Kids clubs, spa, water sports, beach service
Tradeoff
  • 20-40% more expensive than city equivalents
  • Farther from Dragon Bridge, Con Market, city nightlife
City Hotels (Han River)
Best For

Business travelers, short stays, city-first explorers, digital nomads

Wins
  • 20-40% cheaper at the same star rating
  • Walkable restaurants, bars, and night market
  • Central for meetings and Hoi An/Hue day trips
Tradeoff
  • Beach is a 10-15 min Grab, $2-4 each way
  • Standard rooftop pools, no resort facilities
  • Not recommended for families with young kids

Which Should You Book

Match it to your trip

Book Beachfront If You Want:
  • A leisure trip of 3+ nights centered on the beach
  • A resort pool, spa, and kids club on-site
  • To swim spontaneously, not on a schedule
  • A quieter setting away from city traffic
Book City If You Want:
  • A short 1-2 night stay or business trip
  • Walkable access to Dragon Bridge and the night market
  • 20-40% lower rates at the same star level
  • A base for Hoi An or Hue day trips
Know Before You Book

The honest tradeoffs

Beachfront Hotels
Tradeoffs
  • 20-40% pricier than city hotels at the same star rating
  • City sights are a 10-15 min Grab away
  • Non Nuoc especially is isolated after dark, limited walkable nightlife
City Hotels
Tradeoffs
  • Beach is a planned trip, not a walk out the door
  • Pools are standard rooftop tanks, not resort-grade
  • Not a good fit for families wanting a beach holiday
Final Verdict

Which one should you actually book?

Book beachfront unless you have a specific reason not to. The pattern holds up trip after trip: travelers who book city hotels planning to hit the beach regularly end up going less than they meant to, because every trip takes effort. Travelers who book beach and want a night in the city just call a $3 Grab.

Book city if you're here for work with a packed meeting schedule, doing mostly day trips to Hoi An or Hue where the beach isn't the point, or the price gap genuinely matters to your budget. Otherwise, the beach hotel is the safer default, and you'll actually use it.
