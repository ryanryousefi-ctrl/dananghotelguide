The term gets thrown around loosely. Here's what it actually means in Da Nang, and which hotels back it up with real service.

What "Private Beach" Actually Means Here

Start with the legal reality: every beach in Vietnam is public property. The government doesn't grant hotels the right to shut out non-guests, full stop.

So strictly speaking, any listing that implies a hotel owns its beach is overstating things. What's actually on offer, and what the better Da Nang properties do deliver, is still worth paying attention to: a section of that public beach the hotel has taken over operationally, kitted out with its own staff and equipment.

In practice that means dedicated sunbeds you don't have to fight for, waitstaff who'll bring drinks and food to your chair, towels that get replaced instead of reused, a clear boundary marking where the hotel's zone ends and the open public beach begins, and often a beach bar built into the setup. For most people booking a "private beach hotel," this is the actual experience they're after. The gap between that and true exclusivity is a legal technicality, not a dealbreaker, but it's one you should know about going in.

What to check before you book: Make sure the beach is genuinely a walk-out situation from the hotel, not across a road. A few Da Nang hotels get marketed as beachfront when there's traffic between you and the sand.

A La Carte is the case in point here: guests have to cross Vo Nguyen Giap to reach My Khe Beach. Whatever that hotel offers, it isn't private beach access.

Hotels That Actually Manage Their Section Well

01
Hyatt Regency Da Nang Resort and Spa
Non Nuoc Beach · Long dedicated beach stretch · Best-managed hotel beach in Da Nang
$160-280/night

If you want the benchmark for how a managed beach section should run, this is it. The hotel sits directly on Non Nuoc Beach with a long stretch of hotel-controlled sand: no shortage of sunbeds, waitstaff working the rows, fresh towels on request, and a beach bar to anchor the whole setup.

Location does a lot of the work here too. Non Nuoc sits quieter than My Khe to begin with, so the hotel's section ends up noticeably less crowded than comparable properties further north.

What separates Hyatt Regency from others in this guide is consistency. Staffing doesn't thin out and service doesn't slip even when the hotel is fully booked.

For anyone building a trip around beach time specifically, this is the property to start with. It also pairs with a strong pool setup, six pools on top of the beach access, making it the most complete water-focused resort in the city.

Naman Retreat
Non Nuoc Beach · Quiet curated beach · Adults-focused · Intimate setting

Scale down and quiet up: that's the trade Naman Retreat makes. Its section of Non Nuoc Beach is smaller than Hyatt's, and it matches the rest of the property's unhurried, deliberate tone.

Fewer sunbeds sounds like a downside until you realize the guest count is scaled to match, so you're unlikely to feel boxed in. Staff stay attentive because they're not stretched thin.

Between the beach section, the garden layout, and Naman Spa (one of the better spas in the city), this is the strongest pick for travelers who rank quiet above everything else.

Furama Resort Da Nang
My Khe Beach · Garden-to-beach layout · Established resort · Good beach service

Furama has been running its beach operation longer than most competitors in this city, and it shows in the layout: a mature garden-to-beach path that puts you on sand directly, backed by sunbeds, service staff, and shade structures that feel built in rather than bolted on.

It doesn't have Hyatt Regency's polish, but the beach service holds up fine, and the My Khe location, the main strip in Da Nang, puts you closer to city restaurants and nightlife than the Non Nuoc properties. Solid value for what you're paying given the size of the operation.

My Khe Beach · Beach club operation · Direct beachfront · 5-star

Being a large five-star operation means the beach facilities are properly resourced rather than an afterthought. If you want a recognizable international brand paired with genuine walk-out beach access, this is a dependable choice.

Non Nuoc Beach · Adults-only · Small intimate beach · Spa-focused resort

Hotels Where Beach Access Isn't So Straightforward

A La Carte Da Nang Beach sits across Vo Nguyen Giap road from My Khe Beach, meaning guests cross a busy road to reach the sand. Whatever OTA listings call it, this isn't a beachfront property.

The rooftop pool there is genuinely good, but don't book it expecting private or even direct beach access.

InterContinental Da Nang Sun Peninsula is its own category. The hotel has a striking private cove beach on Son Tra Peninsula, reached by funicular from the main resort. It's secluded and dramatic, but the funicular ride turns it into more of a planned excursion than a walk-out-and-swim setup, which is worth knowing before you assume it works like the other resorts on this list.

Pullman Da Nang Beach Resort does offer beach access, but the stretch it operates on carries more general public traffic than what you'll find at the Non Nuoc properties. It's a functional beachfront hotel, just with a less clearly bounded section than Hyatt Regency or Naman Retreat manage.

Seasonal Note: When the Managed Beach Experience Falls Apart

Every hotel's beach operation, no matter how well-run in peak season, thins out from October through November. That's typhoon season, and even on days when the weather itself is fine, staffing gets cut and sections may close partially.

Some properties pull their sunbeds in entirely during this stretch and don't put them back out until February or March.

March through September is the real window for beach time in Da Nang, with May through August as the busiest stretch. April and September are shoulder months: weather holds up and rates ease off a bit.

If the beach is the reason you're booking, skip November through February. Sea conditions get rougher and the managed-beach experience gets inconsistent across every property, not just the weaker ones.

One more factor worth knowing: Non Nuoc Beach runs calmer than My Khe because it's more sheltered, which is one more point in favor of Hyatt Regency and Naman Retreat for anyone prioritizing beach time over everything else.

Common Questions

Private Beach Hotels: FAQ

Do Da Nang hotels have private beaches?+

Not legally, no. Vietnamese beaches are public land, so no hotel can lock guests-only access to the sand. What gets marketed as a "private beach" here is a section the hotel manages: dedicated sunbeds, towel service, its own waitstaff, and meaningfully less crowding than the public stretch.

It's a real step up from the public beach, just not the roped-off exclusivity the term implies.

Which Da Nang hotel has the best beach access?+

Hyatt Regency Da Nang runs the best-managed section in the city: a long stretch at Non Nuoc Beach with consistent service, sunbeds that don't run out, and far less crowding than My Khe. Naman Retreat is the quieter, more intimate option if that's the priority.

Sheraton Grand covers the My Khe side with a solid beach club operation.

Is the beach at Hyatt Regency Da Nang private?+

It's managed, not private by law. Hyatt Regency Da Nang controls a large section of Non Nuoc Beach, complete with its own sunbeds, waitstaff, and towel service. Vietnamese law doesn't allow guest-only exclusivity on any beach, but the hotel's section is clearly marked, properly staffed, and noticeably less crowded than the public areas around it.

What does private beach mean at a Vietnamese resort?+

In Vietnam, private beach means a hotel has a managed, dedicated beach zone with its own sunbeds, service staff, and clear demarcation from public beach areas. The beach itself cannot legally be restricted to guests only.

What you get is a significantly better organized experience: reserved sunbeds, towel service, beach bar access, and consistent staffing - rather than scrambling for public chairs.

Is Non Nuoc Beach less crowded than My Khe?+

Yes, significantly. Non Nuoc Beach (where Hyatt Regency, Naman Retreat, and Fusion Maia are located) is quieter than My Khe Beach in 2026.

My Khe is the main city beach with a long stretch of hotels, restaurants, and higher footfall. Non Nuoc, about 7km south, has fewer hotels and a more resort-focused atmosphere.

The beach at Non Nuoc is cleaner and less crowded outside peak Vietnamese holiday periods.
