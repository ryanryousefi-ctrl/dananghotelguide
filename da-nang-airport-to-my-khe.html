Da Nang Airport to My Khe Beach 2026

How to get from Da Nang International Airport to My Khe Beach — Grab, taxi, and what it should cost.

By Ryan Yousefi · Updated May 2026

All Options Step-by-Step Travel Times Night Arrivals My Khe Zones FAQ

All Transport Options Compared

Option Price Time Best For

Grab

For the majority of travellers, Grab is the right choice. The fare is fixed upfront, the driver is accountable, and the app handles navigation entirely.

A GrabCar to central My Khe Beach costs $4-6 USD. Download the app before you arrive and have a payment method set up.

You need data to use it - if you do not have a Vietnamese SIM, buy one at the airport first. See the SIM card guide.

Metered Taxis

Vinasun and Mai Linh taxis queue outside the arrivals exit. Both companies use government-sealed meters and are legitimate options.

Fares to My Khe run about the same as Grab. The advantage over Grab is that you do not need data or a working app - you get in and go.

The disadvantage is no upfront price and no GPS tracking through an app. Confirm the meter is running before you pull away from the kerb.

Avoid unmarked taxis: Drivers who approach you inside the arrivals hall or in unmarked vehicles consistently charge tourist prices - often 3-5x the correct fare. Walk past them and either use your Grab app or head to the official metered taxi queue outside the terminal.

"The trip from Da Nang airport to My Khe Beach takes 10–15 minutes and costs 60,000–90,000 VND by Grab. It's genuinely one of the shortest airport-to-beach distances anywhere in Southeast Asia — which is part of what makes Da Nang so easy to arrive at."

Private Transfers

If you have booked a mid-range or upmarket hotel, check whether they offer airport transfers. Most do, at rates typically between $10 and $20 for the airport-to-My Khe route.

It costs more than Grab but the driver is waiting with your name on a board, the vehicle is confirmed, and there is nothing to figure out on arrival. Worth considering for very late night arrivals, families with young children, or anyone who simply wants a smooth start to the trip.

Step-by-Step: Arriving at Da Nang Airport

Step 1: Clear immigration and customs. Da Nang airport is relatively quick - immigration lines for e-visa holders are separate and usually faster than on-arrival visa processing.

Step 2: If you need a SIM card, there are counters immediately outside the arrivals exit. Viettel is the most reliable network.

It takes 5-10 minutes and costs 100,000-200,000 VND for a tourist plan with sufficient data.

Step 3: For Grab - walk outside the arrivals hall to the designated ride-hailing zone. You will see signs.

Open the app once you have signal, set your destination, confirm the pickup pin is on the outer forecourt rather than inside the building, and book. Driver arrival is typically 3-8 minutes.

Step 4: For metered taxis - look for the official taxi queue immediately outside arrivals. Yellow Vinasun taxis and green Mai Linh taxis are both fine.

The queue is always present.

Step 5: If your hotel has arranged a transfer, look for drivers holding name boards in the arrivals area just beyond the exit gate.

Currency note: You do not need to exchange money at the airport just to get to My Khe. Grab can be paid through the app.

If you want cash for the taxi, there are ATMs inside the arrivals hall. Use Vietcombank or BIDV - lower fees than the dedicated exchange counters.

See the money exchange guide for full details.

Travel Times from Da Nang Airport

Da Nang airport sits roughly 3-4 kilometres west of central My Khe Beach. The route is mostly straightforward urban road - no highway, no significant traffic infrastructure.

Times below are in normal conditions:

Destination Distance Travel Time
My Khe Beach (central strip) ~4 km 10-15 min
My Khe (northern, near TIA) ~5 km 12-18 min
Non Nuoc Beach (resort zone) ~12 km 20-25 min
Son Tra Peninsula ~9 km 18-25 min
Da Nang city centre (Han River) ~4 km 10-15 min

Rush hour (7:30-8:30am and 5-7pm) can add 5-10 minutes. DIFF evenings near the Han River area can be significantly worse.

If you land during rush hour, the impact is minor - the airport-to-beach route avoids the worst congestion points in the city.

Night Arrivals - What to Know

Grab operates 24 hours at Da Nang airport but availability drops after midnight. Between midnight and 5am, you may wait 8-15 minutes for a GrabCar rather than the usual 3-5.

GrabBike drivers are often more available in late-night hours than GrabCar. If you are arriving at 1am or 2am and have normal luggage, expect a slightly longer wait but Grab will still work.

Metered taxis are always present at the airport regardless of hour. There are night-shift drivers who specifically work late arrivals.

If Grab is showing a long wait or high surge pricing at an unsociable hour, the taxi queue is a reliable backup.

Hotels that offer transfers often have late-night options - ask when you book. This is the most stress-free late arrival option if cost is not a concern.

See the full Da Nang airport guide for more detail on overnight arrivals and facilities.

Which Part of My Khe Beach Matters

My Khe is not one uniform beach zone. The strip runs roughly 10 kilometres, and where your hotel sits affects both your travel time from the airport and what the experience is like once you arrive.

Northern My Khe

The quietest section. This is where TIA Wellness Resort sits.

Fewer restaurants and beach clubs than central My Khe. Calmer water, less foot traffic from day-trippers.

Travel time from the airport is slightly longer than central - about 12-18 minutes. If you want a quieter, more resort-focused stay, this end is the right choice.

Central My Khe

The main tourist concentration. Most beachfront hotels, the highest density of seafood restaurants, beach volleyball, and the widest stretch of public beach.

Travel time from the airport is 10-15 minutes. This is where most first-time visitors to Da Nang end up, and it works well for that.

Southern My Khe / Non Nuoc

Where the major resort zone is - Naman Retreat, Fusion Maia, Premier Village, and others. The beach here is less busy with day-trippers.

The resort density is higher. It is 20-25 minutes from the airport.

If you are staying at one of the Non Nuoc resorts, tell your Grab driver the hotel name and confirm on the map - the resorts' entrances are spread across the coastal road and the pin can end up at the wrong gate.

Local Perspective

Ryan's Take on the Airport Transfer

The airport-to-My Khe trip is so short and so cheap that it is genuinely not worth overthinking. Book a GrabCar, it will be there in five minutes, and you will be at your hotel in fifteen.

The only times I have seen people have problems are when they try to negotiate with an unmarked taxi driver who is aggressively offering a "special price" in the arrivals hall, or when they try to book Grab before they have signal outside.

One practical note: if you are going to Non Nuoc and your hotel is a resort with a long driveway, drop the pin at the hotel name in the Grab app rather than on the map. Google's data for resort entrances in Non Nuoc is occasionally wrong.

The hotel name search is more reliable.

Common Questions

Airport to My Khe: FAQ

How long does it take to get from Da Nang airport to My Khe?+

10-15 minutes to central My Khe in normal traffic. The airport is just a few kilometres from the beach strip.

If you are heading to Non Nuoc (the southern resort zone), allow 20-25 minutes. Traffic is rarely severe on this route except during rush hour or public holidays.

Is there a bus from Da Nang airport to My Khe?+

There is no direct airport bus to My Khe Beach. Public buses exist in Da Nang but they do not serve the airport-to-beach route conveniently.

For most tourists, Grab or a metered taxi is the practical option. The trip costs $4-6 and takes under 15 minutes - there is no compelling reason to deal with public transport on arrival.

How much is a taxi from Da Nang airport to My Khe?+

A metered taxi (Vinasun or Mai Linh) from Da Nang airport to My Khe Beach costs roughly 80,000-130,000 VND ($3-5 USD), comparable to Grab. The advantage of Grab is the upfront price and driver tracking.

Avoid unmarked taxis - they consistently overcharge tourists, sometimes by 3-5x the correct fare.

Can I walk from Da Nang airport to My Khe?+

Technically yes at approximately 3-4 kilometres, but the route is not pedestrian-friendly, the heat is significant, and you are likely carrying luggage. A GrabCar costs $4-6 and takes 10-15 minutes.

Save your energy for the beach.

What is the difference between northern, central, and southern My Khe?+

Northern My Khe (closest to TIA Wellness Resort and Son Tra) is the quietest stretch. Central My Khe has more restaurants, beach clubs, and hotels.

Southern My Khe merges into Non Nuoc, where the major resort zone is. Where you are going within My Khe affects your Grab time - north is roughly 12-18 minutes from the airport, Non Nuoc is 20-25 minutes.

What should I do if Grab is not working when I land?+

First check your data connection - you need 4G signal outside the arrivals hall. If Grab is showing no drivers or a very long wait, use a metered taxi from the queue outside arrivals.

Vinasun and Mai Linh are both legitimate. Confirm the meter is on before departure and do not get into any unmarked vehicle, regardless of how convincing the driver's pitch is.
