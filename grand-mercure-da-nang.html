Grand Mercure Da Nang

“Central location with the fastest airport access in the city.”

4-Star City Hotel · Han River / City Centre

Our Take

The Grand Mercure is three kilometres from Da Nang International Airport, eight minutes by Grab.

Dragon Bridge is a five-minute walk and Han Market is ten minutes on foot.

The location is about as central as Da Nang gets, and for guests who are actually going to use the city, eat at street food stalls, visit the Cham Museum, explore the riverfront, do a day trip to Hoi An, this removes more logistical friction than any beach resort position can compensate for.

My Khe Beach is 10 minutes by Grab, which is accessible, not prohibitive.

Rooms run 32-45 square metres with a contemporary regional design language, local art and craft elements alongside modern fixtures.

Upper floors deliver Han River and city views that reward the upgrade, and executive floor rooms add lounge access with complimentary breakfast and evening canapes, which changes the value calculation notably for business stays or longer leisure visits.

Cleanliness and room maintenance are consistently cited as strong points in guest reviews.

The design doesn't try to be distinctive, what it does is maintain standard carefully, and after a week you notice that more than you would after one night.

The Rooms

The Rooms

Standard rooms run 32-45 square metres, clean and consistently maintained even if the property's age shows in some of the finishes. The executive floor is the real value play here: lounge access, complimentary breakfast, and evening canapes that make longer business stays or extended leisure visits noticeably better value than the room rate suggests on its own.

Which room would we choose? An executive floor room with a river-facing upper floor. The lounge access pays for itself on any stay longer than two nights, and the Han River view is worth the modest upgrade.

The Reason to Book It

Han River / City Centre Location

Dragon Bridge and Han Market are both within a short walk, and the airport transfer at 3km is one of the fastest in the city. For guests with an early morning flight or a late arrival, this single fact matters more than most amenities a resort could offer. Dragon Bridge's fire-breathing show on Friday and Saturday evenings is a five-minute walk away, and Accor ALL members earn here at a quality level that makes the points accumulate meaningfully.

Pool + Beach

Pool + Beach

A rooftop pool with city views, compact but functional and atmospheric at the right time of day, this is not a resort pool. It's a city hotel pool that's significantly more enjoyable at sunset with a drink than during peak afternoon swimming hours. My Khe Beach itself is a genuine 10-minute Grab ride away rather than walkable, so factor that into your daily plans if beach time matters. Manage expectations on the pool's size and enjoy it for what it is: a good sunset spot, not a swimming destination.

Food + Breakfast

Food + Drink

The main restaurant runs a solid international buffet breakfast and Vietnamese a la carte, competent rather than exceptional. Most guests end up outside the hotel for dinner, the surrounding streets have restaurants at every price point and quality level, and the Han River waterfront options are 10 minutes on foot. That's the structural advantage of staying in the city centre: the hotel restaurant doesn't need to be great when better options are a short walk away.

Who It's For

Families, Couples & Who It Suits

This suits business travellers, cultural visitors, and anyone who wants walking distance to Da Nang's restaurants and markets more than they want direct beach access. It works less well for families building a trip entirely around a beach and pool, the rooftop pool here is a city amenity, not a day-long destination. If the plan includes serious city exploring alongside beach time as a side trip, this location beats resort seclusion on pure convenience.

What We Like

What We Like

The airport distance

3km, eight minutes by Grab, the most useful proximity in the city for frequent travelers or anyone on an early flight.

Dragon Bridge access

A five-minute walk to the bridge and its weekend fire show, Han Market ten minutes on foot.

The surrounding streets

Restaurant streets immediately around the hotel mean the on-site dining doesn't need to carry the trip.

Accor ALL value

Loyalty points stretch further here, and the executive lounge upgrade is a genuinely good value add on longer stays.

The Tradeoff

This is not a beach hotel, My Khe Beach requires a genuine 10-minute Grab ride, not a walk. The rooftop pool is city-hotel scale, small and functional rather than a resort centrepiece, so don't book here expecting to spend full days poolside. On-site dining is competent but unremarkable, and the honest recommendation is to eat on the surrounding streets most nights. Some floors pick up evening noise from the streets below, worth requesting a higher floor if you're a light sleeper.

Location

Location

Han River, city centre, with Dragon Bridge a 5-minute walk, Han Market 10 minutes, and the airport just 3km away. What this means in practice: you can walk to most of the city's central landmarks and eat wherever you want without planning around taxis, but you're trading direct beach access for that convenience. My Khe Beach is a genuine 10-minute Grab ride, easy enough for a half-day trip but not a stroll-down-for-sunset situation.

Dragon Bridge: 5 min walkAirport: 3 km
Would We Stay Here

YES

For a city-focused trip, yes. The Grand Mercure does exactly what a city centre hotel should do: keep you close to everything, sleep you comfortably, feed you adequately at breakfast, and stay out of the way while you explore. When the airport is 3km away and Dragon Bridge is a five-minute walk, the hotel doesn't need to be more than that.
