Da Nang Hotel Comparison  ·  2026

Four Points by Sheraton vs Novotel Da Nang

The Four Points is on the beach. The Novotel Premier is on the Han River with one of the city's best sky bars. Two strong midrange options, one decision.

Four Points for beach access. Novotel for city exploration and the sky bar.
Overview Location Rooms Amenities Best For Verdict

Hotel Overview

Four Points by Sheraton Danang

  • ★★★★ Beachfront hotel
  • 200 rooms on My Khe Beach
  • Direct beach access, pool
  • Marriott Bonvoy loyalty (base tier)
  • ~15 min from city centre
  • From ~$65/night

Novotel Danang Premier Han River

  • ★★★★★ Upscale city hotel
  • 323 rooms, one of Da Nang's tallest
  • Han River, Level 36 Sky Bar
  • Accor ALL loyalty programme
  • 5 min from Han Market
  • From ~$80/night

The Four Points and Novotel Premier are at different parts of the city with different strengths. The Four Points is a solid beach hotel: nothing exceptional, but competent, well-located on My Khe, and priced to make the beach option accessible without going budget.

The Novotel Premier Han River is a proper upscale hotel. Level 36 is one of the most popular rooftop bars in Da Nang and the river views from high-floor rooms are genuinely impressive. If your priority is the beach, it is the wrong hotel. If you want a city base with evening options walking distance away, it is the right one.

This is the real comparison: beach access versus city walkability. The Four Points gives you the beach in the morning and costs you a Grab every evening. The Novotel gives you Dragon Bridge walking distance and costs you a Grab to the beach. Which matters more depends entirely on how you travel.

Level 36 at the Novotel is the facility that changes the decision for evening-oriented travellers. It is one of the best rooftop bars in the city, the views are strong, and it draws a mix of guests and locals. Four Points has beach access and a pool bar but nothing equivalent in the evening.

Who Each Hotel Is Best For

Book Four Points if…

  • Beach-first travellers who will be in the water every day
  • Marriott Bonvoy members accumulating points
  • Families where beach proximity matters most
  • Budget-conscious guests who still want brand reliability
  • Early-morning swimmers and surfers

Book Novotel Premier if…

  • City explorers who want to walk to dinner and Dragon Bridge
  • Business travellers with city-centre meetings
  • Accor ALL members
  • Guests who care about the rooftop bar experience
  • First-timers who want easy access to Da Nang's main sights

Final Verdict

The Four Points is a beach hotel that does its job: My Khe Beach, pool, competent rooms, reasonable price. Nothing here will disappoint, nothing will surprise you.

The Novotel Premier is the more interesting hotel. Level 36 is worth visiting even if you're not staying there, and the river views from upper floors are among the better city views in Da Nang.

If you are spending your days at the beach and your evenings eating locally, Four Points makes more logistical sense. If the plan involves city exploration, riverside bars, and Han Market mornings, the Novotel is better positioned.

Four Points vs Novotel Premier: FAQ

Four Points or Novotel Premier — which is better value?+

The Four Points is cheaper and gives you direct beach access. The Novotel Premier is $15–20 more per night and gives you a rooftop sky bar and river views. Value depends on your priorities: if beach time is the plan, Four Points is better value. If evenings and city access matter, the Novotel justifies the premium.
