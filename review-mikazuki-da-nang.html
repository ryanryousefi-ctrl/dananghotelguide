Mikazuki Japanese Resort & Spa Review

RY
Ryan Yousefi
Da Nang Hotel Guide · Last updated March 2026 · ★★★★ · From $120/night
WaterparkFamiliesJapanese OnsenAll-Inclusive Vibe

Mikazuki is the Japanese-owned resort south of Da Nang with a waterpark, onsen, and an operating philosophy that makes it feel like a domestic Japanese resort that happened to land on the Vietnamese coast.

Mikazuki Japanese Resorts & Spa sits 18km south of Da Nang proper — further from the city than the Hyatt or Marriott, and positioned at the edge of the Non Nuoc resort zone where it meets the quieter Quang Nam coastline. Its primary market is Japanese tourists and Vietnamese families who want waterpark access.

The onsen — a genuine geothermal-style hot spring bathing facility with multiple indoor and outdoor pools — is the feature that has no equivalent elsewhere in Da Nang. Japanese guests who know what an onsen is supposed to feel like and find it acceptable here is the strongest endorsement the facility can have.

The waterpark is the other headline: four water slides, a lazy river, and a wave pool are genuine family infrastructure, not a token splash area. For families with children between 5 and 14, Mikazuki's waterpark makes it the most entertainment-dense resort in the Da Nang area.

Location & Getting There

Vo Nguyen Giap, Hoa Hai Ward, Ngu Hanh Son District — 18km south of Da Nang city centre. This is the furthest south of the major Da Nang resorts, pushing into Quang Nam province. Airport is 22–25 minutes. Hoi An is 20–25 minutes south — the closest resort to Hoi An on the Da Nang side.

The southern position means the beach here is quieter and less developed than Non Nuoc proper. The trade-off: city dining is a longer Grab trip (90,000–130,000 VND each way), and the area around the resort has less infrastructure than the central Non Nuoc strip. For self-contained resort stays, this matters less. For guests who want to mix resort and city life, the distance is a real cost.

The Hoi An proximity is a genuine asset — 20–25 minutes south gives you easier access to Hoi An Ancient Town day trips than any other Da Nang hotel. For guests planning multiple Hoi An visits, the location advantage is meaningful.

The Rooms

Room categories from standard rooms through family suites to villa configurations. The scale of the property means room variety — there are enough room types to accommodate solo travellers, couples, families, and groups. Family rooms and interconnecting configurations are well-developed for the target market.

Rooms are Japanese-influenced in design — clean lines, natural materials, a preference for uncluttered spaces. The aesthetic is consistent throughout the property. Standard rooms run 40–50sqm. Family suite categories provide separate sleeping areas for children, which is the configuration Mikazuki's primary market needs.

Rates from $120–180/night in shoulder season for standard categories. The waterpark is included in the room rate for guests, which represents strong value given that the waterpark is also open to day visitors at 550,000–700,000 VND per person.

Pool, Beach & Facilities

The waterpark is the defining facility: four water slides including a long outdoor slide, a lazy river for low-intensity floating, a wave pool that produces genuine body-surf conditions, and a children's splash area for younger kids. For families, this is the reason to choose Mikazuki over every other resort in the Da Nang area — nothing else in the city has comparable water entertainment.

The onsen complex operates as a separate bathing experience distinct from the waterpark and outdoor pools. Multiple pools at different temperatures — hot mineral baths, cool plunge pools, outdoor soaking areas overlooking the grounds. The concept is genuinely Japanese in operation, not just themed. It's available to both guests and day visitors.

The beach in front of the resort is quieter than central Non Nuoc — the southern position means fewer resort neighbours and more untouched coastline. Beach service is available but the beach is less developed than at the main Non Nuoc cluster.

Food & Drink

Japanese cuisine is the dining emphasis — a genuine Japanese restaurant with properly made sushi, ramen, teppanyaki, and set menus that Japanese guests find authentic. For non-Japanese travellers, this is a different and more interesting hotel restaurant than the standard international-menu resort kitchen.

The breakfast buffet includes a Japanese breakfast section alongside the international spread — miso soup, pickles, grilled fish, rice. For guests who appreciate Japanese breakfast culture it's genuinely good. The Vietnamese breakfast section is less developed than at the main Vietnamese-brand resorts.

Pool bar and beach bar cover daytime F&B needs. For evening dining outside the resort, Hoi An (20–25 minutes south) opens up good options — the Ancient Town restaurants are destination-quality and the Mikazuki's position makes them the most accessible from any Da Nang area resort.

The waterpark and onsen combination has no equivalent in Da Nang. For families with waterpark-age children or guests who want a Japanese wellness experience, nothing else in the city competes directly.

The Good Stuff

  • Waterpark with slides, lazy river, and wave pool — the best family water entertainment infrastructure in the Da Nang area
  • Genuine onsen complex with no equivalent elsewhere in Da Nang
  • Closest major resort to Hoi An — 20–25 minutes gives best day-trip access
  • Japanese cuisine quality at in-house restaurants is above standard hotel kitchen
  • Quieter beach position away from central Non Nuoc resort density

Watch Out For

  • 18km from Da Nang city — the furthest major resort from the centre, Grab costs compound
  • Primary market is Japanese and Vietnamese families — atmosphere reflects this rather than international resort norms
  • Less international brand recognition than Hyatt/Marriott/Sheraton competitors
  • Onsen and waterpark can be crowded on peak-season weekends with day visitors

Who Should Stay Here

Guests who want the onsen experience alongside beach access. Families who want waterpark infrastructure. Japanese travellers or anyone with a strong affinity for Japanese hospitality standards. Wrong choice for guests wanting to be close to Da Nang city and its restaurant scene.
