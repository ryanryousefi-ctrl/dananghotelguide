Hotel Overview

Pullman Da Nang Beach Resort

  • 187 rooms, Accor/ALL property
  • My Khe Beach, northern section
  • $130-300/night
  • Large main pool, beach access
  • 10-min walk to An Thuong restaurants
  • City-accessible, couples-focused

Hyatt Regency Da Nang Resort & Spa

  • 197 rooms + 63 villas, World of Hyatt
  • Non Nuoc Beach, south Da Nang
  • $165-380/night
  • 6 pools + 120m lazy river
  • Camp Hyatt kids programme
  • 20 min from city, resort-focused

These are two of the most booked mid-to-upper luxury hotels in Da Nang. They compete on price, but they deliver different things.

Pullman is a hotel that happens to be next to the beach; you can walk out and explore the city and An Thuong's restaurants with ease. Hyatt Regency is a destination resort where you might spend four days and barely need to leave.

The location difference is the biggest factor here. Pullman's My Khe position gives you city access, walkable restaurants, and a lively beach scene.

Hyatt's Non Nuoc position is more isolated but quieter and roughly 10 minutes closer to Hoi An for day trips. If you're planning to eat out regularly, explore Da Nang's city, and use the hotel as a base, Pullman has a practical edge.

If you want to stay in and enjoy resort facilities, Hyatt's distance from the city is not a problem.

The pool comparison is not close. Six pools including a 120-metre lazy river at Hyatt versus one large pool at Pullman.

If you're spending most of your time in the water, Hyatt has more to offer. Pullman's counter-argument is that you don't need six pools when An Thuong's restaurants are a 10-minute walk and the city is 12 minutes by Grab.

Who Each Hotel Is Best For

Choose Pullman Da Nang if…

  • You plan to eat out regularly in Da Nang
  • City access and exploration matter to you
  • You're an Accor ALL member
  • Lower nightly rate is a factor
  • Airport proximity matters (early flights)
  • My Khe's livelier beach suits you

Choose Hyatt Regency if…

  • You want maximum pool and resort facilities
  • Travelling with children (Camp Hyatt)
  • You're a World of Hyatt member
  • Hoi An day trips are planned
  • Full resort immersion is the goal
  • Villa or lazy river experience wanted

Final Verdict

For couples who want to explore Da Nang properly - eat at local restaurants, see the city, maybe visit Marble Mountains or Ba Na Hills - Pullman is the more practical base. The beach access is good, the pool is solid, and An Thuong's restaurant strip being walkable is a genuine advantage that Hyatt cannot match.

For families, Hyatt Regency is clearly better: Camp Hyatt, six pools, a lazy river, and a contained resort environment that keeps everyone entertained without needing to Grab anywhere. For World of Hyatt members, the loyalty benefits make the price premium worth it.

Couples planning a resort-only stay with Hoi An day trips should also lean Hyatt. Everyone else who wants to actually explore Da Nang: Pullman.

Common Questions

Pullman vs Hyatt Regency: FAQ

Which is better for couples - Pullman or Hyatt Regency Da Nang?+

Pullman suits couples who want to explore Da Nang city and restaurants alongside beach time - the An Thuong restaurant strip is a 10-minute walk. Hyatt Regency suits couples who want full resort immersion and are happy to Grab into the city.

Both are good; the split is between exploration base vs resort cocoon.

Which is better for families - Pullman or Hyatt Regency Da Nang?+

Hyatt Regency is clearly better for families. Camp Hyatt is a structured kids programme, there are 6 pools including a 120m lazy river, and the Non Nuoc beach setting suits resort-focused family holidays.

Pullman has a large pool and beach access but no dedicated kids programme. See our family hotels guide for more options.

Can you walk to restaurants from Pullman Da Nang?+

Yes. The An Thuong restaurant area is roughly a 10-minute walk from Pullman Da Nang.

This is a genuine advantage over Hyatt Regency, which requires a 20-minute Grab ride to reach similar dining options. For independent travellers who want to eat out, Pullman's position is significantly more convenient.
