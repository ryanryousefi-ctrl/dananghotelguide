Pullman vs Hyatt Regency Da Nang

Similar price, opposite strategy. Pullman puts you next to the city; Hyatt Regency gives you six pools and a reason to never leave.

Editor's Pick

Hyatt Regency Da Nang Resort & Spa

Why It Wins

Six pools, a 120-metre lazy river, and Camp Hyatt beat one good pool and a walkable restaurant strip for most travelers. Pullman is the smarter pick only if you actually plan to leave the hotel and eat in the city every night.

Side by Side

The Pools

Pullman
Hyatt Regency

Pullman has one large, well-kept pool next to the beach. Hyatt Regency has six, including a 120-metre lazy river, which is simply more water than most guests will use in a stay.

Hyatt Regency — not close

The Property

Pullman
Hyatt Regency

Pullman sits on My Khe's northern section, a 10-minute walk from An Thuong's restaurant strip and 12 minutes from the city centre by Grab. Hyatt Regency's Non Nuoc site is a larger, more self-contained resort complex with 63 pool villas alongside standard rooms, but it's a 20-minute Grab from town.

Depends on your trip style

The Rooms

Pullman
Hyatt Regency

Pullman runs 187 rooms with no villa option. Hyatt Regency has 197 rooms plus 63 pool villas, giving guests who want more space and privacy an option Pullman doesn't offer at any price.

Hyatt Regency — for room variety

The honest tradeoffs

Pullman Da Nang
Watch Out For

  • Beach here is livelier and more local, less of a private resort feel
Hyatt Regency Da Nang

  • Higher rate than Pullman for comparable room categories
  • Non Nuoc setting is quieter but more isolated
Final Verdict

Which one should you actually book?

For couples who want to explore Da Nang, eat at local restaurants, and use the hotel as a base rather than a destination, Pullman is the more practical choice. The beach is good, the pool is solid, and An Thuong being a 10-minute walk away is a real advantage Hyatt can't match.

For families, and for anyone who wants a resort stay with everything on site, Hyatt Regency wins clearly. Camp Hyatt, six pools, a lazy river, and pool villas justify the extra Grab ride into town.

Our Pick: Hyatt Regency Da Nang for resort stays, Pullman for city-based trips

Common Questions

Pullman vs Hyatt Regency: FAQ

Which is better for couples - Pullman or Hyatt Regency Da Nang?+

Pullman suits couples who want to explore Da Nang city and restaurants alongside beach time - the An Thuong restaurant strip is a 10-minute walk. Hyatt Regency suits couples who want full resort immersion and are happy to Grab into the city.

Both are good; the split is between exploration base vs resort cocoon.

Which is better for families - Pullman or Hyatt Regency Da Nang?+

Hyatt Regency is clearly better for families. Camp Hyatt is a structured kids programme, there are 6 pools including a 120m lazy river, and the Non Nuoc beach setting suits resort-focused family holidays.

Pullman has a large pool and beach access but no dedicated kids programme. See our family hotels guide for more options.

Can you walk to restaurants from Pullman Da Nang?+

Yes. The An Thuong restaurant area is roughly a 10-minute walk from Pullman Da Nang.

This is a genuine advantage over Hyatt Regency, which requires a 20-minute Grab ride to reach similar dining options. For independent travellers who want to eat out, Pullman's position is significantly more convenient.
