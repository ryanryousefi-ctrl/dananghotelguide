Budget Guide — Da Nang — 2026

Da Nang on a Budget

Real daily costs from $30 to $200 — with actual numbers, not ranges so wide they mean nothing.

Updated 2026 · Local editor

The rumors are true. Yes, you can live in Da Nang for $1,000 a month -- comfortably. Yes, you can spend $1 on arguably the best meal of your life. Yes, the cost of living here is, pound for pound, better than anywhere else in the world. That part isn't up for debate.

The thing is -- everyone is different, and the less you spend has to directly correlate with how comfortable you are being uncomfortable. A $20/night hotel a block from the beach may be a perfectly fine place to sleep. It is not a place for a family of four to spend more than two hours at a time. A $200/night hotel might serve you worse pho for $20 than you'd find on the street for $1.50.

Your goals and your tolerance define your budget here, not the other way around. But apples to apples -- your money goes farther in Da Nang than almost anywhere else on the planet. That's not a selling point. It's just a fact.

Daily Budget Tiers

What Your Budget Gets You

Budget
$30–45 USD/day

Hostel dorm or basic guesthouse. Street food and banh mi for most meals. Grab for transport. One cheap beer per day. No paid attractions. Hoi An day trip by public bus.

Mid-Range
$70–110 USD/day

3-star beach-area hotel. Mix of local restaurants and An Thuong cafes. Grab everywhere. One paid attraction or day trip. Two to three drinks per day. All-in, comfortable travel.

Comfort
$140–200 USD/day

4-star hotel with pool, breakfast. Restaurant dinners, one or two cocktails. Private car for day trips. Ba Na Hills entry included. No budget compromises on quality.

Accommodation

Where to Stay Without Overpaying

The My Khe beach strip (Vo Nguyen Giap) runs the full spectrum from budget guesthouses to 5-star resorts, often within 100 metres of each other. A budget guesthouse back from the beach costs $12-22/night with clean rooms, AC, and hot water. Beachfront is roughly double. The price jump is for the view and direct sand access — the beach is public and 2-3 minutes' walk from anywhere on the strip.

An Thuong neighbourhood (the expat area behind the beach) has the best mid-range value: walkable to the beach, walking distance to the best cafes and bars, and hotel prices roughly 15-20% cheaper than the beachfront strip.

Food & Drink

Where Your Food Money Goes

Street food and markets are the best value. A full breakfast at Con Market (banh mi, xoi, or pho) runs 25,000-55,000 VND ($1-2.40). A sit-down bowl of mi quang — Da Nang's signature noodle dish — costs 40,000-60,000 VND at a local restaurant. Tourist-facing restaurants on An Thuong are still affordable by Western standards ($5-15 per person) but charge 3-5x what a local place charges for similar quality.

The Hoi An day trip food budget is worth noting separately: Old Town restaurants are markedly more expensive than Da Nang for the same dishes, partly because most are licensed to operate within the historic zone. Budget an extra $8-15 per person for food if you're spending a day in Hoi An.

Common Questions

How much does a trip to Da Nang cost per day?
Budget travellers can manage on $30-45 USD per day (hostel, street food, Grab). Mid-range travellers spending on a decent hotel, sit-down restaurants, and day trips should budget $70-110 per day. Comfort travellers with a 4-star hotel, quality meals, and private transfers typically spend $140-200 per day. All estimates exclude flights.
Is Da Nang cheaper than Hoi An?
Da Nang is slightly cheaper for accommodation and significantly cheaper for food and transport. Hoi An's Old Town restaurants are priced for tourists. Street food and local restaurants in Da Nang give better value. Day-trip costs from Da Nang to Hoi An add around $4-20 round-trip depending on whether you use the public bus or Grab.
How much does accommodation cost in Da Nang?
Hostels and budget guesthouses: $12-22 per night. Mid-range hotels near the beach: $45-80 per night. Good 4-star hotels: $90-140 per night. Prices are 20-30% higher in peak season (June-August) and around Vietnamese public holidays.
What is the cheapest way to get around Da Nang?
Grab is cheapest for most in-city trips — 25,000-60,000 VND ($1-2.50) per ride. Renting a scooter costs 100,000-150,000 VND ($4-6) per day if you have experience. Taxis with meters also work, but Grab removes the need to negotiate.
Is Da Nang cheap for food?
Street food and local restaurants are very affordable. A full meal at a local restaurant runs 50,000-80,000 VND ($2-3.50). Tourist-facing cafes on An Thuong average 150,000-250,000 VND ($6.50-11) per person. A local beer costs 30,000-50,000 VND. Fine dining at top hotel restaurants runs 500,000-1,200,000 VND ($22-50) per person.
