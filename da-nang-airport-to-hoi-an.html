Other Ways to Get There

Traditional
Official Taxi
250,000–400,000 VND to city · Metered

Walk outside arrivals to the official taxi rank. Use Vinasun (green/white) or Mai Linh (green) only. Do not go with anyone who approaches you inside the terminal. Slightly more expensive than Grab but reliable when the app isn't loading. Meter runs from the moment you get in — for Hoi An, confirm the driver understands the destination before leaving.

Groups & Families
Private Van
$25–50 USD typical · Pre-book

Four or more people with full luggage will find a standard GrabCar a squeeze. A 7-seat GrabCar is available in the app, or pre-book a dedicated van through your hotel or a transfer service. For large families heading to Hoi An, split across two Grabs or book a single van — the van usually works out cheaper per person and easier in practice.

Included
Hotel Transfer
Varies — often $15–30 USD · Pre-book via hotel

Most luxury resorts in Da Nang and Hoi An offer private airport transfers, and at the upper end of the market this is often the smoothest option. Intercontinental, Four Seasons, and Naman all provide this service. Compare the hotel's quoted rate against Grab before deciding — for a 10-minute ride to My Khe, Grab is almost always cheaper.

Going straight to Hoi An?

The Airport to Hoi An Decision

Hoi An is 25–28km from Da Nang Airport — about 30–40 minutes in normal traffic. That's far enough that the transfer choice matters more than it does for a 10-minute ride to My Khe.

Grab works and is the right call for most people. But on a Hoi An run, a pre-booked car makes more sense than it would for a short city hop. The longer distance, the late-night arrival risk, and the unfamiliarity of finding the right Grab zone after a long flight all tip the balance. A driver with your name on a sign, confirming the destination before departure, is worth something on this particular journey.

Staying at Hoiana?
Hoiana Resort & Golf

Hoiana is 10km south of Hoi An Ancient Town — roughly 55km from Da Nang Airport. Allow 50–65 minutes and expect to pay 350,000–500,000 VND by Grab, or $25–40 USD for a private car. Both Rosewood Hoi An and Hoiana Hotel & Suites offer private airport transfers. For guests arriving late or in a group, this is the cleanest option — ask your resort at booking.

Common Questions

How do I get from Da Nang Airport to my hotel?
Grab is the easiest option for most travelers — fixed upfront price, no negotiation, pick up outside arrivals. Download the app before your flight. For late-night arrivals, families, or travelers who want everything arranged in advance, a pre-booked private transfer is worth the extra cost.
How much does a Grab from Da Nang Airport cost?
To My Khe Beach: typically 60,000–100,000 VND (~$2.50–$4). To the city centre or An Thuong: 70,000–120,000 VND. To Hoi An: 200,000–300,000 VND. Prices are shown upfront in the app before you confirm — no surprises.
Can I use Grab from Da Nang Airport?
Yes. Grab works at Da Nang Airport. Open the app after clearing customs, book a GrabCar, and pick up at the designated ride-hailing zone outside arrivals. Wait times are typically 3–8 minutes in the day. Download and set up payment before your flight — it saves time on arrival.
How far is Hoi An from Da Nang Airport?
About 25–28km by road. In normal traffic, 30–40 minutes by Grab or private car. Can stretch to 45–60 minutes during peak hours (7–9am and 5–7pm weekdays). Hoiana Resort is roughly 55km from the airport — allow 50–65 minutes.
Which taxis are official at Da Nang Airport?
Vinasun (green/white livery) and Mai Linh (green) are the reputable metered companies. The official taxi rank is outside the arrivals exit. Do not go with anyone who approaches you unsolicited inside the terminal.
Is there a bus from Da Nang Airport to Hoi An?
No direct public bus. Shared shuttle buses run the route for 40,000–80,000 VND per person but make multiple stops and take 60–90 minutes. Not ideal for groups with luggage or late-night arrivals. Grab or a private car is more practical in most situations.
