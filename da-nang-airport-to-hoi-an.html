Airport to
Hoi An

Four transport options, real costs, and which one to use based on your situation. The answer is almost always Grab.

Home › Hoi An › Airport to Hoi An

Four options

Da Nang Airport to Hoi An

30–40 minutes by car. About 25km on a straight road south. Grab is best for most people; private transfer is better for late arrivals and families.

📱

Recommended for most

Grab Car

200,000–300,000 VND (~$8–12)

30–40 min · Pickup outside arrivals

Open Grab after clearing customs. Price shown upfront — no haggling. 4-seat covers 1–3 people with standard luggage. GrabCar 7-seat available for larger groups. Prices may surge during peak arrival banks (11am–1pm and 7pm–10pm).

🚗

Private Car / Hotel Transfer

$15–25 USD fixed rate

30–40 min · Driver meets you in arrivals

Pre-book via your hotel or a private car company. Driver waits with your name on a board. Best for late-night arrivals, families with young children, groups with heavy luggage, or first-time Vietnam visitors who want no friction.

🚕

Metered Taxi

250,000–350,000 VND

30–40 min · Official taxi rank outside terminal

Use Vinasun (green/white) or Mai Linh (green) only. Do not go with anyone who approaches you unsolicited in the terminal. Slightly more expensive than Grab but reliable when the app isn't working.

🚌

Shared Shuttle Bus

40,000–80,000 VND per person

60–90 min · Multiple stops

Cheapest option. Multiple stops make the journey slower. Not all shuttles drop to your hotel — may need a short xe om from the drop point. Best for solo budget travellers arriving in daylight with manageable luggage.

Good to know

There is no direct train or bus service from Da Nang Airport to Hoi An. The four options above are the complete picture. Download Grab before your flight and set up payment in advance — it saves significant time on arrival. If you arrive without data, the official taxi rank is outside the arrivals exit.

For four or more travellers, a pre-booked private car often works out cheaper per person than individual Grab seats.

The journey

"The road south from Da Nang passes Marble Mountains and Non Nuoc Beach before turning inland toward the Ancient Town. On a clear day the drive is genuinely scenic."

Practical tips

What to know before you arrive

Most of these trip-ups are avoidable with 5 minutes of prep before boarding your flight.

Before you fly

Download Grab

Install Grab and add a payment method. Cash also works on arrival. Without the app, you're limited to taxis.

Luggage

GrabCar 4-seat limit

One large bag + one carry-on per person is manageable. Four people with four large bags need a 7-seat or private car.

Late arrivals

Pre-book after 10pm

Grab availability is patchy after 10pm. If you land late, a pre-booked hotel transfer is worth the extra cost.

Staying at Hoiana? Different journey

Airport to Hoiana Resort

Hoiana is 10km south of Hoi An Ancient Town — roughly 55km from Da Nang Airport, adding 15–20 minutes to the journey. Grab or taxi costs 350,000–500,000 VND and takes 50–65 minutes. The cleanest option: ask Hoiana or your hotel to arrange a private airport transfer.

⏱

Journey time

50–65 minutes from Da Nang Airport

💰

Approximate cost

350,000–500,000 VND or $20–30 USD private

🏨

Best option

Hotel-arranged private transfer — meets you in arrivals

Rosewood Hoi An and Hoiana Hotel & Suites both offer private airport transfers. For guests at either property, this is the easiest and most reliable option — particularly for late-night arrivals when the resort is 55km from the airport.

Common questions

Airport to Hoi An — FAQ

How much does it cost to get from Da Nang Airport to Hoi An?
Grab car: 200,000–300,000 VND (most travellers). Private hotel transfer: $15–25 USD. Metered taxi: 250,000–350,000 VND. Shared shuttle: 40,000–80,000 VND per person but takes 60–90 minutes with stops.
How long is the drive from Da Nang Airport to Hoi An?
30–40 minutes by Grab or private car in normal traffic. Can stretch to 45–60 minutes during peak hours (7–9am and 5–7pm on weekdays). The shared shuttle takes 60–90 minutes due to multiple stops along the route.
Can I use Grab from Da Nang Airport to Hoi An?
Yes. Grab works well at Da Nang Airport. Open the app after clearing customs, book a GrabCar, and pick up at the designated ride-hailing zone outside arrivals. Wait times are typically 3–8 minutes. Price is shown upfront — no haggling.
Is there a bus from Da Nang Airport to Hoi An?
No direct public bus. Shared shuttle buses run the route and cost 40,000–80,000 VND per person, but they make multiple stops and take 60–90 minutes. Booking desks operate in the arrivals area. Not ideal for large groups or late-night arrivals.
