Da Nang Airport to Hoi An

Every way to get from Da Nang Airport (DAD) to Hoi An — Grab, private car, taxi, and shared shuttle compared with real costs, timings, and practical tips.

✎ Written by Ryan Yousefi, Da Nang & Hoi An resident · 📅 Updated: May 2026 · ⏰ 8–min read

Bottom line

Quick answer — how to get there

Quick answer
Grab car is the best option for most travellers — metered, reliable, and usually 200,000–300,000 VND for the 30–40 minute journey. Pre-book a hotel transfer if arriving late at night or with a large group. Shuttle buses are the cheapest option but take 60–90 minutes with stops. There is no direct train or bus service.

Option 1, Recommended

Using Grab from Da Nang Airport

Grab Car (GrabCar)
200,000–300,000 VND

Grab is Southeast Asia's dominant ride-hailing app and works well at Da Nang Airport. Open the Grab app after clearing customs and baggage claim, set your destination to your Hoi An hotel, and book a GrabCar. The price is shown upfront — no haggling, no surprises.

The Grab pickup zone is outside the terminal arrivals area. Follow the signs for ride-hailing or taxi pickup. Wait times are typically 3–8 minutes. Drivers are usually reliable and professional.

Practical tips:
  • Download Grab before your flight and set up payment (credit card or cash on arrival)
  • GrabCar 4-seat is fine for 1–3 people with standard luggage
  • GrabCar 7-seat is available for larger groups — book from the app
  • Prices may surge during peak arrivals (international flight banks at 11am–1pm and 7pm–10pm)
  • Cash payment is accepted — have VND ready if you prefer not to link a card
  • If the app shows no Grab available (rare), proceed to the official taxi rank

Option 2

Private car & hotel transfers

Hotel Transfer / Private Car
$15–25 USD

Most Hoi An hotels offer airport transfer services, either directly or via a partner company. You pre-book through the hotel website or via email, and a driver meets you in arrivals with your name on a board. The rate is usually $15–25 USD for a private car to central Hoi An, though luxury properties may charge more for their branded vehicles.

When to pre-book a private transfer:
  • Arriving after 10pm when Grab availability can be patchy
  • Travelling with young children or elderly guests who benefit from meet-and-greet
  • Groups with substantial luggage where a 7-seat vehicle is needed
  • When you'd prefer the convenience of someone waiting — especially after a long-haul flight with connections
  • First visit to Vietnam where navigating an unfamiliar app with no local SIM is a concern

Independent private car companies also operate this route — search for "Da Nang Airport to Hoi An private car" to find options at similar or lower rates than hotel transfers.

Option 3

"The trip from Da Nang airport to Hoi An takes 45–60 minutes by private taxi and costs 300,000–450,000 VND. Grab is cheaper but sometimes surge-prices. For 4+ people, a booked private transfer is usually the cleanest option."

Metered taxi

Metered Taxi (Official)
250,000–350,000 VND

Official metered taxis are available at Da Nang Airport from the designated taxi rank outside arrivals. The journey to central Hoi An typically runs 250,000–350,000 VND on the meter. Reputable companies to use:

  • Vinasun — green and white, widely considered the most reliable
  • Mai Linh — green, also reliable and widely used

Avoiding taxi scams: Da Nang Airport has unofficial taxi touts who may approach you inside the terminal or outside. Do not go with anyone who approaches you unsolicited. Use only vehicles with the company name clearly marked on the door and a functioning meter. If a driver says the meter is broken or offers a flat rate, decline and find another taxi.

Taxis are slightly more expensive than Grab for the same journey, but are useful when Grab isn't available or when you prefer not to use the app.

Option 4

Shared shuttle bus

Shared Shuttle Bus
40,000–80,000 VND per person

Shared shuttle buses run between Da Nang Airport and central Hoi An, making multiple stops along the route. They're significantly cheaper than Grab or taxi, but the journey takes 60–90 minutes because of the shared stops and route. Not all shuttles drop directly to your hotel — you may need a short xe om or Grab from the drop point.

Shuttle is best when:
  • You're travelling solo or with one partner and carrying manageable luggage
  • Cost is the primary consideration
  • You have no time pressure and are arriving in daylight hours
Shuttle is not ideal when:
  • You have large or multiple bags (difficult to manage on a shared vehicle)
  • Arriving at night (some shuttles don't operate late)
  • You have young children or mobility considerations

Shuttle booking desks operate in the Da Nang Airport arrivals area. You can also pre-book online through companies like Baolau or similar aggregators.

Practical advice

Tips for the journey

Late arrival tip
If your flight arrives after 10pm, Grab availability can be limited and wait times longer. Pre-booking a hotel transfer or private car is worth the slightly higher cost for peace of mind on a late arrival.
Families with prams and young children
Standard Grab and taxis don't have child seats. Pre-book a private transfer if you're travelling with infants or need a specific vehicle type. Most private car companies can accommodate prams in the boot without issue.
Get a Vietnamese SIM card on arrival
Grab requires mobile data and a phone number. SIM card counters are in the Da Nang Airport arrivals area — Viettel and Vinaphone both work. A tourist SIM with data costs around 100,000–200,000 VND. This is the single most useful thing you can do immediately upon arrival.
Exchange some cash at the airport
Even if you plan to pay Grab by card, having 500,000–1,000,000 VND in cash for your first day is sensible. ATMs and exchange counters are available in the arrivals area. Airport exchange rates are reasonable — you don't need to exchange a large amount.

What about motorbike taxis? Grab motorbike (GrabBike) is available and costs 80,000–130,000 VND to Hoi An — but it's only practical for solo travellers with a small daypack. Luggage is impossible on a motorbike for this distance. Street xe om (motorbike taxis) at the airport are not recommended — agree a price if you do use one, and expect significant negotiation.

Common questions

Da Nang Airport to Hoi An — FAQ

How long does it take to get from Da Nang Airport to Hoi An?
The journey is typically 30–40 minutes by car or Grab, depending on traffic. The distance is approximately 25–30km along a good road. Rush hour (7–9am and 4:30–6:30pm) can add 10–15 minutes. Late afternoon arrivals sometimes coincide with heavier traffic near Da Nang city centre.
How much does a Grab from Da Nang Airport to Hoi An cost?
A Grab GrabCar from Da Nang Airport (DAD) to central Hoi An typically costs 200,000–300,000 VND depending on car type, time of day, and demand. GrabBike is cheaper (80,000–130,000 VND) but only practical without luggage. Prices may increase during surge periods — evening arrivals after international flights can sometimes push rates higher.
Can I use Grab at Da Nang Airport?
Yes. Grab works at Da Nang Airport. Open the app after clearing customs and baggage claim, set your destination to Hoi An, and book. Wait times are usually 3–8 minutes. The Grab pickup zone is outside the terminal — follow signs for ride-hailing pickup. Do not go to the taxi queue if you've already booked a Grab.
Should I pre-book a car or just use Grab?
For most arrivals, Grab is fine and easier than pre-booking. Pre-booking makes more sense for: late-night arrivals after 10pm, groups with a lot of luggage, families with young children, or if you want someone waiting with a name board. Hotel transfers tend to cost $15–25 USD and include that meet-and-greet convenience.
Is there a shuttle bus from Da Nang Airport to Hoi An?
Yes, shared shuttle buses operate between Da Nang Airport and central Hoi An. They typically cost 40,000–80,000 VND per person and stop at multiple points. Journey time is longer (60–90 minutes) because of the shared stops, but they're a budget-friendly option for solo travellers with manageable luggage.
