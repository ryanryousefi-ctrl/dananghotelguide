Da Nang Beach Guide — 2026

Non Nuoc Beach

Da Nang's resort coast: quieter sand, huge pools, and some of the city's best hotels, with one important tradeoff.

Updated August 2026 · Ryan Yousefi · 12 min read

The three things you're actually deciding on

Beach, Resort, Location

The Beach Quieter Than My Khe Wider sand, calmer water, nobody selling sunglasses off a tray.
The Resort This Is Why You Come Pool complexes, spas, villas. The resort is the vacation here.
The Location The Tradeoff Nothing to walk to. You're not isolated, you'll just use Grab more.
The short version

Should You Stay At Non Nuoc?

✓Yes, if
  • You're booking a five-star and plan to actually use the pool, not just look at it
  • You've got kids and need a resort big enough to absorb three straight days of pool time
  • You want the calmest, widest stretch of sand in Da Nang
  • Hoi An is a bigger priority than the city centre for this trip
  • You're a couple who wants to not think about anything for a week
  • You're chasing Bonvoy or Hyatt points and want to burn them somewhere good
✕Maybe not, if
  • You want to walk to dinner without getting in a car
  • Nightlife or bar-hopping is part of the trip
  • You're on a budget and were hoping to eat cheap, local food most nights
  • You want a neighbourhood with some energy after dark
  • This is a short trip and you don't want travel time eating into it
  • You like the idea of wandering somewhere new on foot in the evening

The largest resort cluster in Da Nang

Where To Stay At Non Nuoc

Seven properties share this stretch of coastline, each doing a slightly different job. Filter by what matters to you.

Family Pick
Hyatt Regency Danang
Central Non Nuoc · beachfront · Best for families

Six pools, a lazy river, and a kids club that actually functions, meaning your kids stay in it and you get an hour to yourself. Rooms run large and the layout spreads guests out so it never feels as packed as the room count suggests. Not the most interesting resort on the strip. It's the one that rarely disappoints anyone, which is its own kind of achievement.

Pool Pick
Sheraton Grand Danang
Non Nuoc resort corridor · beachfront · Best for pool space

Biggest footprint on the strip, multiple pool areas spread across several buildings, so there's always somewhere to swim even at full occupancy. That scale cuts both ways, it can feel less personal than the Marriott's villas or the Melia's smaller-scale design. Marriott Bonvoy members often find better redemption value here than at the Marriott property itself.

See Sheraton Grand Prices →
Bonvoy Luxury Pick
Danang Marriott Resort & Spa
Central Non Nuoc · beachfront villas · Best for Bonvoy and privacy

The villa product is the reason to pick this one, standalone units with private pools that make a resort strip with six other hotels on it feel genuinely private. The Quan Spa is the best on the strip and Bonvoy redemptions run strong here at peak season. Central pool and restaurant scene feels a notch below the Hyatt's if you're not using the villas.

See Marriott Prices →
International Resort Pick
Melia Danang Beach Resort
South Non Nuoc · beachfront · Best for a quieter big-brand stay

Not marketed as adults-only, but the design and guest mix skew that way, which suits couples who find the Hyatt too busy or the Sheraton too sprawling. Strong pool setup, design-forward rooms, quieter position toward the southern end of the strip. Skip it if you need a dedicated kids club, the offering here is lighter than Hyatt's or the Marriott's.

See Melia Prices →
Design / Wellness Pick
Naman Retreat
Just south of the main strip · Best for design and a sense of place

Architecturally unlike anything else on this coastline. Vietnamese architect Vo Trong Nghia used bamboo structures and native planting throughout, and the long central pool is one of the best in the country. Sits slightly further from the main cluster, technically between Non Nuoc and Hoi An, which some guests read as a plus and others as an inconvenience.

See Naman Retreat Prices →
Wellness Pick
TIA Wellness Resort (formerly Fusion Maia)
South Non Nuoc · Best for a genuine reset

Different from everything else on this list on purpose. Yoga, spa treatments, and included wellness programming instead of a standard pool-and-restaurant setup. Room rates run high for what you get in pure hotel terms, but that's not what you're buying here. Don't book it expecting Hyatt-level pool scene, book it expecting to feel noticeably better after three days than you did on arrival.

See TIA Wellness Prices →
Value Pick
Sandy Beach Non Nuoc Resort
Non Nuoc Beach · Best for the same coastline at a lower rate

The answer for people who want the Non Nuoc setting, the wide beach, the pool time, without paying five-star international-brand rates. Facilities are simpler than the Hyatt or Marriott and the finish level is a step down, but you're still on the same sand and the same calm water. Sensible pick if the location matters more to you than the brand name on the towel.

For the resort-and-golf crowd

The Golf Corridor

If golf is part of the trip, Non Nuoc's location makes logical sense, both major courses in the area are a short drive from the resort strip, closer than they'd be from the city centre.

BRG Da Nang Golf Resort

A Greg Norman-designed links course on the coast north of the Non Nuoc strip, roughly 20-25 minutes by car. Consistently ranked among Vietnam's best.

Montgomerie Links

Colin Montgomerie's design sits between Da Nang and Hoi An, which puts it closer to Non Nuoc than to most other bases in the city.

Set your expectations correctly

What About Restaurants?

Stay In

Every resort on this strip runs multiple restaurants, and the quality is genuinely good, several run breakfast spreads better than half the sit-down restaurants on An Thuong.

The tradeoff is price. Resort dining runs at resort prices, and if you eat every meal in-house for a week-long stay, it adds up fast.

Grab Out

There's almost nothing to walk to between the resorts, no independent cafes, no beach bars, no street food carts. If you want variety, you're calling a Grab.

The nearest local food clusters sit up near the Marble Mountains junction. See the dining guide for specific spots worth the trip.

Grab Changes The Equation

Non Nuoc reads as isolated until you factor in Grab. A car to the city, Marble Mountains, or Hoi An is cheap and shows up fast most hours of the day. You're not stranded, you're just planning around a car instead of your feet.

What the beach itself is actually like

The Beach

Swimming
Calmer than My Khe's exposed northern stretch. The Marble Mountains headland gives a little shelter from northerly swell. Best February through August.
Sunrise
East-southeast facing, so mornings light up early and clean. Worth setting an alarm for at least once.
Walking
Wide, uncrowded sand in front of the resorts. Fewer vendors and foot traffic than My Khe's central strip.
Resort Beach
Every major property runs sunbeds, towel service, and a beach bar directly on the sand in front of the hotel.
Seasonal Conditions
October and November bring typhoon swell from the South China Sea. Conditions get unpredictable, follow the flag system, it's there for a reason.
Lifeguards
All the major resorts run trained lifeguards and flag systems at their beach fronts during the main season.

Set The Alarm Once

Non Nuoc faces east-southeast, so the light comes up fast and clean over open water. No headland blocking it, no buildings in the way. One morning is enough to see why people bother.

Why parents keep coming back here

Why Non Nuoc Works For Families

Big Pools
Multiple pools per property means kids and adults aren't fighting over the same 20 metres of water.
Kids Clubs
The Hyatt's in particular is staffed and structured enough that kids actually want to stay in it.
Beach
Wide, gentle-entry sand with lifeguards on duty at the main resort fronts.
Large Resort Grounds
Room to spread out, nobody's tripping over another family's sun loungers.
Villas & Residences
The Marriott's villa product gives bigger families their own space and a private pool.
Quiet
No bar noise carrying into your room at 11pm. It's a resort strip, not a nightlife strip.

Best bets: Hyatt Regency, Sheraton Grand, or the Danang Marriott if you want the villa option. See the full family hotels guide.

For a different kind of trip

For Couples

Strip out the kids-club energy and Non Nuoc is one of the better couples bases in Vietnam, quiet, spa-heavy, and built around resort dining rather than nightlife. Naman Retreat and TIA Wellness Resort lean into this directly, and the Marriott's villas give privacy neither of the bigger resorts can match. If a spa day and a good beachfront dinner sound like a real vacation to you, this is the right stretch of coast.

Set expectations before you book

What Non Nuoc Is Not

Not An Thuong
No bar street, no walkable cluster of restaurants and cafes. That neighbourhood energy lives at My Khe.
Not A Nightlife District
There's essentially nothing to do after dinner except go back to your room or the resort bar.
Not A Walk-Everywhere Neighbourhood
Everything between resorts is a Grab ride, not a stroll.
Not Central Da Nang
You're 20 minutes from the city, not in it. Plan trips in rather than expecting to duck out casually.

And that's the point. Non Nuoc trades neighbourhood energy for resort quality and quiet, on purpose, and it does that trade well.

The idea most people miss

Do Both. Split The Stay.

You don't have to pick one beach for the whole trip. Book 3-4 nights at My Khe or An Thuong first, walk to dinner, try the bars, get the city energy out of your system. Then move to Non Nuoc for the last 2-3 nights and let the resort take over.

It solves the actual tradeoff instead of forcing you to choose a side of it. You get the food and the nightlife and the walkability early in the trip, then the pool, the quiet, and the wide sand right before you fly home. Most Da Nang trips run 6-8 nights, split-staying costs you one extra hotel checkout and nothing else.
