Da Nang vs Hanoi

Beach city or historic capital? Here's the simple answer and which one fits your trip better.

Seconds, not paragraphs

The Short Answer

Choose Da Nang
A vacation

Beach, resorts, easier pace, families, Hoi An nearby, outdoor life.

Choose Hanoi
A culture-first city trip

History, food, the Old Quarter, culture, cooler-season city energy.

Our take
It depends on time

Want a vacation: Da Nang. Want a culture-first city trip: Hanoi. Have enough time: do both.

Editor's Pick

Da Nang

The Easier Vacation

Da Nang is easier to relax into. My Khe Beach outside the hotel, short Grab rides across a manageable city, real family infrastructure, and Hoi An 30 minutes away. Son Tra Peninsula and the Marble Mountains sit within a short drive, and the resorts here deliver genuine 5-star product without asking much of you.

The tradeoff: it doesn't have Hanoi's historical depth or street-level intensity. What it gives you instead is a slower rhythm you can actually settle into.

Hanoi for History and Culture

When the City Itself Is the Attraction

Hanoi wins when history and street life are the point. The Old Quarter's 36 streets, Hoan Kiem Lake, the Temple of Literature, museums layered across a thousand years of capital city, and a café culture built into the architecture. One gives you My Khe outside the door. The other gives you the Old Quarter outside the door.

Tradeoff: no beach, heavier traffic, more intensity, and less resort-style downtime. It doesn't lose to Da Nang, it wins a completely different trip.

Common Questions

Should I visit Da Nang or Hanoi?
They serve very different trips. Da Nang is Vietnam's beach city, modern, comfortable, built around the ocean. Hanoi is Vietnam's capital, a 1,000-year-old city with the Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem Lake, and proximity to Ha Long Bay. For a full Vietnam itinerary of 10 days or more, visit both. Flying between them takes 1 hour 20 minutes.
Is Da Nang or Hanoi better for families?
Da Nang is generally easier for families. Beach infrastructure keeps younger children naturally entertained, Ba Na Hills offers a full-day resort experience, and the city's layout is less chaotic than Hanoi's Old Quarter. Hanoi is manageable with children but the traffic intensity requires more management.
Does Hanoi have beaches?
No. Hanoi is inland, the nearest beach options require a 3–4 hour journey. This is the most decisive practical difference between the cities: if beach access is any part of your trip goal, Da Nang is the answer.
What is the weather like in Hanoi vs Da Nang?
Hanoi has four proper seasons, a cold winter (December–February), a hot wet summer, and a pleasant autumn. Da Nang has two seasons: a long hot dry season (February–August) and a shorter wet/typhoon season (September–November). For beach weather year-round, Da Nang wins. For genuine seasonal variety, Hanoi's late September to November window is exceptional.
Which is better for a first trip to Vietnam?
Both work as first stops but offer different entry points. Hanoi gives the most complete picture of Vietnam's historical identity. Da Nang is more comfortable and less overwhelming. For a first trip combining both, fly into Hanoi, travel south to Da Nang, and exit through Ho Chi Minh City.
