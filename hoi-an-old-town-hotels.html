Three zones

What does walkable actually mean?

"Walkable to Old Town" gets used loosely. Here's what the three zones actually feel like.

Under 5 min
You are inside the Ancient Town. Maximum immersion, minimum facilities.
5–15 min
Riverside strip. Anantara, La Siesta, Almanity. Walk across a footbridge — pleasant 12–15 min.
15+ min
Outer zone. Use a Grab motorbike for consistency. Not really walkable.

Good to know

The Ancient Town's pedestrian zone closes to motorbikes during certain hours — make this a feature, not a problem. Hotels immediately inside the zone have the most atmospheric setting but the least in-room facilities. The sweet spot for most travellers is the 10–15 minute riverside strip: walk when you want to, Grab when you don't.

Area decision

Old Town vs An Bang Beach

Old Town is right if

  • Evening walkability matters most
  • You want to dip into restaurants and cafes all day
  • Short stay (1–3 nights) with a packed itinerary
  • You're here for tailors, Ancient Town, heritage
  • Visiting Oct–Feb (rough sea season)

An Bang is right if

  • Beach is the primary reason you're here
  • You want more space and pool time
  • Longer stay (4+ nights) with slower pace
  • Budget doesn't include nightly transport back
  • Visiting March–September (calm sea season)

Common questions

Old Town hotels — FAQ

What does walkable to Hoi An Old Town actually mean?
Under 5 minutes: you are inside the Ancient Town. 5–10 minutes: the boundary zone where small boutique hotels sit. 10–15 minutes: the riverside strip (Anantara, La Siesta, Almanity) — this is the most common "walkable" claim and it's legitimate. Over 15 minutes means Grab is a better idea than walking.
Which Old Town hotel has the best pool?
Anantara Hoi An Resort has the best pool in the walkable zone — a well-designed riverside pool with good service. For the best rooftop pool closest to the Ancient Town entrance, Hoi An Chic Hotel is the pick.
Should I stay in Old Town or near the beach?
Stay in Old Town for walkability, evening restaurant access, and tailor trips. Stay near An Bang Beach if beach access is your priority. The 4km between them means transport every time you want the other — plan your base around which matters more to your trip.
Is Anantara or La Siesta better for a couple?
Anantara for the overall hotel experience — better pool, better breakfast, more polished service. La Siesta if spa treatments are part of the plan — the on-site spa here is genuinely good and having it in-house changes the trip. Both are legitimate choices.
