Distance matters here

How Close Is Close Enough?

"Walkable to Old Town" gets used loosely. Here's what it actually means at each distance.

Under 5 min
Inside the Zone

You are inside or right at the edge of the Ancient Town. Maximum immersion, minimum facilities, small heritage rooms, rarely a pool.

5-15 min
The Riverside Strip

Anantara, Hotel Royal, La Siesta. Walk across a footbridge, a pleasant 12-15 minutes. This is the most common legitimate "walkable" claim, and it's real.

15+ min
The Outer Zone

Use a Grab motorbike for consistency here. Technically walkable, not really practical, especially after dinner.

The Tradeoff

Old Town-area hotels generally don't deliver the huge beachfront-resort experience of Four Seasons, Palm Garden, or Hoiana. That's fine, tell yourself that going in. The pedestrian zone also closes to motorbikes during certain hours, a feature for walking, not a problem. The sweet spot for most travelers is the 10-15 minute riverside strip: walk when you want to, Grab when you don't.
