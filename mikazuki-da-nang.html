Mikazuki Japanese Resorts & Spa Da Nang

“Two waterparks, a real onsen, and nothing else in Da Nang like it.”

4-Star Japanese Onsen Resort · Lien Chieu

Our Take

Mikazuki sits in Lien Chieu district, about 30 minutes from the main My Khe Beach strip and the city centre.

The location is not incidental, the resort was built for a specific kind of guest: families with children who want waterpark days, Japanese and Korean travellers who want proper onsen protocols and teppanyaki, and people who want a self-contained resort world that doesn't require going elsewhere.

If you're in that category, Mikazuki over-delivers for the price. If you're not, it's a long way from everything.

The property is Japanese-operated with two waterparks, indoor and outdoor, two lazy rivers, a big wave pool, the HYPER SLIDE, an onsen, a rooftop infinity pool with a bar, and five restaurants.

There is nothing like it in Da Nang.

Water park entry is included for hotel guests, not an add-on, built into the rate.

Guests with kids consistently call out the waterpark as the reason they came and the reason they'd return.

"Not too amazing" is the single most critical review we found, which tells you something about how consistently the facility performs.

The onsen follows authentic Japanese protocol too: gender-segregated facilities, mineral hot spring water, indoor pools, dry sauna, steam room.

The Himalayan salt sauna specifically gets called out in guest reviews, "felt so good" is the phrasing that keeps appearing.

This is not a Vietnamese spa with Japanese theming, it's the real structure of a Japanese communal bath experience, which is either exactly what you want or not your thing at all.

The Rooms

The Rooms

This property's value proposition sits in the facilities, not the room category. Rooms are standard resort-grade, clean and comfortable but not the reason you're booking. Where Mikazuki spends its design energy is on the waterpark, the onsen, and the shared spaces, not on room-level luxury touches. That's a fair trade for the price point, but go in knowing it.

Some renovation work in the Hinode Garden Area started October 2025, worth checking current status before booking if it affects your preferred room category.

Which room would we choose? A standard room facing away from the waterpark if you want quieter evenings, the facilities are the draw during the day regardless of room category, so there's little reason to pay up for a premium room type here.

The Reason to Book It

The Waterpark

Two waterparks, indoor and outdoor, are the defining feature of this resort and the reason to book it over anywhere else in Da Nang. The outdoor park has slides, a wave pool, and a lazy river. The indoor section has the HYPER SLIDE, marketed as the longest indoor slide in Vietnam, which opened December 2024, plus a second lazy river. On a hot day with children in tow, this is an extraordinary facility to have available without leaving your hotel.

A Ninja Park and arcade round out the offering for older kids who've had enough of the water for the afternoon. Entry to all of it is included in the room rate, not a paid add-on the way waterpark access often is at other properties.

Pool + Beach

The Onsen & Rooftop Pool

Beyond the waterpark, the onsen and the rooftop infinity pool are the two facilities worth planning your day around. The onsen follows real Japanese protocol, gender-segregated, mineral hot spring water, dry sauna, steam room, and the Himalayan salt sauna is the specific detail guests keep mentioning. The rooftop infinity pool with a bar is the property's best pure relaxation space, with views over the surrounding area and sea in the distance. If the waterpark energy gets to be too much, this is where you go to sit quietly with a drink. The beach itself is about 2km away and reached by a scheduled shuttle, not a walk-out-the-door situation, so this isn't a beachfront hotel in the conventional sense.

Food + Breakfast

Food + Drink

One guest called the breakfast "by far our favourite breakfast of any hotel, ever." The buffet spans Japanese, American, Asian, and Continental stations with real depth in each direction, the pho and banh mi are specifically called out as highlights in the Vietnamese section. Restaurant food beyond breakfast gets more mixed reviews, the Vietnamese dishes on the dinner menus are consistently described as average, and the overall restaurant quality as "just okay level."

Our recommendation: lean hard into breakfast here, it's genuinely one of the best buffet spreads in the city, and plan at least a couple of dinners off-property for longer stays. The five on-site restaurants are convenient, not destinations in themselves.

Who It's For

Families, Couples & Who It Suits

This is built for families first. Two waterparks, a Ninja Park and arcade, and an included rate structure make it one of the easiest full-day resort bases for parents with kids in Da Nang, no ticketing, no separate admission, just walk down and use it. Japanese and Korean travellers specifically interested in onsen culture are the other core audience, and the resort delivers an authentic version of that experience rather than a themed approximation.

Couples without kids and without a specific interest in onsen culture are a harder fit. The location is remote from the city and the beach, the dining outside breakfast is average, and the property's whole design language is oriented around family activity rather than quiet romance. If that's what you're after, look at a beachfront property on My Khe instead.

What We Like

What We Like

Two waterparks, included

Indoor and outdoor, both built into the room rate rather than sold as a paid add-on. A genuine full-day facility for families.

The authentic onsen

Real Japanese protocol with gender-segregated baths, mineral water, and a Himalayan salt sauna that guests specifically single out.

HYPER SLIDE

Marketed as the longest indoor slide in Vietnam, opened December 2024. A genuine standout for older kids and teenagers.

The rooftop infinity pool

A quieter alternative to the waterpark, with a bar and sea views in the distance, for when the energy needs to drop.

The breakfast buffet

Genuinely exceptional across Japanese, American, Asian, and Continental stations, with standout pho and banh mi.

The Tradeoff

Lien Chieu is a real drive from the city centre and My Khe Beach, roughly 30 minutes each way, so this is a commitment to the resort, not a base for hopping between Da Nang's other attractions. The beach itself requires a scheduled shuttle rather than a walk out the door. Restaurant food outside the breakfast buffet is consistently described as average, "just okay level" is the phrase that keeps showing up in guest reviews, so budget for some evenings off-property if you're staying longer than a couple of nights. If you're not bringing kids and don't have a specific interest in the onsen, the case for staying here weakens considerably against a beachfront alternative closer to the city.

Location

Location

Lien Chieu district, northwest Da Nang, roughly 30 minutes from the Han River city centre and My Khe Beach. What this means for the trip: you're committing to the resort as your base for the day, not using it as a springboard for casual city exploration. The Marble Mountains are five minutes away, which makes for an easy half-day visit, and Hoi An is about 25 minutes, so longer excursions are still realistic. But this isn't a hotel you duck out of for a quick lunch in the Old Quarter. Budget the drive time into your plans, and treat days here as waterpark-and-onsen days rather than sightseeing days.

Would We Stay Here

YES — WITH KIDS

With children, absolutely. Without children and a specific interest in onsen, probably not, the location and food limitations become more significant without the waterpark as the draw. But for families, this is one of the most distinctive and well-priced family resort experiences in Da Nang. No one else is doing this.
