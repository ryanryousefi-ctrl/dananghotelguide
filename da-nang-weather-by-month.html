Da Nang Weather by Month

Temperature, rain, beach conditions and the best months to visit — all in one visual guide.

Ryan Yousefi, Da Nang resident since 2022 Updated August 2026 12 months covered
The short answer

If You Only Read One Section

Three ways to think about timing, depending on what you're optimizing for.

Editor's Pick
March – April

Warm, low rain, comfortable evenings, good beach conditions, and you beat the summer heat and crowds. March is the driest month of the year. April is a notch warmer and loses nothing. This is the safe recommendation for most trips.

Signature Pick
July – August

Peak vacation season. Hot, busy, and the best window if you're tied to school holidays. Sea hits its warmest, beach days are reliable, and the energy in the city is at its highest.

Tradeoff: crowds, heat, higher hotel demand.
Cheapest Weather Gamble
October – November

Hotel rates hit their yearly low and that's tempting. But rainfall and storm risk are real in this window, not theoretical. Only worth it if your plans don't depend on the beach.

Da Nang's weather is split cleanly into two halves. Roughly eight months of the year, from February through early September, are dry, hot, and beach-reliable. The other four, from mid-September through January, are wetter and less predictable, with October standing well apart as the one month I'd actually steer people away from.

None of this means the rest of the year is off-limits. It means you plan around it. Charts and month cards below cover the rest.

Context

What Rainy Season Actually Means

It's not rain, all day, every day

Rainy season doesn't mean constant rain. It means the odds shift against you, and when rain does come, it tends to be heavier and less predictable than a normal shower. October averages 21 rain days out of 31 and roughly 620mm total — more than London gets in a full year. That's not "occasional showers." That's sustained downpours with real flooding risk in low-lying areas.

September and November are more forgiving. City plans, restaurants, and museums work fine in both. It's specifically outdoor and beach time that becomes unpredictable.

✕ Multiple wet days in a row are normal in Sep–Nov, not a bad-luck outlier.
✕ Heavy downpours happen, not just drizzle. Streets flood in low areas.
✕ Storm systems can disrupt flights, ferries, and day trips with little notice.
✕ Ba Na Hills visibility gets worse, sometimes fogged out entirely.

Our take

If beach weather is the priority and you only have a handful of days, don't gamble on October or November. The odds aren't in your favor and you don't have days to spare if it goes wrong.

If you're staying a week or more and hotel value matters more than guaranteed sun, rainy season can still work. Build in flexible plans, expect some indoor days, and treat any clear afternoon as a bonus.

Storm risk

Typhoons: What To Actually Know

August – November

Central Vietnam sits in the typhoon belt

Tropical storms and typhoons can affect Da Nang during the wet season, most often August through November, with October and November historically the highest-risk months. Impacts range from heavy rain and wind to rough seas and flight disruption. Most years pass without a direct serious hit, but it happens often enough to plan around, not ignore.

If you're traveling in that window, buy travel insurance that covers weather disruption, keep your accommodation flexible, and check the forecast in the days before you fly rather than months in advance. This isn't a reason to avoid Vietnam in autumn. It's a reason to build in a plan B.

Heat

When Is Da Nang Hottest?

Late spring through summer, June through August, all averaging 33°C highs. The number understates it. Humidity is what actually gets you, and midday sun (roughly 11am to 3pm) is intense enough that most locals restructure their day around it rather than push through it.

→ Beach early. Best window is before 10am, before the sun is directly overhead.
→ Air conditioning midday. Malls, cafes, and hotel pools with shade all work.
→ Outdoor sightseeing morning or late afternoon, not midday.
→ Evenings cool off enough to be genuinely pleasant for dinner and walking.
Elevation changes everything

Ba Na Hills Is Different

A sunny beach day doesn't guarantee a clear Golden Bridge

Ba Na Hills sits at roughly 1,400m elevation, and that changes the weather system entirely. It's cooler than the coast, cloudier more often, and conditions can shift within an hour, clear one moment and fogged in the next.

A hot, cloudless day at My Khe Beach tells you nothing about what you'll see at the top of the cable car. Check the mountain forecast separately, and check it the morning of your visit, not the week before. This matters most during wet or overcast stretches of the year, when the odds of low cloud at altitude go up sharply.

Mostly, yes. But flooding hits harder there.

Hoi An sits on largely the same regional weather pattern as Da Nang, 30 minutes south. Same wet season, same dry season, same general temperature range. What's different is the impact: the Ancient Town sits low next to the Hoai River, and during heavy October rain the river can overflow into the old streets, sometimes for days at a time.

We won't turn this into a separate weather article. For the full picture on Hoi An, including day-trip timing and what the Ancient Town is like across seasons, see the Hoi An travel guide.

Weather Affects Hotel Prices

Summer costs more. Rainy season costs less.

Hotel rates track demand closely. July and August, when weather is at its most reliable, are also the most expensive weeks of the year. October and November, the wettest months, are consistently the cheapest. Neither is an accident. See the full month-by-month breakdown on the hotel prices guide.

Plan B

If It Rains

A wet day doesn't have to be a wasted day. AEON Mall Da Nang and Lotte Mart both work as full half-day plans, air conditioning, food courts, and Lotte Mart's cinema and arcade if you've got kids in tow. Cafes across the city are built for exactly this, and a spa afternoon is a legitimate use of a rain day, not a consolation prize.

For a longer list of indoor options and how to structure a rainy day with kids or teens, see the Da Nang with kids guide or the Da Nang with teenagers guide. And when none of that appeals, GrabFood delivery and an early night are underrated.

FAQ

Da Nang Weather FAQs

What is the best month to visit Da Nang?
March or April. March averages just 22mm of rain for the whole month with 28°C highs and calm seas. April is a touch warmer at 30°C with slightly more rain but nothing that changes your plans. Both beat the crowds and heat of peak summer.
When is rainy season in Da Nang?
September through November, with October the wettest month by far at roughly 620mm. September is a transition month and often still workable for city plans. Late November usually starts clearing.
Does it rain all day in October?
Not every single hour, but close. October averages 21 rain days out of 31, and the rain that does fall tends to be heavy, sustained downpours rather than brief showers. Plan for very little reliable outdoor or beach time.
When is the hottest month in Da Nang?
June through August, all averaging 33°C highs with high humidity. July and August are the most crowded because Vietnamese school holidays fall in that window.
Is July too hot to visit Da Nang?
Hot, yes. Unbearable, no. 33°C with humidity is intense at midday, but mornings and evenings are comfortable and the sea is at its warmest, around 30°C. Beach before 10am, air conditioning from noon to 3pm, and you manage it fine.
Can typhoons affect Da Nang?
Yes. Central Vietnam sits in the typhoon belt and the risk window runs August through November, peaking in October and November. Most years nothing serious hits directly, but it happens often enough that travel insurance covering weather disruption is worth having if you're visiting in that window.
What is Ba Na Hills weather like?
Different from the beach, often completely. Ba Na Hills sits at roughly 1,400m elevation, which means cooler temperatures, more cloud cover, and conditions that can change within an hour. A clear, hot day at My Khe Beach does not guarantee a clear view from the Golden Bridge. Check the mountain forecast the morning of your visit, separately from the city forecast.
Is December good for the beach?
Not really. December is cooler (23°C highs) with more mixed weather and occasional showers, though the worst of the monsoon has passed. It works well for city exploration, food, and Hoi An, just don't plan a beach-focused trip around it.
