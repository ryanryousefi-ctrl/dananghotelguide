Ancient Town & Riverside

Walk Out the Door and You're There

The Ancient Town puts you inside the UNESCO zone. The riverside is 10-15 minutes on foot and gives you access without the street noise.

Who Should Stay Here

First-time visitors who want to experience Hoi An at dusk and dawn, without day-trippers. The Old Town empties out by 9pm, staying inside it means you get the lantern-lit streets to yourself in the morning. Riverside hotels are the sweet spot: walking distance to everything, usually with a pool, and noticeably quieter at night.

An Bang Beach

4km Away, a Different Pace

An Bang is 4km from the Old Town by bicycle. Less developed than Cua Dai, with better restaurants and a more local feel.

North of An Bang
Off the tourist map

Cam Chau & Cam Thanh

The areas east of the Old Town and south toward the waterways are Hoi An's quietest corner, eco-lodges, homestays, and a few low-key boutique guesthouses in coconut palm villages. Getting around requires a bicycle or motorbike. Good for people who want complete removal from the tourist circuit while still being close to it.

Duy Hai · 10km south of Hoi An

Hoiana

Not Hoi An. A different world.

Hoiana is not Hoi An. It's a luxury resort enclave 10km south of the Ancient Town, with a private beach, an 18-hole golf course designed by Robert Trent Jones Jr., and multiple five-star properties. You don't come here to see Hoi An, you come here to avoid it, then take a 15-minute taxi when you want it.

The Tradeoff

Treat Hoi An Ancient Town as a half-day excursion from here, not a nightly habit. If you want lanterns outside your door every evening, stay closer to town. If the vacation is resort, golf and private beach, Hoiana delivers that better than anywhere else in the region.

Common questions

Where to Stay FAQ

What is the best area to stay in Hoi An?

The Ancient Town is best for first-time visitors who want to walk everywhere and be in the middle of the historic experience. An Bang Beach suits people who want a beach holiday with Hoi An day trips. The riverside is the best overall balance, walkable to Old Town, quieter at night, more hotels with pools and gardens.

Is it better to stay in the Old Town or outside?

Inside the Old Town for the first night or two gives you the full atmosphere at dusk and dawn when the day-trippers are gone. For a longer stay, just outside on the riverfront gives you more space and better value. Most boutique hotels with pools are a 10-15 minute walk from the Ancient Town entrance anyway.

Where should I stay in Hoi An for a beach holiday?

An Bang Beach is the local's beach, quieter than Cua Dai, better restaurants, good surf in season. Four Seasons The Nam Hai is the luxury choice north of town. Cua Dai has more large resort options but the beach has eroded and the area is more touristy.

How far is the beach from Hoi An Old Town?

An Bang Beach is about 4km from the Ancient Town, a 15-minute bicycle ride or a 50,000-80,000 VND Grab. Cua Dai Beach is 5km and slightly easier by road. Four Seasons The Nam Hai is further north at Ha My Beach, about 10km. Hoiana has its own beach about 10km south of town.
