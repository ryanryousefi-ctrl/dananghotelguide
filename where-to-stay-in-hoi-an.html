Where to
Stay in Hoi An

Five distinct areas, each with a different feel. Ancient Town, riverfront, An Bang Beach, Hoiana, and the countryside. Here's how to choose.

Five areas

Choose your base

Where you stay in Hoi An shapes the whole trip. Each area has a different pace, price point, and set of trade-offs.

Ancient Town

Walking distance to everything. Best for first-timers.

Riverside

Quieter than Old Town. Pools and gardens. Still walkable.

An Bang Beach

Local beach. Good restaurants. Four Seasons is here.

Countryside

Rice fields and quiet roads. Needs wheels. Anantara is here.

Hoiana

10km south. Rosewood. Private beach. A different world.

Cam Chau & Cam Thanh

Eco-lodges and homestays beyond the tourist zone.

Area 1 — Historic Core

Ancient Town and Riverside

The Ancient Town puts you inside the UNESCO zone. The riverside is 10–15 minutes on foot and gives you access without the street noise.

Who should stay here

First-time visitors who want to experience Hoi An at dusk and dawn, without day-trippers. The Old Town empties out by 9pm — staying inside it means you get the lantern-lit streets to yourself in the morning. Riverside hotels are the sweet spot: walking distance to everything, usually with a pool, and noticeably quieter at night.

Riverside property with one of the best pools in town. 10-minute walk to the Ancient Town entrance. Rooms are large and the breakfast quality is above average for Hoi An.

Area 2 — Beach

An Bang Beach hotels

An Bang is 4km from the Old Town by bicycle. Less developed than Cua Dai, with better restaurants and a more local feel. Four Seasons Nam Hai sits north of here.

Area anchor · Luxury

Four Seasons Resort The Nam Hai

The finest beach resort in the Hoi An area. Private beach north of An Bang, 100 pool villas, and a service standard you won't find elsewhere in Quang Nam province. Three pools including a children's pool. For everyone else, it's a half-day trip to visit.

Area 3 — 10km South

Hoiana — a different world

Hoiana is not Hoi An. It's a luxury resort enclave 10km south of the Ancient Town, with private beach, an 18-hole golf course designed by Robert Trent Jones Jr., and two five-star properties. You don't come here to see Hoi An — you come here to avoid it, then take a 15-minute taxi when you want it.

Ultra-luxury

Rosewood Hoi An

The flagship Rosewood in Vietnam. Private beach stretch, three pools, multiple restaurants. 120 rooms and residences. Service at a level that justifies rates starting around $400/night. The golf course and spa are the draw for long-stay guests.

Quick guide

Which area for you?

First time in Hoi An

Riverside or Old Town

Walk everywhere. Get the Ancient Town experience at dawn and dusk. Upgrade your second trip to beach or Hoiana.

Beach + History combo

An Bang Beach hotels

Bicycle to Old Town in 20 minutes. Beach in the afternoon. Best of both on a mid-range budget.

Luxury and resort life

Hoiana or Four Seasons

Treat Hoi An Ancient Town as a half-day excursion. The resort is the destination.

Cam Chau and Cam Thanh

The areas east of the Old Town and south toward the waterways are Hoi An's quietest corner — eco-lodges, homestays, and a few low-key boutique guesthouses in coconut palm villages. Getting around requires a bicycle or motorbike. Good for people who want complete removal from the tourist circuit while still being close to it.

Common questions

Where to stay in Hoi An — FAQ

What is the best area to stay in Hoi An?
The Ancient Town is best for first-time visitors who want to walk everywhere and be in the middle of the historic experience. An Bang Beach suits people who want a beach holiday with Hoi An day trips. The riverside is the best overall balance: walkable to Old Town, quieter at night, more hotels with pools and gardens. Each area suits different travel styles.
Is it better to stay in the Old Town or outside?
Inside the Old Town for the first night or two gives you the full atmosphere at dusk and dawn when the day-trippers are gone. But for a longer stay, just outside — the riverfront or residential streets — gives you more space, better value, and still easy walking access to everything. Most boutique hotels with pools are a 10–15 minute walk from the Ancient Town entrance anyway.
Where should I stay in Hoi An if I want a beach holiday?
An Bang Beach is the local's beach — quieter than Cua Dai, better restaurants, good surf in season. Four Seasons Nam Hai is the luxury choice north of town. Sol An Bang, The Linh, and An Bang Seaside Village cover the mid-range. Cua Dai has more large resort options but the beach has experienced erosion and the area is more touristy.
How far is the beach from Hoi An Old Town?
An Bang Beach is about 4km from the Ancient Town — a 15-minute bicycle ride or a 50,000–80,000 VND Grab. Cua Dai Beach is 5km and slightly easier by road. The Four Seasons is further north at Ha My Beach, about 10km from Old Town. Hoiana has its own beach 10km south of town.
