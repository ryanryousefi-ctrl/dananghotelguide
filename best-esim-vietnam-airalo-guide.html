Common Questions

eSIM for Vietnam FAQ

Is Airalo a good eSIM for Vietnam?

In my experience, yes. I've used Airalo across Europe and Asia, and it's my everyday mobile data provider living in Vietnam. It's not the only eSIM option, but of the ones I've personally used, it's the one I keep coming back to.

Should I buy my Vietnam eSIM before I fly?

Yes. Install the Airalo app and set up your Vietnam eSIM while you still have a normal internet connection, ideally before you leave home. That way your phone connects the moment you land instead of you hunting for airport wifi first.

Does an eSIM replace my regular phone number?

No. An Airalo eSIM adds a second, data-only line alongside your existing SIM. Your normal number, calls, and texts keep working through your primary SIM; the eSIM just handles data.

Can I use an Airalo eSIM to hotspot my laptop?

Yes, most Airalo data plans support hotspotting and tethering. I've used mine as a backup internet connection for laptop work more than once. Just know hotspotting a laptop burns through data noticeably faster than phone-only use.

Is a physical Vietnam SIM cheaper than Airalo?

Sometimes, especially for long stays or if you need a local Vietnamese phone number. For a trip where convenience and keeping your existing number matter more, an eSIM is usually the better trade-off.
