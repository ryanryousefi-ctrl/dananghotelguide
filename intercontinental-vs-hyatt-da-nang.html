Hotel Comparison · Da Nang's Two Most-Compared Resorts

InterContinental vs Hyatt Regency Danang

A clifftop private bay on Son Tra against a big, reliable beach resort on Non Nuoc. Nearly twice the price separates them.

Hotel A

InterContinental Sun Peninsula

VS

Hotel B

Hyatt Regency Danang

Editor's Pick

InterContinental Sun Peninsula

Why It Wins

The Hyatt is easier and usually costs half as much. But the InterContinental is the hotel worth traveling to Da Nang specifically to experience. The Son Tra setting, the private bay, the Bill Bensley design and overall sense of place put it in a different category, and nothing else in Vietnam competes with it directly.

The 30-Second Verdict

Drama and price versus scale and value

InterContinental Sun Peninsula

Best For

Honeymooners, anniversaries, couples who want the most dramatic resort in Vietnam and don't mind paying for it

Wins

  • Private bay, clifftop Son Tra setting
  • MICHELIN-recognized La Maison 1888
  • Bill Bensley design throughout

Tradeoff

  • 25-30 min from the city, you're committing to the resort
  • Nearly double the Hyatt's rate

Hyatt Regency Danang

Best For

Families, groups who want scale and reliability, travelers who want better value at five-star standard

Wins

  • Six pools including a 120m lazy river
  • Direct Non Nuoc beachfront, wider and calmer
  • Excellent kids programme

Tradeoff

  • Conventional design, won't wow anyone
  • Non Nuoc is still 20 minutes from city restaurants

The Property

InterContinental
Hyatt Regency

The InterContinental is carved into a forested hillside, connected by funiculars, descending toward its own private bay. The Hyatt is flat, wide, and built for scale, six pools and direct beachfront spread across a big footprint on Non Nuoc. One is a landscape statement, the other is a well-run machine.

InterContinental

The Beach

InterContinental
Hyatt Regency

The InterContinental's beach sits inside a sheltered, private bay with no public access, genuinely calmer water. The Hyatt's Non Nuoc stretch is open and wide, wider than most beaches on My Khe, with direct pool-to-sand access for families staying close to the water.

The Rooms

Five pool levels cascade toward the bay, and every room carries Bill Bensley's dark-wood, carved-archway design language. The Hyatt's rooms are generous and well-configured at 58 sqm and up, but they're built to be comfortable, not to be photographed.

Which Should You Book?

Match the hotel to your trip

Book InterContinental If You Want:

  • The single most dramatic resort setting in Vietnam
  • A private bay instead of a shared open beach
  • MICHELIN-recognized dining on property
  • A honeymoon or milestone trip where price isn't the deciding factor

Book Hyatt Regency If You Want:

  • Six pools and a lazy river that actually keeps kids busy
  • Direct, wide beachfront access on Non Nuoc
  • Roughly half the InterContinental's nightly rate
  • A large resort that runs like clockwork
Know Before You Book

The honest tradeoffs

InterContinental Sun Peninsula

Watch Out For
  • 25-30 minutes from the city, and every off-property meal costs a taxi both ways
  • $250-600/night, roughly double the Hyatt for a comparable stay
  • Limited kids facilities, this is an adult-focused resort

Hyatt Regency Danang

Watch Out For
  • Non Nuoc is still 20 minutes from Da Nang's restaurant scene
  • Some villa categories are starting to feel dated
  • A large resort footprint can feel impersonal next to the InterContinental's intimacy

Final Verdict

Which one should you actually book?

If this is a honeymoon, anniversary, or the one big splurge of the trip, book the InterContinental. The setting alone justifies the premium, and La Maison 1888 is the best restaurant attached to any hotel in the region.

If you're traveling with kids, watching the budget, or just want a big, reliable beach resort that delivers without drama, book the Hyatt. Six pools and direct Non Nuoc beach access do more for a family trip than any amount of architectural flourish.

Asked which is the stronger hotel overall, with no other constraints, it's the InterContinental. It's just not the right call for every trip.

Our Pick: InterContinental Sun Peninsula
