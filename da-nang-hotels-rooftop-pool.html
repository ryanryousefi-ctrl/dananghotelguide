Da Nang Hotels with Rooftop Pool 2026

Five hotels with rooftop pools ranked by view quality, pool size, and overall experience. A La Carte leads - but each has something different to offer depending on location and budget.

✎ Written by Ryan Yousefi · 📅 Last updated: May 2026 · ⏰ 6-min read
What to Know Hotels Ranked FAQ

What to Know First

Da Nang has a good spread of rooftop pool hotels, mostly concentrated along My Khe Beach and in the city centre near the Han River. Most are at mid-range hotels rather than five-star resorts (the big five-stars generally have ground-level or beach-adjacent pools instead).

The key distinction: some rooftop pools are plunge pools (small, for cooling off and photos) while others are genuinely swimmable pools. A La Carte has the largest and most functional rooftop pool in Da Nang. Others listed here are smaller.

Best time to use rooftop pools: Sunset (5:30-6:30pm) for the views and light. Avoid midday (11am-2pm) from May-September when rooftops are extremely hot. For photography, the golden hour before sunset gives excellent light in both directions.

"A rooftop pool in Da Nang is the best $20–30/night upgrade you can make. In the summer heat, an elevated pool with a sea view transforms the daily routine from 'hot beach' to 'blue water two ways.'"

Rooftop Pool Hotels Ranked

01
A La Carte Da Nang Beach
My Khe Beach · 360-degree infinity rooftop · Sea + mountain views
$70-100/night

A La Carte is the most famous rooftop in Da Nang and it earns the reputation. The infinity pool is genuinely large - you can actually swim laps - with 360-degree views: the South China Sea to the east, the Marble Mountains and city to the west. The rooftop also has sun loungers, a bar area, and good service. The hotel is across the road from My Khe Beach (not beachfront), but the rooftop is the attraction here. Strong value at $70-100/night given the pool quality. The crowd tends younger and social. Best sunset view of any rooftop pool in Da Nang.

02
Azura Da Nang
An Thuong area · Boutique rooftop · City and partial sea views
$60-90/night

Azura is a boutique hotel in the An Thuong area - walkable to the restaurant strip and a short walk from My Khe Beach. The rooftop pool is smaller than A La Carte but in a more intimate setting. Views are city-facing with partial sea glimpses depending on your angle. Better for independent travellers who want a social boutique environment and don't need a full-size pool. The rooftop bar is good for evening drinks. Slightly cheaper than A La Carte in low season.

03
Brilliant Hotel Da Nang
Han River · Rooftop pool + bar · Best for DIFF fireworks views
$60-90/night

Brilliant Hotel is on the Han River, which gives the rooftop pool a completely different view to the My Khe beach hotels - it looks directly at the iconic Dragon Bridge and Han River bridge, with the city spread below. During the DIFF International Fireworks Festival (late June-July), the rooftop is one of the best positions in Da Nang for viewing the fireworks. The pool itself is modest in size but the Han River setting is unique. If DIFF fireworks or river views are the priority, Brilliant Hotel is the obvious pick.

04
Wyndham Soleil Da Nang Beach
My Khe Beach · Sky pool · Sea views
$80-120/night

Wyndham Soleil has a "sky pool" concept - high elevation rooftop pool with My Khe Beach sea views. The pool is on the smaller side but the elevation gives strong views. The hotel is relatively new with contemporary design and solid facilities. Good for Wyndham Rewards members. Slightly pricier than A La Carte and Azura but the Wyndham brand delivers consistent standards. The sky pool is primarily for cooling off and views rather than lap swimming.

Book Wyndham Soleil on Booking.com →
05
Silk Path Grand Da Nang
City location · Rooftop pool · City views · Newer property
$70-110/night

Silk Path Grand is a newer entry with a rooftop pool offering city views rather than direct sea views. Good for city-centre stays where you want rooftop pool access without being on the beach strip. The facilities are clean and modern, the pool is functional rather than spectacular, and the city-adjacent location works well for exploring Da Nang. A solid choice if you're prioritising location over view quality.

Common Questions

Rooftop Pool Hotels: FAQ

Which Da Nang hotel has the biggest rooftop pool?+

A La Carte Da Nang Beach has the largest and most famous rooftop infinity pool in Da Nang - a genuinely large pool with 360-degree views east over the South China Sea and west to the Marble Mountains. Most other rooftop pools in Da Nang are smaller plunge pools or compact pools rather than full-size swimming pools.

Which Da Nang hotel has the best rooftop view?+

A La Carte Da Nang Beach has the most celebrated 360-degree view - sea to the east, Marble Mountains and city to the west. Brilliant Hotel on the Han River has exceptional views for the DIFF fireworks period, looking directly at Dragon Bridge. Wyndham Soleil has good My Khe sea views. The best view depends on whether you want ocean, river, or city.

Are rooftop pools heated at Da Nang hotels?+

Most rooftop pools in Da Nang are not heated. During the warm season (April-September) this is not an issue as air temperatures reach 30-35°C. During the cooler months (November-February) rooftop pools can feel cold with air temperatures at 18-22°C. Check with your specific hotel if travelling in the cooler season.
