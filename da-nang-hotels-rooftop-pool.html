Editor's Top Pick

TMS Hotel Da Nang Beach
Best Overall Rooftop Pool

If the pool is why you're booking the hotel, start with TMS. The high-floor infinity pool sits directly above the My Khe beachfront with an unobstructed coastal view toward Son Tra Peninsula. At this height the pool stops being a hotel amenity and starts being a reason to come.

The combination is hard to beat: central beachfront position on Vo Nguyen Giap, an infinity edge that disappears into open ocean, and a view that spans north up the coastline to the mountains of Son Tra. Sunrise from this pool over the South China Sea is one of the better things Da Nang can offer a first-time visitor.

Best overall is the only honest label here.

My Khe vs Son Tra Views

Central My Khe
  • Direct ocean ahead
  • Beach energy below
  • Sunrise directly over the water
  • Central beachfront position
  • Dramatic straight coastline perspective
North / Son Tra Side
  • Son Tra Peninsula in the frame
  • Wider, less dense coastal perspective
  • Mountains behind the coastline
  • Different ocean angle
  • Less congested building backdrop
Our Take
Want the classic Da Nang rooftop shot? My Khe.   Want mountains and coastline in the frame? Look farther north toward Son Tra.

Best Time for the Rooftop Pool

Sunrise / Morning

Best light over the ocean. Empty pool. The Da Nang beach faces east, so sunrise hits the water directly from the rooftop.

Midday

Strongest sun, best for swimming. Hottest part of the day is when the pool is most useful, not just photogenic.

Late Afternoon

Temperatures ease. Atmosphere picks up. The pool starts becoming social around 4-5pm at most properties.

Sunset

Worth noting: Da Nang's beach faces east. The sun sets behind the city, not into the ocean. You get colour in the sky, not a sun-drops-into-water moment.

Rooftop Pool Questions

TMS Hotel Da Nang Beach is our top pick for best overall rooftop pool. The high-floor infinity pool sits directly above My Khe beachfront with an unobstructed coastal view toward Son Tra. M Hotel Danang is the luxury and design alternative if the hotel aesthetic matters as much as the view.
Yes. Several Da Nang hotels have rooftop infinity pools along the My Khe beachfront. TMS, M Hotel, voco Ma Belle, Peninsula, SALA, Radisson, HAIAN, and Mandila are all confirmed rooftop infinity pool properties on or near Vo Nguyen Giap.
For My Khe ocean views: TMS and M Hotel. For Son Tra Peninsula views: Peninsula Hotel Danang and Hilton Garden Inn. For the widest panoramic view across city and coast: SALA Danang Beach Hotel. The right answer depends on what you want in the frame.
Most rooftop pools in Da Nang are not heated. From April through September this is not an issue. Between November and February rooftop pools can feel cool. Check directly with the hotel if you are visiting in the cooler months.
Most Da Nang hotels restrict rooftop pool access to guests. Some properties offer day pass access via their rooftop bar, but this varies by hotel and availability. Contact the hotel directly if you are not a guest and want pool access.
Early morning gives the best light and an empty pool. Late afternoon brings better temperatures and atmosphere. One important note: Da Nang's beach faces east. The sun rises over the ocean but sets behind the city. From a rooftop pool you will see a colourful sky at sunset -- but not the sun dropping into the water.
