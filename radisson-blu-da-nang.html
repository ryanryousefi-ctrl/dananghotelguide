Radisson Blu Resort Danang

“The rooftop infinity pool with panoramic beach views.”

Our Take

The Radisson Hotel Danang opened in 2022 on My Khe Beach, 182 rooms, rooftop infinity pool, modern rooms with balconies and ocean views, and a Radisson brand framework that runs a reliable, well-understood service model.

This is a newer city-beach hotel, not a resort complex, and it sits at a price point below the Hyatt and Sheraton while offering a contemporary product that opened in the current decade rather than the previous one.

The rooftop pool earns rave reviews, great views appears in every description of it.

The rooms are clean and modern with ocean-facing balconies that deliver on the beach-hotel promise, the breakfast gets consistent praise for variety, and warm, attentive service is the most consistent guest assessment.

A note on the Radisson brand in Da Nang worth being precise about: this property is Radisson, not Radisson Blu, a standard hotel-tier brand rather than the premium tier.

If you've seen listings for a Radisson Blu Resort Danang with a waterpark, that property does not appear to exist, the waterpark resort in Da Nang is Mikazuki, a separate Japanese-operated property in Lien Chieu district.

The confirmed Radisson property in Da Nang is this hotel, on My Khe Beach, without a waterpark.

The Rooms

The Rooms

Modern rooms with balconies and ocean views, clean, functional, contemporary. Entry rooms are around standard four-to-five-star city hotel size. The design is polished without being particularly distinctive, what it is consistently, is maintained properly, guests describe clean rooms and no maintenance surprises, which at a 2022 opening is expected but worth confirming is actually delivered. Four-person family rooms at 70 square metres offer real space for travelling families, and executive suites at 70 square metres are also available at the top of the standard range.

Which room would we choose? A family room if travelling with kids, the 70sqm size is a genuine differentiator versus most beach hotels at this price. Otherwise, a standard ocean-view room delivers the core experience without the upgrade cost.

The Reason to Book It

The Rooftop Infinity Pool

The rooftop infinity pool is the hotel's signature feature, elevated over My Khe Beach with panoramic views that are a genuine highlight of a stay here. It's the reason guest reviews consistently single this hotel out, great views appears in nearly every description of the pool, and it's a legitimate step up from a standard hotel pool without requiring resort-scale pricing.

Pool + Beach

Pool + Beach

The beach is directly accessible from the hotel, which is the fundamental test for any My Khe Beach hotel, and it passes cleanly, sunbeds, beach service, the full setup. Combined with the rooftop infinity pool overhead, this covers both the classic beach day and the elevated pool-with-a-view experience without needing to choose between them.

Food + Breakfast

Food + Drink

Varied breakfast and nice breakfast appear in reviews consistently, a proper buffet spread rather than a token hotel offering. This is one area where the Radisson performs above what its rate tier might suggest, worth factoring into the value calculation against pricier neighbors on the same stretch of beach.

Who It's For

Families, Couples & Who It Suits

Families get real value from the 70sqm four-person rooms, genuine space that most beach hotels at this price don't offer. Couples and Radisson Rewards members benefit from the fresh 2022 build and reliable service standards without paying resort-complex prices. It suits travelers who want a straightforward beach holiday with a good rooftop pool over those chasing an expansive resort infrastructure with a kids' club and multiple restaurants, this property doesn't attempt that scale.

What We Like

What We Like

The rooftop infinity pool

Elevated over My Khe Beach with panoramic views, consistently the standout element in guest reviews.

Direct beach access

Right on My Khe, sunbeds and full beach service without complication.

Fresh facilities

A 2022 opening means rooms and amenities still feel new, with no maintenance surprises reported.

Family rooms

70sqm for four people, genuine family space that's rare at this price point on My Khe.

The Tradeoff

This isn't a resort complex, there's no kids' club and the spa is limited by resort standards, so guests expecting Hyatt- or Sheraton-scale facilities should recalibrate. The design is polished but not distinctive, nothing here is an architectural statement. It's a 14-15 minute taxi to Dragon Bridge and the city centre, and worth being clear that this is the standard Radisson tier, not Radisson Blu, and there is no waterpark on site despite some listings implying otherwise.

Location

Location

My Khe Beach, with the restaurant strip a short walk, the airport 12 minutes, and Dragon Bridge 14 minutes. The hotel is a city-beach hotel on the My Khe strip, urban accessible, beach-adjacent, not resort-isolated. Hoi An is 30 minutes south for a day trip.

Beach: DirectDragon Bridge: 14 min taxi
Would We Stay Here

YES

Yes, for a straightforward beach holiday with a good rooftop pool and a new hotel that hasn't had time to deteriorate yet. The Radisson does exactly what a well-run, post-2020 beach hotel should do: clean rooms, good breakfast, rooftop views, beach access. No single element is extraordinary. The whole package is consistently executed at a price that doesn't require justifying.

Common Questions

Radisson Blu Resort Danang: FAQ

Is Radisson Blu Resort Danang worth staying at in Da Nang?

Yes, the Radisson Blu Danang is a polished five-star beach resort with strong pool facilities, direct My Khe Beach access, and a reliable upper-upscale service standard.

How much does Radisson Blu Resort Danang cost per night?

Rates start around $150–$195/night in low season. Peak season runs $260–$390/night.

Where is Radisson Blu Resort Danang located in Da Nang?

On My Khe Beach, approximately 7 km from Dragon Bridge and 9 km from Da Nang Airport.
