Best Family Hotels in Da Nang

If your kids judge hotels entirely by the pool, Da Nang is an easy sell. If they want a waterpark, there's a clear answer. If you want beach plus easy city access, the list narrows fast.

Updated August 2026 · Independently selected · We went. We picked.

Editor's Top Pick: Best Overall Family Resort

Sheraton Grand Danang Resort

My An Beach · Full-Service Family Resort · IHG

Sheraton Grand is the easy recommendation for a family that wants the classic Da Nang resort vacation. The pool is enormous - genuinely a destination by itself, not just a hotel amenity. Direct My An beach access, multiple dining options, and enough resort ground that parents don't need to plan every hour. The kids club adds structured programming for younger children. The resort is large enough that it absorbs busy periods without feeling crowded. Tradeoff: for restaurants and nightlife, you'll be Grabbing into town.

Da Nang Signature Pick: The Hotel Kids Will Remember

Da Nang Mikazuki Japanese Resorts & Spa

My Khe Area · Waterpark Resort · Japanese Theme

If the kids get a vote, this may be the winner.

Mikazuki isn't the hotel you'd choose for walking around Da Nang. That's not the point. You come here because the kids can spend half the trip in the water. The waterpark and pool complex is the whole experience - slides, wave pools, and a sprawling Japanese-themed resort property that turns the hotel itself into the destination. Full family day-pass options mean you don't even need to stay to use the facilities. Tradeoff: the location is more removed from My Khe restaurants and city action than most resorts on this list.

Resort or My Khe?

Resort (My An / Non Nuoc)

Better if the kids will spend hours in the pool and you want easy days without much planning. The resort is the plan.

My Khe

Better if your family will be sightseeing every day and wants restaurants, Da Nang city access and the beach nearby.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best family hotel in Da Nang?
Sheraton Grand Danang Resort is the easiest all-around recommendation. Large pool, direct My An beach, kids club, multiple dining options, and enough resort space that you don't need to plan every hour. For something different, Hyatt Regency has the best kids club on the coast, and Mikazuki has a waterpark that makes the hotel the destination.
Which Da Nang family hotel has a waterpark?
Da Nang Mikazuki Japanese Resorts & Spa has the most extensive waterpark and pool entertainment complex in Da Nang. It is purpose-built around the pool and water experience - slides, wave features, multiple pools, Japanese theme. The location is further from the city than My Khe or My An resorts, but the kids rarely notice.
Should families stay at My Khe or a resort in Da Nang?
For younger kids who will spend most of their time in the pool, a resort at My An or Non Nuoc makes more sense - the pools are larger and the resort grounds give more space. For older kids and families planning lots of sightseeing, My Khe is more convenient for restaurants and city access. Furama at My Khe splits the difference with a proper resort feel and better city proximity than the southern resorts.
Which Da Nang family hotel has the best kids club?
Hyatt Regency Danang has one of the best kids clubs on the Da Nang coast - dedicated space, structured activities, and genuinely gives parents a break. Sheraton Grand also runs a good kids club. Mikazuki's waterpark functions as an all-day kids entertainment facility even without a formal club structure.
Are there family villas in Da Nang?
Premier Village Danang Resort offers private pool villas directly on My An beach, making it the strongest villa option for families needing their own space. The InterContinental on Son Tra also has villa accommodation at a premium price point. Furama has bungalow-style options that give more space than standard hotel rooms.
