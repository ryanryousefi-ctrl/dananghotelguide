Worth Knowing

My Khe or Resort Coast?

My Khe central and north is better for families who plan to explore — restaurants walkable, city accessible, shorter stays work fine with a simpler hotel. The southern My Khe and Non Nuoc resort strip is better for families who want a self-contained compound: bigger pools, more space, quieter and calmer water for young kids. For toddlers especially, the southern stretch is noticeably easier.

Common Questions

Family Hotels FAQ

What is the best family hotel in Da Nang?

Hyatt Regency Danang Resort & Spa is the strongest family resort overall: four terraced pools, a dedicated kids club, wide calm beach, and room options from standard to private pool villas. For families wanting a water park, Da Nang Mikazuki is in a category of its own. For private villa space, Premier Village gives every unit its own pool.

Which area is best for families in Da Nang — My Khe or Non Nuoc?

Non Nuoc and the southern My Khe stretch are better for families who want a self-contained resort: more space, quieter water, bigger pools. My Khe central is better for families who want to explore, eat out each night, and use the hotel as a base. For toddlers, the calmer water on the southern stretch is noticeably easier.

Do Da Nang family hotels have good swimming pools?

Yes. Hyatt Regency has four pools, the Sheraton Grand has a dedicated pool complex with beach club, and Mikazuki has a full water park with slides. Premier Village has private pools per villa. Even mid-range options like Four Points by Sheraton and Muong Thanh Luxury have rooftop pools with sea views. Pool quality is generally strong across the family hotel category in Da Nang.

What is the best budget family hotel in Da Nang?

Four Points by Sheraton Danang offers the best value family stay with a beachfront rooftop pool, decent family rooms, and Sheraton service at a four-star price. Muong Thanh Luxury is another strong value pick with an infinity pool and spacious rooms. Both are on My Khe Beach with direct sand access.
