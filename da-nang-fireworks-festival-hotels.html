About DIFF 2026

2026 Schedule & Teams

The 2026 festival runs on Saturday nights with the theme "Da Nang - United Horizons":

  • May 30 (Opening - "Nature"): Vietnam (Da Nang) vs. China
  • June 6 ("Heritage"): France vs. Vietnam (Z121 Vina Pyrotech)
  • June 13 ("Culture"): Japan vs. Italy
  • June 20 ("Creativity"): Germany vs. Macau (China)
  • June 27 ("Vision"): Australia vs. Portugal
  • July 11 (Finale): Top two teams

Opening Night

Vietnam vs. China kicks off the festival at 8 PM, focusing on "Nature."

Finale

The top two teams from the regular rounds compete for the championship on the closing night, July 11.

Key Information for Visitors

  • Time: Fireworks displays typically start around 8:00 PM.
  • Location: The main stage and spectator stands are on Tran Hung Dao Street along the Han River, Son Tra District.
  • Tickets: Prices range from roughly $38-$153 USD (approx. 900,000-3,800,000 VND) depending on seating category. Premium tickets for opening and finale nights sell out fast.
  • Best Views: Official grandstands offer the clearest sightlines. Popular free viewing spots include the Han River bridges (Dragon Bridge, Han River Bridge) and local rooftop cafes.
  • Traffic: Expect significant congestion in the city center before and after each show.

The Festival

About DIFF

The Da Nang International Fireworks Festival runs over several weekends in June and July each year. Teams from different countries compete on alternating nights, launching displays from barges over the Han River. It is the biggest event on Da Nang's calendar and the city fills up fast - most good hotels on the river sell out 2-3 months ahead of festival weekends.

The fireworks launch between Dragon Bridge and Han Bridge, which means your view depends entirely on where you're staying. The best positions are the high-rise hotels on Bach Dang Street facing the river directly. A rooftop bar or a river-view room on a high floor gives you an unobstructed show. Ground-level spots on the promenade fill with crowds early - being above it all is worth paying for.

When to book: festival weekends sell out weeks or months ahead. If you know the dates you want, book immediately. Mid-week nights around festival weekends are easier to secure at short notice and often close to standard rates.

What matters in a hotel: height, river-facing orientation, and whether there is a rooftop bar or balcony. A ground-floor room at the Melia is not the same as a 30th-floor river-view room. When booking for DIFF, always request the highest floor available and confirm the room faces the Han River side.

Where to Stay

Best Hotel Areas for DIFF

Your hotel location during the Da Nang International Fireworks Festival matters more than almost any other factor. The fireworks launch from barges over the Han River between Dragon Bridge and Han River Bridge — so proximity to Bach Dang Street, the main riverside promenade, is the single most important variable when choosing where to stay.

Best for fireworks views
Dragon Bridge & Bach Dang Area
The hotels clustered around Dragon Bridge and Bach Dang Street — Novotel Premier, Hilton, Melia Vinpearl, Wink, Four Points — sit in the heart of the DIFF viewing zone. High-floor river-facing rooms here give you unobstructed sightlines to the launch barges. This is the area to prioritise if watching the fireworks is your primary reason for visiting during festival season. See the full Han River riverside hotels guide for a complete breakdown.
Runner-up position
Han River Bridge & Golden Bay
Properties slightly further north — like the Wyndham Danang Golden Bay — sit above the main crowd zone but still get an elevated view of the fireworks. Less intense atmosphere, easier post-show navigation, with somewhat lower prices than the prime Bach Dang strip. A practical trade-off if the Bach Dang hotels are sold out or above budget.
Skip for fireworks
My Khe Beach Hotels
Beach hotels on My Khe are 3–4km from the Han River. You won't see the fireworks from your room and getting to the river and back involves Grab queues in heavy crowd traffic after each show. If fireworks viewing matters, beach hotels are better suited to the non-festival portion of your trip. Consider splitting your stay: river hotel on festival nights, beach hotel for the rest.

Best Family Hotel Areas for Fireworks Week

Families should prioritise the Han River area during DIFF — but not necessarily the front-row Bach Dang hotels. The promenade gets very crowded on festival nights and managing young children in a dense crowd after 9pm can be difficult. Better options for families include hotels one or two blocks back from the waterfront, or properties with private high-floor balconies where children can watch safely. The Melia Vinpearl has family rooms and is well-suited to parents with younger children. See the full family hotels guide for more options across the city.

Budget Tips for Fireworks Season

DIFF weekend nights typically run 20–40% above standard rates at riverside hotels — sometimes more at top properties. Here's how to manage costs:

  • Book mid-week nights at normal rates. Festival competition nights are Saturdays. Monday–Thursday during the same weeks are usually at or close to standard rates.
  • Consider the Brilliant Hotel. The best-value riverside option with rooftop bar access starts from around $65/night — significantly below the five-star options.
  • Check the full hotel prices guide for seasonal baseline rates: Da Nang hotel prices by area and season.
  • Use the monthly index to see how June/July rates compare to the rest of the year: Da Nang hotel prices by month.

When to Book — DIFF Booking Timeline

The earlier you book for DIFF weekend nights, the better your options and rates. As a general guide:

  • 3–6 months out: Best river-view room selection at Novotel Premier, Hilton, and Melia Vinpearl. Rates still close to standard.
  • 1–2 months out: River-view rooms at top properties often sold out. Mid-range options (Wink, Four Points) still available but prices rising.
  • 2–4 weeks out: Limited riverside options remain. Prices at remaining inventory may be 30–50% above normal.
  • Last minute: Festival weekend inventory is largely gone at riverside hotels. Check again for cancellations or consider hotels one block back.

Always verify the official DIFF schedule before booking — dates are typically confirmed by January/February each year. Check official dates before booking non-refundable rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Da Nang International Fireworks Festival?
DIFF typically runs over several weekends in June and July, with competing national teams performing on alternating nights. Exact dates change each year - check the official DIFF website or the Da Nang tourism board for the current schedule. The festival has been running since 2008 and draws hundreds of thousands of visitors each year.
How far in advance should I book for DIFF?
Book as early as possible - ideally 2-3 months ahead for festival weekend nights. The Novotel Premier, Melia Vinpearl, and Hilton sell out first. If those are gone, check the Wink and Four Points. Mid-week nights during the festival period are easier to secure and closer to normal rates.
Do I need a river-view room or is a rooftop bar enough?
Either works, but they are different experiences. A high-floor river-view room (Novotel Premier, Melia, Hilton) gives you a private, unobstructed view from your window - comfortable, quiet, yours alone. A rooftop bar (Wink 25F, Brilliant, Sky36 at Novotel) gives you the view with atmosphere and drinks, but you share it with other guests and walk-in visitors. Festival rooftops fill up fast - arrive early and being a hotel guest helps.
Which hotel has the highest viewpoint for DIFF?
The Wyndham Danang Golden Bay's infinity pool sits on the 29th floor, making it the highest dedicated viewing point. The Novotel Premier's Sky36 bar is also high up. For room-based views, both the Melia Vinpearl and Hilton have high-rise towers where upper-floor rooms get a clear, elevated view across the river.
Can I watch DIFF from a beach hotel?
Not well. Beach hotels on My Khe are 3-4km from the Han River and won't give you a view of the fireworks. You'd need to Grab to the river (10-15 minutes) and join the promenade crowds. Getting back after the show can be slow - crowds are dense and Grabs take time to arrive. If DIFF is the main reason you're visiting, stay on the river.
Are hotel prices higher during DIFF?
Yes. Festival weekend nights typically run 20-40% above standard rates at river hotels, sometimes more at the top properties. Mid-week nights during the festival period are usually close to normal. Book early for the best rate and check whether cancellation is free in case your plans change.
