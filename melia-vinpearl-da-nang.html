Our Take

Melia Vinpearl sits on the Han River with Dragon Bridge visible from the river-facing rooms and a 420-square-metre outdoor pool facing the water.

When fireworks festivals run, which they do with some frequency in Da Nang and more or less constantly in June, you're watching from the pool or from your room.

One guest described watching fireworks displays from the water on multiple occasions during their stay, a specific kind of Da Nang city experience that beach resort guests don't get.

Dragon Bridge is a five-minute walk, Han Market is five minutes on foot, and Vincom Mall, cinema, supermarket, restaurants, arcade, is directly adjacent to the building.

For families or longer stays, this is the most self-sufficient hotel position in Da Nang city centre.

Rooms are spacious and clean with some of the best city views available from any hotel in central Da Nang, upper-floor river and bay view rooms deliver panoramas that stand up to anything at the beach resorts, just in the opposite direction.

Bathrooms are large with rainfall showers, and family apartments are well-configured for parents with children.

One honest note: a handful of reviews have mentioned elevator wait times during busy periods, and a general feeling that the property is beginning to show its age in certain areas.

The rooms themselves remain clean and comfortable, the concern is more about the overall energy of the property than specific maintenance failures.

The Rooms

The Rooms

Room sizes and views are competitive with beach resort properties despite the city location, contemporary design without feeling cold, large bathrooms with rainfall showers, and family apartments configured well for parents travelling with children. Elevator waits during busy periods and a general sense the property is aging in places are worth knowing, though they don't extend to the room quality itself.

Which room would we choose? An upper-floor river and bay view room. The panoramas genuinely compete with beach resort views, and it's the best way to catch the fireworks festivals when they run.

The Reason to Book It

Pool Facilities

A 420-square-metre outdoor pool plus an indoor pool make this the best pool setup of any city hotel in Da Nang, with Dragon Bridge views from the river-facing sides. The indoor pool is a genuine facility advantage over most city hotels for days when the weather doesn't cooperate. The pool area does get crowded at peak times, a fair trade-off of staying at a larger, well-known city property, but the combination of scale and river views is not matched by any other non-beach hotel in Da Nang.

Pool + Beach

Pool + Beach

This is a city hotel, not a beach resort, and honesty about that matters more than marketing. My Khe Beach is 10 minutes by Grab, close enough for daily trips, not walkable. If the trip is primarily about beach time, one of the beachfront properties makes more sense. If it's a city-focused trip, Han Market, street food, Dragon Bridge, museum visits, Hoi An day trips, the pool here covers the resort-swimming itch better than any other hotel in the city centre.

Food + Breakfast

Food + Drink

A massive spread with options for everyone is the most common guest description, and it holds up, a proper buffet covering Vietnamese, Chinese, Korean, and Western dishes with real depth rather than token variety. The room gets crowded and noisy by 8am, so if quiet matters to your morning, go at 7 instead and get the same spread with none of the crowd.

Who It's For

Families, Couples & Who It Suits

Families benefit most from the Vincom Mall adjacency and well-configured apartment rooms, there's genuinely enough here to keep a family occupied for a rainy afternoon without leaving the building. Couples doing a city-focused trip get real value from the pool and the walkable access to Dragon Bridge and Han Market. It suits longer stays, three nights or more, better than a quick overnight, since the location's convenience compounds the longer you're based here.

What We Like

What We Like

The pool facilities

420sqm outdoor pool plus an indoor pool, the best pool setup of any Da Nang city hotel by a clear margin.

Dragon Bridge access

Both the bridge and Han Market are five minutes on foot, removing most of the logistical friction of a city stay.

Vincom Mall

Directly adjacent, cinema, supermarket, a full restaurant floor. The most self-sufficient hotel position in the city centre.

Fireworks views

Visible from the pool and river-facing rooms during Da Nang's fireworks festivals, especially frequent in June.

The Tradeoff

Not a beach hotel, the beach is a 10-minute Grab ride, not a walk. Staff quality reviews are genuinely mixed, some guests describe warm, attentive service, others have noted unfriendly attitudes and rare smiles, and the experience appears to vary by department and shift rather than being uniformly one way. The breakfast room gets crowded and noisy after 8am, and the property feels dated in some areas despite competitive room quality. None of these are dealbreakers, but arrive with realistic expectations about staff consistency.

Location

Location

Han River, city centre, with Dragon Bridge and Han Market both five minutes on foot and Vincom Mall directly adjacent. What this means for the trip: almost everything in the city core is walkable, and the pool and mall mean you don't need to leave the building on a slow day. My Khe Beach is a genuine 10-minute Grab ride, easy for a half-day trip but not a stroll down for sunset.

Dragon Bridge: 5 min walkBeach: 10 min Grab
Would We Stay Here

YES

For a city-focused trip, yes, particularly for more than three nights. The Vincom adjacency, the pool, the Han River fireworks, and the central location make it the most practical long-stay base in Da Nang's city centre. Arrive with realistic expectations about staff consistency and get to the breakfast room before 8. Both are manageable once you know them in advance.
