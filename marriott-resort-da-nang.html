Da Nang Marriott Resort & Spa, Non Nuoc Beach Villas

“Every one of the 200 villas has a real private pool, with the Marble Mountains over the garden wall.”

Our Take

The moment you leave the main road and the gates close behind you, something shifts.

The Marriott sits on Non Nuoc Beach, and every villa you can see from the entrance path faces the water.

The Marble Mountains are visible to the southwest, five limestone formations rising abruptly from flat ground, and when the light goes golden in the late afternoon they look unreal enough to stand on the villa terrace and forget what you were supposed to be doing.

This is Marriott International's flagship property in Da Nang, opened in 2020, and one of the quieter beach stretches in the city.

Non Nuoc sees a fraction of the foot traffic of the main My Khe strip, and most guests staying here are precisely because of that.

The private pool is the main event.

There's something about having a proper swimming pool entirely to yourself that you don't fully appreciate until you've done it, laps at 6am or midnight, no schedule to work around, no shared-pool etiquette.

Da Nang has plenty of good beach hotels.

It has far fewer places where you can swim in complete privacy with the Marble Mountains visible over your garden wall, and that specific combination is harder to find than it sounds.

The Rooms

The Villas

All 200 units have a private pool, not a shared one, not a plunge tub tacked onto the balcony. The one-bedroom villas run about 100 square metres with a separate outdoor living area, and the presidential options go up to 500 square metres, becoming multi-bedroom family retreats with full kitchens. Design is contemporary Vietnamese: white render, dark timber, ceramic tiles, outdoor furniture that doesn't look cheap after two seasons.

The indoor-outdoor flow is well done, the living space opens fully to the terrace and pool rather than having a balcony bolted on as an afterthought. Which room would we choose? A one-bedroom pool villa for most stays, it's the sweet spot between space and value. For a family trip, one of the multi-bedroom presidential villas with a kitchen is worth the jump.

The Reason to Book It

Private Pool Villas

Every villa comes with its own private pool. This is the property's entire proposition, and it delivers, even if the resort as a whole runs busier than the villa format suggests. Two mornings in the private pool before breakfast, one afternoon at the beach, both work, but the pool is the reason you'll remember the stay.

A central resort pool exists for guests who want a more social swimming environment or overflow space, but it's beside the point here. The villa pool is what you're paying for, and on a quiet Non Nuoc stretch with the Marble Mountains as backdrop, it earns its price.

Pool + Beach

Pool + Beach

Non Nuoc runs directly in front of the resort with a cleaner, quieter character than the busier sections of My Khe Beach to the north. The resort's stretch of sand has sunbeds, shade, and beach service, and the water is generally calmer here too. Most guests split time between the villa pool and the beach rather than favoring one entirely, and both genuinely work, the villa pool for privacy and early mornings, the beach for the wider view and the sound of the sea.

Food + Breakfast

Food + Drink

The Street Food Kitchen handles casual dining with a Vietnamese-forward menu running through pho, banh mi, and rice plates alongside bao and fresh spring rolls. It's solid for a hotel casual restaurant, it doesn't try to be something it isn't. Breakfast is a proper spread and consistently the best-reviewed meal on property.

Three outlets total means this isn't a deep restaurant scene, so plan at least one dinner out. The Marble Mountains are five minutes by taxi and Hoi An is 25 minutes, both directions give good food options without much travel time if hotel dining starts to feel repetitive.

Who It's For

Families, Couples & Who It Suits

Couples get the most out of the one-bedroom villas, private pool, quiet beach, no entry-level room to compete for. Families traveling with kids do well in the multi-bedroom presidential villas with kitchens, though the all-villa pricing floor makes this a splurge rather than a default family choice. Marriott Bonvoy members should weigh cash rates against points, redemption value here is exceptional for the property tier, often the best move if you've been accumulating points. This isn't the property for travelers chasing a lively resort social scene, the appeal is the opposite of that.

What We Like

What We Like

Real private pools

Every villa, not a shared pool or a token plunge tub. Swim at 6am or midnight without working around anyone else's schedule.

Non Nuoc Beach

Quieter and cleaner than the busy My Khe strip further north, with calmer water and genuine beach service.

The Marble Mountains backdrop

Visible from many villa terraces and one of the best views from any hotel property in the city, especially at golden hour.

Bonvoy redemption value

One of the strongest points redemptions in Vietnam at this quality tier, worth checking before booking with cash.

The Tradeoff

You're 13km from Da Nang's city centre, which isn't prohibitive, Grab runs reliably and isn't expensive, but you're in resort mode, not city-base mode. If you want street food stalls and Han River wandering every night, you're in a taxi both ways. Despite the all-villa format, this reads as a large, busy resort rather than an intimate one, and breakfast buffet service in particular can thin out at peak hours, not the hushed, private experience the marketing implies. Dining is good but limited, three outlets isn't a deep restaurant scene, and the all-villa pricing means there's no entry-level room option, so the floor price here is genuinely higher than at comparable beachfront hotels.

Location

Location

Non Nuoc Beach in the Ngu Hanh Son district, 13km south of Da Nang's city centre and 15km from the airport. What this means for the trip: you're in resort mode, not city-base mode. If you want street food stalls and Han River evenings every night, that's a taxi both ways. If you want to spend most of your time at your villa pool and the beach with a day trip to Hoi An built in, this location works in your favor. The Marble Mountains are five minutes by taxi, close enough for a spontaneous half-day, and Hoi An is about 25 minutes by car, not a full-day commitment.

Common Questions

Da Nang Marriott Resort & Spa, Non Nuoc Beach Villas: FAQ

Is Da Nang Marriott Resort & Spa, Non Nuoc Beach Villas worth staying at in Da Nang?

Yes, among Da Nang's finest villa resort experiences. The Marriott Non Nuoc Beach Villas combine the brand's five-star service culture with private pool villas on one of the city's quieter beach stretches.

How much does Da Nang Marriott Resort & Spa, Non Nuoc Beach Villas cost per night?

Villa rates start from around $280/night in low season. Peak season runs $480–$800+/night.

Where is Da Nang Marriott Resort & Spa, Non Nuoc Beach Villas located in Da Nang?

On Non Nuoc Beach in the Ngu Hanh Son district, 13 km south of Da Nang city and 15 km from the airport.
