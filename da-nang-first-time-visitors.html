First Visit — Da Nang — 2026

Da Nang, First Trip

10 things to know before you arrive. Skip the surprises.

Updated 2026 · Local editor · Based in Da Nang since 2022

Before You Arrive

When I got to Da Nang in 2021 it felt like a vast, mysterious place. Every corner had something new and slightly unknown — and that feeling, where anxiety meets excitement, is what travel is actually about.

If you're anything like me, you've already watched 50 YouTube videos. You've Google Street Viewed the block your hotel is on. You feel ready. You're mostly right.

Below are my top 10 tips to get you the rest of the way. But honestly — still watch the videos. I have a section dedicated to them further down this page.

Before you go

10 Things to Know First

1

Stay near My Khe Beach

The beach strip along Vo Nguyen Giap is the best base — close to the sand, close to An Thuong's cafes and restaurants, and 10–15 minutes from the city center by Grab. The area around An Thuong 2–4 roads has the best walkable options.

See our where to stay guide for specific neighbourhood breakdowns.
2

Download Grab before you land

Grab is Vietnam's main ride-hail app. It works immediately on arrival at Da Nang airport — open it in the arrivals hall and a car will be there in 3–5 minutes. Most city rides cost 40,000–80,000 VND. No haggling, no scams.

3

Beach mornings are best

My Khe is most enjoyable before 10am. Locals swim from 6am, vendors are active, the water is calm, and the heat hasn't peaked. After midday in summer (June–August), the beach is best avoided in favour of air-conditioning, cafes, or indoor attractions.

4

Da Nang is spread out — but manageable

The beach, the city center, Marble Mountains, and Son Tra Peninsula are in four different directions — but nothing is more than 20–30 minutes by Grab. Don't try to walk between zones. Plan by area: beach/peninsula in the morning, city center in the evening.

5

Go to Hoi An at least once

30km south, 40 minutes by Grab. The Ancient Town is genuinely impressive, the food is better than Da Nang, and the atmosphere is completely different. A day trip leaving at 7:30am and returning by 4pm is the standard approach. Don't skip it.

See our Hoi An markets guide for what to buy there.
6

Cash still matters

Local restaurants, street food, markets, and most tuk-tuks are cash only. ATMs are widely available near Han Market and at the airport. Carry 200,000–500,000 VND in small denominations (20,000 and 50,000 notes) for daily spending.

Note: major hotels and tourist restaurants accept cards, but don't count on it at local spots.
7

Heat changes your schedule

June through August regularly hits 34–38°C with high humidity. Do outdoor activities early morning or late afternoon. Build in midday rest — a long lunch, a cafe, or the hotel pool. This isn't a warning; it's a structural feature of the climate that local residents also work around.

8

Crossing streets takes practice

Vietnamese traffic doesn't stop for pedestrians at unmarked crossings. Walk at a steady, predictable pace and let the motorbikes flow around you — don't freeze. At busy intersections, wait for a local to cross and move with them. It's less intimidating than it looks within an hour.

9

The airport is very close

Da Nang International Airport is 3–5km from the city center and 5–8km from My Khe Beach hotels. A Grab to most beach hotels takes 10–20 minutes and costs 60,000–120,000 VND. No need to pre-book expensive hotel transfers unless you arrive very late at night.

10

Food is very inexpensive at local spots

A bowl of mì Quảng or pho at a local restaurant costs 40,000–80,000 VND ($1.60–$3.20). Bánh mì from a street cart is 25,000–35,000 VND. Fresh sugarcane juice is 15,000 VND. Eating well in Da Nang costs almost nothing if you go where locals eat — An Thuong 4, the area behind the beach, and around Con Market.

Common Questions

Where should first-time visitors stay in Da Nang?
My Khe Beach is the best base. The beach is 20km long, most mid-range and luxury hotels are here, and Grab gets you to the city center in 10 minutes. The An Thuong neighbourhood behind the beach has the best cafes, restaurants, and bars.
Is Da Nang safe for tourists?
Da Nang is one of the safer cities in Vietnam. Violent crime targeting visitors is rare. The main risks are bag snatching from motorbikes in busy areas (keep bags away from the road) and standard tourist scams near attractions. Grab eliminates most transport scam risk.
Do I need cash in Da Nang?
Yes. Markets, local restaurants, and street food vendors are almost always cash only. ATMs are widely available near Han Market and at the airport. Carry 200,000–500,000 VND in small denominations.
Is Da Nang expensive?
Mid-range by Southeast Asian standards. Local food is very cheap — 40,000–80,000 VND per meal. Grab rides are inexpensive. The main costs are accommodation and Ba Na Hills admission. A comfortable mid-range trip costs USD 80–150 per person per day.
