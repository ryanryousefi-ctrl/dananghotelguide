The guide behind the guide

An honest guide
from someone
who lives here.

I moved to Da Nang in 2022. My wife is Vietnamese. My son was born here. This is not a travel assignment — it's home, and I write about it the way I'd explain it to a friend who asked for the real version.

I have 20 years in journalism and spent several years in hotel operations before moving here. I know the difference between a hotel that photographs well and one that actually delivers — and I say so either way. No hotel has ever paid to be ranked higher on this site.

Da Nang is genuinely one of the best-value beach destinations in Southeast Asia. The bad bookings happen because people choose the wrong area, not the wrong hotel.

Editor's No. 1

Best Overall · Non Nuoc Beach · 5-Star

Hyatt Regency Danang
Resort & Spa

The best balance of quality, location, and value in Da Nang. Non Nuoc Beach direct access, massive lagoon pools, and genuinely good food — without the InterContinental price tag. This is where most repeat visitors end up when they stop overthinking it.

The pools alone justify the stay. Non Nuoc is quieter than My Khe, the beach is better, and the resort just works.
