Hyatt Regency Da Nang vs Furama Resort

Non Nuoc's polished Hyatt versus My Khe's characterful original. Same price tier, very different experiences, your choice depends on which beach and which vibe you want.

✎ Written by Ryan Yousefi · 📅 Last updated: May 2026 · ⏰ 8-min read

Furama Resort Danang

Hotel Overview

Hyatt Regency Da Nang Resort & Spa

  • ★★★★★ Full-service resort
  • 196 rooms + 37 villas
  • Non Nuoc, south Da Nang
  • 5 pools including 100m beachfront pool
  • World of Hyatt loyalty benefits
  • From $150/night

Furama Resort Danang

  • ★★★★★ Boutique resort character
  • 198 rooms + villas
  • My Khe, South My Khe strip
  • 3 pools including lagoon pool
  • Independent, no loyalty programme
  • From $120/night

Both hotels sit in Da Nang's luxury resort tier, both have direct beach access, and both offer the full resort package, but they feel remarkably different. The Hyatt Regency is polished, contemporary, and enormous: its 100-metre pool alone is a statement.

Furama trades scale for soul, it has been here since the 1990s and the tropical gardens and lagoon pool have a depth that newer resorts can't replicate. The Hyatt wins on modernity and facilities; Furama wins on atmosphere.

The key location difference: Hyatt sits on Non Nuoc beach, which is quieter and more Hoi An–adjacent. Furama is on My Khe beach, which is livelier and closer to Da Nang city and the An Thuong restaurant strip.

If you're combining Da Nang with a Hoi An day trip, Hyatt's position saves ~20 minutes each way.

The Hyatt's 100-metre beachfront pool is genuinely impressive and one of the longest in Da Nang. Camp Hyatt gives families a structured programme that Furama's Kids Club doesn't quite match.

Furama counters with La Maison 1888, the only Michel Roux-affiliated restaurant in Da Nang, and the mature lagoon pool, which is far more photogenic than any standard resort pool.

Who Each Hotel Is Best For

Choose Hyatt Regency Da Nang if…

  • You're a World of Hyatt member
  • Hoi An day trips are a priority
  • You want the longest pool in the area
  • A structured kids programme matters (Camp Hyatt)
  • You prefer Non Nuoc's quieter beach
  • Modern contemporary design appeals more than character

Choose Furama Resort Danang if…

  • Atmosphere and character matter more than modernity
  • The lagoon pool and mature tropical gardens appeal
  • La Maison 1888 fine dining is a priority
  • You want My Khe proximity to An Thuong restaurants
  • Budget is a factor, Furama often runs ~$30 cheaper
  • You prefer a smaller, more intimate resort scale

Final Verdict

For most travellers the Hyatt Regency is the more logical choice: better facilities, structured kids programme, World of Hyatt benefits, and a better position for Hoi An day trips. If you're ticking boxes, the Hyatt ticks more of them.

But Furama wins on something harder to quantify, soul. The lagoon pool, the mature garden setting, and La Maison 1888 give it a character that newer resorts struggle to replicate.

If you value atmosphere and want proximity to Da Nang's restaurant scene, Furama at a lower price point is the smarter pick.

Loyalty members should default to the Hyatt. Everyone else: decide between Non Nuoc quietness and My Khe vibrancy first, then let everything else follow.

Common Questions

Hyatt Regency vs Furama: FAQ

Is Hyatt Regency better than Furama Da Nang?+

The Hyatt Regency has more pools, a structured kids programme, World of Hyatt loyalty benefits, and is closer to Hoi An. Furama has more character, better gardens, La Maison 1888 dining, and is closer to Da Nang city and restaurants.

Hyatt wins on facilities; Furama wins on atmosphere.

Which is more family-friendly, Hyatt Regency or Furama Da Nang?+

Both are good family options. The Hyatt Regency edges ahead with Camp Hyatt, a more structured kids programme, and 5 pools.

Furama has the Coral Kids Club and garden space that families appreciate. See our family hotels guide for more options.

Which hotel is closer to Hoi An?+

Hyatt Regency is closer to Hoi An, roughly 35 minutes versus 45 minutes from Furama. Both require a taxi or Grab; neither has direct shuttle service to Hoi An, though both can arrange transport.
