Da Nang Nightlife Guide

Where to go out in Da Nang: beach clubs, rooftop nightclubs, club nights, Dragon Bridge fire show, and the best spots on the strip. Independently covered.

Updated August 2026 · Independently selected · We went. We picked.

Editor's Top Pick

Kalà Kalà Beach Club

Ngũ Hành Sơn · Beach Club · $$$

The most serious beach club on the Da Nang coast. Thatched-roof beach huts, foam parties, DJ sets, and a proper sound system on the actual sand. Production quality that normally only shows up in Bali or Phuket (the foam party setup, the laser rigs, the crowd energy) is all here at 51 Võ Nguyên Giáp.

The Ngũ Hành Sơn location puts it slightly south of the main My Khe tourist strip, which keeps it from feeling like a hotel beach bar. The crowd is mixed international and local, dressed-up without a door policy enforcing it. Arrive before 9 PM. Call ahead on weekends.

Paradise Beach

My Khe · Beach Club · $$

The most relaxed night on the strip. Outdoor cinema, cold drinks, actual sand.

Where Kalà Kalà is about production, Paradise Beach is about the night itself. Outdoor cinema screen on the sand, BOGO cocktail promotions, and an open-air bar that doesn't try to be anything it isn't. Daytime it's family-appropriate; by 8 PM the crowd shifts and the vibe settles into something easy and genuinely pleasant.

Better suited for a long evening than a proper club night, the kind of place you end up staying longer than intended. Check Instagram for BOGO promo nights before you go.

Dragon Bridge Fire & Water Show

Every Friday and Saturday night at 9 PM, the Dragon Bridge (a 666-metre steel structure shaped as a dragon) breathes fire and water for 10–15 minutes. Free. Legitimately impressive. The best viewing position is the Bach Dang riverfront walkway on the west bank. Riverfront bars fill from 8 PM on show nights, so arrive by 7:30 PM if you want a table.

Before You Go Out

Drink-driving enforcement in Da Nang is strict. Zero-tolerance laws have been in place since 2020 and are actively enforced on main roads at night. Grab is the standard exit from any evening out; wait times are short in most areas even after 1 AM. GrabBike arrives faster than GrabCar after midnight on weekends.

The city's nightlife splits across two main zones. The Han River waterfront (Bạch Đằng) has rooftop clubs, Dragon Bridge views, and the riverside party venues. Dragon Bridge breathes fire every Friday and Saturday at 9 PM, and riverfront venues fill from 8 PM on show nights. The My Khe and Ngũ Hành Sơn beachfront (Võ Nguyên Giáp) is where the beach clubs operate: Kalà Kalà, Malibu, and Paradise Beach all sit on or near the sand. The Hải Châu city centre holds the larger nightclubs including ADM Club, Bloom, and For You Club.

For hotels within walking distance of the clubs: Han River hotels are closest to Sky36, OQ Club, and Dragon Bridge. My Khe Beach hotels put you walking distance from the beach clubs. See our full Da Nang bars guide for additional venues including cocktail bars, live music, and pubs.

Common Questions

What is the nightlife like in Da Nang?
Da Nang has a varied and growing nightlife scene: rooftop bars, craft beer taprooms, live music venues, cocktail lounges, and hidden speakeasies. It's less intense than Hanoi's Old Quarter or HCMC's Bui Vien, but more varied than most cities its size. The main nightlife districts are the Han River waterfront, the An Thượng expat area behind My Khe Beach, and the city centre for cocktail bars. Most venues close between 1–2am, with some pushing to 3am on weekends.
When does Dragon Bridge breathe fire?
Dragon Bridge breathes fire and water every Friday and Saturday night at 9 PM. The show lasts around 10–15 minutes and is completely free. The best viewing spots are along Bach Dang waterfront on the west bank, or from the bridge deck itself. Arrive by 8:15 PM on weekends to secure a good position, the riverfront fills quickly. Waterfront bars and Sky36 rooftop are popular places to watch with a drink in hand.
Is it safe to go out at night in Da Nang?
Da Nang is one of Vietnam's safest cities for nightlife. Street crime targeting tourists is rare. The main safety consideration is traffic: use Grab instead of your own motorbike after drinking. Vietnam enforces drink-driving laws actively in Da Nang, with on-the-spot fines and licence confiscation. The expat bar districts are well-lit and busy until late. Solo travellers, including women, generally report feeling comfortable across all main bar areas.
What is the best area for nightlife in Da Nang?
For rooftop bars and views: the Han River waterfront. For expat pubs, craft beer, and late nights: An Thượng behind My Khe Beach. For serious cocktails and hidden bars: Hải Châu city centre. For beach clubs and the brewery: the Vo Nguyen Giap beachfront strip. For the most Vietnamese nightlife experience: the large local bars in Ngu Hanh Son district, cheap, loud, and genuinely local.
What time do bars close in Da Nang?
Most Da Nang bars close between 1–2am on weeknights. On Friday and Saturday nights, closing times push to 2–3am at the busier expat venues. Sky36 at Novotel closes at 2am daily. Bamboo 2 Bar on the riverfront regularly runs past 2am. New Golden Pine is one of the city's latest-closing options. There's no enforced citywide closing time, most venues wrap naturally by 2am.
