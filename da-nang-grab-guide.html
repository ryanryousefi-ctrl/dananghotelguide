Landing In Da Nang

Exit the arrivals hall and walk about 50 metres to the zone signed Xe Cong Nghe (ride-hailing), near Lane 1-2 at the international terminal, around pillars 2-3 at the domestic terminal. It's separate from the taxi rank, and there's a Grab help desk and free wifi there. Typical wait for an assigned car is 2-10 minutes.

The terminal building blocks signal in places, so book once you're outside. See the Da Nang airport guide for the full arrivals walkthrough, including where taxis queue if you'd rather skip the app.

Local Tip

Don't book while you're still wandering through baggage claim. Get outside, figure out exactly where you are relative to the signed zone, then book. Confirm the pin matches your actual spot before you tap confirm.

Late Arrivals

Grab runs 24 hours, but driver availability thins out after midnight. If you land after 1am, budget for a possible 10-15 minute wait. Metered taxis (Vinasun and Mai Linh, both legitimate and metered) queue outside arrivals as a reliable backup.

GrabFood

GrabFood in Da Nang

The Feature Tourists Underestimate

This is one of the most useful parts of Grab for a visitor. Vietnamese food, pizza, burgers, Korean, Japanese, coffee, breakfast, dessert, late-night food: ordered from a menu with photos and delivered by motorbike, usually inside 30 minutes.

Worth Understanding

Delivery Is Normal Here

In Vietnam, food delivery can become part of everyday life in a way that surprises visitors from countries where delivery means high menu markups, big fees, tips, slow service, and a $40 dinner becoming a $60 one.

Da Nang's density, the sheer number of restaurants packed into a small area, a motorbike delivery culture that's been running for years, lower delivery costs, and short urban distances make delivery unusually practical here.

For a lot of residents, ordering coffee, lunch, dinner, dessert, or groceries through the app is completely routine, not a treat, not a splurge, just how the day works.

Hotel Delivery

Can GrabFood Deliver to My Hotel?

Yes.

Set your hotel as the delivery address. Drivers generally deliver to the building, then wait outside, at the entrance, or at reception, depending on the hotel's policy. Not every driver will come up to your room.

Our Take

GrabUnlimited Stops Feeling Like a Transport App

For someone living in Vietnam or spending real time here, GrabUnlimited is an easy purchase, because Grab stops being just a transport cost. It's rides, food, groceries, and delivery all in one place.

For a traveler staying several days and actually using all three (a ride, a food order, a Mart run), the membership can pay for itself quickly. It doesn't always pay for itself. If you're barely ordering anything, skip it.

Always Check Offers Before You Pay

Grab often runs member benefits, restaurant promotions, ride offers, and merchant deals alongside GrabCoins. GrabUnlimited discounts usually apply automatically at checkout, but extra offers can sometimes be selected manually depending on current eligibility. Before you tap order, check the offers screen.

Paying

How You Pay

Card

Link a card in-app for cashless rides and orders. Most major international cards work, but occasional declines happen, so have a backup ready.

Cash

Still widely accepted with drivers. If your card isn't cooperating, tell the driver "cash" when they arrive and round up to the nearest 10,000₫ note.

GrabPay

Top up an in-app wallet to skip card declines entirely. Useful if you're staying long enough to make setup worthwhile.

If you haven't topped up GrabPay and aren't sure your card will charge correctly, pay cash. Prices display in VND before you confirm either way.

Hoi An

Can I Take Grab to Hoi An?

Yes.

The outbound trip is straightforward: book from Da Nang like any other ride, 45-60 minutes, roughly $12-18 by GrabCar. Getting back is the less predictable part. Fewer drivers circulate in Hoi An's old town, and afternoon surge pricing is common.

If you hate uncertainty, arrange a private return transfer through your hotel before you leave Da Nang. See the Hoi An transport guide for the full breakdown.

Local Tip

For Hoi An, think about your return transportation before you book the outbound leg. A booked return removes the one part of the day that can actually go sideways.

Vinasun and Mai Linh are the two reputable metered taxi companies in Da Nang, both using tamper-sealed meters. Fares run comparable to GrabCar on most routes. The main difference is you don't see the final price until you arrive. Avoid unmarked taxis or drivers who approach you first in tourist areas; these are consistently reported overcharging foreign visitors.

Local Perspective

Ryan's Take on Getting Around Da Nang

I use Grab almost daily. For anything involving luggage, a group, or an airport run, it's the obvious choice.

For solo hops around My Khe or into the city, I often take GrabBike. It's fast, cheap, and in light rain you barely get wet if the trip is under 10 minutes.

The one situation where I skip Grab entirely is late-night DIFF. After the fireworks end, the Han River area sees a surge of people all trying to book rides at once. Prices triple. I walk a kilometre away from the crowd first, then book. Prices are usually back to normal within 15 minutes of clearing the main area.

If you're going to Hoi An for a day trip, Grab works fine outbound. Getting back is trickier: availability in the old town is lower, and surge is common in the late afternoon. I either book a return private transfer or accept the ride home might cost $15-20 on a busy day.

Common Questions

Grab in Da Nang FAQ

Does Grab work in Da Nang?

Yes. Grab is the standard ride, food delivery, and grocery delivery app across Da Nang, covering the city and the beach strip from Son Tra down past Non Nuoc. It runs 24 hours, though driver availability thins out after midnight.

Can I use Grab from the airport?

Yes. Exit arrivals, walk about 50 metres to the zone signed Xe Cong Nghe (ride-hailing), and book once you're outside with signal. It's a separate area from the taxi rank. Typical wait is 2-10 minutes.

Can foreigners use Grab in Vietnam?

Yes. You don't need a Vietnamese phone number to register. An international number works. Download the app and add a payment method before you land so you're ready at arrivals.

Can GrabFood deliver to my hotel?

Yes. Add your hotel as the delivery address and include the hotel name in the notes. Drivers typically wait at the entrance or reception rather than coming up to your room, so watch the driver's location and be ready to come down.

What is GrabMart?

GrabMart delivers from convenience stores and supermarkets: water, snacks, toiletries, sunscreen, fruit, basic groceries. Useful if you're staying somewhere for several days and don't want to make a supply run.

Is GrabUnlimited worth it?

Depends how much you use Grab. Staying a week or more and using GrabCar, GrabFood, and GrabMart regularly, the discounts can cover the membership fee within a few orders. For a two or three day trip with light use, probably not worth the setup.

Can I take Grab to Hoi An?

Yes, the outbound trip is straightforward. Coming back is less predictable. Fewer drivers circulate in the old town, especially in late afternoon. If you don't want to gamble on availability, arrange a return transfer in advance.

GrabBike or GrabCar?

GrabBike for solo trips with light bags: cheaper and faster through traffic. GrabCar for luggage, groups, rain, or the airport. Split between two people, GrabCar often costs about the same per person as GrabBike.
