Weather Guide · Da Nang · 2026

Worst Time to Visit Da Nang

October and November. Not the whole answer — but that's the short version. What the bad months actually look like.

By Ryan Yousefi · Updated May 2026

The worst months to visit Da Nang are October and November. These are not months where it rains a bit in the afternoon — this is Central Vietnam's typhoon corridor at full strength. Multi-day downpours, rough seas, flooding in low-lying areas near the Han River, beach closed, no swimming. If your trip is beach-centred and you have date flexibility, avoid these months.

That said, there are degrees. A week in late October might be fine. A week that catches a typhoon landfall will not. This page breaks down what each bad-weather month actually looks like so you can make a real decision.

Month-by-Month: The Bad Weather Months

Month Conditions Beach Verdict

What Actually Happens During Da Nang Rainy Season

Having lived here since 2022 and been through three rainy seasons: October is not "a bit wet." When a front sits over Central Vietnam, it rains without stopping for 3–5 days at a time. Not tropical-downpour-then-sunshine rain. Continuous grey rain, the road outside the hotel floods to 30cm in some areas, and the beach is off-limits with red flags up the whole strip.

The Han River District and Ngu Hanh Son District have lower-lying areas that flood. Most hotels are above the flood line, but getting around the city during heavy rain by foot is unpleasant. Grab still works but takes longer.

September is a risk, not a disaster. You might get a good week. You might arrive to a tropical storm. The probability is meaningfully higher than April. If you're travelling with children or have mobility concerns, September is not worth the gamble.

Typhoons

Central Vietnam sits in the typhoon corridor. Da Nang's typhoon season is roughly August to November, peaking in October. Most years, storms track slightly north or south without a direct landfall on Da Nang itself — but there are exceptions. [VERIFY: recent years' significant typhoon events near Da Nang]

A typhoon near-miss means 48 hours of strong wind and heavy rain even if the eye passes 100km away. Hotels remain open. Flights sometimes divert or cancel. Beach is off-limits for the duration. There is no prediction reliable enough to book around a specific storm — the risk is structural to the season.

If You Have to Visit in Bad-Weather Months

Sometimes dates are fixed. Here's how to make it work:

  • Base yourself in Hoi An instead of Da Nang. Hoi An (25 min south) is equally affected by rain but better built for non-beach activities: Ancient Town, tailors, cooking classes, river boat trips. It is less depressing when it's raining.
  • Book a city hotel, not a beach resort. Paying resort prices for a hotel you can't use the pool or beach at is not value. The Novotel Premier Han River or similar city-centre hotel is the right call — you get Level 36 for sunsets and Han Market for mornings regardless of weather.
  • Indoor activities that work: Ba Na Hills (30 min west, above cloud level sometimes — cool and different), cooking classes, My Son Sanctuary (covered temples, 1.5h south), marble carving shops at Marble Mountains (indoor), spas.
  • Plan for flexible accommodation. Book with free cancellation. If a typhoon tracks toward Da Nang, the right call is to not go. Hotels understand this and Booking.com's free-cancellation policy is your protection.

Shoulder Season: September and Late February

Not all of Da Nang's off-peak period is bad weather. Two shoulder windows work reasonably well:

  • Late February to mid-March: Prices are 20–30% lower than peak, it is drier than you'd expect, and the beach is usually swimmable by March. A gamble on February is often worth it for the price difference.
  • Early September: The first week or two of September often still have decent beach conditions before the rain really sets in. If this is your window, the savings vs peak are real. Watch the typhoon forecast in the 10 days before you travel.

Stuck with October or November dates?

Book a city hotel with free cancellation. The Novotel Premier Han River works regardless of weather — the Dragon Bridge fireworks run year-round on weekends, Han Market is covered, and Level 36 has good views even through cloud cover.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the worst time to visit Da Nang?+

October and November are the worst months for Da Nang. This is peak rainy season — the Central Vietnam coast gets heavy rainfall, flooding is possible in low-lying areas, and the sea is rough and unsafe for swimming. Typhoons are a real risk through mid-November.

Does Da Nang have a rainy season?+

Yes. Da Nang's rainy season runs from September to January, with October and November being the worst months. Unlike Da Lat or Ho Chi Minh City which get afternoon showers, Central Vietnam gets heavy prolonged rain that can last days. Some years see coastal flooding.

Is December a bad time to visit Da Nang?+

December is not ideal for beach activities. It is cooler (22-25°C), the sea is rough, and there is moderate rain risk. However, it is not as bad as October-November. If you must travel in this period, Hoi An (25 min south, less beach-dependent) makes a better base.

Is September safe in Da Nang?+

Early September can still be good beach weather. The risk increases significantly from mid-September as the typhoon season peaks and rain begins. If travelling in September, book with free cancellation and monitor the typhoon forecast in the 10 days before you travel.
