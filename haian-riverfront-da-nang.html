HAIAN Riverfront Hotel Da Nang

City Hotel · Han River Area

A city-center hotel near the Han River with an indoor pool and spa, built for travelers who want to be downtown rather than at the beach.

A note on how this page was built: we have not personally stayed at HAIAN Riverfront Hotel Da Nang. This profile is compiled from the hotel's published amenities and the pattern of verified guest reviews on major booking platforms, not a firsthand inspection. Where we're not confident in a detail, we've left it out rather than guess. Treat this as a research-backed overview to help you decide whether to look closer, not a substitute for reading recent guest reviews yourself before booking.

What the Listings Show

HAIAN Riverfront Hotel sits in Da Nang's city center with a view toward the river, a short walk from Indochina Riverside Mall and roughly 2,297 feet from the Cham Museum. Guest review scores are consistently strong, with a review base in the four figures, unusually high for a property at this price point.

Listed facilities include an on-site spa (Aroma Spa, offering manicure/pedicure, body treatments and body scrubs), an indoor pool, and free WiFi throughout. Guest feedback repeatedly calls out attentive staff, clean rooms, and the central location as the strongest points.

This is a city hotel, not a resort: the pitch across every listing is proximity to Da Nang's downtown riverside sights, not beach access. Treat it as a base for exploring the Han River area, Dragon Bridge, and the city's dining scene.

Who It's Likely Right For

Who It's Likely Right For

Based on the location and amenities, this fits travelers whose priority is the city center: proximity to Dragon Bridge, the riverside dining and bar strip, and the Cham Museum, rather than beach time. The indoor pool and spa make it a reasonable rainy-season pick too, when a beach stay loses some of its appeal anyway.

Who should look elsewhere: anyone whose Da Nang trip is built around My Khe or Non Nuoc beach time. My Khe Beach is about 3.8 km away, roughly a 10-minute taxi ride, not walkable, and this hotel isn't trying to be a beach hotel.

The Honest Gap in This Page

We can tell you what the hotel advertises and what guest review patterns suggest. We can't yet tell you how good the breakfast actually is, whether traffic noise from the riverside road reaches the rooms, or how the indoor pool compares to photos. Those are the kinds of details that only come from an actual stay, and we'd rather say that plainly than invent them.

Location

Location

Da Nang city center, near the Han River, a short walk from Indochina Riverside Mall and about 2,297 feet from the Cham Museum. Dragon Bridge and the riverside restaurant and bar strip are both close by. My Khe Beach is about 3.8 km away, roughly a 10-minute taxi or Grab ride, not within walking distance.
